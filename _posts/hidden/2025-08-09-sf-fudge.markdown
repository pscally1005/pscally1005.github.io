Description: Fudge made simple and healthy!  With just 3 ingredients, a microwave, and a few minutes of time, you can whip up this easy dessert for a crowd. The <a href="/recipes/chocolate-bar">unsweetened chocolate</a> and <a href="/misc/natural-peanut-butter">peanut butter</a> are full of <a href="/misc/fats">healthy fats</a> and <a href="/misc/fiber">fiber</a>, and the lack of <a href="/misc/hidden-sugar">added sugar</a> prevents <a href="/misc/diabetes">blood sugar spikes</a> and <a href="/misc/insulin-resistance">energy crashes</a>.  A <a href="/misc/fats">higher fat</a> and <a href="/misc/carbs">lower carb</a> snack is a great way to keep you feeling satiated throughout the day.  For other fudge recipes, check out my <a href="/recipes/oatmeal-fudge-bars">Peanut Butter Fudge Bars</a> and <a href="/recipes/keto-fudge">Bittersweet Keto Fudge</a>
Instructions: 
- Line a small airtight container with parchment paper. <a href="https://amzn.to/3ITznMk">These</a> are the ones I have; they're 6.2" x 4.5" 

- Break your chocolate into chunks, and add to a medium microwave safe bowl. Microwave in 30 second intervals, stirring in between, until just fully melted, about 2 minutes total
- <center><img src="/assets/Food/Finger Food/SF Fudge/melted.jpg" alt="" class="instruction-image"></center>

- You can also replace the <a href="/recipes/chocolate-bar">100% chocolate bar</a> with 1/4 cup (56 g) melted coconut oil and 11 tbsp (56 g) cocoa powder

- Add the <a href="/recipes/natural-peanunt-butter">peanut butter</a> and <a href="/recipes/sugar-free-syrup">sugar free syrup</a> to the melted chocolate, and stir with a <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0C37QM1K3?ref=t_ac_view_request_product_image&campaignId=amzn1.campaign.1OO1S5W7ZMYBB&linkCode=tr1&tag=poormanprotei-20&linkId=amzn1.campaign.1OO1S5W7ZMYBB_1751469633605">silicone spatula</a> until fully combined
- <center><img src="/assets/Food/Finger Food/SF Fudge/bowl.jpg" alt="" class="instruction-image"></center>

- I've gone with <a href="/misc/nuts#peanuts">peanut butter</a> here, but <a href="/misc/nuts#almonds">almond butter</a>, <a href="/misc/nuts#cashews">cashew butter</a>, <a href="/misc/seeds#sesame-seeds">tahini</a>, or any other <a href="/recipes/natural-peanut-butter">nut/seed butter</a> will totally work

- Spread the fudge batter into your container. Optionally top with flaky salt
- <center><img src="/assets/Food/Finger Food/SF Fudge/pan.jpg" alt="" class="instruction-image"></center>

- Cover with a lid, and let rest on the counter for 4 hours to set before slicing. Store fudge at <b>room temperature</b>; not in the fridge
- <center><img src="/assets/Food/Finger Food/SF Fudge/cut.jpg" alt="" class="instruction-image"></center>
