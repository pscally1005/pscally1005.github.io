Description: These easy to prepare protein bars pack 18 g of <a href="/misc/protein">protein</a> in just 179 <a href="/misc/calories">calories</a>, require only 9 ingredients and 10 minutes to put together, and can be easily taken on the go.  They're <a href="/misc/hidden-sugar">sugar free</a>, gluten free, a good source of both <a href="/misc/fats">healthy fats</a> and <a href="/misc/fiber">fiber</a>, and of course high in <a href="/misc/protein">protein</a>.  They also boast a high contents of the micronutrients <a href="/misc/nutrient-alphabet#R">riboflavin</a>, <a href="/misc/vitamin-b12">vitamin B12</a>, <a href="/misc/nutrient-alphabet#E">vitamin E</a>, calcium, <a href="/misc/magnesium">magnesium</a>, <a href="/misc/nutrient-alphabet#M">manganese</a>, and <a href="/misc/nutrient-alphabet#P">phosphorus</a>.  These protein bars will be my simple protein source on the go during my upcoming vacation, as they hold together quite well during travel. Instructions: 
- Preheat your oven to 350F, and line a small pan with parchment paper. The one I'm using is 10" x 6", though a standard 9 x 5" bread pan will also work. The protein bars will just be a little thicker, so they may require an additional few minutes of baking time

- In a medium bowl, combine together all ingredients until you have a thick brownie batter, almost like a spread. Transfer to the pan
- <center><img src="/assets/Food/Protein Powder/Baked Protein Bars/raw.jpg" alt="" class="instruction-image"></center>

- Any <a href="/recipes/natural-peanut-butter">nut butter</a> will work.  I've gone with almond today because it was what I had on hand, but cashew butter, peanut butter, tahini, sunflower seed butter, etc. would all be good as well

- My protein powder is unflavored, but if yours is flavored then omit the sweetener

- You can also use milk (dairy or non-dairy) will work in place of water

- Bake at 350F for about 30 minutes, or until a toothpick to the center comes out clean. Let cool totally in the pan before cutting into bars. Store protein bars in the fridge or freezer in an airtight bag
