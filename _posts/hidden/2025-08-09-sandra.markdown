Description: This protein banana bread is made with oat flour and is sweetened with sugar free syrup. Oh and it's not my recipe lol, it's my brother's girlfriend's (with some slight deviations). They brought it over one time and I knew I had to try making it for myself because it was very good. This healthy banana bread is nearly identical in taste and texture to the classic, while being higher in <a href="/misc/protein">protein</a> and <a href="/misc/fiber">fiber</a>, lower in <a href="/misc/calories">calories</a>, and with no <a href="/misc/hidden-sugar">added sugar</a>
Instructions: 
- Preheat your oven to 350F. Line a 9x5" bread pan with parchment paper, and lightly grease the paper

- Mash your banana with the back of a fork.  Whisk in the rest of the wet ingredients - yogurt, syrup, milk, and vanilla

- In a separate bowl, whisk together the dry ingredients - oat flour, whey protein, baking soda, cinnamon, baking powder, salt, and ginger
- <center><img src="/assets/Food/Meme/Sandra/both.jpg" alt="" class="instruction-image"></center>

- Add the wet ingredients into the dry, and mix with a <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0C37QM1K3?ref=t_ac_view_request_product_image&campaignId=amzn1.campaign.1OO1S5W7ZMYBB&linkCode=tr1&tag=poormanprotei-20&linkId=amzn1.campaign.1OO1S5W7ZMYBB_1751469633605">silicone spatula</a> until just fully combined. Don't over mix. Optionally, fold in 1/2 cup (85 g) chopped walnuts or chocolate chips
- <center><img src="/assets/Food/Meme/Sandra/bowl.jpg" alt="" class="instruction-image"></center>

- Transfer the batter to the pan, and smooth out the top
- <center><img src="/assets/Food/Meme/Sandra/raw.jpg" alt="" class="instruction-image"></center>

- Bake at 350F for about 40 minutes. Without opening the door, turn off the oven, and let sit in there for 5 minutes. The banana bread should be lightly browned, where a toothpick comes out nearly clean and the internal temperature is around 205F
- <center><img src="/assets/Food/Meme/Sandra/baked.jpg" alt="" class="instruction-image"></center>

- Transfer to a wire rack to totally cool (at least 1 hour) before slicing
