Description: This homemade, high fiber chicory root syrup is extremely low in calories and high in fiber, while being slightly sweet and flavored with vanilla. The secret is inulin, a soluble fiber from the chicory root that benefits gut bacteria by acting as a prebiotic. This sugar free syrup is also calorie free, and works as a less sweet substitute for honey or maple syrup.
Instructions:
- Add water to a small mason jar. Microwave for about 60 seconds, or until the water is about 150F

- Add the inulin 1 tbsp at a time, cover, and shake to fully dissolve the inulin. Repeat until you've mixed in all the inulin

- Remove the lid, and microwave for 20 seconds. Add the liquid monk fruit, vanilla, and salt, and shake to fully combine

- Let the syrup rest at room temperature for at least 10 minutes before serving. Store in the fridge for up to 1 week
