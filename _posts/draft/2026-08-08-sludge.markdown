Description: It ain't stupid if it works.  3 ingredient, no bake, vegan, sugar free, and gluten free peanut butter chocolate protein bar?  This "sludge brick" is just a swirling of cocoa powder and powdered peanut butter and sugar free syrup, for a dead simple bar with 18 g of protein in 180 calories.
Instructions:
- In a small bowl, mix together cocoa powder and sugar free syrup with a silicone spatula. Set aside.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meme/Sludge/chocolate.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- In a small bowl, mix together powdered peanut butter and sugar free syrup with a silicone spatula. Set aside.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meme/Sludge/pb.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Line a small container with parchment paper. Add the chocolate mixture to the bottom, then the peanut butter on top. Lightly swirl together. Optionally top with flaky salt.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meme/Sludge/container.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Freeze for at least 2 hours before eating.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meme/Sludge/cover2.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- This whole bar has about as much caffeine as half a cup of coffee, so don't have it too late in the day. Alternatively, if you used carob instead of cocoa, it will be less bitter and caffeine free.
