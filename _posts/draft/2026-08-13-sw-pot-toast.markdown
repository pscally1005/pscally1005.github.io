Description: I just found this awesome idea the other day - sweet potato toast.  Just take some large slices of sweet potatoes, air fry (or toast) them, and then top with whatever you like!  What a great gluten-free and grain-free alternative to standard toast without typical storebought bread.
Instructions:
- Preheat your air fryer to 400F.

- Take a large sweet potato, and cut 4 large slices from the center, about 50 g each or 200 g total.  With the test of the sweet potato, you can cut them into cubes and roast them for a side with dinner.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Sw Pot Toast/uncut.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Sw Pot Toast/cut.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Optionally lightly spray the top with oil, and optionally sprinkle with flaky salt.

- Air fry at 400F for about 5 minutes, or until soft, tender, and lightly browned.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Sw Pot Toast/cooked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Top with whatever you got!
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Sw Pot Toast/topped.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
