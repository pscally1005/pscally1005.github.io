Description: Do you know those Carb Balance wraps? The low carb ones with a whopping 18 g of fiber per wrap, and only 60 calories? Well I'm making a copycat version of those today. Oat fiber is our star, which provides fiber for no calories, simply being the isolated fiber that you'd find in oats. Vital wheat gluten adds protein and structure, keeping these wraps from falling apart. These wraps are nutritionally equivalent to the storebought ones, containing nearly identical calories, protein, fat, fiber, and net carbs!
Instructions:
- Whisk together the dry ingredients in a large bowl - oat fiber, vital wheat gluten, whole wheat flour, salt, and baking powder.

- Add in the oil and water, and mix until you form a dough. Cover and let the dough rest in the fridge for 30 minutes.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/Carb Balance/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Divide the dough into 8 equal pieces, about <b>61 g</b> each. Roll into a 1/4" thick tortilla, about 8" in diameter.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/Carb Balance/raw.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Heat a large pan over medium-high heat. Cook a wrap for about 30 seconds on each side, or until lightly browned.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/Carb Balance/cooked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Transfer to a plate, and cover with a clean towel to prevent it from drying out. Repeat with the remaining tortillas.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/Carb Balance/cooled.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
