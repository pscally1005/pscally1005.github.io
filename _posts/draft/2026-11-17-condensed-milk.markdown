Description: Homemade sweetened condensed milk, but <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar free</a>! Using <a href='https://amzn.to/4zMyIm3'>nonfat milk powder</a> and <a href='/recipes/sugar-free-syrup'>sugar free syrup</a> turns this <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar bomb</a> pantry staple into a <a href='/misc/calories'>low calorie</a> and <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar free</a> ingredient! Try it out in my new <a href='/recipes/pumpkin-truffles'>High Fiber Pumpkin Truffles</a>!
Instructions:
- Add <a href='https://amzn.to/4zMyIm3'>powdered milk</a> to a <a href='https://amzn.to/46ccg8m'>medium pot</a> over medium heat. Toast for about 5 minutes, or until browned and smells like caramel. Stir frequently, or it will stick and burn.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Sweet Spreads/Condensed Milk/powder.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Remove from the heat, and add in the <a href='/recipes/sugar-free-syrup'>sugar free syrup</a> and <a href='https://amzn.to/43MkDqr'>vanilla extract</a>. Whisk vigorously to break up the <a href='https://amzn.to/4zMyIm3'>milk powder</a> clumps.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Sweet Spreads/Condensed Milk/potFinal.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- <a href='https://amzn.to/4q2FwWu'>Strain</a> into a <a href='https://amzn.to/4vamaBm'>mason jar</a> to remove any remaining clumps of <a href='https://amzn.to/4zMyIm3'>milk powder</a>. Let it cool uncovered at room temperature for 30 minutes before covering with a lid and transferring to the fridge.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Sweet Spreads/Condensed Milk/jarFinal.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- <a href='#recipe'>This recipe</a> is designed to make 14 oz (396 g) of condensed milk, enough to exactly substitute a standard size 14 oz (396 g) can of <a href='https://link.amazon/B0gAsxJJN'>sweetened condensed milk</a>.

- You can also use <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#honey'>honey</a> or <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#maple-syrup'>maple syrup</a> in place of <a href='/recipes/sugar-free-syrup'>sugar free syrup</a>, but this will add a signifcant amount of <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar</a> and <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a>.

- Compared to a standard can of <a href='https://link.amazon/B0gAsxJJN'>storebought sweetened condensed milk</a> (<b>2 tbsp, 39 g</b>), the homemade version with <a href='/recipes/sugar-free-syrup'>sugar free syrup</a> contains significantly fewer <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a> and <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a>, with no <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>added sugar</a>.
