Description: Do you know those carb balance wraps?  The ones that are low in calories with a ton of fiber?  Well this is my homemade version of them. It's a crepe batter made from psyllium husk that's extremely low calorie and high fiber. This recipe makes 1 medium sized tortilla that contains an astounding 7 g of fiber in a measly 11 calories.
Instructions:
- Preheat a medium nonstick pan over medium heat with a tiny spray of oil

- In a small bowl, whisk together psyllium husk, salt, and baking powder. Optionally add some garlic powder and onion powder (1/2 tsp, 1.5 g, each)

- Pour in the water and whisk together. Wait 60-90 seconds for the psyllium husk to begin to gel

- Pour your batter into the pan, and smooth out to an even and thin circle with the back of a spoon
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/Psyllium wrap/raw.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Over medium heat, cook the first side for 2-3 minutes, or until the edges look set, and the top has small bubbles and holes. Flip, and cook the other side for an additional 1-2 minutes
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/Psyllium wrap/cooking.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- |
  This recipe makes one high fiber wrap. You can meal prep multiple of these and store in the freezer for later use.<br><br>

  If making multiple ones at a time, I would recommend mixing each wrap's batter separately before cooking, instead of scaling up everything and simply adding to the bowl all together.<br><br>

  Doing so will cause too much of the psyllium to gel, and the batter will be too thick to spread in the pan for any wrap after the first one
