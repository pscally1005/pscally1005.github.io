Description: The "no" in this Nohito has a double meaning. Not only is it a mocktail, being alcohol free, but it's also sugar free, being sweetened with liquid monk fruit. The combination of fresh mint and lime makes a perfectly refreshing beverage for these hot summer months.
Instructions:
- Add your mint leaves and lime juice to a tall glass. Using the back of a wooden spoon, crush together the mint and lime

- Add liquid monk fruit, ice, and water to the glass. Stir together, and garnish with a like wedge
Notes:
- You can either use standard (flat) water or sparking water / club soda / seltzer
