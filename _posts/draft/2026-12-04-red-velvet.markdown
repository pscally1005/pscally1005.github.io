Description: Each of these red velvet cheesecake bars comes in at under 160 calories with nearly 17 g of protein. That makes these bars high protein, in addition to being sugar free, gluten free, and naturally dyed. For a similar protein bar recipe, check out my High Protein Brownie Cheesecake Bars!
Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 350F. Line a 9" square baking pan with parchment paper. Lightly grease it with oil.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Protein Powder/Red Velvet/red-velvet-bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- In large bowl, mix together all the red velvet ingredients until just fully combined with a silicone spatula.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Protein Powder/Red Velvet/red-velvet-bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- In a separate large bowl, mix together all the cheesecake ingredients until just fully combined with a silicone spatula.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Protein Powder/Red Velvet/cheesecake-bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Pour about 3/4 of your red velvet batter into the pan, about <b>593 g</b> or <b>2 1/2 cups</b>.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Protein Powder/Red Velvet/red-velvet-pan.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Evenly and carefully spread the cheesecake mixture over the top of the red velvet layer.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Protein Powder/Red Velvet/cheesecake-pan.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Dollop the rest of your red velvet batter over the cheesecake batter, lightly swirling it in with a butter knife.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Protein Powder/Red Velvet/raw.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Bake at 350F for about 20-25 minutes, or until the edges are set and the top is still slightly soft.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Protein Powder/Red Velvet/baked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Transfer the pan to the fridge. Let it cool for at least 1 hour before cutting into 9 squares.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Protein Powder/Red Velvet/cut.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- Either granulated monk fruit or allulose will work for your sweetener.

- Pickled beet juice is used to slightly dye the red velvet layer without the use of synthetic food dyes.
