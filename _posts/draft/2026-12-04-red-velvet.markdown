Description: Each of these red velvet cheesecake barscomes in at under 160 <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a> with nearly 17 g of <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a>. That makes these bars <a href='/misc/high-protein'>high protein</a>, in addition to being <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar free</a>, <a href='/misc/celiac'>gluten free</a>, and naturally dyed. For a similar <a href='/protein-bar'>protein bar recipe</a>, check out my <a href='/recipes/protein-cheesecake-brownies'>High Protein Brownie Cheesecake Bars</a>!
Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 350F. Line a <a href='https://amzn.to/3YY2H9q'>9" square baking pan</a> with parchment paper. <a href='https://link.amazon/B00fjxkKE'>Lightly grease it with oil</a>.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Protein Powder/Red Velvet/red-velvet-bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- In <a href='https://amzn.to/4zyFnzN'>large bowl</a>, mix together all the red velvet ingredients until just fully combined with a <a href='https://amzn.to/40F2aK0'>silicone spatula</a>.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Protein Powder/Red Velvet/red-velvet-bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- In a separate <a href='https://amzn.to/4zyFnzN'>large bowl</a>, mix together all the cheesecake ingredients until just fully combined with a <a href='https://amzn.to/40F2aK0'>silicone spatula</a>.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Protein Powder/Red Velvet/cheesecake-bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Pour about 3/4 of your red velvet batter into the pan, about <b>593 g</b> or <b>2 1/2 cups</b>.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Protein Powder/Red Velvet/red-velvet-pan.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Evenly and carefully spread the <a href='/recipes/easy-cheesecake'>cheesecake</a> mixture over the top of the red velvet layer.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Protein Powder/Red Velvet/cheesecake-pan.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Dollop the rest of your red velvet batter over the cheesecake batter, lightly swirling it in with a butter knife.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Protein Powder/Red Velvet/raw.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Bake at 350F for about 20-25 minutes, or until the edges are set and the top is still slightly soft.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Protein Powder/Red Velvet/baked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Transfer the pan to the fridge. Let it cool for at least 1 hour before cutting into 9 squares.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Protein Powder/Red Velvet/cut.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- Either <a href='https://amzn.to/4ceFnvP'>granulated monk fruit</a> or <a href='https://amzn.to/4mzsH5p'>allulose</a> will work for your sweetener.

- Pickled beet juice is used to slightly dye the red velvet layer without the use of synthetic food dyes.
