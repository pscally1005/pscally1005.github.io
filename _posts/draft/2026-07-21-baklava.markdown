Description: |
  What makes this baklava healthier?  Firstly, it's <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar free</a>, using <a href='/recipes/sugar-free-syrup'>sugar free syrup</a> in the syrup you pour on top instead of <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>white sugar</a> and <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#honey'>honey</a>.<br><br>

  Secondly, it's <a href='/misc/dairy#butter'>butter</a> free. Instead of brushing each layer with melted <a href='/misc/dairy#butter'>butter</a>, you simply lightly brush them with water.<br><br>

  The result is a delicious and nutty <a href='/recipes/healthier-dessert'>dessert</a> that's a good source of <a href='/misc/fats'>healthy fats</a> and <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a> from the <a href='/misc/nuts'>nuts</a>!<br><br>

  The <a href='/misc/nuts#pistachios'>pistachios</a> are crunchy and the <a href='https://amzn.to/4uKqFT3'>phyllo</a> is light and crispy for a warming and cinnamon flavored dessert.
Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 350F. Line a <a href='https://amzn.to/4aiCsjh'>9x13" pan</a> with parchment paper, and <a href='https://amzn.to/3Hdg0gk'>lightly grease with oil</a>.  Prepare a small glass of water

- Use a <a href='https://amzn.to/4q0AUjI'>food processor</a> to roughly chop your <a href='/misc/nuts#pistachios'>pistachios</a>, and transfer to an empty bowl. Mix with <a href='/recipes/sugar-free-syrup'>sugar free syrup</a>, cinnamon, and cloves to make a sticky filling

- Add a <a href='https://amzn.to/4uKqFT3'>phyllo sheet</a> to the base of the pan, folding the edges if necessary.  Lightly brush with water.  Repeat for a total of 5 sheets

- Wearing a <a href='https://amzn.to/414BfHP'>kitchen glove</a> to prevent sticking, crumble about 1/4 of your pistachio mixture (~85 g or 1/3 cup) onto the <a href='https://amzn.to/4uKqFT3'>phyllo</a>, lightly pressing into the dough
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Baklava/make.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- |
  Repeat with the layering and brushing for 4 more sheets, and then the next 1/4 of your <a href='/misc/nuts'>nut</a> mixture.  Do this 3 times, so you have 4 "sections" of <a href='https://amzn.to/4uKqFT3'>phyllo</a>, with 5, 4, 4, and 4 layers of sheets respectively, for a total of 17 <a href='https://amzn.to/4uKqFT3'>phyllo sheets</a>.<br><br>

  Each "section" will be separated by 1/4 of your nut mixture.  Top the final <a href='https://amzn.to/4uKqFT3'>phyllo sheet</a> with your remaining pistachio mix

- Using a sharp <a href='https://amzn.to/4jlDKwc'>knife</a>, cut your baklava into 24 squares
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Baklava/cut.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Bake for 30 minutes at 350F.  Lower the oven to 300F, and bake for an additional 45-60 minutes.  The top will be lightly browned, dried, and flaky, and the baklava will separate from each other.  Let it cool on the counter for 15 minutes
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Baklava/no-syrup.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Microwave your water and <a href='/recipes/sugar-free-syrup'>sugar free syrup</a> in a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>small bowl</a> for 2 minutes, covered with a plate. Pour on top of the baked baklava
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Baklava/with-syrup.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Chill in the fridge for at least 1 hour before eating.  Either eat cold, or air fry for 3 minutes at 400F to make it crispier
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Baklava/cover2.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Baklava/cover3.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:

  You can find <a href='https://amzn.to/4uKqFT3'>phyllo dough</a> in the frozen food section, or you can order it on Amazon <a href='https://amzn.to/4uKqFT3'>here</a>.<br><br>

  My pack of <a href='https://amzn.to/4uKqFT3'>phyllo</a> is 16 oz (454 g), and contains 17 sheets.  You should have 4 "sections" of layered sheets, where the bottom one has 5 <a href='https://amzn.to/4uKqFT3'>phyllo</a> sheets, and the other 3 each have 4 pieces of <a href='https://amzn.to/4uKqFT3'>phyllo</a> layered on each other.<br><br>

  You'll have <a href='/misc/nuts#pistachios'>pistachio</a> filling between each of the 4 sections and on top of the final one.
