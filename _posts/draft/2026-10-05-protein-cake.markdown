Description: |
  Happy Birthday to me 🎂 🎉!  How can you have your cake and eat it too on a <a href='/misc/high-protein'>high protein</a>, <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>low sugar</a> diet?  With this cake of course!  With <a href='/misc/dairy#whey'>protein powder</a>, <a href='/misc/dairy#yogurt'>Greek yogurt</a>, and <a href='/misc/meat#egg-whites'>egg whites</a> in the cake itself, plus more <a href='/misc/dairy#whey'>protein powder</a> and <a href='/misc/dairy#cottage-cheese'>cottage cheese</a> in the <a href='/recipes/protein-frosting'>frosting</a>, this cake will have you questioning ever eating a <a href='/protein-bar'>protein bar</a> again.<br><br>

  The <a href='/misc/veggies#pumpkin'>pumpkin</a> provides moistness without the need for <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a> like <a href='/misc/dairy#butter'>butter</a> or <a href='/misc/olive-oil'>oil</a>. The <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oat flour</a> makes it <a href='/misc/celiac'>gluten free</a>, and the <a href='https://amzn.to/3SqwsMO'>liquid monk fruit</a> and vanilla <a href='/misc/dairy#whey'>protein powder</a> provide just enough sweetness to round out this delicious and <a href='/recipes/healthier-dessert'>healthy dessert</a> for all to enjoy!
Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 350F. Line 2 <a href='https://amzn.to/4mxxkxl'>9" circle cake pans</a> with parchment paper, and <a href='https://amzn.to/3Hdg0gk'>lightly grease</a> them with <a href='/misc/olive-oil'>oil</a>

- Add the wet ingredients to a bowl, and mix with a <a href='https://amzn.to/40F2aK0'>silicone spatula</a> until fully combined.

- Add in dry ingredients, and mix with a <a href='https://amzn.to/40F2aK0'>silicone spatula</a> until fully combined
- <img src='/assets/Food/Protein Powder/Protein Cake/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Divide the mixture evenly between the 2 pans, about 2 cups (480 g) each
- <img src='/assets/Food/Protein Powder/Protein Cake/raw.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Bake at 350F for about 20-30 minutes, or until the top is set, the edges are browned, a toothpick to the center comes out clean, and the <a href='https://amzn.to/4kmobG2'>internal temperature</a> is about <a href='https://amzn.to/4kmobG2'>205F</a>. Let the cake cool totally in the pan before removing, about 1 hour
- <img src='/assets/Food/Protein Powder/Protein Cake/baked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- In a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>medium bowl</a>, mix together your (blended) <a href='/misc/dairy#cottage-cheese'>cottage cheese</a> with vanilla <a href='/misc/dairy#whey'>whey</a> and vanilla extract to make your frosting
- <img src='/assets/Food/Protein Powder/Protein Cake/frosting.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Remove the 2 cakes from their pans. Spread about 1/3 of your frosting (~115 g) onto one cake. Top with the other cake. Frost the top and the sides of the cake
- <img src='/assets/Food/Protein Powder/Protein Cake/frosted.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- <a href='/misc/dairy#almond-milk'>Unsweetened almond milk</a> can be used in place of <a href='/misc/dairy#skim-milk'>skim milk</a> (same amount)

- 1/4 cup (50 g) <a href='https://amzn.to/4ceFnvP'>granulated monk fruit</a> can be used instead of <a href='https://amzn.to/3SqwsMO'>liquid monk fruit</a>

- 1 cup (112 g) <a href='/misc/nuts#almonds'>almond flour</a> can be used in place of <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oat flour</a>, which will make it finer, more moist, and lower in <a href='/misc/carbs'>carbs</a>, but higher in <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a> and <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a>

- I like vanilla <a href='/misc/dairy#whey'>protein powder</a> for both the cake and the <a href='/recipes/protein-frosting'>frosting</a>, but you can use <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>chocolate</a> for the cake itself, though I would keep the <a href='/recipes/protein-frosting'>frosting</a> with the vanilla
