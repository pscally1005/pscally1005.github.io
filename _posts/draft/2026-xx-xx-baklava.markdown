Description: |
  What makes this baklava healthy?  Firstly, it's <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar free</a>, using <a href='/recipes/sugar-free-syrup'>sugar free syrup</a> in the syrup you pour on top instead of <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>white sugar</a> and <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#honey'>honey</a>.<br><br>

  Secondly, it's <a href='/misc/dairy#butter'>butter</a> free. Instead of brushing each layer with melted <a href='/misc/dairy#butter'>butter</a>, you simply lightly dip them in water.<br><br>

  Finally, this homemade healthy baklava is <a href='/misc/celiac'>gluten free</a>, using <a href='https://amzn.to/3QbZba1'>rice paper</a> instead of Phyllo dough.<br><br>

  The result is a delicious and nutty <a href='/recipes/healthier-dessert'>dessert</a> that's a good source of <a href='/misc/fats'>healthy fats</a> and <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a> from the <a href='/misc/nuts'>nuts</a>!
Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 350F. Line a <a href='https://amzn.to/4q0gY0f'>9" pie pan</a> with parchment paper, and <a href='https://amzn.to/3Hdg0gk'>lightly grease with oil</a>

- Prepare a wide and flat bowl with cold water to dip your <a href='https://amzn.to/3QbZba1'>rice paper</a> into

- Use a <a href='https://amzn.to/4q0AUjI'>food processor</a> to roughly chop your <a href='/misc/nuts#pistachios'>pistachios</a>, and transfer to an empty bowl. Mix with <a href='/recipes/sugar-free-syrup'>sugar free syrup</a>, cinnamon, and cloves to make a sticky filling
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Baklava/filling.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Lightly dip a piece of <a href='https://amzn.to/3QbZba1'>rice paper</a> in water, and add to the base of your pan. Repeat until you have a total of 8 layered sheets
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Baklava/section-1-layers.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Sprinkle about 1/4 of your <a href='/misc/nuts#pistachios'>pistachio</a> mixture (~70 g or 1/3 cup) onto the <a href='https://amzn.to/3QbZba1'>rice paper</a>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Baklava/section-1-filling.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Repeat with the dipping and layering of 8 sheets and 1/4 of your <a href='/misc/nuts'>nut</a> mixture. Top the final <a href='https://amzn.to/3QbZba1'>rice paper</a> with your remaining 1/4 of your <a href='/misc/nuts#pistachios'>pistachio</a> mix
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Baklava/built.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Using a sharp <a href='https://amzn.to/4jlDKwc'>knife</a>, cut your bakalava into 16 pieces
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Baklava/cut.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Bake for about 40 minutes at 350F, or until the top is golden brown and a toothpick to the center comes out clean
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Baklava/baked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Microwave your water and <a href='/recipes/sugar-free-syrup'>sugar free syrup</a> in a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>small glass bowl</a> for 2 minutes. Pour on top of the baked Baklava
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Baklava/poured.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Chill in the fridge for at least 1 hour before eating
Notes:
- My pack of <a href='https://amzn.to/3QbZba1'>rice paper</a> contains 32 sheets of about 9" in diameter. So each of the 4 "sections" will contain 8 layered sheets of <a href='https://amzn.to/3QbZba1'>rice paper</a>

- You should have 4 "sections" each of 8 pieces of <a href='https://amzn.to/3QbZba1'>rice paper</a> layered on each other, with <a href='/misc/nuts#pistachios'>pistachio</a> filling between each section and on top of the final one.
