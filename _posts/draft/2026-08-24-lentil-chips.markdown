Description: These tortilla chips are <a href='/misc/celiac'>gluten free</a>, <a href='/misc/grains'>grain free</a>, and lower in <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a>. They're made with <a href='/misc/beans#red-lentils'>lentils</a>, which are a good source of <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a> and <a href='/misc/protein'>plant based protein</a>, and only use a minimal amount of <a href='/misc/olive-oil'>oil</a>, keeping them lower in <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a>. Pair them with <a href='/hummus'>hummus</a> or any of your favorite <a href='/recipes/savory-sauces'>dips</a>, and you have the perfect snack!
Instructions:
- Add <a href='/misc/beans#red-lentils'>lentils</a>, water, and salt to a <a href='https://amzn.to/3SsDLn7'>large blender</a>. Let the <a href='/misc/beans#red-lentils'>lentils</a> soak for 2 hours on the counter before blending until smooth.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Lentil Chips/blend.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Preheat your oven to 350F, and line a large <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>cookie sheet</a> with parchment paper. Pour the batter onto your prepared <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>baking sheet</a>, and spread it thin to the edges of the pan.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Lentil Chips/raw.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Bake at 350F for about 20 minutes, or until lightly golden brown, and the surface is set and dry to the touch. Transfer to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4qQNmn2'>cooling rack</a> for about 5 minutes.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Lentil Chips/bake1.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Cut your large <a href='/recipes/tortilla'>tortilla</a> into small triangles, about 96 chips. Season on the <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>cookie sheet</a> with <a href='/misc/olive-oil'>oil</a> and spices.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Lentil Chips/cut.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Return the chips to the oven at 350F for about 20 minutes, or until golden and crispy but not burnt.  Transfer to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4qQNmn2'>wire rack</a> to cool completely. They will crisp more as they cool. Store at room temperature for up to 1 week.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Lentil Chips/bake2.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- Any kind of <a href='/misc/beans#red-lentils'>lentils</a> will work here.  <a href='/misc/beans#brown-lentils'>Brown</a>, <a href='/misc/beans#red-lentils'>red</a>, <a href='/misc/beans#green-lentils'>green</a>; whatever you got.  I went with <a href='/misc/beans#brown-lentils'>brown lentils</a> here.

- To cut the large <a href='/recipes/tortilla'>tortilla</a> into 96 <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#chips'>chips</a>, what I did was cut it into quarters, then each quarter in half length-wise.  Then did 5 cuts to make 6 equal squares, and then cut each square in half diagonally.
