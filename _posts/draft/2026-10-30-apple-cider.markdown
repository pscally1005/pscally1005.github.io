Description: |
  Nothing is better than making apple spread from fresh picked apples in the fall. But that got me thinking: can I make apple cider using my homemade No Sugar Added Apple Spread?<br><br>

  Apple spread is just concentrated apples, like applesauce on crack. By reconstituting the apple spread with water, you can make a gram for gram homemade apple cider that sweet and rich in apple cinnamon flavor.
Instructions:
- Whisk together apple spread and water in a large bowl. Pour into a medium glass or mug.
