Description: <a href='/misc/grains#farro'>Farro</a> is an ancient wheat based <a href='/misc/grains'>grain</a> that cooks and tastes like <a href='/misc/grains#brown-rice'>brown rice</a>, but with a more chewy texture. This nutritious <a href='/misc/grains'>whole grain</a> is a great way to mix it up from your usual <a href='/misc/grains#white-rice'>rice</a>. I've paired it here with some roasted <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken breast</a>, roasted <a href='/misc/veggies#broccoli'>broccoli</a>, and <a href='/misc/dairy#goat-cheese'>goat cheese</a>. These bowls have over 50 g of <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> in under 550 <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a>, with plenty of complex <a href='/misc/carbs'>starches</a>, gut promoting <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a>, brain supporting healthy <a href='/misc/fats'>fats</a>, and delicious flavor
Instructions: 
- Add water and salt to a medium pot. Cover and bring to a boil over high heat. Add in the farro, cover, reduce heat to medium low, and let simmer for about 25 minutes, or until all water has absorbed
- <center><img src='/assets/Food/Chicken/Farro Bowls/farro-cooked.jpg' alt="" class="instruction-image"></center>

- Meanwhile, preheat your oven to 400F, and line a large cookie sheet with parchment paper. Toss the broccoli with lemon juice, oil, lemon pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, and black pepper. Roast at 400F for about 30 minutes, or until browned
- <center><img src='/assets/Food/Chicken/Farro Bowls/broccoli-roasted.jpg' alt="" class="instruction-image"></center>

- Cut your chicken into thin cutlets, and add to a large bowl. Season with vinegar, oil, garlic powder, onion powder, black pepper, and salt. Add to a parchment lined cookie sheet, and roast at 400F until the internal temperature is 165F (about 14 minutes). Let rest for a few minutes before dicing into bite sized pieces
- <center><img src='/assets/Food/Chicken/Farro Bowls/chicken-cooked.jpg' alt="" class="instruction-image"></center>

- Prepare 6 airtight containers, and evenly divide your food. I got about 150g, 125g, and 100g respectively of broccoli, farro, and chicken per bowl.  Top each bowl with 1 oz (28 g) of goat cheese
- <center><img src='/assets/Food/Chicken/Farro Bowls/containers.jpg' alt="" class="instruction-image"></center>
