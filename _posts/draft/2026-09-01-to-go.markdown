Description: This is my go to for when I'm traveling. I just mix together protein powder, sweetener, and flavoring in a Ziploc bag, and then all I need is water. This is especially good for flights, where I take an empty shaker bottle through security, dump in the powder, and fill up water at the water fountain. What a great way to get clean protein on the go without spending an arm and a leg for a protein bar filled with junk ingredients.
Instructions:
- Add all ingredients to a small airtight bag, and shake together.

- To make a protein shake, combine your dry mix with <b>1 1/2 cup (360 g) water</b>.
Notes:
- Either whey protein or pea protein will work here.  You can't use casein, as it will absorb too much liquid.

- I'm using unflavored protein powder with added cocoa powder, cinnamon, and monk fruit, but any flavor of protein powder (like vanilla or chocolate) will work in place of the unflavored.  With a flavored protein powder, you can omit the cocoa, cinnamon, and sweetener

- Milk powder is used to make up for the lack of milk, as without it, the protein shake would taste much too watery
