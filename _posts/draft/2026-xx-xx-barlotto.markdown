Description: |
  Instead of <a href='/misc/grains#white-rice'>white arborio rice</a>, I'm making a risotto inspired dish using <a href='/misc/grains#barley'>barley</a> instead. Being a <a href='/misc/grains'>whole grain</a>, <a href='/misc/grains#barley'>barley</a> is much higher in <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a>, <a href='/misc/nutrient-alphabet'>vitamins, minerals</a>, and <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> compared to standard <a href='/misc/grains#white-rice'>white rice</a>.<br><br>

  This barlotto is also vegetarian (depending on your broth), dairy free, and stirring free. What does that mean?  It means you won't have to labor over a pot of risotto, stirring continuously for an hour. Instead, you use your <a href='https://amzn.to/49TUS9E'>slow cooker</a> to make the <a href='/misc/grains#barley'>barley</a> creamy. Just check back after work, and you're good to go for this <a href='/recipes/sides'>side dish</a>!  For another risotto hybrid dish, check out my <a href='/recipes/butternut-squash-risotto'>Butternut Squash RisOATto</a> or <a href='/recipes/congee'>Brown Rice Congee Base</a>.
Instructions:
- The night before, soak your <a href='/misc/grains#barley'>barley</a> in 5 cups (1,200 g) water with 1 tbsp (18 g) salt. Cover, and refrigerate overnight

- In the morning, discard the soaking liquid. Add the soaked <a href='/misc/grains#barley'>barley</a> to a <a href='https://amzn.to/49TUS9E'>slow cooker</a> with broth, nutritional yeast, and black pepper

- Cover and cook on low for 8 hours, stirring occasionally
- <img src='/assets/Food/Sides/Barlotto/crockpot.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- Alternatively, instead of broth, you can use 1 tbsp (18 g) <a href='https://amzn.to/4c6GlII'>better than bouillon</a>, 1 tbsp (12 g) <a href='https://amzn.to/3QiSinj'>bouillon powder</a>, or 3 pieces (12 g) of <a href='https://amzn.to/4sUCq9c'>bouillon cubes</a>, plus the same amount (3 cups, 720 g) of water
