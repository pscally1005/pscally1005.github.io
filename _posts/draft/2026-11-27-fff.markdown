Description: |
  These pumpkin spice bars are perfect for those on a Keto diet, or for those in bulking season. This <a href='/misc/calories'>high calorie</a> snack is extremely high in <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a> and <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a>, while super low in <a href='/misc/carbs'>net carbs</a>.<br><br>

  They're packed with <a href='/misc/fats'>heart-healthy monounsaturated fats</a> from <a href='/misc/olive-oil'>extra virgin olive oil</a>, <a href='/misc/fiber#insoluble'>insoluble fiber</a> from <a href='https://link.amazon/B04N3ngTi'>oat fiber</a>, moisture and creaminess from the <a href='/recipes/pumpkin-puree'>pumpkin puree</a>, and pumpkin spice flavor from the cinnamon, ginger, nutmeg, and cloves.<br><br>

  Each of these 25 squares has 100 <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a> with 10 g of <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a> and 11 g of <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a>, with under 2 g of <a href='/misc/carbs'>net carbs</a>!
Instructions:
- Add <a href='/misc/olive-oil'>oil</a> to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4zyFnzN'>small bowl</a>, and microwave for 1 minute to warm it.

- Add the warm <a href='/misc/olive-oil'>oil</a> to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4zyFnzN'>large bowl</a> with the spices (cinnamon, ginger, nutmeg, cloves, and salt). Whisk together, and let it rest for about a minute so the spices can bloom in the <a href='/misc/olive-oil'>oil</a>.

- Add the wet ingredients (<a href='/recipes/pumpkin-puree'>pumpkin puree</a>, <a href='https://amzn.to/43MkDqr'>vanilla extract</a>, and <a href='https://amzn.to/3SqwsMO'>liquid monk fruit</a>) to the bowl. Whisk until smooth.

- Add the <a href='/misc/psyllium-husk'>psyllium husk</a> and <a href='https://link.amazon/B04N3ngTi'>oat fiber</a>. Fold with a <a href='https://amzn.to/40F2aK0'>silicone spatula</a> until fully combined.
- <img src='/assets/Food//Finger Food/FFF/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Transfer the mixture to a parchment-lined <a href='https://amzn.to/3YY2H9q'>9" square pan</a>.
- <img src='/assets/Food//Finger Food/FFF/pan.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Freeze for 1 hour before slicing into 25 squares.
- <img src='/assets/Food//Finger Food/FFF/cut.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
- <img src='/assets/Food//Finger Food/FFF/plate.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
