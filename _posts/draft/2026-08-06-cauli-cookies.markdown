Description: |
  I've already made Cauliflower Biscuits, so why not Cauliflower Cookies. I've taken the Cauliflower Biscuits recipe I made yesterday and swapped out the spices for cinnamon, sweeteners, and of course, chocolate.<br><br>

  The result is chewy, gooey, sweet, and slightly crispy lower calorie cookies. These are gluten free, sugar free, and vegan, making them a perfect snack!
Instructions:
- In a small bowl, combine together flax seed with sugar free syrup, vanilla extract, and liquid monk fruit to make your "flax egg". Let it sit for at least 5 minutes to thicken.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meme/Cauli cookies/flax.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Preheat your oven to 400F. Line a large <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>cookie sheet</a> with parchment paper.

- In batches, pulse your thawed cauliflower in a food processor until you end up with a rice consistency. Don't over blend, or it will end up watery and mushy. Transfer the cauliflower to a large bowl.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meme/Cauli cookies/cauli.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Add the almond flour, oat flour, baking powder, cinnamon, and salt to the bowl. Combine with a silicone spatula.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meme/Cauli cookies/dry.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Add in the flax egg, and combine to form a dough.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meme/Cauli cookies/dough.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Fold in your chocolate chips.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meme/Cauli cookies/chocolate.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Divide your dough into 9 equal balls, about <b>66 g</b> each. Add to the cookie sheet, lightly flatten down, and shape into a circle.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meme/Cauli cookies/raw.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Bake at 400F for about 30 minutes, or until the sides and bottoms are lightly browned and dried.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meme/Cauli cookies/baked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Let the cookies cool on the cookie sheet for 10 minutes before transferring to a wire rack.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meme/Cauli cookies/plate.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- If you have frozen riced <a href='/misc/veggies#cauliflower'>cauliflower</a>, you don't need to use a <a href='https://amzn.to/4q0AUjI'>food processor</a> at all.  Just combine in a bowl.
