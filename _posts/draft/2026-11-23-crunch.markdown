Description: These homemade Crunch bars combine rich chocolate with crispy popped quinoa for a delicious, crunchy treat. Made with just a handful of simple ingredients and naturally sweetened with honey, they're a homemade alternative to traditional Crunch bars with fewer calories and less added sugar.
Instructions:
- |
  Preheat a medium stainless steel pan over medium heat without any oil. Add the quinoa to the pan, and shake to a single layer. Cook with frequent shaking for about 30 seconds, or until the popping sounds slow down and the quinoa starts to smell nutty. Transfer to a plate to cool at room temperature for about 5 minutes.<br><br>

  For a more in-depth guide, check out my Single Serving Popped Quinoa.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Quinoa Crunch/quinoa.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Combine the melted coconut oil, cocoa powder, honey, vanilla, and salt in a large bowl, until smooth.

- Add the quinoa, and mix until fully combined with a silicone spatula.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Quinoa Crunch/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Evenly spread the mixture into a parchment-lined 9x5" bread pan, pressing it down firmly.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Quinoa Crunch/pan.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Chill in the fridge for 1 hour. Let it sit on the counter for 5 minutes before cutting into 8 squares. Store in the fridge.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Quinoa Crunch/cut.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- The homemade chocolate of 3 tbsp (41 g) coconut oil, 1/2 cup (40 g) cocoa powder, and 2 tbsp (42 g) honey has roughly the same sweetness of about 75% chocolate. Adjust the amount of honey to taste depending on how sweet you prefer your chocolate.

- I'm using honey here, but other good options are maple syrup, agave, or sugar free syrup.
