Description: |
  These homemade Crunch bars combine rich chocolate with crispy <a href='/recipes/popped-quinoa'>popped quinoa</a> for a delicious, crunchy treat. Made with just a handful of simple ingredients and naturally sweetened with <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#honey'>honey</a>, they're a homemade alternative to traditional <a href='https://link.amazon/B006FaR62'>Crunch bars</a> with fewer <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a> and less <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>added sugar</a>.<br><br>

  For another <a href='/recipes/popped-quinoa'>popped quinoa</a> recipe, you can make <a href='/recipes/quinoa-scotcheroos'>Popped Quinoa Scotcheroos</a> using <a href='/recipes/popped-quinoa'>popped quinoa</a>, <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>chocolate</a>, and <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a>!
Instructions:
- |
  Preheat a medium <a href='https://amzn.to/4pREQ61'>stainless steel pan</a> over medium heat without any oil. Add the <a href='/misc/grains#quinoa'>quinoa</a> to the pan, and shake to a single layer. Cook with frequent shaking for about 30 seconds, or until the popping sounds slow down and the <a href='/misc/grains#quinoa'>quinoa</a> starts to smell nutty. Transfer to a plate to cool at room temperature for about 5 minutes.<br><br>

  For a more in-depth guide, check out my <a href='/recipes/popped-quinoa'>Single Serving Popped Quinoa</a>.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Quinoa Crunch/quinoa.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Combine the melted <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut oil</a>, <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>cocoa powder</a>, <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#honey'>honey</a>, <a href='https://amzn.to/43MkDqr'>vanilla</a>, and salt in a <a href='https://amzn.to/4zyFnzN'>large bowl</a>, until smooth.

- Add the <a href='/recipes/popped-quinoa'>popped quinoa</a>, and mix until fully combined with a <a href='https://amzn.to/40F2aK0'>silicone spatula</a>.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Quinoa Crunch/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Evenly spread the mixture into a parchment-lined <a href='https://amzn.to/3YUjIkN'>9x5" bread pan</a>, pressing it down firmly.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Quinoa Crunch/pan.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Chill in the fridge for 1 hour. Let it sit on the counter for 5 minutes before cutting into 8 squares. Store in the fridge.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Quinoa Crunch/cut.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- The <a href='/misc/chocolate-percentages'>homemade chocolate</a> of 3 tbsp (42 g) <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut oil</a>, 1/2 cup (40 g) <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>cocoa powder</a>, and 2 tbsp (42 g) <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#honey'>honey</a> has roughly the same sweetness of about 75% chocolate. Adjust the amount of <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#honey'>honey</a> to taste depending on how sweet you prefer your chocolate.

- I'm using <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#honey'>honey</a> here, but other good options are <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#maple-syrup'>maple syrup</a>, <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#agave'>agave</a>, or <a href='/recipes/sugar-free-syrup'>sugar free syrup</a>.
