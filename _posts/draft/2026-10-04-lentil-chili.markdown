Description: Today I'm making a spin off my Slow Cooker Chili in 2 ways. Firstly, I'm going with lentils instead of beans to save someone from being a gassy girl at work. Secondly, I'm using a leftover block of tofu in my fridge as my main protein source instead of turkey or beef, which makes this chili both vegetarian and vegan.
Instructions:
- Add lentil and broth to a medium pot. Cover and bring to a boil over high heat. Reduce heat to medium low, and let simmer (covered) for about 25 minutes, or until all the water has been absorbed. Transfer the cooked lentils to a large slow cooker.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meatless/Lentil Chili/lentils.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Heat a large pan over medium heat with oil. When hot, add in the drained block of tofu. Let it sear without touching it for about 5 minutes to develop browning before mashing with a wooden spoon. Cook for an additional 5 minutes, or until the water has cooked off. Transfer the cooked tofu to a large slow cooker.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meatless/Lentil Chili/tofu.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Meanwhile, cut your peppers and onions into a medium dice. Once the pan from the tofu is empty, add in your peppers and onions with chili powder, cumin, and salt. Add a splash of water, cover, and cook with occasional stirring over medium heat until soft, about 10 minutes. Transfer the veggies to a large slow cooker.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meatless/Lentil Chili/veggies.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- In your slow cooker, add crushed tomatoes, diced tomatoes, minced garlic, and cocoa powder. Mix with the lentils, tofu, and veggies.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meatless/Lentil Chili/before.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Cover and cook on low for 8 hours.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meatless/Lentil Chili/after.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
