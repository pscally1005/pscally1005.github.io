Description: |
  It may be fall, but this Mexican Bean Salad is full of summer flavors. The fresh cilantro paired with the <a href='/misc/veggies'>veggies</a>, <a href='/misc/beans'>beans</a>, and spices makes this a delicious and nutritious <a href='/recipes/sides'>side dish</a>.<br><br>

  Even more, it features an <a href='/misc/fats'>oil free</a> <a href='/salad-dressings'>dressing</a>, getting whole-food, <a href='/misc/fats'>healthy fats</a> from <a href='/misc/nuts#walnuts'>walnuts</a>. Finally, the <a href='/misc/beans'>beans</a> add <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a>, <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a>, <a href='/misc/iron'>iron</a>, and <a href='/misc/nutrient-alphabet#F'>folate</a> to this vegan <a href='/recipes/sides'>side dish</a>.
Instructions:
- Drain and rinse your <a href='/misc/beans'>beans</a>, and add to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4zyFnzN'>large bowl</a>. Cut your <a href='/misc/veggies#pepper'>pepper</a>, <a href='/misc/veggies#onion'>onion</a>, and jalapeno into a small dice. Roughly chop your cilantro, and mix into the <a href='/misc/veggies'>veggies</a> and <a href='/misc/beans'>beans</a>.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Sides/Mex Bean Salad/veggies.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Blend together <a href='/misc/nuts#walnuts'>walnuts</a>, <a href='/misc/fruit#lime-juice'>lime juice</a>, mustard, <a href='/misc/veggies#garlic'>minced garlic</a>, <a href='https://amzn.to/3SqwsMO'>liquid monk fruit</a>, salt, paprika, garlic powder, cumin, and cayenne pepper in a <a href='https://amzn.to/3VHhgMM'>small blender or food processor</a>. Thin out with water as needed.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Sides/Mex Bean Salad/dressing.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Pour the <a href='/salad-dressings'>dressing</a> over the <a href='/salad'>salad</a>, and mix to combine.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Sides/Mex Bean Salad/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- I'm using <a href='/misc/beans#pinto-beans'>pinto</a> and <a href='/misc/beans#kidney-beans'>kidney beans</a> here, but any kind of <a href='/misc/beans'>bean</a> will work, such as <a href='/misc/beans#chickpeas'>chickpeas</a>, <a href='/misc/beans#black-beans'>black beans</a>, <a href='/misc/beans#cannellini-beans'>cannellini beans</a>, or even <a href='/misc/beans#brown-lentils'>lentils</a>.

- |
