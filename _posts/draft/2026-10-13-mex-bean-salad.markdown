Description: It may be fall, but this Mexican Bean Salad is full of summer flavors. The fresh cilantro paired with the veggies, beans, and spices makes this a delicious and nutritious side dish. Even more, it features an oil free dressing, getting whole-food, healthy fats from walnuts. Finally, the beans add protein, fiber, iron, and folate to this vegan side dish.
Instructions:
- Drain and rinse your beans, and add to a large bowl. Cut your pepper, onion, and jalapeno into a small dice. Roughly chop your cilantro, and mix into the veggies and beans.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Sides/Mex Bean Salad/veggies.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Blend together walnuts, lime juice, mustard, minced garlic, liquid monk fruit, salt, paprika, garlic powder, cumin, and cayenne pepper in a small blender or food processor. Thin out with water as needed.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Sides/Mex Bean Salad/dressing.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Pour the dressing over the salad, and mix to combine.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Sides/Mex Bean Salad/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- I'm using pinto and kidney beans here, but any kind of bean will work, such as chickpeas, black beans, cannellini beans, or even lentils.
