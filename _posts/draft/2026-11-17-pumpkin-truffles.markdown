Description: These high fiber, sugar free pumpkin truffles feature my newly homemade Dairy Free, Sugar Free, Sweetened Condensed Milk. Mix that with some pumpkin puree, pumpkin spices, and high fiber flours, and you'll have yourself an easy and quick high fiber snack!
Instructions:
- Add all ingredients to a large bowl. Mix together with a silicone spatula to form a dough.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Pumpkin Truffle/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Roll into 60 equally sized balls, about <b>1 1/2 tbsp</b> or <b>20 g</b> each.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Pumpkin Truffle/plate.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Optionally, use a toothpick to make ridges on the side to make it look like a pumpkin. Garnish with a chocolate chip on top.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Pumpkin Truffle/cover.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- If you were to make this recipe using a standard can of storebought condensed milk, it would nearly double the calories and add over 3 g of added sugar per truffle. While this difference may not look like a lot, this is <b>per 1 truffle</b>, so the differnence can add up quick if you have a couple.
