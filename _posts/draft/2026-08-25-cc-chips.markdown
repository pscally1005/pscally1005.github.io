Description: These 1 ingredient cottage cheese chips are made with just <a href='/misc/dairy#cottage-cheese'>cottage cheese</a> (who wudda thunk it?).  Alright fine, I'm also using <a href='https://amzn.to/40gmjGE'>everything bagel seasoning</a> too.  They're <a href='/misc/high-protein'>high protein</a>, dead simple, tasty, and cheap.  What more can you ask for?
Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 350F.  Line a large <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>cookie sheet</a> with parchment paper.

- Dollop your <a href='/misc/dairy#cottage-cheese'>cottage cheese</a> onto the prepared pan, making 16 circles of about <b>14 g</b> (1 tbsp) each.  Flatten into a circle, and optionally top with <a href='https://amzn.to/40gmjGE'>everything bagel seasoning</a>.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/CC Chips/raw.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Bake at 350F for about 30 minutes, or until the edges are golden brown and the centers are set.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/CC Chips/baked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Transfer to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4qQNmn2'>cooling rack</a> to completely cool, about 20 minutes.
