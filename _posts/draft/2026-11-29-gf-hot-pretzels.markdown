Description: |
  These Gluten Free Hot Pretzels will remind you of all those baseball games and Auntie Ann's visits from before you were diagnosed with Celiac disease. They have that classic flavor, texture, and taste, but without any of the gluten. Using this King Arthur Gluten Free Flour Blend, along with granulated monk fruit and extra virgin olive oil, we can create not only a gluten free hot pretzel, but a healthy one as well.<br><br>
  
  For another gluten free pretzel recipe, check out my Gluten Free Savory Pretzels, which use almond flour, mozzarella cheese, and garlic powder for a cheesy and savory pretzel. Or for a standard hot pretzel, see my Whole Wheat Hot Pretzels recipe!
Instructions:
- Combine the gluten free flour blend, granulated sweetener, yeast, salt, xanthan gum, and baking powder. Add the water, egg, and oi, and mix until a dough forms.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/GF Hot Pretzels/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Knead the dough until smooth, about 10 minutes. Transfer it to a lightly greased bowl.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/GF Hot Pretzels/knead.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Cover and let rise at room temperature until almost doubled, about 60 minutes.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/GF Hot Pretzels/rest.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Preheat your oven to 425F. Line 2 cookie sheets with parchment paper.

- Add 4 quarts (16 cups, 3,840 g) of water to a large pot with 1/4 cup (72 g) baking soda and 2 tbsp (24 g) granulated monk fruit. Bring to a boil over high heat.

- Meanwhile, evenly divide the dough into 12 portions, about 84 g each. Roll into a long rope, about 12" long, and shape into a pretzel.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/GF Hot Pretzels/shape.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- One at a time, add a pretzel to the boiling water. Boil for about 5 seconds, or until they go from sinking to floating. Remove with a spider, and place onto your cookie sheet. Sprinkle with coarse salt. Repeat for the remaining pretzels.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/GF Hot Pretzels/boil.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/GF Hot Pretzels/raw.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Bake at 425F for about 20 minutes, or until dark brown and the internal temperature is 205F.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/GF Hot Pretzels/bake.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Transfer to a wire rack to cool for 10 minutes before serving.
Notes:
- For a cinnamon sugar pretzel instead of a standard salted pretzel, mix together 2 tbsp (24 g) granulated monk fruit with 1 tsp (3 g) cinnamon. Dip the boiled pretzels in the cinnamon instead of sprinkling them with coarse salt.

- Either granulated monk fruit or allulose will work as your sweetener, both in the pretzels and in the cinnamon sugar. You can also use white or brown sugar if you prefer and don't mind the extra sugar and calories.
