Description: |
  These Gluten Free Hot Pretzels will remind you of all those baseball games and Auntie Anne's visits from before you were diagnosed with <a href='/misc/celiac'>Celiac disease</a>. They have that classic flavor, texture, and taste, but without any of the <a href='/misc/celiac'>gluten</a>.<br><br>
  
  Using this <a href='https://link.amazon/B02kdkQzE'>King Arthur Gluten Free Flour Blend</a>, along with <a href='https://amzn.to/4ceFnvP'>granulated monk fruit</a> and <a href='/misc/olive-oil'>extra virgin olive oil</a>, we can create not only a gluten free hot pretzel, but a healthy one as well.<br><br>
  
  For another gluten free pretzel recipe, check out my <a href='/recipes/gluten-free-pretzels'>Gluten Free Savory Pretzels</a>, which use <a href='/misc/nuts#almonds'>almond flour</a>, <a href='/misc/dairy#mozzarella'>mozzarella cheese</a>, and garlic powder for a cheesy and savory pretzel. Or for a standard hot pretzel, see my <a href='/recipes/pretzel'>Whole Wheat Hot Pretzels</a> recipe!
Instructions:
- Combine the <a href='https://link.amazon/B02kdkQzE'>flour</a>, <a href='https://amzn.to/4ceFnvP'>granulated sweetener</a>, yeast, salt, <a href='https://link.amazon/B0ipykmPW'>xanthan gum</a>, and baking powder in a <a href='https://amzn.to/4zyFnzN'>large bowl</a>. Add the water, <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>egg</a>, and <a href='/misc/olive-oil'>oil</a>, and mix until a dough forms.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/GF Hot Pretzels/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Knead the dough until smooth, about 10 minutes. Transfer it to a <a href='https://link.amazon/B00fjxkKE'>lightly greased</a> bowl.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/GF Hot Pretzels/knead.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Cover and let rise at room temperature until almost doubled, about 60 minutes.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/GF Hot Pretzels/rest.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Preheat your oven to 425F. Line 2 <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>cookie sheets</a> with parchment paper.

- Add 4 quarts (16 cups, 3,840 g) of water to a large pot with 1/4 cup (72 g) baking soda and 2 tbsp (24 g) <a href='https://amzn.to/4ceFnvP'>granulated monk fruit</a>. Bring to a boil over high heat.

- Meanwhile, evenly divide the dough into 12 portions, about <b>84 g</b> each. Roll into a long rope, about 12" long, and shape into a pretzel.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/GF Hot Pretzels/shape.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- One at a time, add a pretzel to the boiling water. Boil for about 5 seconds, or until they go from sinking to floating. Remove with a <a href='https://amzn.to/49ZXQcQ'>spider</a>, and place onto your <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>cookie sheet</a>. Sprinkle with coarse salt. Repeat for the remaining pretzels.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/GF Hot Pretzels/boil.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/GF Hot Pretzels/raw.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Bake at 425F for about 20 minutes, or until dark brown and the <a href='https://amzn.to/4kmobG2'>internal temperature</a> is <a href='https://amzn.to/4kmobG2'>205F</a>.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/GF Hot Pretzels/bake.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Transfer to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4qQNmn2'>wire rack</a> to cool for 10 minutes before serving.
Notes:
- For a cinnamon sugar pretzel instead of a standard salted pretzel, mix together 2 tbsp (24 g) <a href='https://amzn.to/4ceFnvP'>granulated monk fruit</a> with 1 tsp (3 g) cinnamon. Omit sprinkling the raw pretzels with salt. Lightly spray the baked pretzels with water, and coat them in the cinnamon sugar.

- Either <a href='https://amzn.to/4ceFnvP'>granulated monk fruit</a> or <a href='https://amzn.to/4mzsH5p'>allulose</a> will work as your sweetener in the pretzels, boiling water, and cinnamon sugar. You can also use <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>white or brown sugar</a> if you prefer and don't mind the extra <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar</a> and <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a>.
