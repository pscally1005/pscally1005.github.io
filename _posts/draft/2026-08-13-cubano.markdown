Description: All the flavors of a classic Cubano Sandwich, but without the <a href='/recipes/bread'>bread</a>. It's also lower in <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a> due to <a href='/misc/fats'>leaner</a> <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> instead of <a href='/misc/fats'>fatty</a> pulled pork. The sauce features the classic <a href='/misc/dairy#swiss-cheese'>swiss cheese</a>, while still being creamy without <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#dips'>mayo</a> or heavy cream. Topped with pickles and baked until golden, this <a href='/misc/carbs'>low carb</a>, <a href='/misc/fats'>low fat</a>, and <a href='/misc/high-protein'>high protein</a> Cuban sandwich alternative is sure to hit any cravings!
Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 400F. Heat a <a href='https://amzn.to/465aAxx'>10" cast iron pan</a> on the stove over medium heat. Cut your <a href='/misc/meat#ham'>ham</a> into small cubes. When the pan is hot, add in the <a href='/misc/meat#ham'>ham</a>, and cook for about 5 minutes, or until the outside is lightly browned
- <img src='/assets/Food/Chicken/Cubano/ham.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Add your <a href='/misc/dairy#cottage-cheese'>cottage cheese</a> to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>large bowl</a>, and beat with an <a href='https://amzn.to/45yqsbM'>electric hand mixer</a> to break up any large curds
- <img src='/assets/Food/Chicken/Cubano/cottage-cheese.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Add in the <a href='/misc/meat#ham'>ham</a>, <a href='/recipes/shredded-chicken'>cooked chicken</a>, mustard, <a href='/misc/dairy#swiss-cheese'>swiss</a>, paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, and black pepper.  Mix until fully combined
- <img src='/assets/Food/Chicken/Cubano/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Add your mixture to the <a href='https://amzn.to/465aAxx'>cast iron pan</a>. Top with the rest of your <a href='/misc/dairy#swiss-cheese'>swiss</a> and sliced pickles
# - <img src='/assets/Food/Chicken/Cubano/pre-bake-1.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
# - <img src='/assets/Food/Chicken/Cubano/pre-bake-2.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Chicken/Cubano/pre-bake-3.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Cover with foil and bake at 400F for about 20 minutes, or until steamy and bubbly. Optionally broil the top for 1-2 minutes to further brown the cheese
- <img src='/assets/Food/Chicken/Cubano/post-bake.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Allow to cool for about 5 minutes before serving
- <img src='/assets/Food/Chicken/Cubano/cover1.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- I was only able to find sliced <a href='/misc/dairy#swiss-cheese'>swiss</a> in my store instead of shredded.  I just tore the slices into small chunks for the inside and topping.  Each part gets 2 oz (56 g) of <a href='/misc/dairy#swiss-cheese'>cheese</a>, which is about 2 1/2 slices each in the bowl and on top, as my slices weigh about 23 g.

- For a <a href='/misc/carbs'>low carb</a> Cuban sandwich, serve this on a <a href='/recipes/psyllium-wrap'>High Fiber Psyllium Wrap</a>!
