Description: These <a href='/misc/fiber'>high fiber</a>, <a href='/misc/celiac'>gluten free</a> wraps are virtually <a href='/misc/calories'>calorie</a> free. Each one has a measly 4 <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a>, with an astounding 21 g of <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a>. They're flexible and mild in flavor, making them the perfect vessel for any tacos, crepes, or tortilla pizzas! For other <a href='/misc/fiber'>high fiber</a>, <a href='/misc/calories'>low calorie</a> wrap recipes, check out my <a href='/recipes/psyllium-wrap'>High Fiber Psyllium Wraps</a> and <a href='/recipes/copycat-carb-balance'>Copycat Carb Balance Wraps</a>!
Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 300F.

- Mix together <a href='https://link.amazon/B04N3ngTi'>oat fiber</a>, <a href='/misc/psyllium-husk'>psyllium husk</a>, salt, and water in a <a href='https://amzn.to/4zyFnzN'>large bowl</a>.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/4 Cal Wrap/bowl-before.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Let it sit for about 5 minutes for the <a href='https://link.amazon/B04N3ngTi'>oat fiber</a> and <a href='/misc/psyllium-husk'>psyllium husk</a> to absorb the moisture and form a dough.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/4 Cal Wrap/bowl-after.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Divide the dough into 4 equal portions, about <b>82 g</b> each. Roll into a wide and thin circle between 2 sheets of parchment paper, about 8" diameter and 1/16" thick. Transfer to 2 <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>cookie sheets</a> on the parchment paper.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/4 Cal Wrap/rolled.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Bake at 300F for about 10 minutes, or until the top looks dry.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/4 Cal Wrap/baked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Let the wraps cool completely before transferring to an airtight bag, separating each wrap with parchment paper. Store in the fridge for up to 1 week, or in the freezer indefinitely.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/4 Cal Wrap/plate.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
