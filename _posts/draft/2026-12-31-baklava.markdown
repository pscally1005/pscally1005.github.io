Description: |
  What makes this baklava healthier?  Firstly, it's <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar free</a>, using <a href='/recipes/sugar-free-syrup'>sugar free syrup</a> in the syrup you pour on top instead of <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>white sugar</a> and <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#honey'>honey</a>.<br><br>

  Secondly, it's <a href='/misc/dairy#butter'>butter</a> free. Instead of brushing each layer with melted <a href='/misc/dairy#butter'>butter</a>, you simply lightly dip them in water.<br><br>

  The result is a delicious and nutty <a href='/recipes/healthier-dessert'>dessert</a> that's a good source of <a href='/misc/fats'>healthy fats</a> and <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a> from the <a href='/misc/nuts'>nuts</a>!<br><br>

  The <a href='/misc/nuts#pistachios'>pistachios</a> are crunchy and the phyllo is light and crispy for a warming and cinnamon flavored dessert.
Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 350F. Line a <a href='https://amzn.to/4aiCsjh'>9x13" pan</a> with parchment paper, and <a href='https://amzn.to/3Hdg0gk'>lightly grease with oil</a>

- Use a <a href='https://amzn.to/4q0AUjI'>food processor</a> to roughly chop your <a href='/misc/nuts#pistachios'>pistachios</a>, and transfer to an empty bowl. Mix with <a href='/recipes/sugar-free-syrup'>sugar free syrup</a>, cinnamon, and cloves to make a sticky filling

- Fill a <a href='https://amzn.to/4aiCsjh'>9x13" pan</a> with cold water.  Lightly dip a piece of <a href='https://amzn.to/4uKqFT3'>phyllo dough</a> into the water, and add to the base of your pan. Repeat until you have a total of 8 layered sheets

- Wearing a <a href='https://amzn.to/414BfHP'>kitchen glove</a> to prevent sticking, spread about 1/4 of your pistachio mixture (~85 g or 1/3 cup) onto the <a href='https://amzn.to/4uKqFT3'>phyllo</a> in an even layer
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Baklava/make.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Repeat with the dipping and layering of 8 sheets and 1/4 of your <a href='/misc/nuts'>nut</a> mixture. Top the final <a href='https://amzn.to/4uKqFT3'>phyllo sheet</a> with your remaining 1/4 of your <a href='/misc/nuts#pistachios'>pistachio</a> mix

- Using a sharp <a href='https://amzn.to/4jlDKwc'>knife</a>, cut your baklava into 24 pieces
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Baklava/cut.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Bake for about 40 minutes at 350F, or until the top is golden brown and a toothpick to the center comes out clean
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Baklava/no-syrup.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Microwave your water and <a href='/recipes/sugar-free-syrup'>sugar free syrup</a> in a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>small bowl</a> for 2 minutes. Pour on top of the baked baklava
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Baklava/with-syrup.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Chill in the fridge for at least 1 hour before eating.  Either eat cold, or air fry for 3-5 minutes at 400F to make it slightly crispier
Notes:
- |
  You can find <a href='https://amzn.to/4uKqFT3'>phyllo dough</a> in the frozen food section, or you can order it on Amazon <a href='https://amzn.to/4uKqFT3'>here</a>.<br><br>

  You should have 4 "sections" each of 8 pieces of <a href='https://amzn.to/4uKqFT3'>phyllo</a> layered on each other, with <a href='/misc/nuts#pistachios'>pistachio</a> filling between each section and on top of the final one.

- An equal amount of <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#honey'>honey</a> or <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#maple-syrup'>maple syrup</a> will also work in place of <a href='/recipes/sugar-free-syrup'>sugar free syrup</a>.
