Description: If you need a protein source for dinner, and you only have 5 minutes, why not whip up these quick Vegan Protein Crumbles.  With just pea protein powder, veggie better than bouillon, and water, you can easily make a dough that you can crumble on top of any of your salads or bowls for a quick protein fix.
Instructions:
- Add ingredients to a small bowl. Wearing a kitchen glove to prevent sticking, mix together with your hand to form a dough.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Protein Crumble/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Crumble over whatever you like.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Protein Crumble/cover1.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- You can also make this into a "protein dressing" by doubling the water to 1/2 cup (120 g).  Instead of a dough consistency, you'll end up with a "sauce" or "dressing" that you can drizze over your salads and bowls.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Protein Crumble/dressing.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
