Description: Today I'm making a guacamole inspired dip, but with Greek yogurt as the base instead of avocados. Not because avocados are "higher in fat" or "higher in calories", or because yogurt is "higher in protein". But because dammit I want guac and all I have right now is yogurt. If there's a will, there's a way. For a more standard guac recipe, check out my Holy Guacamole!
Instructions:
- Finely dice an onion, and add to a medium bowl with yogurt, minced garlic, lime juice, paprika, chili powder, cumin, salt, and pepper. Mix together with a silicone spatula until fully combined.
