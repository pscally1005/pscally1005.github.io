Description: |
  These delicious <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar free</a> <a href='/brownies'>brookies</a> have a secret ingredient:<b></b> silken <a href='/misc/beans#tofu'>tofu</a>. It keeps the inside rich and creamy while cutting back on <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a> and adding a touch of <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a>.<br><br>
  
  They're also <a href='/misc/celiac'>gluten-free</a>, using <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oat flour</a>, and <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar-free</a>, using <a href='https://amzn.to/4ceFnvP'>granulated monk fruit</a>. Each large square has just 131 <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a> with 9 g of <a href='/misc/fats'>healthy fats</a> from the <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut oil</a> and <a href='https://link.amazon/B0dAdVfzm'>cacao nibs</a>!
Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 350F. Line a <a href='https://amzn.to/3YY2H9q'>9" square baking pan</a> with parchment paper. <a href='https://link.amazon/B00fjxkKE'>Lightly grease it with oil</a>.

- Prepare the brownie layer by mixing together <a href='/misc/beans#tofu'>tofu</a>, <a href='https://amzn.to/4ceFnvP'>sweetener</a>, <a href='https://amzn.to/43MkDqr'>vanilla</a>, <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oat flour</a>, and <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>cocoa</a> in a <a href='https://amzn.to/4zyFnzN'>large bowl</a> until smooth.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Tofu Brookie/brownie-bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Add the brownie layer to the base of your pan.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Tofu Brookie/brownie-pan.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Prepare the cookie layer by mixing together melted <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut oil</a>, <a href='https://amzn.to/4ceFnvP'>sweetener</a>, <a href='https://amzn.to/43MkDqr'>vanilla extract</a>, cornstarch, and <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oat flour</a> in a <a href='https://amzn.to/4zyFnzN'>large bowl</a> until smooth.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Tofu Brookie/cookie-bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Add the cookie layer on top of the brownie layer, lightly pressing it into the brownie layer. Top with your <a href='https://link.amazon/B0dAdVfzm'>cacao nibs</a>, lightly pressing them into the cookie layer.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Tofu Brookie/cookie-pan.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Bake at 350F for 40-50 minutes, or until the top is golden brown, the edges are set, the <a href='https://amzn.to/4kmobG2'>internal temperature</a> is <a href='https://amzn.to/4kmobG2'>190F</a>, and a toothpick comes out slightly dirty.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Tofu Brookie/baked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Transfer the pan to the fridge. Chill for 1 hour before cutting into 9 squares.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Tofu Brookie/cut.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- Either <a href='https://amzn.to/4ceFnvP'>granulated monk fruit</a> or <a href='https://amzn.to/4mzsH5p'>allulose</a> will work as your sweetener.

- I'm using <a href='https://link.amazon/B0dAdVfzm'>cacao nibs</a> today, but you can also use either <a href='https://amzn.to/4kGEfC5'>sugar free</a> or regular chocolate chips (1/2 cup, 90 g).
