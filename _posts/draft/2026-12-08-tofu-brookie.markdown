Description: These delicious sugar free brookies have a secret ingredient:<b></b> silken tofu. It keeps the inside rich and creamy while cutting back on fat and adding a touch of protein. They're also gluten-free, using oat flour, and sugar-free, using granulated monk fruit. Each large square has just 131 calories with 9 g of healthy fats from the coconut oil and cacao nibs.
Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 350F. Line a 9" square baking pan with parchment paper. Lightly grease it with oil.

- Prepare the brownie layer by mixing together tofu, sweetener, vanilla, oat flour, and cocoa in a large bowl until smooth.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Tofu Brookie/brownie-bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Add the brownie layer to the base of your pan.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Tofu Brookie/brownie-pan.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Prepare the cookie layer by mixing together melted coconut oil, sweetener, vanilla extract, cornstarch, and oat flour in a large bowl until smooth.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Tofu Brookie/cookie-bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Add the cookie layer on top of the brownie layer, lightly pressing it into the brownie layer. Top with your cacao nibs, lightly pressing them into the cookie layer.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Tofu Brookie/cookie-pan.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Bake at 350 F for 40-50 minutes, or until the top is golden brown, the edges are set, the internal temperature is 190F, and a toothpick comes out slightly dirty.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Tofu Brookie/baked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Transfer the pan to the fridge. Chill for 1 hour before cutting into 9 squares.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Tofu Brookie/cut.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- Either granulated monk fruit or allulose will work as your sweetener.

- I'm using cacao nibs today, but you can also use either sugar free or regular chocolate chips (1/2 cup, 90 g).
