Description: Mint has a reputation for growing like a weed, so let's put some of Vic's to good use. Here's a recipe for dairy free creamy yogurt sauce. It has a base of soaked cashews and white beans to make it creamy while still keeping it lactose free. This Mediterranean-ish dip is a perfect alternative to Tzatziki, and pairs well with grilled meats, roasted veggies, or falafel. Or try it as a salad dressing or dip for your raw carrots.
Instructions:
- Add your cashews to a small bowl. Pour in just the liquid from the can of beans. Let the nuts soak in the aquafaba for at least 2 hours at room temperature to soften

- Add all ingredients (cashews, aquafaba, beans, lemon juice, apple cider vinegar, liquid monk fruit, and fresh mint) to a food processor. Blend until smooth and creamy
