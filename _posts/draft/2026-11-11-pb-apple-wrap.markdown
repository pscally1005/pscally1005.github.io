Description: Peanut Butter & Apple is far superior to Peanut Butter & Jelly, and I'm not afraid to admit it. Especially when you cook the apple beforehand, softening it and bringing out its natural sweetness. This simple breakfast or snack grills a Copycat Low Carb Wrap with nut butter and a cinnamon-cooked apple that'll put your average PB&J to shame.
Instructions:
- Cut your apple into a medium dice. Add to a medium pan over medium heat with cinnamon, nutmeg, ginger, cloves, salt, and water. Cover and cook for about 5 minutes, or until lightly browned, softened, and steaming.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Breakfast/PB Apple Wrap/apple.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Transfer the cooked apple aside, and wipe out the pan with a paper towel.

- Spread your nut butter onto a tortilla. Add your cooked apple on top, and fold into a burrito.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Breakfast/PB Apple Wrap/open.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Return the wrap to the pan over medium heat, cut side down. Cook all for sides for about 1 minute each to brown and crisp the tortilla before serving.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Breakfast/PB Apple Wrap/uncut.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
