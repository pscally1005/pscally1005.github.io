Description: When you think about it, <a href='/recipes/evoo-mayo'>mayo</a> is just <a href='/misc/olive-oil'>oil</a> and <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>eggs</a>, things that normally go into a <a href='/recipes/chocolate-cake'>cake</a> anyway. So why not just skip a couple steps and use <a href='/recipes/evoo-mayo'>mayonnaise</a> in your <a href='/recipes/chocolate-cake'>cake</a>. This secret ingredient <a href='/recipes/chocolate-cake'>cake</a> is perfectly moist and airy, with a rich chocolate flavor. Top with whatever <a href='/recipes/protein-frosting'>frosting</a> you like if desired!
Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 350F. Line a <a href='https://amzn.to/3YY2H9q'>9" square baking pan</a> with parchment paper, and <a href='https://amzn.to/3Hdg0gk'>lightly grease it with oil</a>.

- Whisk together all ingredients in a <a href='https://amzn.to/4zyFnzN'>large bowl</a> until just fully combined.
- <img src='/assets/Misc/Unhealthy/Mayo Cake/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Pour your batter into the prepared <a href='https://amzn.to/3YY2H9q'>baking pan</a>.
- <img src='/assets/Misc/Unhealthy/Mayo Cake/raw.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Bake uncovered at 350F for about 30 minutes, or until the top and edges are set and browned, a toothpick to the center comes out clean, and the <a href='https://amzn.to/4kmobG2'>internal temperature</a> is around <a href='https://amzn.to/4kmobG2'>205F</a>.
- <img src='/assets/Misc/Unhealthy/Mayo Cake/baked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Transfer to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4qQNmn2'>wire rack</a>, and let it cool completely before cutting into 16 square slices.
- <img src='/assets/Misc/Unhealthy/Mayo Cake/cut.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- |
  To make a healthier version of <a href='#recipe'>this mayo cake</a> replace the <a href='/misc/grains#white-wheat'>white flour</a> (2 cup, 240 g), <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>white sugar</a> (1 cup, 200 g), and <a href='https://amzn.to/4jFQAsV'>storebought mayo</a> (1 cup, 220 g) with <a href='/misc/grains#whole-wheat'>whole wheat flour</a> (2 cup, 240 g), <a href='https://amzn.to/3Sg1Z7p'>pure granulated monk fruit</a> (1 tbsp, 8.4 g), <a href='https://amzn.to/3Z5tUaF'>liquid monk fruit</a> (1 tbsp, 15 g), and my homemade <a href='/recipes/evoo-mayo'>EVOO Mayo</a> (1 cup, 220 g).<br><br>

  Each baked slice will then be about 45 g.
