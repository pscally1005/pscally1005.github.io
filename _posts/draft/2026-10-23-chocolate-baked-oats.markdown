Description: These protein baked oats are a simple, healthy, and tasty way to meal prep breakfast for the next 4 days. These baked oat bars are gluten-free, sugar-free, lower in fat, and vegan, while still being packed with protein and flavor. This variant is a classic chocolate, but for a fall appropriate recipe, check out my Pumpkin Protein Baked Oats! Or for a banana variation, check out my Banana Bread Protein Baked Oats!
Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 350F. Line a 9" square baking pan with parchment paper, and lightly grease it with oil.

- Add all ingredients to a large bowl. Mix with a silicone spatula until fully combined.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Breakfast/Baked Oats/Chocolate/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Transfer the batter to your pan.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Breakfast/Baked Oats/Chocolate/raw.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Bake at 350F for about 30 minutes, or until a toothpick to the center comes out clean, and the internal temperature is about 205F.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Breakfast/Baked Oats/Chocolate/baked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Transfer to a wire rack. Allow it to cool totally before slicing into 4 large squares.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Breakfast/Baked Oats/Chocolate/cut.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
