Description: This cottage cheese queso dip will convert any cottage cheese haters without them even knowing it. It's a high protein sauce with cottage cheese, cheddar cheese, and taco seasonings that's a great sauce for nachos or dip for chips or veggies.  But don't just limit it to a dip; use the queso as a high protein salad dressing, use it as a Mac and cheese sauce, even make some cheesy beans?
Instructions:
- Combine cottage cheese, cheddar, minced garlic, chili powder, paprika, and cumin in a blender or food processor, until smooth
- <img src='/assets/Food/Savory Sauces/CC Queso/processor.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Transfer to a microwave safe bowl.  Mix a can of diced green chilies into the queso bowl

- Cover with a plate, and microwave for about 1 1/2 - 2 minutes, stirring every 15 seconds, until warm and melty
- <img src='/assets/Food/Savory Sauces/CC Queso/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
