Description: I've been loving miso lately, and I had an interesting idea.  An Asian inspired <a href='/hummus'>hummus</a> using miso, <a href='/misc/fruit#lime-juice'>lime</a>, and <a href='/misc/veggies#ginger'>ginger</a> instead of <a href='/misc/seeds#sesame-seeds'>tahini</a>, <a href='/misc/fruit#lemon-juice'>lemon</a>, and cumin.  This twist on the classic <a href='/hummus'>hummus</a> is the perfect dip for <a href='/misc/veggies'>veggies</a>, <a href='/salad-dressings'>dressing</a> to your <a href='/salad'>salads</a>, or even <a href='/recipes/savory-sauces'>sauce</a> for noodles, packing a umami punch!
Instructions:
- Using a strainer, drain and rinse your can of chickpeas, reserving the aquafaba

- Add all ingredients to a food processor, and blend until super smooth.  Thin out with aquafaba if needed (1-2 tbsp, or 15-30 g)

- Transfer to an airtight container, and garnish with sesame seeds or chopped scallions (most folks call them green onions).  Store in the fridge for up to 1 week. Use as a dip, spread, or dressing
