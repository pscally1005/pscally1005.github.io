Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 350F, and lightly grease a muffin tin.  This recipe will yield a dozen standard sized muffins

- In a large bowl, mix together the wet ingredients until fully combined with a silicone spatula

- In a separate bowl, whisk together the dry ingredients

- Add the dry to the wet, and mix with a silicone spatula until fully combined

- Fold the chocolate chips into the batter.  Evenly divide the batter into the muffin tins

- Bake at 350F for 18-20 minutes, or until lightly browned and a toothpick comes out almost clean.  Cool totally before removing from the pan and serving
