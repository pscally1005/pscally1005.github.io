Description: All the flavors of a classic Cubano Sandwich, but without the bread. It's also lower in <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a> due to <a href='/misc/fats'>leaner</a> <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> instead of <a href='/misc/fats'>fatty</a> pulled pork. The sauce features the classic <a href='/misc/dairy#swiss-cheese'>swiss cheese</a>, while still being creamy without <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#dips'>mayo</a> or heavy cream. Topped with pickles and baked until golden, this <a href='/misc/carbs'>low carb</a>, <a href='/misc/fats'>low fat</a>, and <a href='/misc/high-protein'>high protein</a> Cuban sandwich alternative is sure to hit any cravings!
Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 400F. Heat a <a href='https://amzn.to/465aAxx'>10" cast iron pan</a> on the stove over medium heat. Cut your <a href='/misc/meat#ham'>ham</a> into small cubes. When the pan is hot, add in the <a href='/misc/meat#ham'>ham</a>, and cook for 5-8 minutes, or until the outside is lightly browned
- <img src='/assets/Food/Chicken/Cubano/stove.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- In a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>large bowl</a>, combine <a href='/misc/meat#ham'>ham</a>, <a href='/recipes/shredded-chicken'>cooked chicken</a>, (blended) <a href='/misc/dairy#cottage-cheese'>cottage cheese</a>, mustard, shredded <a href='/misc/dairy#swiss-cheese'>Swiss</a>, paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, and black pepper
- <img src='/assets/Food/Chicken/Cubano/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Add your mixture to the <a href='https://amzn.to/465aAxx'>cast iron pan</a>. Top with the rest of your <a href='/misc/dairy#swiss-cheese'>swiss</a> and sliced pickles
- <img src='/assets/Food/Chicken/Cubano/pre-bake.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Cover with foil and bake at 400F for about 20 minutes, or until steamy and bubbly. Optionally broil the top for 1-2 minutes before serving
- <img src='/assets/Food/Chicken/Cubano/post-bake.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
