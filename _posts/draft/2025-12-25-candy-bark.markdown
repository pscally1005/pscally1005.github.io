Description: Christmas time means candy time, but that doesn't mean forgetting your health goals. So why can't we have both?  Tasty treats that are better for you is what I strive for. And here I'll be making 2 healthier homemade candies.  I'm using a base of <a href='/misc/fruit#apples'>applesauce</a>, honey, and <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oat flour</a>, with a homemade chocolate coating for a candy with only 6 minimal ingredients. By using different extracts in the filling, you can recreate your favorite classics, like <a href='https://amzn.to/4qMMLDD'>Junior Mints</a> or <a href='https://amzn.to/4hQNr6w'>Mounds</a>!
Instructions:
- Line a 9" square pan with parchment paper

- Choose your flavor extract. For Junior Mints or Peppermint Patties, use mint extract. For Mounds or Almond Joys, use coconut extract

- In a medium bowl, combined together the applesauce, sweetener, and extract with a <a href='https://amzn.to/43FEbwe'>silicone spatula</a>. Add in the oat flour, and mix until combined and you have a slightly sticky dough
- <center><img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Candy Bark/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'></center>

- For my sweetener here, I'm using honey, but you can also use either maple syrup or <a href='/misc/sugar-free-syrup'>sugar free syrup</a>. In place of the <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oat flour</a>, you can also substitute <a href='/misc/nuts#almonds'>almond flour</a>

- Transfer the filling mixture to the pan, and flatten to an even thickness, spreading to all edges. Freeze for 10 minutes to harden
- <center><img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Candy Bark/base.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'></center>

- If you want to make almond joys, press 25 almonds (about 1 oz, 28 g) into the top of the mixture in a 5x5 grid, one for each bar that will be cut later

- Microwave your coconut oil for about 1 minute, until melted. Stir in cocoa powder and sweetener with a <a href='https://amzn.to/43FEbwe'>silicone spatula</a>

- Evenly spread the chocolate on top of the filling base. Optionally top with flaky salt, crushed candy canes, or coconut flakes. Freeze for 10 minutes to harden
- <center><img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Candy Bark/top.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'></center>

- Once the chocolate has firmed up, cut into 25 bars. Store in an airtight container in the fridge
- <center><img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Candy Bark/cut.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'></center>
