It has healthy fats from the peanut butter, natural sweetness from the banana and honey, and extra flavor from the cinnamon. All brought together with a humble whole wheat tortilla to make a delicious warm dessert for you and a partner. This also goes great with a scoop of banana ice cream on top for a nice warm and cold contrast Instructions: - Preheat a medium pan over medium heat with oil - Spread peanut butter on a tortilla. Add your banana to the center, and drizzle with honey. Tightly roll the tortilla around the banana -
- Add the tortilla cut side down to the hot pan, and let sit for at least a minute to seal it shut. Brown all 4 sides, and remove from the heat. Sprinkle cinnamon on top, cut in half, and serve -
