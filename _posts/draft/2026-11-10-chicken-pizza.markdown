Description: High protein pizza. No cotrage cheese. No yogurt. No protein powder. Just good, old fashioned meat.  As the crust...
Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 400F. Line 2 large cookie sheets with parchment paper.

- In a large bowl, mix together ground chicken, eggs, cheese, garlic powder, oregano, salt, and pepper until fully combined.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Chicken Pizza/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Evenly divide the chicken mixture into 4 pieces, about <b>155 g</b> each. Flatten into a thin and wide circle on your cookie sheets.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Chicken Pizza/rawDough.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Bake just the crust at 400F for about 25 minutes, or until lightly browned and cooked through.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Chicken Pizza/cookedDough.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Top your cooked crust with sauce and cheese.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Chicken Pizza/topped.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Increase your oven temperature to 450F. Return the pizza to the oven, and bake for an additional 8-10 minutes, or until the sauce and cheese are lightly bubbly. Optionally broil the top to brown the cheese.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Chicken Pizza/baked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Let the pizzas cool for 5 minutes before cutting into 4 slices each.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Chicken Pizza/cut.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- Any other ground meat will work too besides chicken, such as ground turkey or ground beef.
