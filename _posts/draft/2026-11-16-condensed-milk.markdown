Description: Homemade sweetened condensed milk, but sugar free! Using nonfat milk powder and sugar free syrup turns this sugar bomb pantry staple into a low calorie and sugar free ingredient! Try it out in my new High Fiber Pumpkin Truffles!
Instructions:
- Add powdered milk to a <a href='https://amzn.to/46ccg8m'>medium pot</a> over medium heat. Toast for about 5 minutes, or until browned and smells like caramel. Stir frequently, or it will stick and burn.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Sweet Spreads/Condensed Milk/powder.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Remove from the heat, and add in the sugar free syrup and vanilla extract. Whisk vigorously to break up the milk powder clumps.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Sweet Spreads/Condensed Milk/potNew.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Strain into a <a href='https://amzn.to/4vamaBm'>mason jar</a> to remove any clumps of milk powder. Let it cool uncovered at room temperature for 30 minutes before covering with a lid and transferring to the fridge.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Sweet Spreads/Condensed Milk/coverNew.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- <a href='#recipe'>This recipe</a> is designed to make 14 oz (396 g) of condensed milk, enough to exactly substitute a standard size 14 oz (396 g) can of <a href='https://link.amazon/B0gAsxJJN'>sweetened condensed milk</a>.

- You can also use honey or maple syrup in place of sugar free syrup, but this will add a signifcant amount of sugar and calories.

- Compared to a standard can of <a href='https://link.amazon/B0gAsxJJN'>storebought sweetened condensed milk</a>, the homemade version with sugart free syrup contains significantly fewer <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a> and <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a>, with no <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>added sugar</a>.
