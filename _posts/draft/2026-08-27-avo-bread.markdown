Description: |
  Instead of <a href='/recipes/avocado-toast'>avocado toast</a>, what about avocado bread?  Imagine <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#banana-bread'>banana bread</a>, but <a href='/misc/fruit#avocado'>avocado</a> instead.<br><br>

  The <a href='/misc/fruit#avocado'>avocado</a> provides <a href='/misc/fats'>healthy fats</a> and <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> without the need for <a href='/misc/dairy#butter'>butter</a> or <a href='/misc/olive-oil'>oil</a>. It's sweetened with <a href='https://amzn.to/4ceFnvP'>granulated monk fruit</a> instead of <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>white sugar</a> to keep it <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar free</a> and lower in <a href='/misc/carbs'>carbs</a>. And using <a href='/misc/grains#whole-wheat'>whole wheat flour</a> instead of <a href='/misc/grains#white-wheat'>refined white flour</a> means more <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a>, <a href='/misc/nutrient-alphabet'>vitamins, and minerals</a>.<br><br>

  This healthy quick bread is a great snack on its own, warm or cold, but is especially good when topped with <a href='/recipes/berry-jam'>jam</a> or <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>nut butter</a>. Or double up on the <a href='/recipes/avocado-toast'>avocado toast</a> with some <a href='/recipes/guacamole'>guac</a> or sliced <a href='/misc/fruit#avocado'>avocado</a>!
Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 350F. Line a <a href='https://amzn.to/3YUjIkN'>9x5" bread pan</a> with parchment paper, and <a href='https://amzn.to/3Hdg0gk'>lightly grease with oil</a>.

- Mash your <a href='/misc/fruit#avocado'>avocado</a> with the back of a fork in a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>large bowl</a>.  Combine together with the <a href='https://amzn.to/4ceFnvP'>granulated monk fruit</a>, <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>egg</a>, <a href='https://amzn.to/43MkDqr'>vanilla</a>, salt, baking powder, and <a href='/misc/grains#whole-wheat'>whole wheat flour</a>.  Mix until just fully combined with a <a href='https://amzn.to/40F2aK0'>silicone spatula</a>.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/Avo Bread/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Transfer the batter to the pan, and smooth out the top.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/Avo Bread/raw.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Bake at 350F for about 50 minutes, or until the top is lightly browned, a toothpick to the center comes out clean, and the <a href='https://amzn.to/4kmobG2'>internal temperature</a> is about <a href='https://amzn.to/4kmobG2'>205F</a>.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/Avo Bread/baked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Transfer to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4qQNmn2'>wire rack</a> to totally cool (about 1 hour) before slicing.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/Avo Bread/cut.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
