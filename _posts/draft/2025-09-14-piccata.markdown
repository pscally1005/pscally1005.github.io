Description: Ah Chicken Piccata, the better lemon <a href="/misc/meat#chicken-breast">chicken</a> cousin of Chicken Francese. For those unaware, Francese has an <a href="/misc/meat#egg">egg</a> and flour coating on the chicken. In comparison, Piccata has only flour (no egg), and also adds capers into the sauce.  This high <a href="/misc/protein">protein</a> dish has a creamy lemon sauce without tons of <a href="/misc/dairy#butter">butter</a> or oil, and pair great with any <a href="/misc/fiber">vegetables</a> or <a href="/misc/carbs">complex carb</a> <a href="/recipes/sides">side</a>
Instructions: 
- Cut your meat into 6 large pieces. Cover with plastic wrap, and pound with a meat mallet until you have a thin piece of chicken. Repeat with all pieces

- On a large plate, mix together flour, oregano, black pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, and salt. Any flour will do, but today I opted for coconut. Lightly coat your pieces of chicken in flour, and set aside
- <center><img src="/assets/Food/Chicken/Piccata/raw.jpg" alt="" class="instruction-image"></center>

- In a large pan, preheat 1 tsp (5 g) of oil over medium heat. When hot, add 2 pieces of chicken, and sear on both sides until browned. Cook until the internal temperature just reaches 165F, and set aside. Repeat in 2 more batches (1 tsp, 5 g f oil for each batch) for the remaining 4 pieces
- <center><img src="/assets/Food/Chicken/Piccata/cooked.jpg" alt="" class="instruction-image"></center>

- Reduce heat to low. Add lemon juice, capers, garlic, parsley, cornstarch, and lemon pepper to the pan. Whisk and cook until thickened
- <center><img src="/assets/Food/Chicken/Piccata/sauce.jpg" alt="" class="instruction-image"></center>

- As the sauce simmers, slice your chicken on a bias. Add the chicken to a plate, and pour the sauce over the chicken. Optionally garnish with a slice of lemon, and serve

- Serve with a <a href="/recipes/sides">side</a> of <a href="/recipes/roasted-vegetables">roasted</a> or <a href="/recipes/vegetable-medley">sauteed</a> veggies, such as spinach, broccoli, cauliflower, or asparagus. Also pair with healthy starches, such as a <a href="/recipes/baked-sweet-potato">baked potato</a>, <a href="/recipes/rice-and-beans">brown rice</a>, <a href="/recipes/red-lentils">lentils</a>, or <a href="/recipes/couscous">couscous</a>
- <center><img src="/assets/Food/Chicken/Piccata/plate.jpg" alt="" class="instruction-image"></center>
