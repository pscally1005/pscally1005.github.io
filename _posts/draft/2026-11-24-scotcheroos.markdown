Description: |
  Scotcheroos are the lesser known but superior version of a <a href='/recipes/rice-crispy-treats'>homemade rice crispy treat</a>. They're a no bake dessert made with a base of <a href='https://link.amazon/B0hc1TDhB'>puffed rice</a> and <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>peanut butter</a>, topped with a layer of chocolate.

  These healthy scotcheroos bars combine <a href='/misc/chocolate-percentages'>homemade chocolate</a> with <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>natural peanut butter</a> and homemade <a href='/recipes/popped-quinoa'>popped quinoa</a> for a delicious, crunchy treat. Naturally sweetened with <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#honey'>honey</a>, they're a <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>refined sugar free</a> alternative to <a href='https://saltandbaker.com/scotcharoos/#recipe'>traditional scotcheroos</a> with fewer <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a> and less <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>added sugar</a>.<br><br>

  For another <a href='/recipes/popped-quinoa'>popped quinoa</a> recipe, you can make <a href='/recipes/quinoa-crunch'>Copycat Quinoa Crunch Bars</a> using <a href='/recipes/popped-quinoa'>popped quinoa</a>, <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>chocolate</a>, and <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut oil</a>!
Instructions:
- |
  Preheat a medium <a href='https://amzn.to/4pREQ61'>stainless steel pan</a> over medium heat without any oil. Add the <a href='/misc/grains#quinoa'>quinoa</a> to the pan, and shake to a single layer. Cook with frequent shaking for about 30 seconds, or until the popping sounds slow down and the <a href='/misc/grains#quinoa'>quinoa</a> starts to smell nutty. Transfer to a plate to cool at room temperature for about 5 minutes.<br><br>

  For a more in-depth guide, check out my <a href='/recipes/popped-quinoa'>Single Serving Popped Quinoa</a>.

- Combine the <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>peanut butter</a>, <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#honey'>honey</a>, and <a href='https://amzn.to/43MkDqr'>vanilla</a> in a <a href='https://amzn.to/4zyFnzN'>large bowl</a> until smooth.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Quinoa Scotcheroos/bowls.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Add the <a href='/recipes/popped-quinoa'>popped quinoa</a>, and mix until fully combined with a <a href='https://amzn.to/40F2aK0'>silicone spatula</a>.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Quinoa Scotcheroos/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Evenly spread the mixture into a parchment-lined <a href='https://amzn.to/3YY2H9q'>9" square pan</a>, pressing it down firmly.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Quinoa Scotcheroos/base.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Mix melted <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut oil</a>, <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>cocoa powder</a>, <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#honey'>honey</a>, and salt in a <a href='https://amzn.to/4zyFnzN'>small bowl</a>.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Quinoa Scotcheroos/chocolate.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Evenly spread the melted chocolate over the quinoa base.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Quinoa Scotcheroos/topped.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Chill in the fridge for 1 hour. Let it sit on the counter for 5 minutes before cutting into 25 squares. Store in the fridge.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Quinoa Scotcheroos/cut.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- The <a href='/misc/chocolate-percentages'>homemade chocolate</a> made with 3 tbsp (42 g) <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut oil</a>, 1/2 cup (40 g) <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>cocoa powder</a>, and 2 tbsp (42 g) <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#honey'>honey</a> has a sweetness similar to 75% dark chocolate. Adjust the amount of <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#honey'>honey</a> to taste depending on how sweet you prefer your chocolate.
