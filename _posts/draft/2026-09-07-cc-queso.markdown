Description: This cottage cheese queso dip will convert any <a href='/misc/dairy#cottage-cheese'>cottage cheese</a> haters without them even knowing it. It's a <a href='/misc/high-protein'>high protein</a> sauce with <a href='/misc/dairy#cottage-cheese'>cottage cheese</a>, <a href='/misc/dairy#cheddar'>cheddar cheese</a>, and taco seasonings that's a great sauce for nachos or a <a href='/recipes/savory-sauces'>dip</a> for <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#chips'>chips</a> or <a href='/misc/veggies'>veggies</a>.  But don't just limit it to a <a href='/recipes/savory-sauces'>dip</a>; use the queso as a <a href='/misc/high-protein'>high protein</a> <a href='/salad-dressings'>salad dressing</a>, use it as a Mac & Cheese <a href='/recipes/savory-sauces'>sauce</a>, even make some cheesy <a href='/misc/beans'>beans</a>?
Instructions:
- Combine <a href='/misc/dairy#cottage-cheese'>cottage cheese</a>, <a href='/misc/dairy#cheddar'>cheddar</a>, <a href='/misc/veggies#garlic'>minced garlic</a>, chili powder, paprika, and cumin in a <a href='https://amzn.to/3SsDLn7'>blender</a> or <a href='https://amzn.to/4q0AUjI'>food processor</a>, until smooth

- Fold in a can of diced green chilies
- <img src='/assets/Food/Savory Sauces/CC Queso/processor.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Transfer to a microwave safe bowl, cover with a plate, and microwave for about 1 1/2 - 2 minutes, stirring every 15 seconds, until warm and melty
