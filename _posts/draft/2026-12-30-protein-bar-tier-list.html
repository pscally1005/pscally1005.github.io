Overview
This is my personal tier list of protein bars. I've tried quite a few protein bars over the years, and this is my ranking of them based on taste, texture, nutrition, and cost. Nothing on here is for a specific flavor, just the brand overall.

In my ideal world, a protein bar would be high in protein, low in calories, and taste good. I also want it to be affordable. The bars on this list are ranked based on how well they meet those criteria.

The most important nutritional criterion for a protein bar is it's protein percentage. This is represented by the following formula: (grams of protein ÷ calories) × 100. A good protein bar should have a protein percentage of at least 10%.
