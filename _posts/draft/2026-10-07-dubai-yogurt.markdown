Description: |
  Is <a href='/recipes/dubai-chocolate'>Dubai Chocolate</a> still a big hit? Well, if so, then this healthy <a href='/yogurt'>yogurt bowl</a> is sure to hit the spot. It has a crunchy filling from the chopped <a href='/misc/nuts#pistachios'>pistachios</a> and <a href='https://amzn.to/4m1zWDi'>wheat cereal</a>, a creamy green filling from the <a href='/misc/dairy#yogurt'>yogurt</a> and <a href='https://amzn.to/47oj3Mp'>pudding mix</a>, and a hardened homemade <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar-free</a> chocolate topping made from <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut oil</a>, <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>cocoa powder</a>, and <a href='https://amzn.to/3Sg1Z7p'>monk fruit</a>.<br><br>

  We got <a href='/misc/fats'>fats</a> from the <a href='/misc/nuts#pistachios'>pistachios</a> and <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut oil</a>, <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> from the <a href='/misc/dairy#yogurt'>yogurt</a>, and <a href='/misc/carbs'>carbs</a> and <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a> from the <a href='https://amzn.to/4m1zWDi'>cereal</a>, in about 30 g of <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> for 360 <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a>. Plus, it also only costs $1.88 per serving, much cheaper than those overpriced candy bars.<br><br>

  If you prefer chocolate bars to <a href='/yogurt'>yogurt bowls</a>, then you should check out my <a href='/recipes/dubai-chocolate'>Sugar Free Dubai Chocolate</a> recipe!
Instructions:
- Roughly chop your <a href='https://amzn.to/4m1zWDi'>shredded wheat cereal</a> and <a href='/misc/nuts#pistachios'>pistachios</a>, and add them to a dry, <a href='https://amzn.to/4dBv5Ga'>medium pan</a>. Toast over medium heat for about 5 minutes, or until lightly browned and fragrant. Set aside to cool.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Breakfast/Dubai Yogurt/stove.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- In a <a href='https://amzn.to/4zyFnzN'>large bowl</a>, mix together <a href='/misc/dairy#yogurt'>yogurt</a> and <a href='https://amzn.to/47oj3Mp'>pudding mix</a> with a <a href='https://amzn.to/40F2aK0'>silicone spatula</a>.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Breakfast/Dubai Yogurt/bowl1.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Fold in most of your chopped <a href='https://amzn.to/4m1zWDi'>cereal</a> and <a href='/misc/nuts#pistachios'>nuts</a>, reserving a little bit for the top.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Breakfast/Dubai Yogurt/bowl2.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Evenly divide your <a href='/misc/dairy#yogurt'>yogurt</a> mixture into 4 containers, about <b>255 g</b> (~1 cup) in each.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Breakfast/Dubai Yogurt/divide.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Combine together melted <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut oil</a>, <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>cocoa powder</a>, <a href='https://amzn.to/3Sg1Z7p'>sweetener</a>, and salt in a <a href='https://amzn.to/4zyFnzN'>small bowl</a>.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Breakfast/Dubai Yogurt/chocolate.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Drizzle your chocolate mixture over the 4 <a href='/yogurt'>yogurt bowls</a>, about <b>15 g</b> (~1 tbsp) in each. Top each bowl with your remaining mix-in mixture.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Breakfast/Dubai Yogurt/topped.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Refrigerate for at least 20 minutes, or until the chocolate has hardened, before serving.
