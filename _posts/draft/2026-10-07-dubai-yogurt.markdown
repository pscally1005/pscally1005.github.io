Description: |
  Is Dubai Chocolate still a big hit? Well. if so, then this healthy yogurt bowl is sure to hit the spot. It has a crunchy center from the chopped pistachios and wheat cereal, a creamy green filling from the yogurt and pudding mix, and a hardened homemade sugar-free chocolate topping made from coconut oil, cocoa powder, and pure granulated monk fruit.<br><br>

  We got fats from the pistachios and coconut oil, protein from the yogurt, and carbs and fiber from the wheat cereal, in about 30 g of protein for 360 calories. It also only costs $1.88 per serving, much cheaper than those overpriced candy bars.<br><br>

  If you prefer chocolate bars to yogurt bowls, then you should check out my Sugar Free Dubai Chocolate recipe!
Instructions:
- Roughly chop your shredded wheat cereal and pistachios, and add them to a dry, medium pan. Toast over medium heat for about 5 minutes, or until lightly browned and fragrant. Set aside to cool.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Breakfast/Dubai Yogurt/stove.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- In a large bowl, mix together yogurt and pudding mix with a silicone spatula. Fold in most of your chopped cereal and nuts, reserving a little bit for the top.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Breakfast/Dubai Yogurt/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Evenly divide your yogurt mixuture into 4 containers, about <b>255 g</b> (~1 cup) in each.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Breakfast/Dubai Yogurt/divide.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Combine together melted coconut oil, cocoa powder, pure granulated monk fruit, and salt in a small bowl.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Breakfast/Dubai Yogurt/chocolate.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Pour your melted chocolate over the 4 yogurt bowl, about <b>15 g</b> (~1 tbsp) in each. Top each bowl with your remaining mix-in mixture.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Breakfast/Dubai Yogurt/topped.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Refrigerate to allow the chocolate to harden on top before serving.
