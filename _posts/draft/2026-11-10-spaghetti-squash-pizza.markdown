Description: This pizza alternative uses cooked spaghetti squash and mozzarella cheese as a base. It's high protein, low carb, and gluten free. For another low carb, high protein pizza recipe, check out my Ground Chicken Pizza!
Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 400F. Line 2 large cookie sheets with parchment paper.

- Add the spaghetti squash to a paper towel, and squeeze out as much water as possible.

- Mix together spaghetti squash, egg, mozzarella, parmesan, garlic, and oregano in a large bowl.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Spaghetti Squash Pizza/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Evenly divide the mixture 4 ways, about <b>70 g</b> each. Make 4 rounds, about 8" in diameter and 1/8" thick.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Spaghetti Squash Pizza/raw.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Bake at 400F for about 25 minutes, flipping halfway, or until browned.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Spaghetti Squash Pizza/baked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Top your cooked crust with <a href='/recipes/savory-sauces'>sauce</a> (2 tbsp, 32 g per pizza) and <a href='/misc/dairy#mozzarella'>cheese</a> (1/2 oz, 14 g per pizza).
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Spaghetti Squash Pizza/topped.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Increase your oven temperature to 450F. Bake for an additional 8-10 minutes, or until the sauce is hot and the cheese is melted.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Spaghetti Squash Pizza/cooked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Let the pizzas cool for 5 minutes before cutting into 4 slices each.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Spaghetti Squash Pizza/cut.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- To reheat, air fry at 400F for 3-5 minutes, or until warm and crispy.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Spaghetti Squash Pizza/cover.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- My 2 cups (310 g) spaghetti squash weighed about 120 g after draining out the excess water.

- You can also use low moisture part skim <a href='/misc/dairy#mozzarella'>mozzarella</a> instead of <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a> free.
