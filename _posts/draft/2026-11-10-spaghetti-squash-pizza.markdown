Description: This pizza alternative uses <a href='/recipes/spaghetti-squash'>cooked spaghetti squash</a> and <a href='/misc/dairy#mozzarella'>mozzarella cheese</a> to create a thin, crispy, and <a href='/misc/dairy#mozzarella'>cheesy</a> pizza crust. It's <a href='/misc/high-protein'>high protein</a>, <a href='/misc/carbs'>low carb</a>, and <a href='/misc/celiac'>gluten free</a>. For another <a href='/misc/carbs'>low carb</a>, <a href='/misc/high-protein'>high protein</a> pizza recipe, check out my <a href='/recipes/ground-chicken-pizza'>Ground Chicken Pizza</a>!
Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 400F. Line 2 large <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>cookie sheets</a> with parchment paper.

- Add the <a href='/misc/veggies#spaghetti-squash'>spaghetti squash</a> to a paper towel, and squeeze out as much water as possible.

- Mix together <a href='/misc/veggies#spaghetti-squash'>spaghetti squash</a>, <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>egg</a>, <a href='/misc/dairy#mozzarella'>mozzarella</a>, <a href='/misc/dairy#grated-cheese'>parmesan</a>, garlic, and oregano in a <a href='https://amzn.to/4zyFnzN'>large bowl</a>.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Spaghetti Squash Pizza/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Evenly divide the mixture into 4 portions, about <b>70 g</b> each. Shape each portion into an 8" round, about 1/8" thick.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Spaghetti Squash Pizza/raw.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Bake at 400F for about 25 minutes, flipping halfway, or until browned.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Spaghetti Squash Pizza/baked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Top your cooked crust with <a href='/recipes/pasta-sauce'>sauce</a> (2 tbsp, 32 g per pizza) and <a href='/misc/dairy#mozzarella'>cheese</a> (1/2 oz, 14 g per pizza).
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Spaghetti Squash Pizza/topped.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Increase your oven temperature to 450F. Bake for an additional 8-10 minutes, or until the <a href='/recipes/pasta-sauce'>sauce</a> is hot and the <a href='/misc/dairy#mozzarella'>cheese</a> is melted.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Spaghetti Squash Pizza/cooked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Let the pizzas cool for 5 minutes before cutting into 4 slices each.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Spaghetti Squash Pizza/cut.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- To reheat, <a href='https://amzn.to/3FuWETp'>air fry</a> at 400F for 3-5 minutes, or until warm and crispy.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Spaghetti Squash Pizza/cover.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- My 2 cups (310 g) of <a href='/recipes/spaghetti-squash'>cooked spaghetti squash</a> weighed about 120 g after squeezing out the water.

- Low moisture part skim <a href='/misc/dairy#mozzarella'>mozzarella</a> can also be used in place of <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a> free.
