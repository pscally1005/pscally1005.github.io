Description: Homemade peanut butter cups are a simple but fun hands on dessert activity. Unlike Reese's, which have a mushy chocolate and a grainy center, these homemade peanut butter cups have a firm and snappy chocolate, with a filling that's runny and actually tastes like peanuts. Perfect for the Halloween season, this homemade candy is a tastier alternative!
Instructions:
- Line a standard 12 cup muffin pan with muffin liners.

- Add chocolate chips and coconut oil to a small bowl. Microwave in 20 second intervals, stirring in between with a silicone spatula, until you have a smooth melted chocolate.
- <img src='/assets/Misc/Unhealthy/Peanut Butter Cups/choc.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Add 1/2 tbsp (7.5 g) of melted chocolate to the bottom of each muffin cup, and tilt the pan to cover the full bottom.
- <img src='/assets/Misc/Unhealthy/Peanut Butter Cups/bot.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Add 2 tsp (10 g) of peanut butter to the center of each peanut butter cup.
- <img src='/assets/Misc/Unhealthy/Peanut Butter Cups/mid.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Finally, top each peanut butter cups with an additional 1/2 tbsp (7.5 g) of melted chocolate.
- <img src='/assets/Misc/Unhealthy/Peanut Butter Cups/top.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Refrigerate for at least 30 minutes for the chocolate to harden before serving.
