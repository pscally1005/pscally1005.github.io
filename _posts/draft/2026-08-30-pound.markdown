Description: Traditional pound cake gets its name because it contains a pound each of eggs, sugar, butter, and flour. So naturally, I made a healthy one. A quarter pound of each ingredient. Because apparently that's how math works.
Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 350F, and line 2 <a href='https://amzn.to/3YUjIkN'>9x5" bread pans</a> with parchment paper (only 1 if doing a 1/4 batch). Lightly <a href='https://amzn.to/3Hdg0gk'>spray the paper with oil</a>

- Beat your egg in a large bowl. Whisk with the granulated monk fruit. Whisk in the cottage cheese. Mix in the whole wheat flour with a silicone spatula until just fully combined.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meme/Pound/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Transfer your batter to your prepared pan, and smooth out the top.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meme/Pound/raw.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Bake at 350F oven for about 50 minutes (uncovered), or until the top is fully set and golden brown, a toothpick to the center comes out almost clean, and the internal temperature is at least 205F.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meme/Pound/baked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Transfer to a wire rack to cool completely (about 1 hour) before slicing.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meme/Pound/cut.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
