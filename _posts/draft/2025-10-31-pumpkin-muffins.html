These higher calorie healthy pumpkin muffins are very high in heart healthy mono-unsaturated fats from all the almond butter and almond flour, as well as both fiber and vitamin E. The pumpkin is not only perfect for the season, but it keeps the muffins moist without the need for any additional added butter or oil. Containing only honey and chocolate chips, these gluten free muffins are also lower in sugar without compromising on flavor! Instructions: - Preheat your oven to 350F, and liberally grease a muffin tin. This recipe will yield 48 mini muffins, or 12 standard sized muffins - In a large bowl, mix together the wet ingredients until fully combined with a silicone spatula - I've gone with almond butter here, but any nut or seed will work, such as cashew butter, peanut butter, pistachio butter, pumpkin seed butter, sunflower seed butter, or tahini - For the sweetener, you can use honey, maple syrup, or sugar free syrup (see notes below) - In a separate bowl, whisk together the dry ingredients - I've gone with almond flour here, but oat flour (1 cup, 90 g) will also work - Add the dry to the wet, and mix with a silicone spatula until fully combined - Fold the chocolate chips into the batter. Evenly divide the batter into the muffin tins - Bake at 350F for until lightly browned, and a toothpick comes out clean. About 12-14 minutes for mini muffins, or 18-20 minutes or standard sized muffins. Cool totally before removing from the pan and serving ---
Note that if you make standard size muffins, each serving is the equivalent of eating 4 mini muffins.
