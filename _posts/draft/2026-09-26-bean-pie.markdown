Description: |
  So yesterday, to celebrate the <a href='https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mid-Autumn_Festival'>Mid-Autumn Festival</a>, I made my rustic looking, <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar-free</a>, <a href='/misc/celiac'>gluten-free</a>, vegan, wholesome <a href='/recipes/mooncakes'>White Bean Mooncakes</a>. But then I had a dumb idea: let's make it into a pie instead. I took the dough mixture and pressed it into a <a href='https://amzn.to/4q0gY0f'>pie pan</a> before parbaking it, and then poured in my filling and baked the full pie. The result is a pie that tastes just like yesterday's <a href='/recipes/mooncakes'>mooncakes</a>, but in a different shape and with a fraction of the effort.
Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 350F. Drain and rinse your <a href='/misc/beans#cannellini-beans'>beans</a>, reserving the liquid.

- In a <a href='https://amzn.to/4zyFnzN'>large bowl</a>, mix together <a href='/misc/nuts#cashews'>cashew butter</a> and <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut flour</a>. Add the aquafaba and mix until a slightly dry dough forms.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Mooncakes/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Press about 3/4 of your crust dough (~276 g) into a <a href='https://amzn.to/4q0gY0f'>9" pie pan</a>, reserving any leftover scraps.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Mooncakes/Pie/crust-raw.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Parbake the crust at 350F for about 15 minutes, or until lightly browned.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Mooncakes/Pie/crust-baked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Meanwhile, add the <a href='/misc/beans#cannellini-beans'>beans</a> to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4q0AUjI'>food processor</a> with <a href='/misc/fruit#lemon-juice'>lemon juice</a>, <a href='https://amzn.to/43MkDqr'>vanilla extract</a>, and <a href='https://amzn.to/3Sg1Z7p'>monk fruit</a>. Blend until smooth.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Mooncakes/processor.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Pour your blended filling mixture into the baked pie crust.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Mooncakes/Pie/raw.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Crumble the remaining crust mixture on top of the filling.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Mooncakes/Pie/raw-topped.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Bake the pie at 350F for about 30 minutes, or until the filling is set, the topping is a light golden brown, and the edges of the crust are golden brown.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Mooncakes/Pie/baked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Allow the pie to cool totally before cutting into 12 slices.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Mooncakes/Pie/cover2.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- I'm using a can of drained and rinsed <a href='/misc/beans#cannellini-beans'>cannellini beans</a>, but any other <a href='/misc/beans'>beans</a> will work, such as <a href='/misc/beans#black-beans'>black</a>, <a href='/misc/beans#pinto-beans'>pinto</a>, <a href='/misc/beans#kidney-beans'>kidney</a>, or <a href='/misc/beans#chickpeas'>chickpeas</a>.

- |
  The filling tastes mildly sweet. If you prefer a sweeter pie, add more sweetener to taste. Other sweetener options include the following:
- INGREDIENTS(mooncakesweeteners)

- <a href='/misc/nuts#cashews'>Cashew butter</a> works well for its neutral flavor, but <a href='/misc/nuts#almonds'>almond butter</a> will also work. To make it <a href='/misc/nuts'>nut</a> free, use <a href='/misc/seeds#sesame-seeds'>tahini</a>. I would not recommend using <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>peanut butter</a>, or these will distinctly taste like <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanuts</a>.

- 1/2 cup (120 g) aquafaba is roughly the amount of bean juice you should get from a 15.5 oz can. If you don't have enough aquafaba, you can use water for the rest.
