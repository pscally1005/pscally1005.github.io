Description: |
  Summer means <a href='/recipes/ice-cream'>ice cream</a> right? But with health goals in mind, it's hard to both have your <a href='/recipes/chocolate-cake'>cake</a> and eat it too.<br><br>

  But you can with this recipe!  This sugar free frozen yogurt is also lower in both <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a> and <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a> than traditional <a href='/recipes/ice-cream'>ice cream</a> or fro-yo. It's also higher in <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> and <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a> than any other frozen treat you've ever had!
Instructions:
- In a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>large bowl</a>, beat together the <a href='/misc/dairy#yogurt-whole-milk'>yogurt</a> with a <a href='https://amzn.to/45yqsbM'>hand mixer</a> on medium speed for about 2 minutes to beat in as much air as possible

- Add in your vanilla, vodka, <a href='https://amzn.to/47w8h7R'>inulin</a>, and salt.  Beat until fully combined

- Slowly beat in the <a href='https://amzn.to/4mzsH5p'>allulose</a> until fully mixed
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Fro Yo/bowl1.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Slowly beat in the <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>cocoa powder</a> until fully mixed
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Fro Yo/bowl2.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Transfer the frozen yogurt to a wide and flat airtight container, and freeze
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Fro Yo/container.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Every half an hour, churn the fro-yo by mixing with a <a href='https://amzn.to/40F2aK0'>silicone spatula</a>.  Do this 6 times for a total of 3 hours
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Fro Yo/churned.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Store in the freezer. Let it sit out for 5 minutes before scooping and serving
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Fro Yo/scooped.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- |
  Unlike other <a href='/misc/calories'>zero calorie</a> sweeteners, <a href='https://amzn.to/4mzsH5p'>allulose</a> will lower the freezing point like <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar</a> would, making the frozen yogurt less icy and more scoopable.<br><br>

  Vodka and <a href='https://amzn.to/47w8h7R'>inulin</a> are also used to help with the texture due to the lack of <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar</a> and low amount of <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a>. It is not recommended that you omit these

- |
  You can even use <a href='/misc/dairy#whey'>vanilla whey protein powder</a> (5 scoops, 150 g) in place of <a href='https://amzn.to/4mzsH5p'>allulose</a> to act as both a <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar free</a> sweetener and adding some <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a>.<br><br>

  You may need to let the fro-yo sit on the counter for a bit longer before being able to scoop it though
