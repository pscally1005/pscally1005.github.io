It's also multi-purpose, as I'm using this dough recipe to make both Ground Beef Empanadas and Sweet Potato Pierogi. This finger food is the perfect appetizer or meal for a group, and is a great activity for you and a partner to do together! Instructions: - Preheat your oven to 350F, and line a large cookie sheet with parchment paper - Add the mozzarella to a large glass bowl. Cut the cream cheese into cubes, and add on top. Microwave for 1 minute, stirring halfway with a silicone spatula -
- Add in the almond flour, and mix in with the spatula. Crack in the egg, mix with the spatula, then knead with your hands until you have a smooth, elastic, and slightly sticky dough -
- Line your counter with parchment paper, and add the dough on top. Add another piece of parchment on top, and roll out to 1/8" thick -
- Using a cookie cutter or wine glass, cut your dough into circles about 3.5" in diameter. You should end up with about 12 circles, about 27 g each -
- Transfer your dough circles to the prepared pan. Add a heaping tablespoon of filling onto half of the circle, leaving a 1/2" border. Fold the dough over to make semicircles, and seal the dough shut with a fork. Repeat for all pieces of dough -
- Bake at 350F for 20 minutes, flipping them over halfway, or until golden brown. Cool for 10 minutes before serving -

Empanada Filling {% assign i = site.data[folder]['empanadas-ing'] %} {% include ingredients.html ing=i recipe="false" %}
  1. Heat a medium pan over medium heat with olive oil. Finely dice an onion, and add to the pan. Cook for about 5 minutes, or until transparent and lightly caramelized.

  2. Add ground meat to the pan, and cook until browned and no longer pink.

  3. Season with garlic powder, onion powder, paprika, cumin, and salt. Stir to combine, then remove from heat

  4. Let the meat cool completely before using as filling

Pierogi Filling {% assign i = site.data[folder]['pierogi-ing'] %} {% include ingredients.html ing=i recipe="false" %}
  1. Preheat your air fryer (or oven) to 400F

  2. Wash 2 medium (400 g) sweet potatoes, prick with a fork, and wrap in foil

  3. Air fry for 1 hour (or bake for 1.5 hours), until fork tender.

  4. Add your roasted potato to a medium bowl, and season with garlic powder, onion powder, paprika, cumin, and salt. Mash until smooth.

  5. Let the potatoes cool completely before using as filling
