Instructions: - Preheat your oven to 350F. Line a 9" square pan with parchment paper, and lightly grease the paper with oil - In a large bowl, whisk together the dry ingredients - whole wheat flour, inulin, monk fruit, cocoa powder, baking powder, and salt - Add in the wet ingredients (vanilla, egg, almond butter, and water), and combine with a silicone spatula until combined and you have a thick but liquidy brownie batter -
- Pour the batter into your prepared pan, and top with chocolate chips -
- Bake at 350F for about 20 minutes, or until a toothpick comes out nearly clean -
- Allow to cool for at least 30 minutes in the pan in the fridge before cutting into 16 bars -
--- Notes
  1. For another recipe with inulin, check out my Keto Bread Loaf Recipe
  2. To make Copycat Fiber One Cookie Bars instead, replace the cocoa powder with powdered peanut butter (1/2 cup, 48 g)

  3. To make Copycat Great Value Fiber Brownies instead, replace the monk fruit with granulated sugar (6 tbsp, 72 g), and replace the sugar free chocolate chips with regular chocolate chips (2 tbsp, 30 g). The Walmart knockoffs use added sugar instead of sugar alcohols, which is why they have 90 calories instad of 70.
