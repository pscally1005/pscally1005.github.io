Description: Summer is winding down, but there's still enough time for some cool and refreshing drinks.  Today is a simple limeade made with fresh squeezed lime juice and liquid monk fruit.  This sugar free and low calorie refreshing treat is perfect for a hot summer day without spiking blood sugar.
Instructions:
- Shake together all ingredients in an airtight container. Refrigerate for up to 1 week
Notes:
- I'm making limeade today, but you can obviously make lemonade instead with lemon juice instead of lime juice

- Fresh squeezed limes (or lemons) are recommended.  If you are to use bottled though, opt for a lime (or lemon) juice that is 100% juice not from concentrate

- This limeade is sugar free, being sweetened with liquid monk fruit.  A standard limeade or lemonade sweetened with sugar would have 110 calories with 27 g of added sugar
