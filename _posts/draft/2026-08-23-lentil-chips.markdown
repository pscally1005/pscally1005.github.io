Description: These tortilla chips are gluten free, grain free, and lower in fat. They're made with lentils, which are a good source of fiber and plant based protein, and only use a minimal amount of oil, keeping them lower in calories. Pair them with hummus or any of your favorite dips, and you have the perfect snack!
Instructions:
- Add lentils, water, and salt to a large blender. Let the lentils soak for 1 hour on the counter before blending until smooth.

- Preheat your oven to 350F, and line a large cookie sheet with parchment paper. Pour the batter onto your prepared baking sheet, and spread it thin to the edges of the pan.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Lentil Chips/raw.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Bake at 350F for about 20 minutes, or until lightly golden brown, and the surface is set and dry to the touch. Transfer to a cooling rack to fully cool.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Lentil Chips/bake1.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Cut your large tortilla into small triangles, about TODO. Season on the cookie sheet with oil and spices.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Lentil Chips/cut.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Return the chips to the oven at 350F for about 15 minutes, mixing halfway, or until golden and crispy but not burnt.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Lentil Chips/bake2.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Transfer to a wire rack to cool completely. They will crisp more as they cool. Store at room temperature for up to 1 week.
