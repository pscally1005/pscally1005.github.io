Description: |
  I love having <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>eggs</a> for <a href='/recipes/breakfast'>breakfast</a>, but I don't like spending 15 minutes making food, just to do it again the next day. <a href='#recipe'>This recipe</a> serves as a way to prep a whole week's worth of <a href='/recipes/breakfast'>breakfast</a> to store in the freezer for easy reheating in the morning.<br><br>

  On the menu is <a href='/misc/veggies#spinach-fresh'>spinach</a> and <a href='/misc/veggies#onion'>onion</a> <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>scrambled eggs</a> with <a href='/misc/dairy#cheddar'>cheddar cheese</a> and <a href='/recipes/salsa'>salsa</a>, wrapped in a <a href='https://link.amazon/B030CixA0'>low carb wrap</a>. Each burrito contains about 20 g of <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> in 270 <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a>, with an astounding 21 g <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a>.<br><br>

Instructions:
- Heat a <a href='https://amzn.to/4rdR0HI'>12" pan</a> over medium heat with a <a href='/misc/olive-oil'>oil</a>. Finely dice your <a href='/misc/veggies#onion'>onion</a>, and add it along with defrosted <a href='/misc/veggies#spinach-fresh'>spinach</a> to the <a href='https://amzn.to/4rdR0HI'>pan</a>.

- Season with minced garlic, garlic powder, onion powder, paprika, chili powder, and black pepper. Cover with a lid, and cook until the <a href='/misc/veggies#onion'>onion</a> is translucent, about 10 minutes.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Breakfast/Egg Wrap/archive/veggies.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Remove from the heat, and crack in your <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>eggs</a>. Beat the <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>eggs</a> with your <a href='https://amzn.to/40F2aK0'>spatula</a> until everything is fully mixed. Return to the burner, lower the heat to medium low, and cook until <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>eggs</a> just set, about 10 minutes.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Breakfast/Egg Wrap/archive/eggs.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Remove from the heat, and mix in <a href='/misc/dairy#cheddar'>cheese</a>, <a href='/recipes/salsa'>salsa</a>, and <a href='/recipes/hot-sauce'>hot sauce</a>.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Breakfast/Egg Wrap/archive/cheese.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Let the mixture totally cool before dividing it evenly across 6 tortillas, about TODO g each. Wrap each in aluminum foil, and store in the freezer.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Breakfast/Egg Wrap/archive/filled.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Breakfast/Egg Wrap/archive/foil.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- To reheat, wrap a frozen burrito in a damp paper towel, and microwave for 2-3 minutes, flipping halfway, or until heated thoroughly. For a crispy <a href='https://link.amazon/B030CixA0'>, brown all sides in a preheated pan on the stove.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Breakfast/Egg Wrap/archive/plate.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Each of my <a href='https://link.amazon/B030CixA0'>tortillas</a> weighs about 43 g with a diameter of about 8 inches.
