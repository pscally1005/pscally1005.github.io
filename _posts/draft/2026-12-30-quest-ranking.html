Epilogue
In making this ranking, I took 1-2 bites of each Quest Bar to judge the flavors side by side. I probably ate the equivalent of 1 Quest Bar, but was left with 10 partially eaten bars. You may be wondering: what did I do with all the remaining Quest Bars?

Food processor to the rescue! I added all the remaining protein bars to my food processor along with some other ingredients I had lying around my kitchen.
{% assign f = 'quest-brownies' %} {% include ingredients.html file=f %} I blended everything until smooth, and then transferred to a parchment-lined, lightly greased 9" square pan. I then baked the protein brownies at 350F for about 60 minutes, or until the top and edges were set, and a toothpick came out nearly clean. I transferred the brownies to the fridge, and let them cool for 1 hour before cutting into 16 squares.

Each of these 16 protein brownies contains 164 calories, with 16 g of protein and 11 g of fiber. They're gluten-free, sugar-free, and high-protein!
