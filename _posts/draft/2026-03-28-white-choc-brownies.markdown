Description: After making my Tangy White Chocolate with buttermilk powder and coconut oil, I wanted to put it to good use.  What I came up with were these no bake protein brownies.  They feature a base of protein powder, carob powder, unsweetened applesauce, liquid monk fruit, oat flour, and coconut flour, all topped with a ganache made out of the homemade white chocolate.  They're entirely sugar free - brownies and ganache included, only featuring liquid monk fruit as an external sweetener.  Each bar packs 10 g of protein, along with 3 g of fiber and 6 g of fat, making one or two of these a great pre or post workout snack!
Instructions:
- Line a pan with parchment paper. I'm using a 7x11" pan here, but you can use a standard 9" square

- In a large bowl, mix together the dry ingredients for the brownies

- Add in the wet ingredients, and combine with a silicone spatula until you have a fully combined and sticky dough
- <img src='/assets/Food/Protein Powder/White Choc Brownie/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Transfer the batter to your prepared pan. Smooth out the top. Freeze for 30 minutes to harden

- Roughly chop your chocolate, and add to a medium microwave safe bowl with water. Microwave in 15 second intervals, stirring in between with a silicone spatula, until you have a smooth ganache
- <img src='/assets/Food/Protein Powder/White Choc Brownie/both.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Pour the white chocolate ganache on top of the brownie base. Tilt the pan to spread the ganache over the entire brownie
- <img src='/assets/Food/Protein Powder/White Choc Brownie/topped.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Freeze for at least 2 hours before slicing into 12 bars. Slightly separate them so they're not touching
- <img src='/assets/Food/Protein Powder/White Choc Brownie/cut.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Freeze until fully solid, about 2 hours. Store in the freezer with parchment paper between each bar
- <img src='/assets/Food/Protein Powder/White Choc Brownie/cover2.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
