Description: |
  Did you know that you can eat <a href='/misc/grains#bulgur'>bulgur</a> without cooking it? Oh, you don't even know what <a href='/misc/grains#bulgur'>bulgur</a> is. I guess that's fair...<br><br>

  Well, it's a type of parboiled, cracked <a href='/misc/grains#whole-wheat'>wheat</a> berry that you could either cook like <a href='/misc/grains#brown-rice'>rice</a>, or do what I'm doing here. Just soak the <a href='/misc/grains#bulgur'>bulgur</a> overnight in salted water, and it's ready to eat in the morning!
Instructions:
- Add <a href='/misc/grains#bulgur'>bulgur</a>, water, and salt to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4zyFnzN'>large bowl</a>. Cover with a lid, and refrigerate overnight, or about 8 hours, until hydrated and tender, and most of the water is absorbed.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Sides/Bulgur/before.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Sides/Bulgur/after.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Drain off any excess water. For me, this was about 2 cups (480 g).
Notes:
- <a href='#recipe'>This recipe</a> makes about 6 2/3 cups of soaked <a href='/misc/grains#bulgur'>bulgur</a>, where each cup is about 180 g.
