Description: Summer may be winding down, but there's still time for tasty and healthy refreshing dessert.  On the menu for today is Watermelon Sorbet, make with only 3 ingredients:<b></b> watermelon, lime juice, and salt.  Similar to Banana Nice Cream, if you blend frozen watermelon, you can get a great low calorie, sugar free, and fat free sorbet!
Instructions:
- Cut your watermelon into 1" cubes. Add to an airtight container or bag. Freeze overnight.

- The following morning, add your frozen watermelon to a food processor with lime juice (or lemon juice) and salt. Blend until you have a smooth sorbet.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Watermelon Sorbet/processor.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Transfer to an airtight container, and store in the freezer. Let it sit out for about 5 minutes prior to serving.
Notes:
- You can also use grapes (either red or green) in place of watermelon.  Just add whole grapes to an airtight container and freeze overnight before blending with lime juice (or lemon juice) and salt.
