Description: |
  Why eat a protein bar when you can have an oatmeal cream pie?  This delicious and <a href='/misc/high-protein'>high protein</a> <a href='/recipes/healthier-dessert'>dessert</a> has 30 g of <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> in just 250 <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a>, while also containing a good amount of <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a> from the <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oats</a> and <a href='/misc/fats'>healthy fats</a> from the <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>nut butter</a>.<br><br>

  The cookies are made of <a href='/misc/dairy#casein'>casein protein powder</a>, <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oats</a>, <a href='https://amzn.to/4sgDH9S'>powdered peanut butter</a>, and <a href='/misc/meat#egg-whites'>egg whites</a>, while the filling contains <a href='/misc/dairy#cream-cheese'>cream cheese</a> and <a href='/misc/dairy#yogurt'>Greek yogurt</a>.  These sandwich cookies are full of cinnamon flavor, and are the perfect pre-workout snack!
Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 350F. Line 2 large <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>cookie sheets</a> with parchment paper

- In a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>large bowl</a>, whisk together the dry ingredients - <a href='/misc/dairy#casein'>casein</a>, <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>rolled oats</a>, <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oat flour</a>, <a href='https://amzn.to/4sgDH9S'>powdered peanut butter</a>, cinnamon, baking powder, <a href='/misc/veggies#ginger'>ginger</a>, nutmeg, and salt

- Add in the liquid ingredients - <a href='/misc/meat#egg-whites'>egg whites</a>, <a href='/misc/fruit#apple'>applesauce</a>, <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>nut butter</a>, vanilla extract, and <a href='https://amzn.to/3SqwsMO'>liquid monk fruit</a>. Mix until fully combined with a <a href='https://amzn.to/40F2aK0'>silicone spatula</a>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Protein Powder/Cream Pies/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Scoop the batter onto the prepared pans to make 12 cookies, or 6 per pan. Each cookie should be about 40 g. Spread to a thin and even circle using the back of a spoon
- <img src='/assets/Food/Protein Powder/Cream Pies/raw.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Bake at 350F until lightly golden brown, about 5-7 minutes. Allow them to cool totally on the sheet
- <img src='/assets/Food/Protein Powder/Cream Pies/baked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- In a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>medium bowl</a>, mix together the filling with a <a href='https://amzn.to/40F2aK0'>silicone spatula</a> - (softened) <a href='/misc/dairy#cream-cheese'>cream cheese</a>, <a href='/misc/dairy#yogurt'>Greek yogurt</a>, <a href='/misc/dairy#casein'>casein protein powder</a>, vanilla extract, and <a href='https://amzn.to/3SqwsMO'>liquid monk fruit</a>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Protein Powder/Cream Pies/icing.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Spread the filling batter onto the bottom side of 6 cookies, about 30 g of cream filling each. Top with the remaining 6 cookies
- <img src='/assets/Food/Protein Powder/Cream Pies/iced.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
