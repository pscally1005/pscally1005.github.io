Description: |
  This homemade marshmallow fluff contains only 3 ingredients; 2 if you exclude water (I don't). It's sweetened with <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#maple-syrup'>maple syrup</a>, making it <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>refined sugar free</a>. Not only does this pack more flavor than standard <a href='https://link.amazon/B01x3kci4'>storebought fluff</a>, but it has significantly fewer <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a> and <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar</a> too!<br><br>

  Do you know what the best use for marshmallow fluff is, besides a Fluffer Nutter or making <a href='/recipes/classic-fudge'>fudge</a>? Making <a href='/recipes/rice-crispy-treats'>Homemade Rice Crispy Treats</a>!
Instructions:
- Add <a href='https://link.amazon/B0guGB2NI'>gelatin</a> and cold water to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4rb3CiD'>large metal bowl</a>. Whisk together, and set it aside to bloom.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Sweet Spreads/Fluff/gelatin.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Bring <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#maple-syrup'>maple syrup</a> and water to a boil over high heat. Once boiling, continue to boil for 2 minutes.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Sweet Spreads/Fluff/boil.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Slowly pour the hot syrup into the <a href='https://link.amazon/B0guGB2NI'>gelatin</a> while mixing on low speed with an <a href='https://amzn.to/45yqsbM'>electric hand mixer</a>.

- Increase to high speed, and mix for at least 10 minutes, or until it becomes fluffy, white, and airy, like marshmallow fluff. Be patient, it will come together!
- <img src='/assets/Food/Sweet Spreads/Fluff/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Transfer to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4hcIXsT'>large container</a>.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Sweet Spreads/Fluff/container.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Chill for at least 2 hours before using. Store in the fridge.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Sweet Spreads/Fluff/cover5.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- <a href='#recipe'>This recipe</a> makes approximately 5 cups (480 g) of marshmallow fluff.
