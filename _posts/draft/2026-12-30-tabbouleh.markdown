Description: Tabbouleh, also spelled as tabouli or tabouleh, Is a Lebanese salad side dish of fresh herbs and veggies, bulgur wheat, and a lemony dressing. This salad with some grains brings out all the best flavors of the raw herbs and veggies, and is the perfect light side dish on a warm summer night.
Instructions:
- Add broth (or water) to a small pot. Cover and bring to a boil of high heat. When boiling, add in the bulgur, cover, and remove from the heat. Let sit for 15-30 minutes, or until the bulgur is tender and all water is absorbed. Remove the lid, and set aside to cool
- <img src='/assets/Food/Sides/Tabbouleh/bulgur.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- In a large bowl, whisk together the dressing ingredients - lemon juice, extra virgin olive oil, minced garlic, salt, pepper, coriander, and cinnamon
- <img src='/assets/Food/Sides/Tabbouleh/dressing.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Finely chop the parsley and mint, and add to the bowl. Dice the cucumber, tomato, and scallions, and add to the bowl, and mix together
- <img src='/assets/Food/Sides/Tabbouleh/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Chill until ready to serve. It's best to make this the day before to let the flavors come together
Notes:
- For a gluten free option, swap the bulgur for quinoa or brown rice
