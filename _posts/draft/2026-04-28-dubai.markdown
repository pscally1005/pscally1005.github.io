Description: |
  In case you haven't heard of this viral trend, Dubai style Chocolate is a <a href='https://amzn.to/40XU6EF'>thick chocolate bar</a> filled with <a href='/misc/nuts#pistachios'>pistachio butter</a> and a crunchy kataifi dough.  I'm a little late to the party, but here's my healthy spin on it.<br><br>

  The <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>chocolate</a> outside and <a href='/misc/nuts#pistachios'>pistachio</a> filling are <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar free</a>, being sweetened with <a href='https://amzn.to/4ceFnvP'>monk fruit</a>. The crunchiness comes from a mixture of plain shredded wheat cereal and <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>unsweetened coconut flakes</a>. And I'm using a mixture of <a href='/misc/seeds#sesame-seeds'>tahini</a> and homemade <a href='/misc/nuts#pistachios'>pistachio butter</a> for a creamy, crunchy, and delicious filling.
Instructions:
- In a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>small bowl</a>, microwave <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut oil</a> for 1 minute, until fully melted. Mix in <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>cocoa powder</a>, <a href='https://amzn.to/4ceFnvP'>granulated sweetener</a>, and salt with a <a href='https://amzn.to/40F2aK0'>silicone spatula</a> until smooth

- Pour half of your melted chocolate (80 g) into the <a href='https://amzn.to/40XU6EF'>thick chocolate mold</a>. Spread to all sides, and freeze as you prepare the filling
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Dubai/choc-1.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Roughly chop your wheat cereal to make a shredded crumb. Add to a medium pan over medium heat with the <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut flakes</a>. Toast for about 5 minutes, or until lightly browned and fragrant. Set aside
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Dubai/crunchy.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- In a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>medium bowl</a>, mix together <a href='/misc/nuts#pistachios'>pistachio butter</a>, <a href='/misc/seeds#sesame-seeds'>tahini</a>, and <a href='https://amzn.to/4ceFnvP'>granulated monk fruit</a>. Set aside
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Dubai/pistachio-butter.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Combine the cereal/<a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut</a> mixture with the <a href='/misc/nuts#pistachios'>pistachio butter</a> mixture.  Add your pistachio/crunchy mixture on top of the base of chocolate, packing it down to all sides
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Dubai/middle.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Pour your remaining melted chocolate over the filling
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Dubai/choc-2.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Chill for at least 20 minutes to harden the chocolate. Store in the fridge
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Dubai/bar.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
