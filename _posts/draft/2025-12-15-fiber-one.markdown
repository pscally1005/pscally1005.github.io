Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 350F.  Line a 9" square pan with parchment paper, and lightly grease the paper with oil

- In a large bowl, whisk together the dry ingredients - flour, inulin, monk fruit, cocoa powder, baking powder, and salt

- To make <a href='https://amzn.to/495pUN5'>Fiber One Cookie Bars</a> instead, replace the cocoa powder with powdered peanut butter

- Add in the wet ingredients (almond butter and water), and combine with a silicone spatula until combined and you have a thick but liquidy brownie batter
- <center><img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Fiber One/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'></center>

- Pour the batter into your prepared pan
- <center><img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Fiber One/raw.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'></center>

- Bake at 350F for about 30 minutes, or until a toothpick comes out nearly clean
- <center><img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Fiber One/baked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'></center>

- Allow to cool for at least 30 minutes in the pan in the fridge before cutting into 16 bars
