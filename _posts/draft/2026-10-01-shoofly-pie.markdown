Description: |
  No I did not make up "Shoofly Pie", though it sounds like a term I would coin.  A Shoofly Pie is a traditional Pennsylvania Dutch <a href='/recipes/healthier-dessert'>dessert</a> or <a href='/recipes/breakfast'>breakfast</a>.  It features a <a href='/recipes/pie-crust'>pie crust</a> and a <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>brown sugar</a> crumble top, with a <a href='https://amzn.to/4xnOctZ'>molasses</a> filling.<br><br>

  To make this healthier and lower in <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar</a>, I'm using my homemade <a href='/recipes/pie-crust'>Healthier Graham Cracker Pie Crust</a>, and replacing the <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar</a> topping with homemade <a href='/recipes/whipped-cream'>Aquafaba Whipped Cream</a>.<br><br>

  The main difference comes in the filling though.  I'll be lowering the full 1 cup of <a href='https://amzn.to/4xnOctZ'>molasses</a> to a more manageable 1/4 cup, and swapping out the <a href='https://amzn.to/4xnOctZ'>standard molasses</a> for <a href='https://amzn.to/4h7fdfj'>blackstrap molasses</a>.<br><br>

  Compared to <a href='https://amzn.to/4xnOctZ'>standard molasses</a>, <a href='https://amzn.to/4h7fdfj'>blackstrap molasses</a> is bolder in flavor and lower in <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar</a>.  It also contains notable amounts of some <a href='/misc/nutrient-alphabet'>nutrients</a> like <a href='/misc/calcium'>calcium</a>, <a href='/misc/nutrient-alphabet#C'>copper</a>, <a href='/misc/magnesium'>magnesium</a>, <a href='/misc/nutrient-alphabet#M'>manganese</a>, and <a href='/misc/potassium'>potassium</a>.  Though mainly, <a href='https://amzn.to/4h7fdfj'>blackstrap molasses</a> contains a significant amount of <a href='/misc/iron'>iron</a>, to the point where small doses of <a href='https://amzn.to/4h7fdfj'>blackstrap molasses</a> are often recommended for those with <a href='/misc/iron'>iron</a> deficiency.<br><br>

  To replace the remaining <a href='https://amzn.to/4xnOctZ'>molasses</a> that I left out, I will be using <a href='/recipes/sugar-free-syrup'>sugar free syrup</a>.  This provides the extra liquid needed for the filling, as well as sweetening the pie for minimal <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a>.  Finally, I'm mixing some delicious spices directly into the pie, including cinnamon, nutmeg, ginger, and cloves.<br><br>

  If you don't like the taste of <a href='https://amzn.to/4h7fdfj'>molasses</a>, then you probably won't like this pie.  But for me, the filling is a delicious, rich, and bold flavor full of gingerbread, caramel, and pumpkin spice notes.  It's a warming and filling <a href='/recipes/healthier-dessert'>dessert</a> or snack that's surprisingly easy to throw together.
Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 350F.

- Add <a href='/recipes/sugar-free-syrup'>sugar free syrup</a> to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4zyFnzN'>large microwave-safe bowl</a>. Microwave for 2 minutes, until hot.

- Add in the <a href='https://amzn.to/4h7fdfj'>molasses</a>, cinnamon, ginger, nutmeg, salt, and cloves. Whisk together to fully incorporate the spices.

- Crack in the <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>egg</a> and add the baking powder. Vigorously whisk to ensure there are no clumps of baking powder.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meme/Shoofly/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Transfer your batter to a prepared <a href='/recipes/pie-crust'>pie crust</a>. The filling should be about the consistency of <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#maple-syrup'>maple syrup</a>.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meme/Shoofly/raw.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Bake uncovered at 350F for about 45 minutes. The top and edges will be set, but the center will jiggle slightly when the side of the pan is tapped.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meme/Shoofly/baked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Let the pie chill in the fridge for at least 4 hours before topping with whipped cream. Cut into 8 slices.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meme/Shoofly/topped.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meme/Shoofly/cut.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:

  A <a href='https://brooklynfarmgirl.com/amish-shoofly-pie/#recipe'>traditional Shoofly Pie</a> would normally use a full cup (320 g) of <a href='https://amzn.to/4xnOctZ'>standard molasses</a> in the filling.  Instead, here I cut it down to 1/4 cup (80 g), went for <a href='https://amzn.to/4h7fdfj'>blackstrap molasses</a> instead of the <a href='https://amzn.to/4xnOctZ'>standard molasses</a>, and swapped out the hot water for hot <a href='/recipes/sugar-free-syrup'>sugar free syrup</a>.<br><br>

  <a href='https://brooklynfarmgirl.com/amish-shoofly-pie/#recipe'>Traditional</a> recipes also typically have a <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>brown sugar</a> crumble topping.
