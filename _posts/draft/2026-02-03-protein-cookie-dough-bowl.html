Notes
  1. I'm using almond butter here, but any natural nut or seed butter will work, such as cashew butter, peanut butter, sunflower seed butter, or tahini

  2. If you don't mind the extra sugar, net carbs, and calories, you can use honey (1/2 tbsp, 10 g) instead of liquid monk fruit as your sweetener, as well as opt for regular chocolate chips instead of the sugar-free variety
