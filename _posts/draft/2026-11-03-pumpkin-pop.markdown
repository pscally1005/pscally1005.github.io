Instructions:
- Mix all ingredients together in a <a href='https://amzn.to/4zyFnzN'>large bowl</a> to form a dough.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Protein Powder/Pumpkin Pops/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Roll into balls, about <i>32</i> total, <b>21 g</b> each, and place onto a plate or <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>cookie sheet</a>.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Protein Powder/Pumpkin Pops/plate.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Stick a toothpick into each one, and store in the refrigerator.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Protein Powder/Pumpkin Pops/cover-1.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- Any <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>nut or seed butter</a> will work, such as <a href='/misc/nuts#almonds'>almond butter</a>, <a href='/misc/nuts#cashews'>cashew butter</a>, <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a>, <a href='/misc/seeds#sesame-seeds'>tahini</a>, <a href='/misc/seeds#pumpkin-seeds'>pumpkin seed butter</a>, or even <a href='/recipes/pea-butter'>homemade pea butter</a>.

- <a href='/misc/dairy#casein'>Casein</a> can be used as a 1:1 substitute for <a href='/misc/dairy#pea-protein'>pea protein</a>. I have not tried this with <a href='/misc/dairy#whey'>whey</a>.

- |
  Optionally, you can coat these in melted chocolate. Freeze the <a href='#recipe'>pumpkin pops</a> with the toothpick for about 30 minutes before dipping in melted chocolate.<br><br>

  The weights of the <a href='#recipe'>pumpkin pops</a> both without and with chocolate are about <b>21 g</b> and <b>32 g</b>, respectively, meaning each <a href='#recipe'>pumpkin pop</a> holds about <i>11 g</i> of melted chocolate.<br><br>

  Doing this <i>32</i> times means that you would need <i>roughly</i> 2 cup (340 g) of melted chocolate for the entire batch, or roughly a standard size 12 oz (340 g) bag of chocolate chips. However, it's always helpful to have extra melted chocolate, as you will need excess to be able to properly coat them.

- Note that adding chocolate adds a significant amount of <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar</a>, <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a>, and <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a>, and no longer makes these <a href='/misc/high-protein'>high protein</a>.
