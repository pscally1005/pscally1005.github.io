Description: My famous <a href='/misc/veggies#broccoli'>broccoli</a> gets its name from my college's running club pasta parties, which we hosted the night before our races. I would make this every time, and it was always a massive hit and gone in an instant. If you're not really a fan of <a href='/misc/veggies#broccoli'>broccoli</a>, I'd really recommend giving this a try. It really masks the gross flavors of <a href='/misc/veggies#broccoli'>broccoli</a> with a properly seasoned dish. I always paired this with my <a href='/recipes/penne-casserole'>Penne and Meat Casserole Bake</a>.
