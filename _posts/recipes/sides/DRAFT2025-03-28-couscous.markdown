Description: Did you know that couscous is technically pasta? It contains 1 ingredient:<b></b> durum semolina, the same flour used for pasta, which unfortunately means it's not gluten free. This simple and delicious grain is like rice, but it cooks in just 5 minutes. Here, I'm softening an onion before adding in diced tomatoes and beans, giving my couscous extra bulk for my side dish
Instructions: 
- Preheat a medium pot over medium heat with olive oil. Dice your onion, and add to the pot with salt. Cover, and cook with occasional stirring until soft and translucent, about 5 minutes

- When the onion is soft, add in balsamic and spices (garlic powder, paprika, and black pepper)

- Add in the diced tomatoes and beans (not drained or rinsed). Increase heat to high, cover, and bring to a boil

- When boiling, remove from the heat and add in the couscous. Mix, cover, and let sit for 5 minutes. Fluff with a wooden spoon
