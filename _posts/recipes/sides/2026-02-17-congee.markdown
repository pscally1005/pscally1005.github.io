Description: Have you ever had congee? It's a savory <a href='/misc/grains#brown-rice'>rice</a> porridge popular in many Asian countries, and feels similar to risotto or even <a href='/oatmeal'>oatmeal</a>.  This recipe uses <a href='/misc/grains#brown-rice'>brown rice</a> for added nutrition and a nuttier flavor.  Customize it with your favorite toppings for a comforting meal or <a href='/recipes/sides'>side dish</a>.
Instructions:
- Add <a href='/misc/grains#brown-rice'>rice</a>, water, salt, and <a href='/misc/veggies#ginger'>ginger</a> to a <a href='https://amzn.to/46ccg8m'>medium pot</a>. Cover and bring to a boil over high heat

- Reduce heat to medium low, and let simmer (covered) with occasional stirring for about 60-90 minutes, or until thick and creamy
- <img src='/assets/Food/Sides/Congee/pot.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Top with whatever you like, such as a fried <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>egg</a>, cooked <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a>, roasted <a href='/misc/veggies'>vegetables</a>, <a href='/misc/veggies#mushrooms'>mushrooms</a>, or kimchi.  Here, I went with <a href='/misc/beans#edamame'>edamame</a>, soy sauce, and carmelized <a href='/misc/veggies#onion'>onions</a>.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Sides/Congee/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
