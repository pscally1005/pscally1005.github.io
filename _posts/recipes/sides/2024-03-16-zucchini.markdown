Description: This is something I've wanted to try ever since getting an air fryer, and I'm finally getting around to it. <a href='/misc/veggies#zucchini'>Zucchini</a> cut into fries, and breaded with <a href='/misc/dairy#grated-cheese'>parmesan</a> cheese (they're <a href='/misc/celiac'>gluten free</a>!). Healthy, delicious, and easy
Instructions:
- Preheat your air fryer to 400F

- Wash your zucchini, and cut into wedges. Add the zucchini to a strainer with salt. Mix, and let sit for about 10 minutes to draw out excess moisture. Transfer to a bowl

- Toss the zucchini in a light coating of olive oil. Add in the grated cheese and seasonings (basil, thyme, garlic, onion, pepper, and salt). Coat all the fries in the breading
- <center><img src='/assets/Food/Sides//Zucchini/zucchini-3.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'></center>

- Arrange fries in a single layer in your air fryer basket. You may have to do multiple batches if everything doesn't fit

- Lightly spray the tops with oil, and air fry at 400F for 8-10 minutes, or until the cheese is crispy and the zucchini is tender
