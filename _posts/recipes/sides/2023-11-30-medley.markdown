Description: I made some variation of this dish all the time. Most often, I use this in my morning <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>eggs</a>, like <a href='/recipes/frittata'>Spinach and Onion Frittata</a> and <a href='/recipes/spinach-mushroom-scrambled-eggs'>Spinach & Mushroom Scrambled Eggs</a>.  Or pair it as your <a href='/misc/veggies'>vegetable</a> to a meal with just a <a href='/misc/meat'>meat</a> and <a href='/misc/carbs'>carb</a>, like <a href='/recipes/indian-chicken'>Indian Chicken and Potatoes</a> or <a href='/recipes/marinated-chicken'>Marinated Chicken with Air Fried Plantains</a>.
Instructions:
- Heat a pan on medium heat with <a href='/misc/olive-oil'>olive oil</a>. Cut your <a href='/misc/veggies#onion'>onions</a> into a small dice, and add to the pan with salt. Cover and cook with occasional stirring until they start to turn translucent, about 10 minutes

- In a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>small bowl</a>, mix together the spices, and set aside - chili powder, paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, cumin, and black pepper

- When the <a href='/misc/veggies#onion'>onions</a> are translucent and lightly browned, add in the chopped <a href='/misc/veggies#spinach-fresh'>spinach</a> and spices. Mix together, cover, and cook over medium heat until most of the water has cooked off, and the <a href='/misc/veggies#spinach-fresh'>spinach</a> is done to your liking; about 20 minutes

- Stir in the <a href='/misc/apple-cider-vinegar'>apple cider vinegar</a> and <a href='/misc/fruit#lime-juice'>lime juice</a>, taste for additional seasonings, and serve
Notes:
- Here, I'm having these <a href='/misc/veggies'>veggies</a> as a <a href='/recipes/sides'>side</a> to a serving of <a href='/recipes/tilapia'>Air Fried Fish</a> and <a href='/recipes/slow-cooked-beans'>Slow Cooker Dried Beans</a>
