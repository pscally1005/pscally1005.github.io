Description: Ah <a href='/misc/grains#brown-rice'>rice</a> and <a href='/misc/beans'>beans</a>, the versatile <a href='/recipes/sides'>side</a> to nearly every meal. If you've noticed that most of my recipes are just <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a>+<a href='/misc/veggies'>vegetable</a>, this is why. I prefer to keep my <a href='/misc/carbs'>carb</a> separate, and that way I can decide if I want to have it or not for dinner. Most of the time when I have a <a href='/misc/carbs'>carb</a> <a href='/recipes/sides'>on the side</a>, I make some variation of <a href='/misc/grains#brown-rice'>rice</a> and <a href='/misc/beans'>beans</a>, as it's easy, cheap, and pretty healthy as far as <a href='/misc/grains'>grains</a> go.  <a href='/misc/grains#brown-rice'>Brown rice</a> is so much better for you than <a href='/misc/grains#white-rice'>white rice</a>, as <a href='/misc/grains#brown-rice'>brown rice</a> contains the <a href='/misc/grains'>whole grain</a>, being much less <a href='/misc/processed-foods'>processed</a> and a good source of <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a> and <a href='/misc/nutrient-alphabet'>micronutrients</a>.  My other go to <a href='/misc/carbs'>carb</a> <a href='/recipes/sides'>side</a> is <a href='/recipes/baked-sweet-potato'>Baked Sweet Potatoes</a>, so check that out too
Instructions:
- Add the <a href='/misc/grains#brown-rice'>rice</a> to a <a href='https://amzn.to/46ccg8m'>medium pot</a> with the water and bouillon powder (or alternatively you can use low sodium broth, or water+1/2 tsp salt). Drain and rinse your <a href='/misc/beans'>beans</a> with a strainer, and add to the pot as well

- Cover and bring your pot to a boil over high heat. When boiling, reduce heat to low, cover and cook about 45 minutes (this will depend on your brand of <a href='/misc/grains#brown-rice'>rice</a>)

- When the <a href='/misc/grains#brown-rice'>rice</a> is done, add in the rest of the seasonings (paprika, chili powder, cumin, garlic powder, onion powder, and black pepper), mix, and adjust to taste. I prefer to add in the <a href='/misc/beans'>beans</a> in the beginning, but can you wait until now to add them as well
Notes:
- Optionally, you can rinse the <a href='/misc/grains#brown-rice'>rice</a> prior to cooking. This makes the <a href='/misc/grains#brown-rice'>rice</a> drier and fluffier, instead of wet and clumpy (which I honestly prefer ngl, so I don't bother with this). Using a <a href='https://amzn.to/4q2FwWu'>fine mesh strainer</a>, rinse the <a href='/misc/grains#brown-rice'>rice</a> under cold water until the water runs clear
