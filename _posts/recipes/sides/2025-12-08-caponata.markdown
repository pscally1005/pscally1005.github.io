Description: Caponata is a Sicilian based <a href='/misc/veggies#eggplant'>eggplant</a> dish that's typically served cold as a <a href='/recipes/sides'>side</a> or a <a href='/recipes/savory-sauces'>dip</a>. It typically features cooked <a href='/misc/veggies#tomato'>tomatoes</a>, <a href='/misc/veggies#onion'>onion</a>, and <a href='/misc/veggies#celery'>celery</a>, with fancier versions also including capers, <a href='/misc/fruit#raisins'>raisins</a>, <a href='/misc/nuts#pine-nuts'>pine nuts</a>, and <a href='/misc/fruit#olives'>olives</a>. Most recipes tend to use excessive amounts of <a href='/misc/fats'>oil</a> though, sometimes up to 1/2 cup for just 2 <a href='/misc/veggies#eggplant'>eggplants</a>, as well as <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>added white sugar</a>. My version on the other hand cuts back on the <a href='/misc/fats'>excess oil</a> for a lower <a href='/misc/calories'>calorie</a> and healthier <a href='/recipes/sides'>side dish</a>, while also being <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>added sugar free</a>.  <a href='/misc/fruit#olives'>Olives</a> and <a href='/misc/nuts#pine-nuts'>pine nuts</a> provide whole food sources of <a href='/misc/fats'>healthy fats</a>, while <a href='/misc/veggies#eggplant'>eggplant</a>, <a href='/misc/veggies#tomato'>tomatoes</a>, and <a href='/misc/fruit#raisins'>raisins</a> provide <a href='/misc/carbs'>natural sweetness</a> to round out the dish
Instructions:
- Wash your eggplant, and cut into a medium dice, leaving the skin on. Add the eggplant to a large strainer, and mix with salt. Let sit for at least 20 minutes for the salt to draw out the excess moisture from the eggplant
- <img src='/assets/Food/Sides/Caponata/drained.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Preheat your oven to 400F, and line a large cookie sheet with parchment paper. Toss the drained eggplant with oil and pepper. Roast at 400F for about 30 minutes, or until browned and soft
- <img src='/assets/Food/Sides/Caponata/eggplant.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Meanwhile, heat a large pan over medium heat. Cut your onion and pepper into a medium dice and finely dice the celery. Add the veggies to the pan with oil and salt. Cover and cook until softened and lightly browned, about 8 minutes
- <img src='/assets/Food/Sides/Caponata/veggies.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Meanwhile, toast your pine nuts (or any other <a href='/misc/nuts'>nuts</a> or <a href='/misc/seeds'>seeds</a>) in a small dry pan over medium heat for about 3 minutes, or until lightly browned

- Add the pine nuts and the sauce ingredients to the pan with the veggies. Reduce heat to medium low, cover, and simmer until the eggplant is done roasting
- <img src='/assets/Food/Sides/Caponata/sauce.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Stir in the roasted eggplant, and cook for an additional 3 minutes to incorporate. Allow to cool totally before serving, ideally the next day
