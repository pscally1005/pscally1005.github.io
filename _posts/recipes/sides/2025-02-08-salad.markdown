Description: I've previously posted a full in-depth post as to why your <a href='/salad'>salads</a> are lame, with a guide for building the perfect <a href='/salad'>salad</a>; check out <a href='/misc/salad'>You're Doing Salad Wrong</a>.  But today, I decided to actually measure out and document the <a href='/salad'>salad</a> that I make all the time.  I make this large <a href='/salad'>salad</a> base at least twice a week, and use it as the basis of many of my meals. Just add this as a base to a bowl, and top it with whatever <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a>, <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a>, and <a href='/misc/carbs'>carbs</a> you desire for a delicious and nutritious meal
Instructions:
- Thinly slice your <a href='/misc/veggies#lettuce'>lettuce</a>, and add to a <a href='/misc/kitchen-products#salad-spinner'>salad spinner</a>. Rinse in the sink, spin the <a href='/salad'>salad</a>, and drain out the water

- Wash your <a href='/misc/veggies#pepper'>peppers</a> and <a href='/misc/veggies#tomato'>tomatoes</a>, and cut into a medium dice. Add to the <a href='/salad'>salad</a>

- Crumble the <a href='/misc/dairy#feta'>feta cheese</a> on top of the <a href='/misc/veggies'>vegetables</a>

- Add in your seasonings - <a href='/misc/veggies#garlic'>minced garlic</a>, lemon pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, and black pepper. Mix with tongs
- <img src='/assets/Food/Sides/Salad/mixed.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- Seasoning the <a href='/misc/veggies'>vegetables</a> makes them taste way better, but it also releases their water. Store the <a href='/salad'>salad</a> in the strainer part of the <a href='/misc/kitchen-products#salad-spinner'>salad spinner</a>, with the strainer in the <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>large bowl</a>. When you go to eat the <a href='/salad'>salad</a>, just lift the strainer out of the bowl and discard the released water
