Description: Here are 3 methods that I commonly use for making <a href='/misc/veggies#spaghetti-squash'>spaghetti squash</a> - oven (my preferred), <a href='https://amzn.to/49TUS9E'>crockpot</a>, or <a href='https://amzn.to/46PSfov'>instant pot</a>. <a href='/misc/veggies#spaghetti-squash'>Spaghetti squash</a> is such a versatile <a href='/misc/veggies'>vegetable</a>, since it can either be seasoned however you want, or mixed with any of your favorite <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#sauces'>pasta sauces</a>, such as <a href='/recipes/pasta-sauce'>Simple Pasta Sauce</a>, <a href='/recipes/pasta-with-clam-sauce'>Healthier Clam Sauce</a>, <a href='/recipes/evoo-pesto'>Lightened Extra Virgin Olive Oil Pesto</a>, or <a href='/recipes/bolognese'>Dutch Oven Bolognese Sauce</a>.
Instructions:
- |
  <i>Prep</i><br>
  Rinse your <a href='/misc/veggies#spaghetti-squash'>spaghetti squash</a>, pat it dry, and prick it with a fork.  Microwave your <a href='/misc/veggies#spaghetti-squash'>spaghetti squash</a> for 5 minutes, flipping halfway, to make it easier to cut in half if necessary

- |
  <i><a href='https://amzn.to/49TUS9E'>Slow Cooker</a></i><br>
  Cut in half <b>lengthwise</b>, and scoop out the seeds<br><br>

  Cover and cook for 3-4 hours on low for a 4-5 lb (1,816-2,270 g) <a href='/misc/veggies#spaghetti-squash'>spaghetti squash</a>. Be careful not to overcook, or it will be mush (been there before lol)

  Fluff with a fork into a bowl, season, and serve

- |
  <i>Oven</i><br>
  Cut in half <b>lengthwise</b>, scoop out the seeds, and sprinkle your seasonings inside<br><br>

  Roast cut side down for about 45 minutes at 400F on lined <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>cookie sheet</a> with parchment paper

  Fluff your <a href='/misc/veggies#spaghetti-squash'>spaghetti squash</a> with a fork into a bowl. Season to taste

- <img src='/assets/Food/Sides/Spaghetti Squash/spaghetti-squash-7.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- |
  <i><a href='https://amzn.to/46PSfov'>Pressure Cooker</a></i><br>
  Cut your <a href='/misc/veggies#spaghetti-squash'>spaghetti squash</a> in half <b>crosswise</b>, and scoop out the seeds

  Add ½-1 cup (120-240 g) water to the base of the pot and place in the steamer grate. Place the <a href='/misc/veggies#spaghetti-squash'>spaghetti squash</a> on top of the grate<br><br>

  Set to sealing and pressure cook for 9 minutes on Vegetable Steam. Let pressure naturally release for 5 minutes before manually releasing. Cook in 2 batches if it doesn’t fit

  Fluff with a fork into a bowl, season, and serve
