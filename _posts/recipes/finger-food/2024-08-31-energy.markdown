Description: No bake energy bites were the first <a href='/recipes/healthier-dessert'>healthy baking</a> recipe I had ever made. I had gotten this recipe from a friend 6 years ago or so, so I feel like it's time to bring it back. I've made some light modifications, like swapping the honey for <a href='/misc/fruit#dates'>dates</a> instead. These energy bites are a great grab and go snack, or topping to your morning <a href='/misc/dairy#yogurt'>Greek yogurt</a>.  For other no bake bite sized snacks, check out my <a href='/recipes/trail-mix-balls'>Trail Mix Balls</a>, <a href='/recipes/chickpea-chows'>Chickpea Chows</a>, or my <a href='/recipes/carrot-cake-bites'>Carrot Cake Energy Bites</a>
Instructions:
- Add your <a href='/misc/fruit#dates'>dates</a> to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>medium bowl</a>, and pour hot water over them. Let soak for 10 minutes.  Blend the <a href='/misc/fruit#dates'>dates</a>, soaking water, and <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>nut butter</a> until smooth in a <a href='https://amzn.to/4q0AUjI'>food processor</a>. Transfer to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>large bowl</a>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Energy Bites/energy-bites-processor.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Mix the rest of your ingredients together in the bowl. Chill in the fridge for at least 30 minutes so the <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oats</a>, <a href='/misc/seeds#chia-seeds'>chia seeds</a>, and <a href='/misc/seeds#flax-seeds'>flaxseeds</a> can absorb the moisture
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Energy Bites/energy-bites-bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Lightly wet your hands to prevent sticking, and roll into balls. Store in an airtight bag or container in the fridge
Notes:
- Both <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>quick oats</a> or <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>rolled oats</a> will work here

- Any sort of <a href='/misc/nuts'>nut</a> or <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>seed butter</a> will work here.  Normally, I use either <a href='/misc/nuts#almonds'>almond</a> or <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a>, but here I've used <a href='/misc/seeds#sesame-seeds'>tahini</a> for a friend with a <a href='/misc/nuts'>nut</a> allergy.  Other good options would be <a href='/misc/seeds#sunflower-seeds'>sunflower butter</a>, <a href='/misc/nuts#pistachios'>pistachio butter</a>, <a href='/misc/seeds#pumpkin-seeds'>pumpkin seed butter</a>, <a href='/misc/nuts#cashews'>cashew butter</a>, etc.

- In place of the <a href='/misc/fruit#dates'>dates</a> and water, you can use 1/4 to 1/3 cup (84 to 112 g) of honey, maple syrup, or <a href='/recipes/sugar-free-syrup'>sugar free syrup</a>.  Adjust sweetener to taste

- Optionally, add some mix-ins.  Good choices are 1/2 cup (90 g) mini chocolate chips, 1/2 cup (85 g) <a href='/misc/fruit#raisins'>raisins</a>, or 1/2 cup (70 g) chopped <a href='/misc/nuts'>nuts</a>
