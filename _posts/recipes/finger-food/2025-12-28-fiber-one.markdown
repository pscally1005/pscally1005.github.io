Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 350F.  Line a <a href='https://amzn.to/3YY2H9q'>9" square pan</a> with parchment paper, and <a href='https://amzn.to/3Hdg0gk'>lightly grease the paper with oil</a>

- In a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>large bowl</a>, whisk together the dry ingredients - <a href='/misc/grains#whole-wheat'>whole wheat flour</a>, <a href='https://amzn.to/47w8h7R'>inulin</a>, <a href='https://amzn.to/4ceFnvP'>granulated monk fruit</a>, <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>cocoa powder</a>, baking powder, and salt

- Add in the wet ingredients (vanilla, <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>egg</a>, <a href='/misc/nuts#almonds'>almond butter</a>, and water), and combine with a <a href='https://amzn.to/43FEbwe'>silicone spatula</a> until combined and you have a thick but liquidy brownie batter
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Fiber One/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Pour the batter into your prepared pan, and top with chocolate chips
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Fiber One/raw.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Bake at 350F for about 20 minutes, or until a toothpick comes out nearly clean
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Fiber One/baked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Allow to cool for at least 30 minutes in the pan in the fridge before cutting into 16 bars
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Fiber One/cut.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- For another recipe with <a href='https://amzn.to/47w8h7R'>inulin</a>, check out my <a href='/recipes/keto-bread-loaf'>Keto Bread Loaf Recipe</a>

- To make <a href='https://amzn.to/495pUN5'>Copycat Fiber One Cookie Bars</a> instead, replace the <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>cocoa powder</a> with <a href='https://amzn.to/4sgDH9S'>powdered peanut butter</a> (1/2 cup, 48 g)

- To make <a href='https://www.walmart.com/ip/Great-Value-Chocolate-Fudge-Fiber-Brownie-Baked-Bars-Value-Pack-0-89-oz-12-Count/163790745?fulfillmentIntent=Pickup&filters=%5B%7B%22intent%22%3A%22fulfillmentIntent%22%2C%22values%22%3A%5B%22Pickup%22%5D%7D%5D&classType=VARIANT&athbdg=L1600&from=/search'>Copycat Great Value Fiber Brownies</a> instead, replace the <a href='https://amzn.to/4ceFnvP'>granulated monk fruit</a> with granulated <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar</a> (6 tbsp, 72 g), and replace the <a href='/recipes/monkfruit-chocolate-chunks'>sugar free chocolate chips</a> with regular chocolate chips (2 tbsp, 30 g).
