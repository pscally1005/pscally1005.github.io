Description: <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#granola'>Granola</a> is a healthy snack; or is it? (cue Vsauce music).  Most store bought <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#granola'>granolas</a> tend to be filled with <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>added sugars</a> in the form of <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#dried-fruit'>sweetened dried fruit</a>, chocolate candies, and <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>corn syrup</a> to bind everything together, as well as <a href='/misc/fats'>unhealthy oils</a>.  Not this <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#granola'>granola</a> though; it cuts down on the <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar</a> and <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a>, leaving you with just the <a href='/misc/nutrient-alphabet'>macronutrients</a> from the food itself.  That being the <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oats</a> and the <a href='/misc/nuts'>nuts</a>, which are the star of the show.  Mix in whatever <a href='/misc/nuts'>nuts</a> you have, toast the <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#granola'>granola</a> in the oven, and use on top of <a href='/misc/dairy#yogurt'>yogurt</a> with fresh <a href='/misc/fruit#blueberries'>berries</a> for a delicious and healthy <a href='/recipes/breakfast'>breakfast</a>!  If you prefer <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#granola'>granola bars</a> to <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#granola'>granola</a>, you should make my <a href='/recipes/granola-bars'>Peanut Butter Banana Granola Bars</a>.
Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 325F, and line a <a href='https://amzn.to/4aiCsjh'>9x13" pan</a> with parchment paper

- Add your <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oats</a> to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>large bowl</a>. Roughly chop your <a href='/misc/nuts'>nuts</a> with a <a href='https://amzn.to/4q0AUjI'>food processor</a>, <a href='/misc/veggies'>vegetable</a> <a href='https://amzn.to/3VHhgMM'>chopper</a>, or <a href='https://amzn.to/4jlDKwc'>knife</a>, and add to the bowl. And in the rest of the dry ingredients (<a href='/misc/seeds#chia-seeds'>chia seeds</a>, cinnamon, and salt) and mix with a spoon
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Granola/granola-2.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Pour in the <a href='/misc/fruit#apple'>applesauce</a>, <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#honey'>honey</a>, and <a href='/recipes/sugar-free-syrup'>sugar free syrup</a>, and mix with your spoon until fully incorporated

- In a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>small bowl</a>, beat an <a href='/misc/meat#egg-whites'>egg white</a> with a fork (or electric mixer) until foamy (about a few minutes by hand or 30 seconds with an electric mixer). Add the <a href='/misc/meat#egg-whites'>egg white</a> to the <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#granola'>granola</a> and mix in fully

- Transfer your mix to the pan, and press evenly with your hands, minimizing gaps

- Bake for about 30 minutes, or until golden brown and lightly crispy. Let cool totally (at least 30 minutes on the counter) before breaking into small or large pieces (or both)
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Granola/granola-6.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- This recipe makes 16 servings. I had a final baked weight of 672 g, meaning each serving is 42 g. Store in a Ziploc bag in the fridge
