Description: Instead of <a href='/misc/grains#white-wheat'>refined white flour</a>, this dough uses <a href='/misc/nuts#almonds'>almond flour</a> and <a href='/misc/dairy#mozzarella'>mozzarella cheese</a> for a <a href='/misc/carbs'>low carb</a> and <a href='/misc/fats'>high fat</a> dough.  It's also multi-purpose, as I'm using this dough recipe to make both <a href='#empanada'>Ground Beef Empanadas</a> and <a href='#pierogi'>Sweet Potato Pierogi</a>.  This <a href='/recipes/finger-food'>finger food</a> is the perfect appetizer or meal for a group, and is a great activity for you and a partner to do together!  For similar <a href='/misc/nuts#almonds'>almond flour</a> and <a href='/misc/dairy#mozzarella'>mozzarella cheese</a> <a href='/recipes/bread'>bread recipes</a>, see my <a href='/recipes/gluten-free-pretzels'>Gluten Free Savory Pretzels</a> and <a href='/recipes/almond-flour-focaccia'>Almond Flour Focaccia</a>.
Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 350F, and line a large <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>cookie sheet</a> with parchment paper

- Add the <a href='/misc/dairy#mozzarella'>mozzarella</a> to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>large glass bowl</a>. Cut the <a href='/misc/dairy#cream-cheese'>cream cheese</a> into cubes, and add on top. Microwave for 1 minute, stirring halfway with a <a href='https://amzn.to/40F2aK0'>silicone spatula</a>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/Almond Flour Focaccia/cheese.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Add in the <a href='/misc/nuts#almonds'>almond flour</a> and a beaten <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>egg</a>, and mix in with the <a href='https://amzn.to/40F2aK0'>spatula</a> to make a combined and slightly sticky dough
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Empanada/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Scoop your dough into 12 equal sized pieces, about 2 tbsp (27 g) each, and roll into balls.  Place on the <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>cookie sheet</a>, and flatten into a round and thin circle, about 1/4" thick
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Empanada/circles.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Add filling to the bottom half of the dough circle, leaving a 1/2" border
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Empanada/open.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Use a <a href='https://amzn.to/4raSjqy'>metal spatula</a> to carefully lift up one end of the dough.  Carefully fold the dough over to make semicircles, and seal the dough shut with your hands
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Empanada/closed.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Bake at 350F for 20 minutes, flipping them over halfway, or until golden brown
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Empanada/cooked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Cool for 10 minutes before serving
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Empanada/cover2.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
