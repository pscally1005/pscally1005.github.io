Description: <a href='/copycat'>Copycat recipes</a> seem to do well, so here I am with another. My Clif bar <a href='/copycat'>copycat</a> version has nearly identical <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a>, <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a>, <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a>, <a href='/misc/carbs'>carbs</a>, and <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a> to the <a href='https://amzn.to/43GaeM1'>originals</a>. However, I'm using more minimally <a href='/misc/processed-foods'>processed</a> ingredients, while opting for <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#honey'>honey</a> as my sweetener instead of <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>refined sugar</a>.  These bars contain <a href='/misc/carbs'>complex carbs</a> and naturally occurring <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugars</a> to fuel your muscles, making them a great snack on the go for a hike, or before/during/after a hard workout.  That in addition to their <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a>, <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a>, and <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a> contents help in recovery, satiety, and digestion.
Instructions:
- Line a <a href='https://amzn.to/3YY2H9q'>9" square baking pan</a> with parchment paper. For a half batch, use a <a href='https://amzn.to/3YUjIkN'>9x5" bread pan</a>

- Mix together the wet ingredients (<a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#honey'>honey</a>, <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>nut butter</a>, and vanilla) in a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>large bowl</a> with a <a href='https://amzn.to/40F2aK0'>silicone spatula</a> until fully combined

- Add in the dry ingredients, and mix with a <a href='https://amzn.to/40F2aK0'>silicone spatula</a>

- Thin out with water as needed to get everything just fully combined together. You should have to fold with the <a href='https://amzn.to/40F2aK0'>spatula</a> to mix. It should look slightly too dry to be able to stick to all the <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oats</a>, but should feel slightly sticky to the touch
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Clif Bars/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Lightly wet your hands (or use <a href='https://amzn.to/414BfHP'>kitchen gloves</a>) and press the dough into all edges of the pan, making sure to pack it down to even thickness
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Clif Bars/pan.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Optionally top with about 2 tsp (10 g) of mini chocolate chips, <a href='/misc/fruit#raisins'>raisins</a>, chopped <a href='/misc/nuts'>nuts</a>, or flaky salt. Here, I'm going with <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>unsweetened coconut flakes</a>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Clif Bars/topped.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Chill in the fridge for at least 2 hours before slicing into bars. Wrap each bar in parchment paper, and store in an airtight bag in the fridge or freezer
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Clif Bars/cut.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- You can use any <a href='/recipes/sugar-free-syrup'>liquid sweetener</a> in these bars, such as the following
- INGREDIENTS(bulkbrowniessweeteners)

- Alternatively, you can sweeten these bars with <a href='/misc/fruit#dates'>dates</a>.  Blend 1 1/4 cup (200 g) of <a href='/misc/fruit#dates'>dates</a> with the <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>nut butter</a> and vanilla until smooth and you have no chunks of <a href='/misc/fruit#dates'>dates</a> left.  You may need more or less water in step 4 than what's called for above.  Add just enough until you get the correct texture

- To make it <a href='/misc/nuts'>nut</a> free, replace the <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a> with either <a href='/misc/seeds#sunflower-seeds'>sunflower seed butter</a> or <a href='/misc/seeds#sesame-seeds'>tahini</a> (1:1).  Replace the <a href='https://amzn.to/4sgDH9S'>powdered peanut butter</a> with vanilla (or chocolate) <a href='/misc/dairy#whey'>whey protein powder</a> (2 scoops, 60 g).  I will be assuming you're using <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#honey'>honey</a> as the sweetener below

- To make mint chocolate clif bars, replace the <a href='https://amzn.to/43MkDqr'>vanilla extract</a> with 1/4 tsp (1.5 g) <a href='https://amzn.to/4xtISFR'>mint extract</a>
