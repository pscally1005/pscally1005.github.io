Description: These <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#chips'>plantain chips</a> are healthy, crunchy, and great with any dip, like <a href='/recipes/classic-tahini-hummus'>Classic Tahini Hummus</a> or <a href='/recipes/ketchup'>Date Sweetened Ketchup</a>. They're the best replacement for standard <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#chips'>chips</a> while still feeling like the real deal (sorry sliced <a href='/misc/veggies#pepper'>peppers</a> and <a href='/misc/veggies#carrots'>carrots</a>, you're great too).  Using a <a href='https://amzn.to/4q3nlQz'>mandoline</a>, you can <a href='https://amzn.to/3FuWETp'>air fry</a> any <a href='/misc/carbs'>starchy</a> <a href='/misc/veggies'>vegetable</a> until crispy, not just <a href='/misc/veggies#plantain'>plantains</a>.  <a href='/misc/veggies#beets'>Beets</a> and <a href='/misc/veggies#sweet-potato'>sweet potatoes</a> make great options too!
Instructions:
- Cut the ends off the <a href='/misc/veggies#plantain'>plantain</a>, and peel with a knife. Using a <a href='https://amzn.to/4q3nlQz'>mandoline</a> set to 1.5 mm (1/16") thickness, thinly slice your <a href='/misc/veggies#plantain'>plantain</a>, and add to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>medium bowl</a>. Drizzle with oil, and season with garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and turmeric
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Plantain Chips/plantain-chips-cut.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Lightly <a href='https://amzn.to/3Hdg0gk'>spray</a> the basket with oil. Arrange in a SINGLE layer in your <a href='https://amzn.to/3FuWETp'>air fryer</a> basket (you will need to fry in multiple batches). Lightly <a href='https://amzn.to/3Hdg0gk'>spray</a> the tops with oil.  I over-crowded them here; try to have none of them touching
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Plantain Chips/plantain-chips-raw.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- <a href='https://amzn.to/3FuWETp'>Air fry</a> at 350F for about 10-12 minutes, or until golden brown, but not burnt.  You can also bake in a 350F oven for 25-30 minutes. Transfer to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4qQNmn2'>wire rack</a> to cool and crisp.  Store leftovers in an airtight container in the fridge
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Plantain Chips/plantain-chips-cooked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Plantain Chips/plantain-chips-cooling.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- You can see my <a href='/misc/veggies#plantain'>plantain</a> here is definitely more yellow than green; try to get as green as possible, as it will make the <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#chips'>chips</a> easier to slice and they'll turn out crispier.  The more yellow it is, the sweeter they will be, but they'll be softer, break apart, and not get crispy
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Plantain Chips/plantain-chips-uncut.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- You can also make <a href='/misc/veggies'>veggie</a> <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#chips'>chips</a> out of <a href='/misc/veggies#sweet-potato'>sweet potatoes</a>!
