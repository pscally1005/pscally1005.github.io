Description: These simple cookies are made with just mashed <a href="/misc/fruit#banana">banana</a> and rolled <a href="/misc/grains#oats">oats</a>. They can be easily customized by switching out the banana for unsweetened applesauce, pumpkin puree, or really anything you can think of. Or feel free to fold in whatever mix-ins you like, such as chocolate chips, <a href="/misc/fruit#raisin">raisins</a>, or chopped <a href="/misc/nuts">nuts</a>.  Or check out any of my other <a href="/cookies">cookie recipes</a>!
Instructions: 
- Preheat your oven to 350F and line a cookie sheet with parchment paper

- In a bowl, add your banana, and mash with the back of a fork until smooth. Mix in the oats, with an optional small pinch of salt
- <center><img src="/assets/Food/Finger Food/2 Ing Oat Cookie/oat-cookies-2.jpg" alt="" class="instruction-image"></center>

- Some other great options instead of banana are:<b></b><br><ul><li>Unsweetened Applesauce</li><li><a href="/recipes/apple-spread">Apple Butter</a></li><li><a href="/recipes/pumpkin-puree">Pumpkin Puree</a></li><li><a href="/recipes/sweet-potato-puree">Sweet Potato Puree</a></li><li><a href="/recipes/roasted-butternut-squash-puree">Butternut Squash Puree</a></li><li><a href="/recipes/berry-jam">Berry Jam</a></li></ul><br>Below is a batch I made with homemade blackberry jam (left) and applesauce (right)
- <center><img src="/assets/Food/Finger Food/2 Ing Oat Cookie/oat-cookies-3.jpg" alt="" class="instruction-image"></center>

- At this step, you can add any other flavors if you like. A dash of cinnamon or vanilla would be great, or you can fold in some mix-ins like chocolate chips or nuts

- Scoop onto your lined tray, and lightly flatten the tops.  Bake at 350F for about 12 minutes. Transfer to a wire rack to cool
