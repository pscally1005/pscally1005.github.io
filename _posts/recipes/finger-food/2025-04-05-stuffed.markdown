Description: These stuffed <a href='/misc/veggies#pepper'>peppers</a> are filled with <a href='/misc/meat#ground-turkey'>ground turkey</a>, <a href='/misc/grains#brown-rice'>brown rice</a>, and taco seasonings.  But they're super customizable, see <a href='#notes'>notes</a> below
Instructions:
- In a small pot, add your <a href='/misc/grains#brown-rice'>rice</a>, water, and salt. Bring to a boil over high heat. Cover, reduce to low, and let simmer for about 20-30 minutes, until fully cooked. Set aside

- Meanwhile, preheat your oven to 400F, and line a <a href='https://amzn.to/4aiCsjh'>9x13" pan</a> with parchment paper

- Cut the <a href='/misc/veggies#pepper'>peppers</a> in half, and scoop out the ribs and seeds.  Rinsing under the sink helps to remove seeds  Place cut side up in your pan

- In a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>large bowl</a>, mix together the filling - cooked <a href='/misc/grains#brown-rice'>rice</a>, <a href='/recipes/ground-meat'>ground meat</a>, <a href='/misc/dairy#mozzarella'>cheese</a>, <a href='/misc/veggies#garlic'>minced garlic</a>, chili powder, garlic powder, onion powder, cumin, and salt
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Stuffed/filling.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Stuff the <a href='/misc/veggies#pepper'>peppers</a>, about 100 g in each <a href='/misc/veggies#pepper'>pepper</a> half. Optionally top with more <a href='/misc/dairy#mozzarella'>cheese</a> or <a href='/recipes/ketchup'>Unsweetened BBQ Sauce</a>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Stuffed/raw.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Cover the pan with aluminum foil, and roast at 400F for about 1 hour. The <a href='/misc/veggies#pepper'>peppers</a> should be tender and the <a href='/misc/meat'>meat</a> fully cooked (at least <a href='https://amzn.to/4kmobG2'>165F</a>)
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Stuffed/cooked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- |
  This post is more of a guide on how to make stuffed peppers; below are different filling options you can try
  <ul>
    <li>Replace the ground turkey with either ground beef or <a href='/misc/ground-chicken'>ground chicken</a></li>
    <li>Vegetarian stuffed peppers using either <a href='/recipes/rice-and-beans'>Classic Rice and Beans</a> or <a href='/recipes/couscous'>Tomato and Bean Couscous</a> as your filling</li>
    <li>Mediterranean stuffed peppers using my <a href='/recipes/mediterranean-potato-salad'>Mediterranean Sweet Potato Salad</a> or <a href='/recipes/bean-salad'>Mediterranean 3 Bean Salad</a></li>
    <li>Fill it with just beans, such as <a href='/recipes/slow-cooked-beans'>Slow Cooker Black Beans</a>, <a href='/recipes/refried-beans'>Refried Pinto Beans</a>, or <a href='/recipes/baked-beans'>No Added Sugar Baked Beans</a></li>
    <li>Replace the <a href='/misc/grains#brown-rice'>brown rice</a> with <a href='/recipes/red-lentils'>red lentils</a>, <a href='/recipes/cauliflower-rice'>cauliflower rice</a>, <a href='/misc/grains#quinoa'>quinoa</a>, or couscous</li>
    <li>Mix in some <a href='/recipes/bbq-sauce'>Unsweetened BBQ Sauce</a> or <a href='/recipes/ketchup'>Date Sweetened Ketchup</a> to the filling, or add on top of the peppers</li>
  </ul>
