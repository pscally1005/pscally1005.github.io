Description: These super simple croutons are made using <a href='/misc/beans#cannellini-beans'>cannellini beans</a> (or any other <a href='/misc/beans'>beans</a> you have).  Depending on your <a href='https://amzn.to/44VXByC'>soy sauce</a>, they can be a great <a href='/misc/celiac'>gluten free</a> alternative, as well as being healthier than traditional croutons.  Just <a href='https://amzn.to/3FuWETp'>air fry</a> or roast the <a href='/misc/beans'>beans</a> until crispy, as use them to top a <a href='/salad'>salad</a>.  They go great with my <a href='/recipes/greek-yogurt-caesar-dressing'>Greek Yogurt Caesar Dressing</a>.
Instructions:
- Drain and rinse your <a href='/misc/beans'>beans</a>, and add to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>medium bowl</a>. Add in the rest of the ingredients (<a href='https://amzn.to/44VXByC'>soy sauce</a>, garlic powder, oregano, and paprika), and toss to combine
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Croutons/croutons-raw.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- <a href='https://amzn.to/3FuWETp'>Air fry</a> at 400F for 15-20 minutes, mixing halfway. You can also bake in a 400F oven instead for 30-45 minutes

Notes:
- The whole batch (cooked) weighs about 100 g, so each serving is about 33 g

- The cover photo for this recipe is a Caesar salad I made by tossing together half a large head of <a href='/misc/veggies#lettuce'>romaine lettuce</a>, 1 serving of my <a href='/recipes/greek-yogurt-caesar-dressing'>Greek Yogurt Caesar Dressing</a>, 1 serving (33 g) of these croutons, and 1/2 oz (15 g) crumbled <a href='/misc/dairy#feta'>feta cheese</a>
