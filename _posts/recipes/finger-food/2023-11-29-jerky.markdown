Description: <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>Chicken</a> jerky is a great <a href='/misc/high-protein'>high protein</a> snack, and it's way cheaper than buying <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#processed-meats'>beef jerky</a> at the store (or use this to make <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#processed-meats'>beef jerky</a> too). It's simple to do, just requiring an overnight marinate of thinly sliced <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken breasts</a>, and a few hours of dehydrating. They're spicy, sweet, salty, healthy, and delicious.
Instructions:
- Thinly slice the <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a>, about 1/4 inch thick. Using slightly frozen <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> will make it easier to slice. Mix with the marinade, and let sit in the fridge overnight (covered if in a bowl, or seal the bag).  You can add 1 tbsp (16 g) gochjang into the marinade as well if you like

- Remove the excess marinade and add to a parchment lined basket. <a href='https://amzn.to/3FuWETp'>Air fry</a> at 370F for about 9-10 minutes, or until the <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> just reaches <a href='https://amzn.to/4kmobG2'>165F</a>. This is to ensure safety and that all salmonella is cooked off prior to dehydrating

- Switch your <a href='https://amzn.to/3FuWETp'>air fryer</a> to dehydrate mode at 170F. Cook for an additional 3 hours (flipping halfway), or until you have your desired texture. Store in an air tight container in the fridge
