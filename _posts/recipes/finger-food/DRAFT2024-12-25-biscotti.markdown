Description: Merry Christmas!  What better way to enjoy a morning Christmas brunch with your family than by dipping homemade biscotti into your coffee or hot chocolate.  This is a small batch of homemade that's free of gluten and refined sugar, and baked in your air fryer, so they're nice and crunchy
Instructions: 
- Preheat your air fryer to 360F, and cut a piece of parchment paper that will fit inside.  You can also use an air fryer liner

- In a large bowl, combine together the dry ingredients - oat flour, almond meal, cornstarch, baking powder, and salt

- Almond meal is ground whole almonds, as opposed to almond flour, which is ground blanched almonds. If you don't have almond meal, then blend some whole almonds in a food processor until fine

- For chocolate biscotti, replace 2 tbsp (10 g) of oat flour with cocoa powder, or maybe stir in some chocolate chips instead of almonds

- In a separate bowl, beat together the wet ingredients - egg, sweetener, and vanilla

- Combine together, and mix until just combined. Fold in roughly chopped nuts

- Transfer to your parchment paper or air fryer liner, and form into a rectangle
- <center><img src="/assets/Food/Finger Food/Biscotti/raw.jpg" alt="" class="instruction-image"></center>

- Air fry at 360F for 12 minutes. Remove from the air fryer, and let cool on a wire rack for 15 minutes. Lower the air fryer temperature to 300F

- After cooling, slice into your biscotti. Air fry at 300F for 10 minutes, flipping halfway through.  Transfer to a wire rack to cool completely
