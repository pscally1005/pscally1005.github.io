Description: What do <a href='/recipes/chicken-fingers'>Gluten Free Chicken Tenders</a>, <a href='/recipes/chicken-nuggets'>Freezer Chicken Nuggets</a>, <a href='/recipes/black-bean-burger'>Protein Black Bean Burgers</a>, and <a href='/recipes/burger-patties'>Simple Burger Patties</a> all have in common?  That's right, a <a href='/recipes/sides'>side</a> of <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#potatoes'>fries</a>. I've made <a href='/recipes/sweet-potato-fries'>Spiced Sweet Potato Fries</a> before by cutting <a href='/misc/veggies#sweet-potato'>sweet potatoes</a> into long, thin <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#potatoes'>fries</a> and <a href='https://amzn.to/3FuWETp'>air frying</a> them. But today, I'm using my <a href='https://amzn.to/4q3nlQz'>mandoline</a> to make waffle fries instead. Compared to traditional <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#potatoes'>fries</a>, these have significantly less <a href='/misc/fats'>oil</a> as they're not deep fried.   Feel free to swap <a href='/misc/veggies#sweet-potato'>sweet potatoes</a> for regular white or yellow <a href='/misc/veggies#potato'>potatoes</a>. All <a href='/misc/veggies#potato'>potatoes</a> are healthy (as long as they're prepared right).  Serve with a <a href='/recipes/sides'>side</a> of <a href='/recipes/bbq-sauce'>Unsweetened BBQ Sauce</a> or <a href='/recipes/ketchup'>Date Sweetened Ketchup</a>
Instructions:
- Wash your <a href='/misc/veggies#sweet-potato'>sweet potato</a>, and slice off the ends (but don't peel it!). Slice the <a href='/misc/veggies#sweet-potato'>sweet potato</a> using a <a href='https://amzn.to/4q3nlQz'>mandoline</a> on the waffle fry setting

- Add your <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#potatoes'>fries</a> to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>glass bowl</a>, and toss with vinegar, <a href='/misc/fats'>oil</a>, and spices. Cover with a plate, and microwave for 5 minutes to soften the inside a little before <a href='https://amzn.to/3FuWETp'>air frying</a>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Waffle Fries/raw.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Add to your <a href='https://amzn.to/3FuWETp'>air fryer</a> basket. You may have to fry in 2 batches. <a href='https://amzn.to/3FuWETp'>Air fry</a> at 400F for about 6-8 minutes, or until the outside is browned and crispy, and the center is soft and tender
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Waffle Fries/cooked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- This recipe only makes 1 serving as that's all that can fit in the <a href='https://amzn.to/3FuWETp'>air fryer</a> at a time, but you can easily scale it up and fry in batches. Or you can bake in the oven, but it will take about double the time and not be as crispy
