Description: Making <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a> cups at home is one of the classic desserts, as it's so easy to do. But instead of double dipping in <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a> with both <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a> and melted chocolate, I've swapped the filling for <a href='https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07SXBL1GF?ref=t_ac_view_request_product_image&campaignId=amzn1.campaign.2L4DOI4F1KV3G&linkCode=tr1&tag=poormanprotei-20&linkId=amzn1.campaign.2L4DOI4F1KV3G_1767022097417'>powdered peanut butter</a>, lowering <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a> and raising the <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a>. Additionally, both the filling and coating are sweetened with monk fruit, making them <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar free</a> as well
Instructions:
- In a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>medium bowl</a>, whisk together <a href='https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07SXBL1GF?ref=t_ac_view_request_product_image&campaignId=amzn1.campaign.2L4DOI4F1KV3G&linkCode=tr1&tag=poormanprotei-20&linkId=amzn1.campaign.2L4DOI4F1KV3G_1767022097417'>powdered peanut butter</a>, sweetener, salt, and cinnamon. Add in the vanilla and <a href='/misc/fruit#apple'>applesauce</a>, and mix with a <a href='https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0C37QM1K3?ref=t_ac_view_request_product_image&campaignId=amzn1.campaign.1OO1S5W7ZMYBB&linkCode=tr1&tag=poormanprotei-20&linkId=amzn1.campaign.1OO1S5W7ZMYBB_1751469633605'>silicone spatula</a> until you have a fully combined dough
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/PB Cups/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Scoop small balls onto a parchment lined plate, about 8 g each. Lightly wet your fingers, and flatten into even disks. Freeze for 15 minutes to harden
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/PB Cups/not-coated.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Meanwhile, clean your bowl, and add in the <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut oil</a>. Microwave for 1 minute to melt, and stir in the <a href='/misc/overshadowed-healthy-foods#chocolate'>cocoa powder</a> and sweetener
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/PB Cups/chocolate.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Using 2 forks, lightly coat each <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a> disc in the chocolate, and transfer back to the plate. Optionally, top with flaky salt. Refrigerate to harden
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/PB Cups/coated.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
