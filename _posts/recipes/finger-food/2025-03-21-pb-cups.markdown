Description: |
  Making <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a> cups at home is one of the classic desserts, as it's so easy to do. But instead of double dipping in <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a> with both <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a> and melted chocolate, I've swapped the filling for <a href='https://amzn.to/4sgDH9S'>powdered peanut butter</a>, lowering <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a> and raising the <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a>. Additionally, both the filling and coating are sweetened with <a href='https://amzn.to/4ceFnvP'>granulated monk fruit</a>, making them <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar free</a> as well.<br><br>

  For a more traditional peanut butter cup recipe, check out my <a href='/recipes/peanut-butter-cups'>Homemade Dark Chocolate Peanut Butter Cups</a>!
Instructions:
- In a <a href='https://amzn.to/4zyFnzN'>medium bowl</a>, whisk together <a href='https://amzn.to/4sgDH9S'>powdered peanut butter</a>, <a href='https://amzn.to/4ceFnvP'>granulated sweetener</a>, salt, and cinnamon. Add in the <a href='https://amzn.to/43MkDqr'>vanilla</a> and <a href='/misc/fruit#apple'>applesauce</a>, and mix with a <a href='https://amzn.to/40F2aK0'>silicone spatula</a> until you have a fully combined dough
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/PB Cups/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Scoop small balls onto a parchment lined plate, about 8 g each. Lightly wet your fingers, and flatten into even disks. Freeze for 15 minutes to harden
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/PB Cups/not-coated.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Meanwhile, clean your bowl, and add in the <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut oil</a>. Microwave for 1 minute to melt, and stir in the <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>cocoa powder</a> and <a href='https://amzn.to/4ceFnvP'>granular sweetener</a>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/PB Cups/chocolate.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Using 2 forks, lightly coat each <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a> disc in the <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>chocolate</a>, and transfer back to the plate. Optionally, top with flaky salt. Refrigerate to harden
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/PB Cups/coated.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
