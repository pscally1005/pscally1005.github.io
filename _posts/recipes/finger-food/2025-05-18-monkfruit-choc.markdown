Description: This recipe is what I make most often as a substitute for chocolate chips.  They're <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar free</a>, contain minimal ingredients, and much cheaper than buying sugar free chocolate chips.  Just mix together equal parts (by weight) of <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut oil</a>, <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>cocoa powder</a>, and <a href='https://amzn.to/4ceFnvP'>granular sweetener</a> for a dark chocolate bar equivalent to about 70%.  This recipe makes 6 oz (168 g) of chocolate, or about of 1 cup of chopped chocolate.  Recently, I used these chocolate chunks in my <a href='/recipes/keto-brownies'>Flourless Keto Brownies</a>, but you can swap them for chocolate chips wherever!
Instructions:
- In a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>small bowl</a>, microwave <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut oil</a> for about 1 minute, until melted. Stir in <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>cocoa powder</a> and <a href='https://amzn.to/4ceFnvP'>granulated sweetener</a> with a <a href='https://amzn.to/40F2aK0'>silicone spatula</a> until smooth
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Monk Fruit Chocolate/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Pour the melted chocolate into <a href='https://amzn.to/4qZKHI9'>molds</a>, and refrigerate for 1 hour to harden. Break into large pieces, or chop into smaller chunks
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Monk Fruit Chocolate/mold.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- |
  If you're going to be <a href='/recipes/healthier-dessert'>baking</a> with this recipe, then you can add 1 large <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>egg yolk</a> into the melted chocolate before chilling it.<br><br>

  Lecithin, a compound found in <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>egg yolks</a> is typically added into chocolate chips to prevent melting while baking.  This is the reason why cookies made with chocolate chips have chocolate that holds its shape, while cookies made chocolate chunks experience much more melting and pools of chocolate.  Adding the raw yolk to the chocolate will prevent the homemade chocolate from melting as much while baking as compare to standard homemade chocolate.<br><br>

  The raw melted chocolate will be much thicker with the <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>egg yolk</a>, more like a dough.  It will still harden and chop the same as a standard bar of chocolate, so follow the same chilling and storing instructions
