Description: |
  Most <a href='/misc/celiac'>gluten free</a> <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#crackers'>crackers</a> are still no healthier than standard <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#crackers'>crackers</a>. In fact, they can be worse for you, having more oil and <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar</a> with a lower <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a> content.<br><br>

  Not these <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#crackers'>crackers</a>; they're <a href='/misc/celiac'>gluten free</a>, vegan, <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar free</a>, <a href='/misc/fats'>oil free</a>, <a href='/misc/nuts'>nut</a> free, and <a href='/misc/grains'>grain</a> free. The base is made up of <a href='/misc/seeds#flax-seeds'>flaxseeds</a> and <a href='/misc/seeds#chia-seeds'>chia seeds</a>, 2 great <a href='/misc/fats'>healthy fats</a> that are both rich in the <a href='/misc/nutrient-alphabet#O'>Omega-3 fatty acid ALA</a>, as well as being good sources of both <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a> and <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a>.<br><br>

  These <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#crackers'>crackers</a> not only taste great and are nice and crispy, but they also won't spike your <a href='/misc/diabetes'>blood sugar</a>.  This recipe is an adapted version of "Herbed <a href='/misc/seeds#flax-seeds'>Flax</a> <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#crackers'>Crackers</a>" from the book Good Energy, and pair perfectly with <a href='/recipes/classic-tahini-hummus'>Classic Tahini Hummus</a>.  For another <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#crackers'>cracker</a> recipe, check out my <a href='/recipes/triscuits'>Copycat Triscuit Crackers</a>
Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 325F, and line 2 large <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>cookie sheets</a> with parchment paper

- In a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>large bowl</a>, whisk together the dry ingredients. Add water, and stir until fully combined and you have a cracker dough
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Flax Crackers/flax-crackers-bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Divide the dough in half, and roll out to 1/8" thick between 2 sheets of parchment paper.  Cut with a <a href='https://amzn.to/3O5nlSH'>pizza wheel</a> or <a href='https://amzn.to/4jlDKwc'>knife</a>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Flax Crackers/flax-crackers-cut.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Bake at 325F for 60 minutes, or until crispy and golden brown
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Flax Crackers/flax-crackers-baked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Let cool totally on a <a href='https://amzn.to/4qQNmn2'>wire rack</a>.  Store in an airtight bag at <b>room temperature</b>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/Flax Crackers/flax-crackers-cooled.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- I ended up with about 8 oz (226 g) of <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#crackers'>crackers</a>. For a 1 oz (28 g) serving, that's about 8 servings total
