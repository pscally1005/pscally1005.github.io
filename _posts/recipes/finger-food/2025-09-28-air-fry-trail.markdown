Description: Using your <a href='https://amzn.to/3FuWETp'>air fryer</a> is a fun way to make a good <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#trail-mix'>trail mix</a>, and this one uses roasted <a href='/misc/beans#chickpeas'>chickpeas</a>, paprika spiced <a href='/misc/nuts#almonds'>almonds</a>, and <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#chips'>banana chips</a>. Simply roast all 3 and toss with your desired spices before combining together. It goes great on a <a href='/salad'>salad</a> or a bowl of <a href='/misc/dairy#yogurt'>yogurt</a>, or even on its own!  For a standard <a href='/misc/fruit'>fruit</a> and <a href='/misc/nuts'>nuts</a> <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#trail-mix'>trail mix</a>, see my <a href='/recipes/trail-mix'>Mixed Nuts & Chocolate Trail Mix</a>
Instructions:
- Preheat your <a href='https://amzn.to/3FuWETp'>air fryer</a> to 400F, and drain and rinse the <a href='/misc/beans#chickpeas'>chickpeas</a>. <a href='https://amzn.to/3FuWETp'>Air fry</a> the <a href='/misc/beans#chickpeas'>chickpeas</a> for about 14 minutes, or until browned and crispy.  Remove to a bowl, and toss with the spices. Set aside

- Heat your <a href='https://amzn.to/3FuWETp'>air fryer</a> back up to 400F. <a href='https://amzn.to/3FuWETp'>Air fry</a> the <a href='/misc/nuts'>nuts</a> for about 5 minutes, or until lightly browned. Remove to another bowl, and toss with spices. Set aside

- Meanwhile, use a <a href='https://amzn.to/4jlDKwc'>knife</a> to cut off the peel of your underripe, green <a href='/misc/fruit#banana'>banana</a>.  Cut as thin as possible with a knife (or <a href='https://amzn.to/4q3nlQz'>mandoline</a>)

- Lower the <a href='https://amzn.to/3FuWETp'>air fryer</a> to 250F. <a href='https://amzn.to/3FuWETp'>Air fry</a> the <a href='/misc/fruit#banana'>banana</a> slices for about 25 minutes, or until lightly browned and dried.  Remove to a third bowl, and toss with the spices

- When totally cooled, mix the 3 bowls together, and transfer to an airtight bag.  Store at room temperature for up to 1 week
