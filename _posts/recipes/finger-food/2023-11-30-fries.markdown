Description: Sweet potato fries are a perfect quick <a href='/recipes/sides'>side</a> to a meal or easy snack. They come together in about 15 minutes, and they're much healthier and more flavorful than regular <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#potatoes'>fries</a>. Everyone knows that chicken nuggets and <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#potatoes'>french fries</a> are a classic combo, so see my <a href='/recipes/chicken-nuggets'>Freezer Chicken Nuggets</a> for a nutritious version of this meal.
Instructions:
- Wash your <a href='/misc/veggies#sweet-potato'>sweet potato</a>, and cut into a fry shape. Add to a bowl, and microwave on high (covered) for 3 minutes

- Toss the <a href='/misc/veggies#sweet-potato'>sweet potato</a> with the <a href='/misc/olive-oil'>oil</a> and the spices

- Lightly <a href='https://amzn.to/3Hdg0gk'>spray</a> the basket with oil, and arrange the fries in a single layer, giving room for the air to circulate. You may need to fry them in 2 batches.  <a href='https://amzn.to/3FuWETp'>Air fry</a> for about 6 minutes at 400F, or until done to your liking
