Description: Here I'm using <a href="/misc/fruit#strawberries">strawberries</a> (or <a href="/misc/fruit#blueberries">blueberries</a>) and <a href="/misc/seeds#chia-seeds">chia</a> seeds to make a delicious <a href="/misc/fake-healthy-foods#jelly">jam</a> that really lets the flavor of the <a href="/misc/fruit">fruit</a> shine. Most fruit jams contain significantly more <a href="/misc/hidden-sugar">sugar</a> than actual fruit, but not this one.  Each serving is about 1 tbsp, or 19g.  <br>&emsp;Pictured here is a rice cake topped with <a href="/recipes/natural-peanut-butter">Homemade Natural Nut Butter</a> (using a blend of <a href="/misc/nuts#peanuts">peanuts</a> and <a href="/misc/nuts#pistachios">pistachios</a>) and the strawberry jam for a delicious snack.  Strawberries and blueberries are lower in pectin compared to other berries, so chia seeds are used as a thickener. You can also check out my <a href="/recipes/berry-jam">Low Sugar Berry Jam</a> for a <a href="/misc/fruit#blackberry">blackberry</a> (or <a href="/misc/fruit#raspberry">raspberry</a>) version, which has no chia seeds since these berries contain more pectin
Instructions: 
- Add your berries to a medium bowl, and microwave until soft. Or you can leave on the counter for a bit to defrost

- In a large pan over medium heat, add the berries and sweetener. Roughly mash with a potato masher

- Bring to a simmer, and let simmer for 2-3 minutes, until thickened

- Remove from heat, stir in the chia seeds and lemon juice, and transfer to an airtight container. The jam will thicken as it cools
