Description: With just 3 simple ingredients, you can make a delicious and healthy frosting for all your healthy cakes, cupcakes, muffins, dips, or whatever else you can think of. Just combine together blended <a href='/misc/dairy#cottage-cheese'>cottage cheese</a>, <a href='/misc/dairy#whey'>vanilla whey protein powder</a>, and <a href='https://amzn.to/43MkDqr'>vanilla extract</a> for a super duper <a href='/misc/high-protein'>high protein</a> frosting that's <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar free</a> and <a href='/misc/fats'>fat free</a>.
Instructions:
- In a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>medium bowl</a>, combine together (blended) <a href='/misc/dairy#cottage-cheese'>cottage cheese</a>, <a href='/misc/dairy#whey'>protein powder</a>, and <a href='https://amzn.to/43MkDqr'>vanilla extract</a>.

Notes:
- You can also use an equal amount of <a href='/misc/dairy#yogurt'>plain Greek yogurt</a> instead of <a href='/misc/dairy#cottage-cheese'>cottage cheese</a>, though you'll need to add 1/2 tsp (3 g) salt

- To make this into a chocolate frosting, add 4 tbsp (20 g) <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>cocoa powder</a>
