Description: Dessert hummus is a great sweet way to enjoy <a href='/misc/beans#chickpeas'>chickpeas</a>. It works really well as a spread on <a href='/recipes/ww-bread'>100% Whole Wheat Bread</a>, as a dip for fruit, on a PB&J, or in your morning <a href='/yogurt'>yogurt</a> or <a href='/oatmeal'>oatmeal</a>. It's naturally sweetened with <a href='/misc/fruit#banana'>bananas</a>, get healthy <a href='/misc/fats'>fats</a> and <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> from the <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut</a> butter, and <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a> from the chickpeas.  It also works as a great filling for a <a href='/recipes/banana-cream-pie'>Banana No-Cream Pie</a>.  Or check out any of my other <a href='/salad-dressings'>hummus recipes</a>!
Instructions:
- The day before, break your bananas into 1" chunks, and add to an airtight bag.  Freeze overnight

- Drain and rinse your chickpeas.  Add all ingredients to a food processor, and blend until smooth.  Optionally add some sweetener, like 1/2 tsp (2.5 g) liquid monk fruit, 1 tbsp (12 g) granulated monk fruit, or 1 tbsp (21 g) honey
- <img src='/assets/Food/Sweet Spreads/BB Hummus/blend.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- If you don't have powdered peanut butter, you can add an additional 2 tbsp (32 g) of peanut butter or tahini

- Store in an air tight container in the fridge. Use it anywhere you'd use peanut butter, such as spreading on toast, or in your oatmeal.
