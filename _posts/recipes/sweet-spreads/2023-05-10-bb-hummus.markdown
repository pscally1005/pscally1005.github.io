Description: Dessert hummus is a great sweet way to enjoy <a href='/misc/beans#chickpeas'>chickpeas</a>. It works really well as a spread on <a href='/recipes/ww-bread'>100% Whole Wheat Bread</a>, as a dip for <a href='/misc/fruit'>fruit</a>, on a PB&J, or in your <a href='/yogurt'>morning yogurt</a> or <a href='/oatmeal'>oatmeal</a>. It's naturally sweetened with <a href='/misc/fruit#banana'>bananas</a>, get <a href='/misc/fats'>healthy fats</a> and <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> from the <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a>, and <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a> from the <a href='/misc/beans#chickpeas'>chickpeas</a>.  It also works as a great filling for a <a href='/recipes/banana-cream-pie'>Banana No-Cream Pie</a>.  Or check out any of my other <a href='/hummus'>hummus recipes</a>!
Instructions:
- The day before, break your <a href='/misc/fruit#banana'>bananas</a> into 1" chunks, and add to an airtight bag.  Freeze overnight

- Drain and rinse your <a href='/misc/beans#chickpeas'>chickpeas</a>.  Add all ingredients to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4q0AUjI'>food processor</a>, and blend until smooth
- <img src='/assets/Food/Sweet Spreads/BB Hummus/blend.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- If you don't have <a href='https://amzn.to/4sgDH9S'>powdered peanut butter</a>, you can add an additional 2 tbsp (32 g) of <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a> or <a href='/misc/seeds#sesame-seeds'>tahini</a>

- Optionally add some sweetener, like 1/2 tsp (2.5 g) <a href='https://amzn.to/3SqwsMO'>liquid monk fruit</a>, 1 tbsp (12 g) <a href='https://amzn.to/4ceFnvP'>granulated monk fruit</a>, or 1 tbsp (21 g) <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#honey'>honey</a>

- Store in an air tight container in the fridge. Use it anywhere you'd use <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a>, such as spreading on toast, or in your <a href='/oatmeal'>oatmeal</a>.
