Description: Ah the humble <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a>. Nice natural, smooth, pourable natural <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut</a> butter. None of that fake no-stir <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut</a> spread stuff, this is the real deal, and is possibly the easiest thing to ever make. As long as you have a good food processor, you'll end up with the best <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut</a> butter you've ever had. Just keep blending until it's super smooth. It might take some time, so don't give up too early! Instructions:
- You can use whatever kind of <a href='/misc/nuts'>nuts</a> you like for this recipe.  <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>Peanuts</a>, <a href='/misc/nuts#almonds'>almonds</a>, <a href='/misc/nuts#pistachios'>pistachios</a>, <a href='/misc/nuts#cashews'>cashews</a>, you name it.  For <a href='/misc/nuts'>nut</a> free alternatives, you can also use unsweetened shredded <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut</a> flakes or any <a href='/misc/seeds'>seeds</a>, such as <a href='/misc/seeds#sunflower-seeds'>sunflower seeds</a> or <a href='/misc/seeds#pumpkin-seeds'>pumpkin seeds</a>, or <a href='/misc/seeds#sesame-seeds'>sesame seeds</a>.  The concept is exactly the same

- If the <a href='/misc/nuts'>nuts</a> are raw, pour them onto a cookie sheet with aluminum foil. Roast for 10 minutes at 350F. If the nuts are already roasted, roast for 5 minutes instead
- <center><img src='/assets/Food/Sweet Spreads/PB/roasted-seeds.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'></center>

- Add just the <a href='/misc/nuts'>nuts</a> into a food processor, and process until smooth (on high). Stop every minute to scrape down the sides
- <center><img src='/assets/Food/Sweet Spreads/PB/pb-2.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'></center>

- Only when it is smooth, season with salt to taste.  You can also add any other flavorings, such as cinnamon, vanilla, or almond extract

- Blend some more to mix it up, make sure it is very very smooth. It should be runny, almost as if it was melted. Store in a mason jar at room temperature
