Description: Did you know that <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#nutella'>Nutella</a> contains only about 13% <a href='/misc/nuts#hazelnuts'>hazelnuts</a>? More than half of <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#nutella'>Nutella</a> is pure <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar</a>, and over a third of it is <a href='/misc/fats'>hydrogenated oils</a>; that's gross. This recipe is a much cleaner <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#nutella'>Nutella</a>, instead being predominantly <a href='/misc/nuts#hazelnuts'>hazelnuts</a>, and sweetened with fully natural overripe <a href='/misc/fruit#banana'>bananas</a>. <a href='https://amzn.to/4rhYDwh'>Carob powder</a> makes it slightly sweeter as well, but feel free to use <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>cocoa powder</a> instead.  Or check out any of my other <a href='/nut-butter'>nut butter recipes</a>!
Instructions:
- Preheat your <a href='https://amzn.to/3FuWETp'>air fryer</a> to 300F. Add raw <a href='/misc/nuts#hazelnuts'>hazelnuts</a> to the basket, and roast for 15 minutes.  If your <a href='/misc/nuts#hazelnuts'>hazelnuts</a> are already roasted, just 5 minutes will do

- Add the <a href='/misc/nuts'>nuts</a> to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4q0AUjI'>food processor</a>, and blend until a super smooth <a href='/misc/nuts#hazelnuts'>hazelnut butter</a> forms, scraping down the sides every minute or so. This can take about 5 minutes; don't stop too early
- <img src='/assets/Food/Sweet Spreads/Nutella/nut-butter.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Only once your <a href='/misc/nuts#hazelnuts'>hazelnut butter</a> is fully made, blend in the rest of the ingredients - <a href='/misc/fruit#banana'>bananas</a>, <a href='https://amzn.to/4rhYDwh'>carob</a> (or <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>cocoa</a>), vanilla, and salt. Blend until smooth and thin out with <a href='/misc/fruit#apple'>applesauce</a> until you have a spreadable texture. Add any sweetener to taste (if desired). Transfer across 2 <a href='https://amzn.to/4vamaBm'>small mason jars</a>, and store in the fridge
- <img src='/assets/Food/Sweet Spreads/Nutella/processor.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Here, I used the <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#nutella'>nutella</a> as a filling in a <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#nutella'>nutella</a> pie, where I used the crust from my <a href='/recipes/peanut-butter-pie'>Peanut Butter Banana Pie</a> recipe, and topped it with <a href='/misc/nuts#pistachios'>pistachios</a> and melted <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Sweet Spreads/Nutella/pie.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
