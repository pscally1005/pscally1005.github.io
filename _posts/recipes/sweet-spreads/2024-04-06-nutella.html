More than half of Nutella is pure sugar, and over a third of it is hydrogenated oils; that's gross. This recipe is a much cleaner Nutella, instead being predominantly hazelnuts, and sweetened with fully natural overripe bananas. Carob powder makes it slightly sweeter as well, but feel free to use cocoa powder instead. Or check out any of my other nut butter recipes! Instructions: - Preheat your air fryer to 300F. Add raw hazelnuts to the basket, and roast for 15 minutes. If your hazelnuts are already roasted, just 5 minutes will do - Add the nuts to a food processor, and blend until a super smooth hazelnut butter forms, scraping down the sides every minute or so. This can take about 5 minutes; don't stop too early -
- Only once your hazelnut butter is fully made, blend in the rest of the ingredients - bananas, carob (or cocoa), vanilla, and salt. Blend until smooth and thin out with applesauce until you have a spreadable texture. Add any sweetener to taste (if desired). Transfer across 2 small mason jars, and store in the fridge -
- Here, I used the nutella as a filling in a nutella pie, where I used the crust from my Peanut Butter Banana Pie recipe, and topped it with pistachios and melted peanut butter -
