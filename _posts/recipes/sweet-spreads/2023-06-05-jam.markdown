Description: As you probably know by now, I love a good PBJ with a low <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar</a> <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#jelly'>jam</a>. This simple berry jam makes use of extra fresh berries that you probably picked up at a farmers market. The pectin in the <a href='/misc/fruit#apple'>apples</a> helps thicken it and make it slightly sweeter, and the little maple syrup really rounds out the flavors.  For a <a href='/misc/seeds#chia-seeds'>chia</a> seed version, be sure to make my <a href='/recipes/strawberry-chia-jam'>Strawberry Chia Jam</a>.  Or pair it with <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>Homemade Natural Nut Butter</a> and <a href='/recipes/ww-bread'>100% Whole Wheat Bread</a> for the classic PB&J combination
Instructions:
- To a 10” pan, add the berries and maple syrup. Mash with a potato masher

- Over medium heat, add in applesauce, and bring to light simmer. Cook for an additional 2-3 minutes

- In a small bowl, mix together oat flour and water to create a slurry, and slowly add to the jam while stirring to avoid clumps. Cook for another 2-3 minutes

- Squeeze in some lemon juice at the end to help preserve it

- Remove from heat, transfer to a container, and let cool at room temperature for half an hour before moving to the fridge
