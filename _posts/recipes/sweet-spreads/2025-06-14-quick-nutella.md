Description: If you want just one serving of a delicious chocolate spread that has no <a href="/misc/hidden-sugar">added sugar</a>, then look no further. Though you could absolutely scale it up and store a larger batch in the fridge. Powdered peanut butter as a base provides peanut flavor and <a href="/misc/protein">protein</a> for minimal <a href="/misc/calories">calories</a>, while carob powder gives it that rich and slightly sweet chocolate taste. This easy Nutella substitute is perfect on toast, in crepes, or as a dip for fruit.  Or check out any of my other <a href="/nut-butter">nut butter recipes</a>!
Instructions: 
- Mix all ingredients together in a small bowl or glass. Use as any other chocolate spread or peanut butter
