Description: A while ago, my brother got me 3 flavored <a href='/nut-butter'>nut butters</a> for my birthday. I've been wanting to recreate them and gift them back to him, so today is finally the day. First up is Chocolate Almond Butter, but be sure to check out the other 2 - <a href='/recipes/maple-cinnamon-peanut-butter'>Maple Cinnamon Peanut Butter</a> and <a href='/recipes/white-chocolate-walnut-butter'>White Chocolate Walnut Butter</a>.  Or check out any of my other <a href='/nut-butter'>nut butter recipes</a>!
Instructions: 
- Add your <a href='/misc/nuts'>nuts</a> to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4q0AUjI'>food processor</a>, and blend until super smooth, scraping down the sides every few minutes. This may take 5-10 minutes depending on your <a href='https://amzn.to/4q0AUjI'>food processor</a>. If it doesn't look and pour like a liquid, keep blending, you're not done yet. Have patience, and wear ear plugs!
- <img src='/assets/Food/Sweet Spreads/Flavored Nut Butters/Almond/nut-butter.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Only once you have the creamiest and smoothest <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>nut butter</a> you've ever seen, add in your salt, and blend for 30 seconds to fully combine

- It's time to flavor your <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>nut butter</a>. Add in the flavoring ingredients, and blend until smooth and fully combined
- <img src='/assets/Food/Sweet Spreads/Flavored Nut Butters/Almond/flavored.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Transfer to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4vamaBm'>mason jar</a> or air tight container, and store at room temperature for up to a week. You can also freeze <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>homemade nut butter</a> for later
Notes:
- If your <a href='/misc/nuts'>nuts</a> are raw, <a href='https://amzn.to/3FuWETp'>air fry</a> them at 300F for 10 minutes, or roast in the oven at 350F for 12 minutes
- <img src='/assets/Food/Sweet Spreads/Flavored Nut Butters/Almond/roasted.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- If you prefer a chunky <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>nut butter</a>, reserve about 1 oz (28 g) of <a href='/misc/nuts'>nuts</a>, and blend in at the end
