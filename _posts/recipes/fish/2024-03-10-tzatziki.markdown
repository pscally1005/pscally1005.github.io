Description: <a href='/misc/fruit#lemon'>Lemon</a> and <a href='/misc/fish#salmon'>salmon</a> go great together, one of my favorite combinations. I've also made a <a href='/recipes/sides'>side</a> of tzatziki sauce here, as well as some roasted <a href='/misc/veggies#brussel-sprout'>brussel sprouts</a>. This is a great light and healthy summery meal with plenty of Greek flavors
Instructions:
- Starting with the Tzatziki. Finely dice your <a href='/misc/veggies#cucumber'>cucumber</a>, and add to a strainer with salt. Let sit for about an hour to draw out excess water

- To a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>medium bowl</a>, combine the <a href='/misc/veggies#cucumber'>cucumber</a>, <a href='/misc/dairy#yogurt'>yogurt</a>, <a href='/misc/fruit#lemon-juice'>lemon juice</a>, <a href='/misc/veggies#garlic'>minced garlic</a>, lemon pepper, garlic powder, and onion powder. Cover and refrigerate until ready to use
- <img src='/assets/Food/Fish/Tzatziki/tzatziki-2.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Moving on to the <a href='/misc/veggies#brussel-sprout'>brussel sprouts</a>. Preheat your oven to 400F, and prepare a large <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>cookie sheet</a> with parchment or a <a href='https://amzn.to/44T3n3X'>silicone liner</a>

- Cut your sprouts in half, and add to the tray. Season with oil, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and black pepper, and toss together with your hands

- Roast the <a href='/misc/veggies#brussel-sprout'>brussel sprouts</a> at 400F for about 30 minutes, or until lightly charred on the outside and tender in the center. Squeeze on some <a href='/misc/fruit#lemon'>lemon</a> or <a href='/misc/fruit#lime-juice'>lime juice</a> when done roasting

- Finally, the <a href='/misc/fish#salmon'>salmon</a>. In a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>small bowl</a>, mix together <a href='/misc/fruit#lemon-juice'>lemon juice</a>, lemon pepper, garlic powder, and onion powder. Rub onto each piece of <a href='/misc/fish#salmon'>salmon</a>

- Roast the <a href='/misc/fish#salmon'>salmon</a> for 10-14 minutes at 400F, or until done to your liking. I like my <a href='/misc/fish#salmon'>salmon</a> fully cooked, so I prefer the higher end of that range and an <a href='https://amzn.to/4kmobG2'>internal temperature</a> of about <a href='https://amzn.to/4kmobG2'>145F</a>, but you might like less
