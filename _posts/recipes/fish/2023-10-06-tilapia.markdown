Description: <a href='/misc/fish#tilapia'>Tilapia</a> fillets are a staple in my freezer, as they're super easy to put together for a quick dinner during the week. They're made even faster by cooking them straight out of the freezer in the <a href='https://amzn.to/3FuWETp'>air fryer</a>. These will be from freezer to plate in just 10 minutes, and put whatever spices you want on here!
Instructions:
- Preheat your <a href='https://amzn.to/3FuWETp'>air fryer</a> to 400F

- <a href='https://amzn.to/3Hdg0gk'>Spray</a> the bottom of the basket with oil, and place in the <a href='/misc/fish'>fish</a>. <a href='https://amzn.to/3Hdg0gk'>Spray</a> the top with <a href='/misc/fish'>fish</a>, and season the top

- <a href='https://amzn.to/3FuWETp'>Air fry</a> for 6-7 minutes, flipping halfway. Season the other half when flipping. Squeeze some <a href='/misc/fruit#lemon-juice'>lemon juice</a> on after
Notes:
- You can also bake the <a href='/misc/fish#tilapia'>tilapia</a> in the oven.  Bake at 400F for about 14 minutes, or until the thickest part reaches <a href='https://amzn.to/4kmobG2'>145F</a>. Squeeze on some <a href='/misc/fruit#lemon-juice'>lemon juice</a>

- If your <a href='/misc/fish#tilapia'>tilapia</a> is defrosted, you can <a href='https://amzn.to/3FuWETp'>air fry</a> at 400F for 4-6 minutes, or bake in a 400F oven for 6-8 minutes
