Description: Manhattan <a href='/misc/fish#clam'>Clam</a> Chowder is a classic <a href='/misc/veggies#tomato'>tomato</a> based broth <a href='/soups-and-stews'>soup</a>. This is my <a href='/misc/fats'>leaner</a> take on the dish, with more <a href='/misc/veggies'>vegetables</a>, less <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a>, and less <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a>. I've cut out the <a href='/misc/dairy#butter'>butter</a> and <a href='/misc/meat#bacon'>bacon</a> to lower the <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a>, added extra <a href='/misc/veggies'>vegetables</a> for more <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a> and <a href='/misc/nutrient-alphabet'>vitamins</a>, and removed the flour to make it <a href='/misc/celiac'>gluten free</a>. If you instead prefer creamy <a href='/soups-and-stews'>soups</a>, you should check out my similar <a href='/recipes/new-england-clam-chowder'>(I'm Banned From) New England Clam Chowder</a>
Instructions:
- Preheat a large pot over medium heat with olive oil. Cut your <a href='/misc/veggies'>vegetables</a> into a small dice, and add to the pot with salt. Cook until the <a href='/misc/veggies#pepper'>peppers</a> and <a href='/misc/veggies#carrots'>carrots</a> are soft, and the <a href='/misc/veggies#onion'>onions</a> are translucent, about 20 minutes. Add in the <a href='/misc/veggies#garlic'>garlic</a>, and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds

- Meanwhile, wash your <a href='/misc/veggies#potato'>potatoes</a>, and cut into a medium dice, leaving the skin on

- To the pot, add your <a href='/misc/veggies#potato'>potatoes</a>, garlic powder, onion powder, thyme, oregano, black pepper, clam juice, canned <a href='/misc/fish#clam'>clams</a> (entire can, not drained), diced tomatoes, and tomato paste. Bring to a boil over high heat, reduce heat to medium, cover, and let simmer for 15-20 minutes, or until the <a href='/misc/veggies#potato'>potatoes</a> are fork tender

- Remove from heat, finish with <a href='/misc/fruit#lemon-juice'>lemon juice</a>, and serve
Notes:
- Any <a href='/misc/veggies#potato'>potatoes</a> will work here like Yukon gold, red, or even <a href='/misc/veggies#sweet-potato'>sweet potatoes</a>
