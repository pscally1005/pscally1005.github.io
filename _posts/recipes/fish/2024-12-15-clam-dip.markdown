Description: This simple clam dip is lower in fat and carbs than most, while being a good source of protein and vitamin B12. It goes well on a sandwich, with chips, on a tortilla, mixed with pasta, or on top of a salad. It can be served both warm and cold, and takes only a few minutes to prepare
Instructions: 
- Cut your onion into a fine dice, and add to a medium pot with olive oil over medium heat. Cook until translucent

- Add the clams (with the juice) to the pot, and cover with a lid.  Bring to a boil over medium heat, and let boil for about 5 minutes. Remove from the heat

- Mix in the rest of the ingredients, and transfer to an 8" square pan.  Bake at 350F until bubbly, about 20 minutes. Serve warm or cold
