Description: This "recipe" (if you can even call it that) was taken from the cookbook Genius Kitchen, and consists of just a sliced <a href='/misc/fruit#avocado'>avocado</a> and a drained can of <a href='/misc/fish#sardine'>sardines</a>. This brain bowl is a great small meal or large snack that's high in <a href='/misc/nutrient-alphabet#O'>omega-3s</a>, <a href='/misc/fats'>monounsaturated fat</a>, <a href='/misc/phytochemicals'>lutein, zeaxanthin</a>, <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a>, and <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a> making it nourishing for both your stomach and your brain.
Instructions:
- Cut your <a href='/misc/fruit#avocado'>avocado</a> into slices, and add to the base of a bowl. Roughly drain your <a href='/misc/fish#sardine'>sardines</a>, and add on top of the <a href='/misc/fruit#avocado'>avocado</a>. Squeeze on some <a href='/misc/fruit#lemon'>lemon</a>/<a href='/misc/fruit#lime-juice'>lime juice</a>, and top with everything bagel seasoning
