Description: <a href='/misc/meat#liver'>Beef liver</a> is one of the most nutritious foods you can eat. Obviously it's high in <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a>, but <a href='/misc/meat#liver'>liver</a> is also a very rich source of <a href='/misc/nutrient-alphabet#A'>vitamins A</a>, <a href='/misc/nutrient-alphabet#R'>B2</a>, <a href='/misc/nutrient-alphabet#P'>B5</a>, <a href='/misc/vitamin-b12'>B12</a>, as well as <a href='/misc/nutrient-alphabet#F'>folate</a>, <a href='/misc/choline'>choline</a>, <a href='/misc/iron'>iron</a>, <a href='/misc/nutrient-alphabet#C'>copper</a>, <a href='/misc/nutrient-alphabet#S'>selenium</a>, and <a href='/misc/nutrient-alphabet#Z'>zinc</a>. The problem is that most people dislike the taste of <a href='/misc/meat#liver'>liver</a>; so let's fix that. I'll be going with a simple formula of mixing in a <a href='/misc/carbs'>carb</a> (<a href='/misc/veggies#sweet-potato'>sweet potatoes</a>) and <a href='/misc/veggies'>vegetable</a> (<a href='/misc/veggies#broccoli'>broccoli</a>) to finely chopped <a href='/misc/meat#liver'>liver</a>, and covering it in a sauce (<a href='/recipes/creamy-pesto'>Creamy Pesto Dip</a>). The result is a delicious and super healthy bowl, and no you can't taste the liver (or the <a href='/misc/dairy#cottage-cheese'>cottage cheese</a>)
Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 400F, and line 2 sheets with parchment paper

- Wash your <a href='/misc/veggies#potato'>potatoes</a>, and cut into a dice, leaving the skin on. Add to one pan, and mix with <a href='/misc/fruit#lemon'>lemon</a>, salt, and black pepper

- To the other pan, add your <a href='/misc/veggies#broccoli'>broccoli</a> with <a href='/misc/fruit#lemon'>lemon</a>, salt, and black pepper

- Roast both the <a href='/misc/veggies#sweet-potato'>sweet potatoes</a> and <a href='/misc/veggies#broccoli'>broccoli</a> for about 30 minutes, or until soft and browned

- Meanwhile, preheat a large <a href='https://amzn.to/465aAxx'>cast iron pan</a> with olive oil over medium high heat. Cut your liver into as small pieces as you can, and add to the pan with salt and black pepper. Cook until no longer pink (><a href='https://amzn.to/4kmobG2'>160F</a>). Set aside
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meme/Liver/4-parts.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Combine together the <a href='/misc/veggies#sweet-potato'>sweet potatoes</a>, <a href='/misc/veggies#broccoli'>broccoli</a>, <a href='/misc/meat#liver'>liver</a>, and sauce in a large <a href='https://amzn.to/4rb3CiD'>metal bowl</a>. Divide into 4 servings, and enjoy
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meme/Liver/combined.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
