Description: |
  Happy St. Patrick's Day! Would you believe me if I told you this bread was made with no food dye? Of course you wouldn't, because this looks too ridiculous to be natural. As I accidentally found out while making <a href='/recipes/banana-bread'>Oatmeal Banana Mini Muffins</a> with <a href='/misc/seeds#sunflower-seeds'>sunflower</a> butter in place of <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut</a> butter, the chlorophyll in the sunflower <a href='/misc/seeds'>seeds</a> reacts with the baking soda, turning the baked good green
  <br><br>
  I've taken that to the extreme here with this Green Bread, which is free of artificial dyes, added oils, <a href='/misc/celiac'>gluten</a>, <a href='/misc/nuts'>nuts</a>, and <a href='/misc/grains'>grains</a>, and is mildly sweet from the <a href='/misc/fruit#banana'>bananas</a> and some <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#honey'>honey</a>. This bread makes a great higher <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a>, lower carb breakfast, and goes well topped with some <a href='/recipes/strawberry-chia-jam'>Strawberry Chia Jam</a> for some color contrast
Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 350F, and line a <a href='https://amzn.to/3YUjIkN'>9x5" loaf pan</a> with parchment paper

- If using raw <a href='/misc/seeds'>seeds</a>, <a href='https://amzn.to/3FuWETp'>air fry</a> for about 15 minutes at 300F, until lightly browned.  If they're already roasted, just <a href='https://amzn.to/3FuWETp'>air fry</a> for about 5 minutes

- In a <a href='https://amzn.to/4q0AUjI'>food processor</a>, blend together your <a href='/misc/seeds#sunflower-seeds'>sunflower seeds</a> on high until a smooth butter forms, about 5-10 minutes. You could also use 1 cup (256 g) of <a href='/misc/seeds#sunflower-seeds'>sunflower butter</a> instead.  Add 1/2 tsp (3 g) salt if your <a href='/misc/seeds#sunflower-seeds'>sunflower seeds</a> are unsalted
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meme/Green/seed-butter.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Only once your super smooth <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>seed butter</a> has formed, add in the rest of the ingredients.  Blend until smooth; the batter should be very liquidy.  Transfer your batter to the pan
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meme/Green/processor.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meme/Green/raw.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Bake at 350F for about 45 minutes. A toothpick should come out clean, and the bread should have an <a href='https://amzn.to/4kmobG2'>internal temperature</a> around <a href='https://amzn.to/4kmobG2'>205F</a>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meme/Green/baked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Let cool for at least 1 hour before slicing.  Don't worry if the inside looks more tan than green; the color will turn more the next day.  Store leftovers in the fridge or freezer
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meme/Green/slice.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
