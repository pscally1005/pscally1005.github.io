Description: This <a href='/misc/veggies#butternut-squash'>butternut squash</a> risOATto doesn't use <a href='/misc/grains#white-rice'>arborio rice</a>.  Instead, I'm using <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>rolled oats</a> as my base.  <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>Oats</a> get just as creamy for a perfect risotto, while also being a <a href='/misc/grains'>whole grain</a> and containing 5x the <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a> and 2x the <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> of <a href='/misc/grains#white-rice'>white rice</a>.  Plus, I always have <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oats</a> on hand; I don't need to buy a specific type of <a href='/misc/grains#brown-rice'>rice</a> just for 1 dish.  The <a href='/misc/veggies#butternut-squash'>butternut squash</a> provides some mild sweetness while also making the risotto creamy without the need for <a href='/misc/dairy#butter'>butter</a>.  The combination of toasting the <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oats</a> and the <a href='/misc/dairy#grated-cheese'>grated cheese</a> gives this dish a classic but elevated feel that you're sure to love.  It's vegetarian and <a href='/misc/celiac'>gluten free</a>, and can be made vegan by swapping the <a href='/misc/dairy#grated-cheese'>grated cheese</a> for nutritional yeast and chicken bouillon for <a href='/misc/veggies'>veggie</a> bouillon.
Instructions:
- Peel and dice the <a href='/misc/veggies#butternut-squash'>butternut squash</a>, and add to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>large bowl</a>.  Season with <a href='/misc/olive-oil'>olive oil</a>, garlic powder, onion powder, black pepper, and salt
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meme/RisOATto/butternut-cut.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meme/RisOATto/butternut-bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Transfer to a parchment lined <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>baking sheet</a>, and roast for 45 minutes at 350F
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meme/RisOATto/butternut-raw.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meme/RisOATto/butternut-cooked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Wait about half an hour before starting the risOATto, since you want the <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oats</a> to be done around the same time as the <a href='/misc/veggies#butternut-squash'>butternut squash</a> is done roasting

- Heat <a href='/misc/olive-oil'>EVOO</a> in a <a href='https://amzn.to/3LNGdVy'>Dutch oven</a> over medium heat.  Add <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oats</a> and bouillon powder, and toast the <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oats</a> until fragrant, about 5 minutes

- Add in the water and simmer uncovered with occasional stirring until creamy (about 10 minutes)
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meme/RisOATto/oats.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Add all the <a href='/misc/veggies#butternut-squash'>butternutsquash</a> into the pot, and vigorously stir until all the <a href='/misc/veggies#butternut-squash'>butternut squash</a> has melted in.  Stir in the <a href='/misc/dairy#grated-cheese'>grated cheese</a>, and serve
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meme/RisOATto/risotto.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meme/RisOATto/containers.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
