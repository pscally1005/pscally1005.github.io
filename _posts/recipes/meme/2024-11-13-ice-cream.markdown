Description: This 3 ingredient ice cream (okay 4 if you count salt) is dead simple, <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar</a> free, and tastes exactly like classic vanilla ice cream. Even Vic agreed that it was spot on to regular vanilla ice cream, and this contains a fraction of the <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar</a>, <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a>, and <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a>, all while having more <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a>, <a href='/misc/calcium'>calcium</a>, and <a href='/misc/vitamin-b12'>vitamin B12</a>.  The ice cream can be scooped right out of the freezer; there's no need to let it sit out and soften! Here are some flavor ideas you can mess around with:<br><ul>
    <li><b>Vanilla</b> - 1 tsp (5 g) vanilla extract</li>
    <li><b>Chocolate</b> - 2 tbsp (10 g) cocoa powder</li>
    <li><b>Mint</b> - 1/4 tsp (1.25 g) mint extract</li>
    <li><b>Cookies & Cream</b> - 2 crushed oreos</li>
    <li><b>Brown Sugar Cinnamon</b> - 1 tsp (3 g) cinnamon, and 1 crushed graham cracker sheet</li>
    <li><b>Berry</b> - 1/2 cup (70 g) frozen (and thawed) berries (strawberries, blueberries, etc.)</li>
    <li><b>Peanut Butter</b> - 1 tbsp (16 g) <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>natural peanut butter</a></li>
    <li><b>Cookie Crumble</b> - 2 chocolate chip cookies</li>
    <li><b>Chocolate Chip</b> - 1 tbsp (15 g) mini chocolate chips</li></ul>
Instructions:
- Place a medium glass, ceramic, or metal bowl (anything but plastic) in the freezer overnight. Refrigerate your can of evaporated milk overnight

- In your bowl, combine together evaporated milk, vanilla (pure is better, not imitation), liquid monk fruit, and a tiny pinch of salt. Beat with a hand mixer until thick and fluffy
-  hour.  Churn with your electric mixer, and return to the freezer for another hour. Repeat

- Transfer the ice cream to an airtight container, and pack down. Freeze for at least 1 more hour before serving. This can be eaten right away, or stored in the freezer indefinitely
