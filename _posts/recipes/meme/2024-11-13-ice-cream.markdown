Description: This 3 ingredient ice cream (okay 4 if you count salt) is dead simple, <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar free</a>, and tastes exactly like classic vanilla ice cream. Even Vic agreed that it was spot on to regular vanilla ice cream, and this contains a fraction of the <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar</a>, <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a>, and <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a>, all while having more <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a>, <a href='/misc/calcium'>calcium</a>, and <a href='/misc/vitamin-b12'>vitamin B12</a>.  The ice cream can be scooped right out of the freezer; there's no need to let it sit out and soften!
Instructions:
- Place a medium glass, ceramic, or <a href='https://amzn.to/4rb3CiD'>metal bowl</a> (anything but plastic) in the freezer overnight. Refrigerate your can of evaporated milk overnight

- In your bowl, combine together evaporated milk, vanilla (pure is better, not imitation), <a href='https://amzn.to/3SqwsMO'>liquid monk fruit</a>, and a tiny pinch of salt. Beat with a <a href='https://amzn.to/45yqsbM'>hand mixer</a> until thick and fluffy
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meme/Ice Cream/ice-cream-whipped.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

-  Freeze for 1 hour.  Churn with your electric mixer, and return to the freezer for another hour. Repeat

- Transfer the ice cream to an airtight container, and pack down. Freeze for at least 1 more hour before serving. This can be eaten right away, or stored in the freezer indefinitely
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meme/Ice Cream/ice-cream-packed.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- <b>Vanilla</b> - 1 tsp (5 g) vanilla extract

- <b>Chocolate</b> - 2 tbsp (10 g) <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>cocoa powder</a>

- <b>Mint</b> - 1/4 tsp (1.25 g) mint extract

- <b>Cookies & Cream</b> - 2 crushed oreos

- <b><a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>Brown Sugar</a> Cinnamon</b> - 1 tsp (3 g) cinnamon, and 1 crushed <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#crackers'>graham cracker</a> sheet

- <b><a href='/misc/fruit#blueberries'>Berry</a></b> - 1/2 cup (70 g) frozen (and thawed) <a href='/misc/fruit#blueberries'>berries</a> (<a href='/misc/fruit#strawberries'>strawberries</a>, <a href='/misc/fruit#blueberries'>blueberries</a>, etc.)

- <b><a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>Peanut Butter</a></b> - 1 tbsp (16 g) <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>natural peanut butter</a>

- <b><a href='/cookies'>Cookie</a> Crumble</b> - 2 chocolate chip cookies
