Description: This healthy green <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>eggs</a> and <a href='/misc/meat#ham'>ham</a> gets its green from <a href='/misc/veggies#spinach-fresh'>spinach</a> instead of artificial food colorings, and the <a href='/misc/meat#ham'>ham</a> from a <a href='/misc/fats'>lean</a> <a href='/misc/meat#ham'>ham steak</a> instead of a <a href='/misc/fats'>fatty</a> cut. The addition of <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>eggs</a> and <a href='/misc/beans#chickpeas'>chickpeas</a> makes this a very <a href='/misc/high-protein'>high protein</a> <a href='/recipes/breakfast'>breakfast</a>, with 55 g of <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> in under 550 <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a>. Moreover, the <a href='/misc/beans'>beans</a> and <a href='/misc/veggies#spinach-fresh'>spinach</a> provide <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a>, <a href='/misc/nutrient-alphabet#F'>folate</a>, and <a href='/misc/iron'>iron</a>, while the <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>eggs</a> add <a href='/misc/fats'>healthy fats</a>, <a href='/misc/vitamin-b12'>vitamin B12</a>, and <a href='/misc/nutrient-alphabet#D'>vitamin D</a>.  It's also kinda salty from the <a href='/misc/meat#ham'>ham</a>, lemon pepper, and <a href='/misc/beans#chickpeas'>chickpeas</a>; you can take that as a bug or a feature.
Instructions:
- Heat a <a href='https://amzn.to/4rdR0HI'>large pan</a> over medium, trim any <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a> off your <a href='/misc/meat#ham'>ham</a>, and cut into small cubes. When hot, add in your <a href='/misc/meat#ham'>ham</a>, and cook (uncovered) until the water cooks off and the <a href='/misc/meat#ham'>ham</a> begins to brown, about 10 minutes. Set the <a href='/misc/meat#ham'>ham</a> aside
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meme/Green Eggs and Ham/ham-cooked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Deglaze with some <a href='/misc/fruit#lemon-juice'>lemon juice</a>, and add in the <a href='/misc/veggies#spinach-fresh'>spinach</a>. Cover and cook until the water has cooked off, and the <a href='/misc/veggies#spinach-fresh'>spinach</a> begins to brown, about 10 minutes. Mix in the spices

- Crack in your <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>eggs</a>, and beat with a <a href='https://amzn.to/3Fw6MeC'>wooden spoon</a>. Lower the heat to medium low, and cook under frequent stirring until the <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>eggs</a> are just barely cooked, about 5 minutes

- Drain and rinse a can of <a href='/misc/beans#chickpeas'>chickpeas</a>. Add in the <a href='/misc/beans#chickpeas'>chickpeas</a>, as well as the <a href='/misc/meat#ham'>ham</a>. Combine together, and divide into 4 servings
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meme/Green Eggs and Ham/full-pan.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
