Description: Traditionally, <a href='/recipes/chili'>chili</a> has a little bit of <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>chocolate</a> or <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>cocoa powder</a> in it. A <i>little</i> bit. But what if it had <i>quite</i> a bit more than a little bit?  Introducing "Chocolate Chili", with a whopping cup of <a href='https://amzn.to/4rhYDwh'>carob powder</a>. It's incidentally <a href='/recipes/meatless'>vegetarian</a>. Have you seen <a href='/misc/meat'>meat</a> prices lately?  I'm not wasting my hard earned money on this potentially stupid idea.
Instructions:
- Cut your <a href='/misc/veggies#onion'>onions</a> and <a href='/misc/veggies#pepper'>peppers</a> into a medium dice

- Add the <a href='/misc/beans'>beans</a>, <a href='/misc/veggies'>veggies</a>, <a href='/misc/veggies#garlic'>minced garlic</a>, canned <a href='/misc/veggies#tomato'>tomatoes</a>, spices (chili powder, cumin, & salt), and <a href='https://amzn.to/4rhYDwh'>carob powder</a> to a <a href='https://amzn.to/49TUS9E'>large slow cooker</a>

- Cover and cook on low for 12 hours

Notes:
- |
  I will be using a full batch of my <a href='/recipes/slow-cooked-beans'>Slow Cooker Dried Beans</a> here, but you can alternatively use five 15.5 oz cans of any <a href='/misc/beans'>bean</a> you like (drained and rinsed).<br><br>

  For the <a href='/misc/beans'>dried beans</a>, soak 1 lb (454 g) the <a href='/misc/beans'>beans</a> overnight in 5 cups (1,200 g) water and 1 tbsp (18 g) salt. Discard the soaking liquid. Add the <a href='/misc/beans'>beans</a> to a <a href='https://amzn.to/49TUS9E'>large slow cooker</a> with 3 cups (720 g) water and 1 tsp (6 g) salt. Cover and cook on low for 8 hours.<br><br>

  This yields ~1,269 g of cooked <a href='/misc/beans'>beans</a>, or a little under 3 pounds

- Any <a href='/misc/beans'>beans</a> will work.  Today, I'm using <a href='/misc/beans#black-beans'>black beans</a>, but <a href='/misc/beans#kidney-beans'>kidney</a>, <a href='/misc/beans#pinto-beans'>pinto</a>, <a href='/misc/beans#navy-beans'>navy</a>, <a href='/misc/beans#chickpeas'>chickpeas</a>, or anything else will work in place

- |
  I normally precook the <a href='/misc/veggies'>veggies</a> and <a href='/misc/meat'>meat</a> before adding them to my <a href='https://amzn.to/49TUS9E'>slow cooker</a> and cooking for 8 hours on low when I made <a href='/recipes/chili'>chili</a>.<br><br>

  However, since I'm making this <a href='/recipes/meatless'>vegetarian</a>, I decided to skip precooking the <a href='/misc/veggies'>veggies</a>.  Instead, I dumped the <a href='/misc/veggies'>vegetables</a> in raw, and cooked for 12 hours on low

- |
  Here's a simple schedule if you plan to <a href='/recipes/slow-cooked-beans'>make the beans from scratch</a> for a 7 pm dinner on a Sunday night
  <ol>
    <li><b>Saturday 2 pm</b>: Soak your 1 lb (454 g) <a href='/misc/beans'>dried beans</a> in 5 cups (1,200 g) water + 1 tbsp (18 g) salt.  Cover and refrigerate for 8 hours.  Cut up the <a href='/misc/veggies#pepper'>peppers</a> and <a href='/misc/veggies#onion'>onions</a>, and store in the fridge so they're prepped for tomorrow morning.</li>

    <li><b>Saturday 10 pm</b>: Drain the soaking liquid.  Add the <a href='/misc/beans'>beans</a> to your <a href='https://amzn.to/49TUS9E'>slow cooker</a> with 3 cups (720 g) water + 1 tsp (6 g) salt.  Cover and cook on low overnight.</li>

    <li><b>Sunday 7 am</b>: Add the chopped <a href='/misc/veggies'>veggies</a> to the <a href='https://amzn.to/49TUS9E'>slow cooker</a> with the <a href='/misc/beans'>beans</a>, along with the rest of the ingredients (<a href='/misc/veggies#garlic'>minced garlic</a>, canned <a href='/misc/veggies#tomato'>tomatoes</a>, spices, and <a href='https://amzn.to/4rhYDwh'>carob powder</a>).  Cover and cook on low for 12 hours</li>

    <li><b>Sunday 7 pm</b>: Eat!</li>
  </ol>

- |
  I feel like people frequently underestimate how much <a href='/misc/caffeine'>caffeine</a> <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>cocoa powder</a> contains.  If I had made this recipe with an equal amount of <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>cocoa</a> instead of <a href='https://amzn.to/4rhYDwh'>carob</a>, each serving would contain about 30 mg of <a href='/misc/caffeine'>caffeine</a>, or the equivalent of 1/3 of a cup of <a href='/misc/caffeine'>coffee</a>.  That's a fairly significant amount of <a href='/misc/caffeine'>caffeine</a> if you're having this for dinner 3 hours before bed.  In comparison, <a href='https://amzn.to/4rhYDwh'>carob powder</a> is <a href='/misc/caffeine'>caffeine</a> free, so you won't have to worry about this interrupting your precious <a href='/misc/sleep'>sleep</a>
