Description: |
  I had the brilliant idea the other day - a <a href='/misc/high-protein'>high protein</a> <a href='/recipes/meatless'>vegetarian</a> lasagna using my <a href='/recipes/cottage-cheese-flatbread'>Cottage Cheese Flatbread</a> as the lasagna noodles. That in combination with the <a href='/misc/dairy#mozzarella'>cheese</a> mix makes this a <a href='/misc/high-protein'>high protein</a> <a href='/recipes/meatless'>vegetarian meal</a> or <a href='/recipes/sides'>side dish</a> that's easy to prepare and feeds four.<br><br>

  Compared to a standard lasagna, this version is significantly lower in <a href='/misc/carbs'>carbs</a> and <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a>, while packing <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> from the <a href='/misc/dairy#cottage-cheese'>cottage cheese</a> that I guarantee you won't taste.  For other healthier Italian dishes, check out my <a href='/recipes/eggplant-parm'>Hassle Free Eggplant Parm</a> and <a href='/recipes/eggplant-rollatini'>Leaner Eggplant Rollatini</a>.
Instructions:
- |
    <b>Noodles</b><br>
    Below is a quick rundown on how to make my <a href='/recipes/cottage-cheese-flatbread'>Cottage Cheese Flatbread</a>.<br><br>

    Add all noodle ingredients (<a href='/misc/dairy#cottage-cheese'>cottage cheese</a>, <a href='/misc/meat#egg-whites'>egg whites</a>, garlic powder, oregano, and <a href='/misc/psyllium-husk'>psyllium husk</a>) to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4q0AUjI'>food processor</a>, and blend until smooth.<br><br>

    Spread across 2 parchment lined <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>baking sheets</a>.<br><br>

    Bake at 350F for 30 minutes, or until lightly browned on the edges and set in the center.<br><br>

    Allow to totally cool before cutting each one in half, leaving you with 4 pieces.

- |
    <b>Sauce</b><br>
    Below is a quick rundown on how to make my <a href='/recipes/pasta-sauce'>Simple Pasta Sauce</a>.<br><br>

    Cut an <a href='/misc/veggies#onion'>onion</a> into a small dice, and add to a preheated <a href='https://amzn.to/46ccg8m'>medium pot</a> with <a href='/misc/olive-oil'>olive oil</a> and <a href='/misc/veggies#garlic'>minced garlic</a> over medium heat.<br><br>

    Cover and cook until translucent, about 5 minutes.<br><br>

    Add in the rest of the sauce ingredients (<a href='/misc/veggies#tomato'>crushed tomatoes</a>, oregano, basil, parsley, garlic powder, onion powder, black pepper, salt, and red pepper flakes).<br><br>

    Reduce the heat to low, and let simmer (covered) for about 30 minutes with occasional stirring.<br><br>

    Let cool completely.

- |
    <b>Cheese</b><br>
    Crack an <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>egg</a> into a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>large bowl</a>, and beat with a <a href='https://amzn.to/40F2aK0'>silicone spatula</a>.<br><br>

    Add in the rest of the cheese ingredients (blended <a href='/misc/dairy#cottage-cheese'>cottage cheese</a>, <a href='/misc/dairy#mozzarella'>shredded mozzarella</a>, garlic powder, onion powder, and parsley).<br><br>

    Mix together, and set aside.

-
    <b>Assemble</b><br>
    Add a spoonful of sauce to the bottom of a <a href='https://amzn.to/3YY2H9q'>9" square baking pan</a>.<br><br>

    Add your first "lasagna sheet" into the pan, and cover with 1/4 of your cheese mix (about 150 g or 2/3 cup).<br><br>

    Add 1/4 of your sauce on top of the cheese (about 200 g or 1 cup).<br><br>

    Repeat with the remaining noodles, cheese, and sauce.<br><br>

    Add some <a href='/misc/dairy#grated-cheese'>grated parmesan cheese</a> on top.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meme/Vegetarian Lasagna/raw.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- <b>Bake</b><br>
    Cover with aluminum foil, and bake at 400F for about 45 minutes, or until the <a href='/misc/dairy#mozzarella'>cheese</a> is bubbly.<br><br>

    Remove the foil, and bake for an additional 15 minutes.<br><br>

    Garnish with parsley, and let it cool for 10 minutes before cutting and serving.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meme/Vegetarian Lasagna/cooked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
