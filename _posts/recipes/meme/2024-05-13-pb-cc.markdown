Description: As someone who is loves <a href='/misc/dairy#cottage-cheese'>cottage cheese</a> and is addicted to <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut</a> butter, this spread should help curb all my <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>peanut butter</a> cravings. It's blended together powdered peanut butter, cottage cheese, and <a href='/misc/fruit#banana'>banana</a>, for a delicious peanut flavored spread for my morning <a href='/yogurt'>yogurt</a>. For the same serving, there is only 1/4 of the <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a>, and over twice the amount of <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> per 100 <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a>.  You can also check out any of my other <a href='/nut-butter'>nut butter recipes</a>!
Instructions:
- Blend together cottage cheese, PB2, overripe banana, cinnamon, and almond extract in a small food processor.  Transfer to an airtight container, and store in the fridge.  Optionally garnish with peanuts and chickpeas as I did here

- Pureed chickpeas can be used instead of cottage cheese, but you will want to add about 1/2 cup (120 g) of unsweetened vanilla almond milk and 1/4 tsp (1.5 g) salt
