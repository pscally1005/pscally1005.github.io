Description: This protein banana bread is made with whole wheat and oat flours, sweetened with sugar free syrup, and features chocolate chips and chopped walnuts. Oh and it's not my recipe lol, it's my brother's girlfriend's. They brought it over one time and I knew I had to try making it for myself because it was very good. This healthy banana bread is nearly identical in taste and texture to the classic, while being higher in <a href="/misc/protein">protein</a> and <a href="/misc/fiber">fiber</a>, lower in <a href="/misc/calories">calories</a>, and with no <a href="/misc/hidden-sugar">added sugar</a>
Instructions: 
- Preheat your oven to 350F. Line a 9x5" bread pan with parchment paper, and lightly grease the paper

- Beat together the wet ingredients in a large bowl with a hand mixer - banana, yogurt, syrup, milk, and vanilla
- <center><img src="/assets/Food/Meme/Sandra/wet.jpg" alt="" class="instruction-image"></center>

- In a separate bowl, whisk together the dry ingredients - whole wheat flour, oat flour, whey protein, baking soda, cinnamon, baking powder, salt, cloves, and nutmeg
- <center><img src="/assets/Food/Meme/Sandra/dry.jpg" alt="" class="instruction-image"></center>

- Roughly chop your walnuts, and add to a small bowl with the chocolate chips
- <center><img src="/assets/Food/Meme/Sandra/mixins.jpg" alt="" class="instruction-image"></center>

- Add the dry ingredients into the wet, and mix with a silicone spatula until just fully combined. Don't over mix. Fold in the mix ins
- <center><img src="/assets/Food/Meme/Sandra/bowl.jpg" alt="" class="instruction-image"></center>

- Transfer the batter to the pan, and smooth out the top
- <center><img src="/assets/Food/Meme/Sandra/raw.jpg" alt="" class="instruction-image"></center>

- Bake at 350F for 40 minutes. Turn off the oven, and leave the banana bread in the oven for an additional 4 minutes. Do not open the door until after this step

- The banana bread should be lightly browned, where a toothpick comes out nearly clean and the internal temperature is around 205F. If it's still soft in the center, then cover with foil and bake until a toothpick comes out nearly clean
- <center><img src="/assets/Food/Meme/Sandra/baked.jpg" alt="" class="instruction-image"></center>

- Transfer to a wire rack to totally cool (at least 1 hour) before slicing
- <center><img src="/assets/Food/Meme/Sandra/sliced.jpg" alt="" class="instruction-image"></center>
