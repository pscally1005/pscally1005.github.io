Description: Plain nonfat <a href="/misc/dairy#greek-yogurt">greek yogurt</a> is a delicious, easy, and cheap source of <a href="/misc/protein">protein</a>, probiotics, <a href="/misc/calcium">calcium</a>, and <a href="/misc/vitamin-b12">vitamin B12</a>.  But some people hate that tang flavor.  I was able to find this <a href="https://amzn.to/4kCXb4u">zero sugar vanilla Greek yogurt</a> that's not only free of artificial sweeteners (it uses allulose, stevia, and monk fruit), but it's also lactose free.  It tastes just like those <a href="/misc/hidden-sugar">sugar</a> filled yogurts without all the <a href="/misc/hidden-sugar">sugar</a>, and it lacks that intense yogurt flavor that some people hate so much (I love it though lol).  You can also check out any of my other <a href="/yogurt">yogurt recipes</a>!
Instructions: 
- In a small bowl, mix together yogurt, peanut butter, cocoa powder, and honey. Top with chocolate chips
