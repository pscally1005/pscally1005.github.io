Description: If you mix together melted <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut oil</a> with <a href='/misc/overshadowed-healthy-foods#chocolate'>cocoa powder</a>, you get a <a href='/recipes/chocolate-bar'>100% Chocolate Bar</a>. Simple as that. Well, you can replace the <a href='/misc/overshadowed-healthy-foods#chocolate'>cocoa</a> with <a href='/misc/dairy#whey'>whey protein powder</a>, either chocolate or vanilla, to end up with a <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar free</a> bar of milk or white chocolate. I preferred the white chocolate with the vanilla <a href='/misc/dairy#whey'>protein powder</a>, but the chocolate was good too.  You can even use <a href='https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07SXBL1GF?ref=t_ac_view_request_product_image&campaignId=amzn1.campaign.2L4DOI4F1KV3G&linkCode=tr1&tag=poormanprotei-20&linkId=amzn1.campaign.2L4DOI4F1KV3G_1767022097417'>powdered peanut butter</a> and chop up the bar to make peanut butter chips!
Instructions:
- Add your <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut oil</a> to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>small bowl</a>, and microwave for 1 minute to melt. Stir in the <a href='/misc/dairy#whey'>protein powder</a> with a <a href='https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0C37QM1K3?ref=t_ac_view_request_product_image&campaignId=amzn1.campaign.1OO1S5W7ZMYBB&linkCode=tr1&tag=poormanprotei-20&linkId=amzn1.campaign.1OO1S5W7ZMYBB_1751469633605'>silicone spatula</a> until smooth. Transfer the batter to a <a href='https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0D9J99TW4?ref=t_ac_view_request_product_image&campaignId=amzn1.campaign.33LWXAZ203YV4&linkCode=tr1&tag=poormanprotei-20&linkId=amzn1.campaign.33LWXAZ203YV4_1751469098772'>chocolate mold</a>, and refrigerate to harden, about 1 hour
Notes:
- You can also use <a href='https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07SXBL1GF?ref=t_ac_view_request_product_image&campaignId=amzn1.campaign.2L4DOI4F1KV3G&linkCode=tr1&tag=poormanprotei-20&linkId=amzn1.campaign.2L4DOI4F1KV3G_1767022097417'>powdered peanut butter</a> to make a <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a> flavored bar.  Just use 5 tbsp (30 g) <a href='https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07SXBL1GF?ref=t_ac_view_request_product_image&campaignId=amzn1.campaign.2L4DOI4F1KV3G&linkCode=tr1&tag=poormanprotei-20&linkId=amzn1.campaign.2L4DOI4F1KV3G_1767022097417'>powdered peanut butter</a> in place of <a href='/misc/dairy#whey'>whey</a>.  My <a href='https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07SXBL1GF?ref=t_ac_view_request_product_image&campaignId=amzn1.campaign.2L4DOI4F1KV3G&linkCode=tr1&tag=poormanprotei-20&linkId=amzn1.campaign.2L4DOI4F1KV3G_1767022097417'>powdered peanut butter</a> is unsweetened and unsalted, so I also added 1/2 tsp (2.5 g) <a href='https://amzn.to/3SqwsMO'>liquid monk fruit</a> and 1/8 tsp (0.75 g) salt
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meme/Protein Chocolate/pb-bar.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Note that the texture will differ depending on your <a href='/misc/dairy#whey'>protein powder</a>. With vanilla, the melted chocolate resembled more like regular melted chocolate. But with my chocolate <a href='/misc/dairy#whey'>protein powder</a>, I ended up with more of a dough, and it was grittier and saltier. Either way, spread the mix into a <a href='https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0D9J99TW4?ref=t_ac_view_request_product_image&campaignId=amzn1.campaign.33LWXAZ203YV4&linkCode=tr1&tag=poormanprotei-20&linkId=amzn1.campaign.33LWXAZ203YV4_1751469098772'>chocolate mold</a> and refrigerate to harden. Both ended up being solid bars of chocolate in the end
