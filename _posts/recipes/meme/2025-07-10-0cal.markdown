Description: No this isn't a joke (okay maybe it is a little bit).  But with just 2 ingredients and a little bit of rounding, you can make 0 <a href='/misc/calories'>calorie</a> <a href='/cookies'>cookies</a>.  I've taken my <a href='/recipes/styrofoam-cookies'>Nut Butter Styrofoam Cookies</a> and just removed the <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a>.  These dead simple meringue <a href='/cookies'>cookies</a> use just <a href='/misc/meat#egg-whites'>egg whites</a> and <a href='https://amzn.to/4ceFnvP'>granulated monk fruit</a> for a light and tasty <a href='/cookies'>cookie</a> with a cloud like texture.
Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 325F, and line a <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>cookie sheet</a> with parchment paper

- In a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>large bowl</a>, beat your <a href='/misc/meat#egg-whites'>egg white</a> with a <a href='https://amzn.to/45yqsbM'>hand mixer</a> until stiff peaks form, about 2 minutes. Gradually beat in the <a href='https://amzn.to/4ceFnvP'>granulated sweetener</a> until you have glossy peaks
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meme/0 Cal/egg-white.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Use a spoon to dollop the cookies onto the tray
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meme/0 Cal/raw.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Bake at 325F for about 12 minutes, or until lightly golden brown. They will feel very soft, but will harden on the tray. Let cool for at least 30 minutes before removing
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meme/0 Cal/baked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- Optionally, mix in some <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>cocoa powder</a> for a chocolate <a href='/cookies'>cookie</a>.  I'm going with 1 tbsp (5 g) of <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>cocoa powder</a> in half the batch, and leaving the other half white.  Note that adding <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>cocoa</a> will <b>triple</b> the <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a> to a whole <b>3</b>.  Oh the horror!

- Just because these are <a href='/misc/calories'>zero calories</a> does <b>not</b> mean you should eat the entire batch at once.  That much <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#sugar-substitutes'>sugar alcohols</a> in one sitting would probably wreck your stomach, or at least give you lethal farts.  You have been warned (maybe I should be warning your friends instead)
