Description: These 3 simple ingredient <a href='/cookies'>cookies</a> come from the cookbook Baking Yesteryear, where their name comes from their interesting texture. Crunchy on the outside, chewy on the inside, these <a href='/cookies'>cookies</a> supposedly have the texture of eating Styrofoam?  If that doesn't sound appealing, then think of these more as single <a href='/misc/nuts#almonds'>almond</a> macaroon <a href='/cookies'>cookies</a> without any filling, or as a <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a> flavored meringue <a href='/cookies'>cookie</a>. I've swapped the granulated <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar</a> for <a href='https://amzn.to/4ceFnvP'>granulated monk fruit</a>, and substituted in any <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>nut butter</a> I want. The original is <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a>, but try <a href='/misc/nuts#almonds'>almond butter</a> for an amaretti <a href='/cookies'>cookie</a> vibe.
Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 325F, and line a <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>cookie sheet</a> with parchment paper

- Separate the white from the <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>yolk</a> of a large <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>egg</a>

- In a large metal or <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>glass bowl</a> with a <a href='https://amzn.to/45yqsbM'>hand mixer</a>, beat the <a href='/misc/meat#egg-whites'>egg white</a> until you have stiff peaks. Gradually add the <a href='https://amzn.to/4ceFnvP'>granulated sweetener</a> 1 tbsp (12.5 g) at a time, until fully incorporated, and you have stiff glossy peaks
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meme/Styrofoam/egg-white.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- If necessary, microwave your <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>nut butter</a> for 30 seconds to soften. Carefully fold the <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>nut butter</a> into the meringue with a <a href='https://amzn.to/40F2aK0'>silicone spatula</a>, trying to deflate it as little as possible.  Leave some streaks of white for contrast
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meme/Styrofoam/pb.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Dollop the mixture onto your sheet using a spoon
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meme/Styrofoam/raw.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Bake for about 12 minutes at 325F, or until lightly browned.  Note that should still feel very soft to the touch. Cool completely on the sheet before removing. Store leftovers in the fridge or freezer
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meme/Styrofoam/baked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- <a href='/misc/meat#egg-whites'>Carton egg whites</a> are not recommended, as they will not beat up as well. Reserve the <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>yolk</a> for something else, like scrambled <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>eggs</a> or <a href='/recipes/hollandaise'>Greek Yogurt Hollandaise Sauce</a>
