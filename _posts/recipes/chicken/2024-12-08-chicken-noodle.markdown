Description: Winter is coming, and that means immune boosting delicious <a href='/soups-and-stews'>stews</a> to keep you warm and help cure your common cold. This remix off the classic Chicken Noodle Soup is packed with more <a href='/misc/veggies'>vegetables</a> and <a href='/misc/chronic-inflammation'>anti-inflammatory</a> spices. The combination of <a href='/misc/veggies#ginger'>ginger</a>, turmeric, <a href='/misc/veggies#garlic'>garlic</a>, cayenne pepper, and more will leave your taste buds satisfied, belly fully, and nose cleared.  It's a dump and go <a href='/soups-and-stews'>stew</a>, like my <a href='/recipes/chicken-stew'>Crockpot Chicken Stew</a>; meaning you just have to cut up the <a href='/misc/veggies'>vegetables</a> and <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a>, add it to the <a href='https://amzn.to/49TUS9E'>crockpot</a>, and have dinner ready for you. No other cooking required besides a box of <a href='/misc/grains#pasta'>pasta</a>!  You can also check out any of my other <a href='/soups-and-stews'>soups and stews</a>!
Instructions:
- Cut your <a href='/misc/veggies#onion'>onion</a>, <a href='/misc/veggies#carrots'>carrots</a>, and <a href='/misc/veggies#celery'>celery</a> into a small dice, and add to a <a href='https://amzn.to/49TUS9E'>large slow cooker</a>. Dump in your bag of defrosted <a href='/misc/veggies#broccoli'>broccoli</a>. Finely cut your <a href='/misc/veggies#ginger'>ginger</a>, and add to the pot as well with your <a href='/misc/veggies#garlic'>minced garlic</a>

- Cut your <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-thighs'>chicken thighs</a> into bite sized pieces, and add to the pot

- Add in your soy sauce, chicken bouillon, dried spices (thyme, garlic powder, onion powder, cumin, black pepper, allspice, turmeric, cinnamon, nutmeg, and cayenne), and water. Mix everything together

- Cover and cook on low for about 6 hours, or until the <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> is at least <a href='https://amzn.to/4kmobG2'>180F</a>, and the <a href='/misc/veggies'>vegetables</a> are tender

- With about 30 minutes left on the <a href='/soups-and-stews'>stew</a>, cook your <a href='/misc/grains#pasta'>pasta</a> on the stove according to instructions.  Mix the cooked <a href='/misc/grains#pasta'>pasta</a> into the finished <a href='/soups-and-stews'>stew</a>, along with <a href='/misc/fruit#lemon-juice'>lemon juice</a>.  Serve
Notes:
- <a href='/misc/grains#whole-wheat'>Whole wheat</a>, egg noodles, <a href='/misc/celiac'>gluten free</a> <a href='/misc/grains#pasta'>pasta</a>, <a href='/misc/beans#chickpeas'>chickpea pasta</a>, or <a href='/misc/beans#red-lentils'>red lentil pasta</a> are all good choices.  Putting the <a href='/misc/grains#pasta'>pasta</a> in raw at the start will cause it to disintegrate and fully mix into the <a href='/soups-and-stews'>stew</a> (I've made that mistake before lol), which is why it has to be added at the end
