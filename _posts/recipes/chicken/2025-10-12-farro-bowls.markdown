Description: <a href='/misc/grains#farro'>Farro</a> is an ancient <a href='/misc/grains#whole-wheat'>wheat based</a> <a href='/misc/grains'>grain</a> that cooks and tastes like <a href='/misc/grains#brown-rice'>brown rice</a>, but with a more chewy texture. This nutritious <a href='/misc/grains'>whole grain</a> is a great way to mix it up from your usual <a href='/misc/grains#brown-rice'>rice</a>. I've paired it here with some roasted <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken breast</a>, roasted <a href='/misc/veggies#broccoli'>broccoli</a>, and <a href='/misc/dairy#goat-cheese'>goat cheese</a>. These bowls have over 50 g of <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> in under 550 <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a>, with plenty of complex <a href='/misc/carbs'>starches</a>, gut promoting <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a>, brain supporting <a href='/misc/fats'>healthy fats</a>, and delicious flavor.
Instructions:
- Add water and salt to a <a href='https://amzn.to/46ccg8m'>medium pot</a>. Cover and bring to a boil over high heat. Add in the <a href='/misc/grains#farro'>farro</a>, cover, reduce heat to medium low, and let simmer for about 25 minutes, or until all water has absorbed
- <img src='/assets/Food/Chicken/Farro Bowls/farro-cooked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Meanwhile, preheat your oven to 400F, and line a large <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>cookie sheet</a> with parchment paper. Toss the <a href='/misc/veggies#broccoli'>broccoli</a> with <a href='/misc/fruit#lemon-juice'>lemon juice</a>, <a href='/misc/olive-oil'>oil</a>, lemon pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, and black pepper. Roast at 400F for about 30 minutes, or until browned
- <img src='/assets/Food/Chicken/Farro Bowls/broccoli-roasted.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Cut your <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> into thin cutlets, and add to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>large bowl</a>. Season with vinegar, <a href='/misc/olive-oil'>oil</a>, garlic powder, onion powder, black pepper, and salt. Add to a parchment lined <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>cookie sheet</a>, and roast at 400F until the <a href='https://amzn.to/4kmobG2'>internal temperature</a> is <a href='https://amzn.to/4kmobG2'>165F</a> (about 14 minutes). Let rest for a few minutes before dicing into bite sized pieces
- <img src='/assets/Food/Chicken/Farro Bowls/chicken-cooked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Prepare 6 airtight containers, and evenly divide your food. I got about 150g, 125g, and 100g respectively of <a href='/misc/veggies#broccoli'>broccoli</a>, <a href='/misc/grains#farro'>farro</a>, and <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> per bowl.  Top each bowl with 1 oz (28 g) of <a href='/misc/dairy#goat-cheese'>goat cheese</a>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Chicken/Farro Bowls/containers.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
