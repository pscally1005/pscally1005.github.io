Description: This simple and healthy meal is a go to favorite of mine. Just roast cubed <a href='/misc/veggies#acorn-squash'>acorn squash</a> and thinly sliced <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken breasts</a> with Italian herbs and spices for a delicious and quick meal. Both the <a href='/misc/meat'>meat</a> and the <a href='/misc/veggies'>vegetables</a> can be prepped ahead of time, ready to stick in the oven when you get home from work. I've paired this here with a <a href='/recipes/sides'>side</a> of roasted <a href='/misc/veggies#potato'>potatoes</a>, but <a href='/misc/grains#brown-rice'>rice</a>, <a href='/misc/beans'>beans</a>, or <a href='/misc/veggies#sweet-potato'>sweet potatoes</a> are also good picks!
Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 400F, and line 2 large <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>cookie sheets</a> with parchment paper

- Wash your <a href='/misc/veggies#acorn-squash'>acorn squash</a>, cut in half, and scoop out the seeds. Cut the <a href='/misc/veggies#acorn-squash'>acorn squash</a> into a medium dice, and add to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>large bowl</a>.  Leave the skins on!

- Season the <a href='/misc/veggies#acorn-squash'>acorn squash</a> with oil, <a href='/misc/fruit#lemon-juice'>lemon juice</a>, <a href='/misc/veggies#garlic'>minced garlic</a>, basil, onion powder, <a href='/misc/dairy#grated-cheese'>grated cheese</a>, lemon pepper, and black pepper

- Transfer to the pan, and roast for 30-40 minutes, or until soft and browned

- As the <a href='/misc/veggies#acorn-squash'>acorn squash</a> roasts, prepare the <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a>. Cut your <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken breasts</a> into large cutlets, and pound to an even thickness. Add to your bowl

- Season the <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> with oil, balsamic, <a href='/misc/fruit#lemon-juice'>lemon juice</a>, basil, thyme, onion powder, garlic powder, salt, and black pepper

- Transfer the <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> to the pan. Roast at 400F for about 13 minutes, or until the <a href='https://amzn.to/4kmobG2'>internal temperature</a> just reaches <a href='https://amzn.to/4kmobG2'>165F</a>
