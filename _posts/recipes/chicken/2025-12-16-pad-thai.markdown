Description: Instead of rice noodles, I'm packing my Chicken Pad Thai full of <a href='/misc/veggies#spaghetti-squash'>spaghetti squash</a> for more <a href='/misc/veggies'>vegetables</a>. The result is a delicious lower <a href='/misc/carbs'>carb</a>, higher <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a> meal that still clocks in under 500 <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a> with over 40 g of <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a>.   Not only is <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a> perfect in the sauce, but the <a href='/misc/fats'>added fats</a>, in combination with the lower <a href='/misc/carbs'>carb</a> content of the <a href='/misc/veggies#spaghetti-squash'>spaghetti squash</a> and the <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> of the <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a>, will help to minimize any spikes in your <a href='/misc/diabetes'>blood sugar</a>.  <a href='/misc/protein'>Protein</a>, <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a>, and <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a> all will give you a more steady <a href='/misc/diabetes'>glucose curve</a>, as opposed to the <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>added sugar</a> and rice noodles typically found in Pad Thai
Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 400F, and line a large <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>cookie sheet</a> with parchment paper

- Cut your <a href='/misc/veggies#spaghetti-squash'>spaghetti squash</a> in half lengthwise, and scoop out the seeds with a spoon. Season with oil, salt, and black pepper

- Roast at 400F, cut side down, for about 1 hour, or until fork tender. Remove from the oven, flip over, and let steam out for 5 minutes

- Meanwhile, preheat a <a href='https://amzn.to/4rdR0HI'>large pan</a> over medium heat with oil. Cut your <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> into bite sized cubes, and add to the pan with salt and black pepper. Sauté until fully cooked (<a href='https://amzn.to/4kmobG2'>165F</a>), about 10 minutes, and set aside in a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>large bowl</a>

- Heat the now empty pan over medium with oil. Cut your <a href='/misc/veggies#pepper'>peppers</a> and <a href='/misc/veggies#onion'>onions</a> into a medium dice, and add to the pan. Cover and cook until soft and lightly browned, about 10 minutes.  Set aside with the <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a>

- Finally, whisk together your sauce in a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>medium bowl</a>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Chicken/Pad Thai/separate.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- When everything is done cooking, fluff the <a href='/misc/veggies#spaghetti-squash'>spaghetti squash</a> with a fork into the bowl.  Mix until fully combined, and divide 6 ways
