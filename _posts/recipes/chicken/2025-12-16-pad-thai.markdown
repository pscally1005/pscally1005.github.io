Description: Instead of <a href='/misc/grains#white-rice'>rice</a> noodles, I'm packing my Chicken Pad Thai full of <a href='/misc/veggies#spaghetti-squash'>spaghetti squash</a> for more <a href='/misc/veggies'>vegetables</a>. The result is a delicious lower <a href='/misc/carbs'>carb</a>, higher <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a> meal that still clocks in under 500 <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a> with over 40 g of <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a>.   Not only is <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a> perfect in the sauce, but the added <a href='/misc/fats'>fats</a>, in combination with the lower <a href='/misc/carbs'>carb</a> content of the <a href='/misc/veggies#spaghetti-squash'>squash</a> and the <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> of the <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a>, will help to minimize any spikes in your <a href='/misc/diabetes'>blood sugar</a>.  <a href='/misc/protein'>Protein</a>, <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a>, and <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a> all will give you a more steady <a href='/misc/insulin-resistance'>glucose curve</a>, as opposed to the <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>added sugar</a> and <a href='/misc/grains#white-rice'>rice</a> noodles typically found in Pad Thai
Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 400F, and line a large cookie sheet with parchment paper

- Cut your squash in half lengthwise, and scoop out the seeds with a spoon. Season with oil, salt, and black pepper

- Roast at 400F, cut side down, for about 1 hour, or until fork tender. Remove from the oven, flip over, and let steam out for 5 minutes

- Meanwhile, preheat a large pan over medium heat with oil. Cut your chicken into bite sized cubes, and add to the pan with salt and black pepper. Sauté until fully cooked (165F), about 10 minutes, and set aside in a large bowl

- Heat the now empty pan over medium with oil. Cut your peppers and onions into a medium dice, and add to the pan. Cover and cook until soft and lightly browned, about 10 minutes.  Set aside with the chicken

- Finally, whisk together your sauce in a medium bowl
- <img src='/assets/Food/Chicken/Pad Thai/separate.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- When everything is done cooking, fluff the spaghetti squash with a fork into the bowl.  Mix until fully combined, and divide 6 ways
