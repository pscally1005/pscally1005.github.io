Description: Chicken Alfredo is typically a <a href='/misc/calories'>caloric</a> bomb with little <a href='/misc/veggies'>vegetables</a>. With <a href='/misc/dairy#butter'>butter</a>, heavy cream, and <a href='/misc/grains#pasta-white'>white pasta</a>, this dish can use some improvement. I'm making a lighter version here with an insane 60 g of <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> per serving. I'm also adding some <a href='/misc/veggies#broccoli'>broccoli</a> for additional volume, and swapping the <a href='/misc/grains#pasta'>pasta</a> for <a href='/misc/veggies#sweet-potato'>sweet potatoes</a>. <a href='/misc/veggies#potato'>Potatoes</a> are cheaper and healthier than <a href='/misc/grains#pasta'>pasta</a>, and it's just what I'm in the mood for today
Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 400F, and line 3 <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>baking sheets</a> with parchment paper

- Wash and dice your <a href='/misc/veggies#sweet-potato'>sweet potatoes</a>, and add to one pan. Season with oil and black pepper (the sauce is salty)

- Add your (defrosted) <a href='/misc/veggies#broccoli'>broccoli</a> to the other 2 pans, and again season with oil and black pepper (the sauce is salty)

- Roast both the <a href='/misc/veggies#broccoli'>broccoli</a> and <a href='/misc/veggies#sweet-potato'>sweet potatoes</a> at 400F until browned and softened, about 30 minutes

- Heat a pan over medium heat with olive oil. Dice your <a href='/misc/veggies#onion'>onion</a>, and add to the pan. Cover and cook until translucent

- Meanwhile, dice your <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> into cubes. Add to the pan along with salt and black pepper when the <a href='/misc/veggies#onion'>onions</a> are translucent. Cook until the <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> is <a href='https://amzn.to/4kmobG2'>165F</a>. Remove from the heat

- As the <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> cooks, prepare your sauce. Blend together all ingredients in a <a href='https://amzn.to/4q0AUjI'>food processor</a> until smooth - <a href='/misc/dairy#cottage-cheese'>cottage cheese</a>, evaporated milk, <a href='/misc/dairy#grated-cheese'>grated cheese</a>, garlic powder, onion powder, and nutmeg
- <img src='/assets/Food/Chicken/Alfredo/separate.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- To assemble, mix together the <a href='/misc/veggies#sweet-potato'>sweet potatoes</a>, <a href='/misc/veggies#broccoli'>broccoli</a>, <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a>, and <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#sauces'>Alfredo</a> sauce in a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>large bowl</a>. Divide into 6 servings (about 500 g each)
