Description: This BBQ pulled chicken is made easy in a <a href='https://amzn.to/49TUS9E'>slow cooker</a>, and features a homemade <a href='/recipes/bbq-sauce'>Unsweetened BBQ Sauce</a>. I paired it with a <a href='/recipes/sides'>side</a> of oil and vinegar coleslaw (not a <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#dips'>mayo</a> based coleslaw), and can be easily made into a sandwich. I always hated shredding <a href='/misc/veggies#cabbage'>cabbage</a>, until I realized I could use my <a href='https://amzn.to/4q0AUjI'>food processor</a> to do the work for me. Give it a go, and check out the homemade sauce, or use your own <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#dips'>BBQ sauce</a>.
Instructions:
- Place the <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-thighs'>thighs</a> into a <a href='https://amzn.to/49TUS9E'>large slow cooker</a>. Add spices (chili powder, paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, black pepper, and cayenne) over the <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a>, and mix to evenly coat it all

- Add half of your <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#dips'>BBQ sauce</a> on top of the <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a>. The other half of the <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#dips'>BBQ sauce</a> will be reserved for at the end

- Cover, and cook on low for 4-6 hours, or until the <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> is at least <a href='https://amzn.to/4kmobG2'>185F</a> and shreds easily

- Transfer the <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> to a <a href='https://amzn.to/43gNqmY'>cutting board</a> or bowl, and shred with 2 forks (or your hands). Return the <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> to the <a href='https://amzn.to/49TUS9E'>slow cooker</a>

- Heat the remainder of your <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#dips'>BBQ sauce</a> and soy sauce in the microwave for about a minute until warm, and pour over your shredded <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a>

- The coleslaw can be made at any time and kept in the fridge, so I like to do it as the <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> cooks. I find that a <a href='https://amzn.to/4q0AUjI'>food processor</a> is the easiest way to shred <a href='/misc/veggies#cabbage'>cabbage</a>. Cut your <a href='/misc/veggies#cabbage'>cabbage</a> into wedges and remove the core. Using the shredder attachment, feed the <a href='/misc/veggies#cabbage'>cabbage</a> down the tube to finely shred it. You can also use a <a href='https://amzn.to/4q3nlQz'>mandoline</a> or a <a href='https://amzn.to/4jlDKwc'>knife</a>. Transfer the <a href='/misc/veggies#cabbage'>shredded cabbage</a> to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>large bowl</a>

- In a glass, mix together the dressing ingredients (<a href='/misc/apple-cider-vinegar'>ACV</a>, <a href='/misc/olive-oil'>oil</a>, <a href='/misc/fruit#lime'>lime</a>, soy sauce, balsamic, dijon, onion and garlic powder, black pepper, and celery seed). Pour over the <a href='/misc/veggies#cabbage'>cabbage</a>, mix, and serve
