Description: This dish pairs sautéed <a href='/misc/veggies#pepper'>peppers</a> and <a href='/misc/veggies#onion'>onions</a>, <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken breast</a>, and a spicy and creamy buffalo sauce. These are quick to come together, with just sautéing the <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> and <a href='/misc/veggies'>vegetables</a>, pouring in the sauce, and serving with a <a href='/recipes/sides'>side</a> of <a href='/misc/grains#brown-rice'>rice</a>
Instructions:
- Mix <a href='/recipes/hot-sauce'>hot sauce</a>, honey, water, and cornstarch in a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>medium bowl</a> or tall glass. Stir immediately after adding the cornstarch, and set aside

- Dice up the <a href='/misc/veggies#pepper'>peppers</a> and <a href='/misc/veggies#onion'>onions</a>, and add to a large skillet with oil and a small pinch of salt. Cover, and cook over medium heat until browned and soft. Set aside

- Meanwhile, dice the <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> into bite sized pieces, and transfer to a bowl. Mix in the seasonings

- <a href='https://amzn.to/3Hdg0gk'>Spray</a> the pan again, and cook the <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> on medium. Cook until <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> is almost done, because the <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> will cook more in the sauce

- Add in the sauce and <a href='/misc/veggies'>vegetables</a> into the pan, and also add the <a href='/misc/dairy#cottage-cheese'>cottage cheese</a>. Combine and cook for about a minute until creamy. Optionally, top with <a href='/misc/dairy#cheddar'>shredded cheese</a>, or serve with <a href='/misc/grains#brown-rice'>rice</a>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Chicken/Buffalo/buffalo-5.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- DO NOT add the <a href='/misc/dairy#cottage-cheese'>cottage cheese</a> to the sauce in step 1, or the acid of the <a href='/recipes/hot-sauce'>hot sauce</a> will curdle it. Add the <a href='/misc/dairy#cottage-cheese'>cottage cheese</a> at the end
