Description: This <a href='https://amzn.to/49TUS9E'>Crockpot</a> <a href='/soups-and-stews'>stew</a> is a perfect dump and go recipe, like my <a href='/recipes/chicken-noodle-stew'>Chicken Noodle Stew</a>; meaning you just have to cut up the <a href='/misc/veggies'>vegetables</a> and <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a>, add it to the <a href='https://amzn.to/49TUS9E'>crockpot</a>, and have dinner ready for you. No other cooking required!. Add all the <a href='/misc/veggies'>vegetables</a>, <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a>, and spices raw, and just forget about it until dinner time. This hearty meal is great during a cold winter. <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-thighs'>Chicken thighs</a> work the best here for the long cooking time, as they won't dry out like <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken breast</a> would.  You can also check out any of my other <a href='/soups-and-stews'>soups and stews</a>!
Instructions:
- Cut the <a href='/misc/veggies#pepper'>peppers</a> and <a href='/misc/veggies#onion'>onions</a> into a medium dice, and cut your <a href='/misc/veggies#broccoli'>broccoli</a> florets into a similar size.  Add to a <a href='https://amzn.to/49TUS9E'>large slow cooker</a> (mine is 6 quarts)

- Dice the <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> into bite sized cubes, and add to the pot

- Measure out all the liquid ingredients in a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>medium bowl</a> - (drained and rinsed) <a href='/misc/beans'>beans</a>, diced tomatoes, water, <a href='/recipes/salsa'>salsa</a>, <a href='/recipes/hot-sauce'>hot sauce</a>, soy sauce, <a href='/misc/veggies#garlic'>minced garlic</a>, <a href='/misc/fruit#lemon-juice'>lemon juice</a>, and balsamic vinegar.  Add to the pot as well

- Finally, mix in your dried spices - paprika, chili powder, garlic powder, onion powder, oregano, basil, black pepper, and cinnamon

- Cover and cook on low for about 6 hours, or until <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> is fully cooked, ideally around <a href='https://amzn.to/4kmobG2'>180F</a>
