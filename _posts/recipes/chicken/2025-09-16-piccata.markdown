Description: Ah Chicken Piccata, the better lemon chicken cousin of Chicken Francese. For those unaware, Francese has an <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>egg</a> and <a href='/misc/grains#white-wheat'>flour</a> coating on the <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a>. In comparison, Piccata has only <a href='/misc/grains#white-wheat'>flour</a> (no <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>egg</a>), and also adds capers into the sauce.  This <a href='/misc/high-protein'>high protein</a> dish has a creamy <a href='/misc/fruit#lemon'>lemon</a> sauce without tons of <a href='/misc/dairy#butter'>butter</a> or oil, and pair great with any <a href='/misc/veggies'>vegetables</a> and <a href='/misc/carbs'>complex carb</a> <a href='/recipes/sides'>side</a>.
Instructions:
- Cut your <a href='/misc/meat'>meat</a> into 6 large pieces. Cover with plastic wrap, and pound with a <a href='/misc/meat'>meat</a> mallet until you have a thin piece of <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a>. Repeat with all pieces

- On a large plate, mix together flour, oregano, black pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, and salt. Any <a href='/misc/grains#white-wheat'>flour</a> will do, but today I opted for <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut</a>. Lightly coat your pieces of <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> in <a href='/misc/grains#white-wheat'>flour</a>, and set aside

- In a <a href='https://amzn.to/4rdR0HI'>large pan</a>, preheat 1 tsp (5 g) of <a href='/misc/olive-oil'>oil</a> over medium heat. When hot, add 2 pieces of <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a>, and sear on both sides until browned. Cook until the <a href='https://amzn.to/4kmobG2'>internal temperature</a> just reaches <a href='https://amzn.to/4kmobG2'>165F</a>, and set aside. Repeat in 2 more batches (1 tsp, 5 g of <a href='/misc/olive-oil'>oil</a> for each batch) for the remaining 4 pieces
- <img src='/assets/Food/Chicken/Piccata/chicken1.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Chicken/Piccata/chicken2.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Reduce heat to low. Whisk together the sauce ingredients in a large glass, and add to the pan. Simmer and mix until thickened
- <img src='/assets/Food/Chicken/Piccata/sauce.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- As the sauce simmers, slice your <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> on a bias. Add the <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> to a plate, and pour the sauce over the <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a>. Optionally garnish with a slice of <a href='/misc/fruit#lemon'>lemon</a>, and serve
Notes:
- Serve with a <a href='/recipes/sides'>side</a> of <a href='/recipes/roasted-vegetables'>roasted</a> or <a href='/recipes/vegetable-medley'>sauteed</a> <a href='/misc/veggies'>veggies</a>, such as <a href='/misc/veggies#spinach-fresh'>spinach</a>, <a href='/misc/veggies#broccoli'>broccoli</a>, <a href='/misc/veggies#cauliflower'>cauliflower</a>, or <a href='/misc/veggies#asparagus'>asparagus</a>. Also pair with healthy <a href='/misc/carbs'>starches</a>, such as a <a href='/recipes/baked-sweet-potato'>baked potato</a>, <a href='/recipes/rice-and-beans'>brown rice</a>, <a href='/recipes/red-lentils'>lentils</a>, or <a href='/recipes/couscous'>couscous</a>.  Today, I'm pairing my Chicken Piccata with <a href='/recipes/roasted-vegetables'>Roasted Brussel Sprouts</a> and <a href='/recipes/air-fried-trail-mix'>Air Fryer Trail Mix</a>!
