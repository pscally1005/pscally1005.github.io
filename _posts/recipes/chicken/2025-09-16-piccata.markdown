Description: Ah Chicken Piccata, the better lemon <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> cousin of Chicken Francese. For those unaware, Francese has an <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>egg</a> and flour coating on the chicken. In comparison, Piccata has only flour (no egg), and also adds capers into the sauce.  This high <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> dish has a creamy lemon sauce without tons of <a href='/misc/dairy#butter'>butter</a> or oil, and pair great with any <a href='/misc/fiber'>vegetables</a> and <a href='/misc/carbs'>complex carb</a> <a href='/recipes/sides'>side</a>
Instructions:
- Cut your meat into 6 large pieces. Cover with plastic wrap, and pound with a meat mallet until you have a thin piece of chicken. Repeat with all pieces

- On a large plate, mix together flour, oregano, black pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, and salt. Any flour will do, but today I opted for coconut. Lightly coat your pieces of chicken in flour, and set aside

- In a large pan, preheat 1 tsp (5 g) of oil over medium heat. When hot, add 2 pieces of chicken, and sear on both sides until browned. Cook until the internal temperature just reaches 165F, and set aside. Repeat in 2 more batches (1 tsp, 5 g of oil for each batch) for the remaining 4 pieces
- <center><img src='/assets/Food/Chicken/Piccata/chicken1.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'></center>
- <center><img src='/assets/Food/Chicken/Piccata/chicken2.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'></center>

- Reduce heat to low. Whisk together the sauce ingredients in a large glass, and add to the pan. Simmer and mix until thickened
- <center><img src='/assets/Food/Chicken/Piccata/sauce.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'></center>

- As the sauce simmers, slice your chicken on a bias. Add the chicken to a plate, and pour the sauce over the chicken. Optionally garnish with a slice of lemon, and serve

- Serve with a <a href='/recipes/sides'>side</a> of <a href='/recipes/roasted-vegetables'>roasted</a> or <a href='/recipes/vegetable-medley'>sauteed</a> veggies, such as <a href='/misc/veggies#spinach'>spinach</a>, <a href='/misc/veggies#broccoli'>broccoli</a>, <a href='/misc/veggies#cauliflower'>cauliflower</a>, or <a href='/misc/veggies#asparagus'>asparagus</a>. Also pair with healthy <a href='/misc/veggies#carbs'>starches</a>, such as a <a href='/recipes/baked-sweet-potato'>baked potato</a>, <a href='/recipes/rice-and-beans'>brown rice</a>, <a href='/recipes/red-lentils'>lentils</a>, or <a href='/recipes/couscous'>couscous</a>.  Today, I'm pairing my Chicken Piccata with <a href='/recipes/roasted-vegetables'>Roasted Brussel Sprouts</a> and <a href='/recipes/air-fried-trail-mix'>Air Fryer Trail Mix</a>!
