Description: Maafe is a West African <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a> <a href='/soup-stew'>stew</a> or <a href='/soup-stew'>soup</a>. This is my non-traditional take on it, trying to add more <a href='/misc/veggies'>vegetables</a> and cut back on some <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a>, since the <a href='/misc/nuts'>nuts</a> already add a lot. It's a great savory way of eating <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a>, and makes a large batch of food for not too much effort.  Use <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>Homemade Natural Nut Butter</a> to step this up even more.  You can also check out any of my other <a href='/soups-and-stews'>soups and stews</a>!
Instructions:
- Cut your <a href='/misc/veggies#onion'>onions</a> and <a href='/misc/veggies#pepper'>bell peppers</a> into a medium dice. Add to a <a href='https://amzn.to/3LNGdVy'>Dutch oven</a> or large pot with a <a href='https://amzn.to/3Hdg0gk'>spray of oil</a> and salt. Cover, and cook over medium heat until the <a href='/misc/veggies#pepper'>peppers</a> soften and the <a href='/misc/veggies#onion'>onions</a> turn translucent, about 10 minutes

- Meanwhile, cut the excess fat off your <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a>, and dice into bite sized cubes. Add to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>large bowl</a>. Season with <a href='/misc/fruit#lemon-juice'>lemon juice</a>, paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and black pepper. Mix, and let marinate on the counter as you prepare everything else. You could also marinate the <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> for a few hours in the fridge prior to cooking
- <img src='/assets/Food/Chicken/Maafe/chicken.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Add the <a href='/misc/veggies#garlic'>minced garlic</a> into the pan, and cook for only 30 seconds, until fragrant

- Stir in the tomato paste and spices - chili powder, paprika, <a href='/misc/veggies#ginger'>ginger</a>, cumin, and cayenne. Cover and let simmer over medium heat for 5 minutes

- In a bowl or glass, whisk together your broth using water and bouillon powder

- Pour in the broth, and stir in the <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a>. Add your raw <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> into the pot

- Cover, and let simmer over medium low heat for 30 minutes, until the <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> just reaches <a href='https://amzn.to/4kmobG2'>165F</a>. Be careful not to over cook it. Season with salt to taste, and optionally garnish with chopped <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanuts</a>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Chicken/Maafe/pot-2.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- I've never tried this, but my guess is that this would be great for a <a href='https://amzn.to/49TUS9E'>crockpot</a>. Do everything above the same, but instead of simmer for 30 minutes on the stove at the end, just cook on low in a <a href='https://amzn.to/49TUS9E'>slow cooker</a> for about 6 hours. I would definitely use <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-thighs'>chicken thighs</a> instead of <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>breasts</a> though
