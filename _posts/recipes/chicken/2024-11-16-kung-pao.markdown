Description: Here's my take on a healthy Kung Pao Chicken. It avoids directly adding in any <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar</a> or <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#honey'>honey</a>, and doesn't use <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugary sauces</a> like hoisin and sriracha. It's mildly sweet from the gochujang and <a href='https://amzn.to/3SqwsMO'>liquid monk fruit</a>, while being bulked up with <a href='/misc/veggies#yellow-squash'>yellow squash</a>. I'm using less <a href='/misc/olive-oil'>oil</a> than typical here, and I've paired it with a <a href='/recipes/sides'>side</a> of healthier <a href='/misc/grains#brown-rice'>brown rice</a> instead of <a href='/misc/grains#white-rice'>white rice</a>. Top it with chopped <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanuts</a>, and you're sure to love this twist on the classic dish.
Instructions:
- Add <a href='/misc/grains#brown-rice'>rice</a>, chicken bouillon, and water to a <a href='https://amzn.to/46ccg8m'>medium pot</a>. Cover and bring to a boil on high heat. Lower to medium, cover, and let simmer for about 30 minutes, until all water is absorbed
- <img src='/assets/Food/Chicken/Kung Pao/kung-pao-rice.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Meanwhile, heat a <a href='https://amzn.to/4rdR0HI'>large pan</a> medium heat with oil. Cut your <a href='/misc/veggies#yellow-squash'>yellow squash</a> into thin quarters, and add to the pot along with salt and black pepper. Cover and cook until the translucent and caramelized. Transfer to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>large bowl</a>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Chicken/Kung Pao/kung-pao-squash-raw.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Chicken/Kung Pao/kung-pao-squash-cooked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- As the <a href='/misc/veggies#yellow-squash'>yellow squash</a> cooks, finely chop your <a href='/misc/veggies#garlic'>garlic</a> and <a href='/misc/veggies#ginger'>ginger</a>, and set aside (<a href='/misc/veggies#garlic'>garlic</a> and <a href='/misc/veggies#ginger'>ginger</a> together). Finely chop the <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanuts</a>, and set aside (separate from the <a href='/misc/veggies#garlic'>garlic</a>/<a href='/misc/veggies#ginger'>ginger</a>). Dice your <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> into 1" cubes
- <img src='/assets/Food/Chicken/Kung Pao/kung-pao-chopped.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Chicken/Kung Pao/kung-pao-chicken-raw.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- When the <a href='/misc/veggies#yellow-squash'>yellow squash</a> is cooked and in the bowl, add the <a href='/misc/veggies#garlic'>garlic</a>, <a href='/misc/veggies#ginger'>ginger</a>, <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a>, and <a href='/misc/olive-oil'>oil</a> to the pan.  Cook over medium heat until <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> reaches <a href='https://amzn.to/4kmobG2'>165F</a>, and transfer to the bowl with the <a href='/misc/veggies#yellow-squash'>yellow squash</a>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Chicken/Kung Pao/kung-pao-chicken-cooked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Meanwhile, mix together your sauce in a medium glass - soy sauce, balsamic, white vinegar, gochujang, <a href='https://amzn.to/3SqwsMO'>liquid monk fruit</a>, and cornstarch.  Mix well; make sure these are no clumps of cornstarch or gochujang
- <img src='/assets/Food/Chicken/Kung Pao/kung-pao-sauce.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- When the <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> is cooked and the pan is empty, add the sauce to the pan over medium heat. Cook for 1 minute with constant stirring, until the sauce is syrupy, glossy, and thickened. Transfer to the bowl
- <img src='/assets/Food/Chicken/Kung Pao/kung-pao-syrup.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Mix together the <a href='/misc/veggies#yellow-squash'>yellow squash</a>, <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a>, and sauce. Add to a bowl with the <a href='/misc/grains#brown-rice'>rice</a> on the side, top with chopped <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanuts</a>, and serve
