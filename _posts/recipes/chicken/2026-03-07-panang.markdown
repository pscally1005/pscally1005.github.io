Instructions:
- Preheat a large pot over medium heat. Cut your <a href='/misc/veggies#onion'>onions</a> and <a href='/misc/veggies#pepper'>peppers</a> into a medium dice, and add to the pot with a splash of water. Cover and cook until the <a href='/misc/veggies#onion'>onions</a> are translucent and the <a href='/misc/veggies#pepper'>peppers</a> are soft, about 10 minutes

- Finely chop your <a href='/misc/veggies#ginger'>ginger</a>, and add to the pot along with <a href='/misc/veggies#garlic'>minced garlic</a>. Toast until fragrant, about 30 seconds

- Cut your <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> into a medium dice. Add the raw <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> to the pot along with <a href='/misc/dairy#coconut-milk'>coconut milk</a>, <a href='https://amzn.to/4sgDH9S'>powdered peanut butter</a>, soy sauce, <a href='https://amzn.to/3SqwsMO'>liquid monk fruit</a>, and spices. Cover and cook over medium low heat for 20 minutes, stirring occasionally, just until the <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> reaches <a href='https://amzn.to/4kmobG2'>165F</a>

- Remove the pot from the heat, and stir in <a href='/misc/fruit#lime-juice'>lime juice</a>. Divide into 6 servings

Notes:
- Optionally serve with a <a href='/recipes/sides'>side</a> of <a href='/misc/grains#brown-rice'>rice</a> (or <a href='/recipes/cauliflower-rice'>cauliflower rice</a>)

- Here I'm using 2 x 13.5 oz cans (810 g) of full fat <a href='/misc/dairy#coconut-milk'>coconut milk</a>, and 1 cup (96 g) of <a href='https://amzn.to/4sgDH9S'>powdered peanut butter</a>.  Alternatively, you can use 2 x 13.5 oz cans (810 g) of lite <a href='/misc/dairy#coconut-milk'>coconut milk</a> and 1/2 cup (128 g) <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>natural peanut butter</a> to get your <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a> from the <a href='/misc/nuts'>nuts</a> instead of the <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut</a>

- Or go full <a href='/misc/calories'>low calorie</a> by using lite <a href='/misc/dairy#coconut-milk'>coconut milk</a> and <a href='https://amzn.to/4sgDH9S'>powdered peanut butter</a>

- Or go full <a href='/misc/calories'>high calorie</a> by using <a href='/misc/fats'>full fat</a> <a href='/misc/dairy#coconut-milk'>coconut milk</a> and <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>natural peanut butter</a>
