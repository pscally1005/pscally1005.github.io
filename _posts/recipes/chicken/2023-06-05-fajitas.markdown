Description: Fajitas are a go to meal, as they're a fun way to build your own food at the table. We'll have some sauteed <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> and <a href='/misc/veggies#pepper'>peppers</a>, along with <a href='/misc/grains#brown-rice'>rice</a>, <a href='/misc/beans'>beans</a>, <a href='/misc/dairy#mozzarella'>cheese</a>, tortillas, <a href='/misc/veggies#lettuce'>lettuce</a>, and <a href='/recipes/tropical-salsa'>Tropical Fruit Salsa</a>. Make tacos, or make it into a bowl, and mix it up as you see fit.  Both the <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> and the <a href='/misc/veggies'>veggies</a> utilize a <a href='/recipes/italian-dressing'>Homemade Sugar Free Italian Dressing</a> in their marinades, in addition to <a href='/misc/fruit#lime-juice'>lime juice</a> and <a href='/recipes/hot-sauce'>hot sauce</a>.
Instructions:
- Cut your <a href='/misc/veggies#pepper'>peppers</a> and <a href='/misc/veggies#onion'>onions</a> into long thin strips, and add to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>large bowl</a>.  Mix with the marinade ingredients - <a href='/recipes/italian-dressing'>Italian dressing</a>, <a href='/recipes/hot-sauce'>hot sauce</a>, <a href='/misc/fruit#lime-juice'>lime juice</a>, and <a href='/misc/olive-oil'>olive oil</a>

- Cut your <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> into small, bite sized cubes, and add to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>large bowl</a>.  Mix with the marinade ingredients - <a href='/recipes/italian-dressing'>Italian dressing</a>, <a href='/recipes/hot-sauce'>hot sauce</a>, <a href='/misc/fruit#lime-juice'>lime juice</a>, <a href='/misc/olive-oil'>olive oil</a>, thyme, allspice, nutmeg, and cinnamon

- You can either cook the <a href='/misc/veggies'>vegetables</a> and <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> right away, or leave them in the fridge for a few hours to marinate

- Heat 2 <a href='https://amzn.to/4rdR0HI'>large pans</a> over medium heat.  Add the <a href='/misc/veggies#pepper'>peppers</a> & <a href='/misc/veggies#onion'>onions</a> to one pan, and the <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> to another, and cover both with a lid.  Cook the <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> to <a href='https://amzn.to/4kmobG2'>165F</a>, and the <a href='/misc/veggies'>veggies</a> until soft and lightly browned, about 10 minutes

- Serve with <a href='/recipes/sides'>sides</a> of <a href='/recipes/rice-and-beans'>Classic Rice and Beans</a>, <a href='/recipes/tortilla'>Oat-Wheat Tortillas</a>, and <a href='/recipes/tropical-salsa'>Tropical Fruit Salsa</a>
