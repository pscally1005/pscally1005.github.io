Description: Fajitas are a go to meal, as they're a fun way to build your own food at the table. We'll have some sauteed <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> and <a href='/misc/veggies#pepper'>peppers</a>, along with <a href='/misc/grains#brown-rice'>rice</a>, <a href='/misc/beans'>beans</a>, cheese, tortillas, <a href='/misc/veggies#lettuce'>lettuce</a>, and <a href='/recipes/tropical-salsa'>Tropical Fruit Salsa</a>. Make tacos, or make it into a bowl, and mix it up as you see fit.  Both the <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> and the <a href='/misc/veggies'>veggies</a> utilize a <a href='/recipes/italian-dressing'>Homemade Sugar Free Italian Dressing</a> in their marinades, in addition to lime juice and hot sauce
Instructions:
- Cut your peppers and onions into long thin strips, and add to a large bowl.  Mix with the marinade ingredients - <a href='/recipes/italian-dressing'>Italian dressing</a>, hot sauce, lime juice, and olive oil

- Cut your chicken into small, bite sized cubes, and add to a large bowl.  Mix with the marinade ingredients - <a href='/recipes/italian-dressing'>Italian dressing</a>, hot sauce, lime juice, olive oil, thyme, allspice, nutmeg, and cinnamon

- You can either cook the vegetables and chicken right away, or leave them in the fridge for a few hours to marinate

- Heat 2 large pans over medium heat.  Add the peppers & onions to one pan, and the chicken to another, and cover both with a lid.  Cook the chicken to 165F, and the veggies until soft and lightly browned, about 10 minutes

- Serve with sides of <a href='/recipes/rice-and-beans'>Classic Rice and Beans</a>, <a href='/recipes/tortilla'>Oat-Wheat Tortillas</a>, and <a href='/recipes/tropical-salsa'>Tropical Fruit Salsa</a>
