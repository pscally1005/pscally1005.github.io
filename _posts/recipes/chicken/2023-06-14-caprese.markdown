Description: A quick caprese <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> is one of my favorites, as I can simply bake the <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a>, and make a <a href='/misc/veggies#tomato'>tomato</a> and balsamic sauce while it's in the oven. The flavor combination of cooked <a href='/misc/veggies#tomato'>tomatoes</a> with balsamic vinegar is a staple in Italian cooking for a reason. Especially when topped with <a href='/misc/daory#mozzarella'>cheese</a>, this recipe makes a great main to a <a href='/recipes/sides'>side</a> of roasted <a href='/misc/veggies#artichoke'>artichokes</a>
Instructions:
- Drain and rinse your <a href='/misc/veggies#artichoke'>artichokes</a>, and add to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>large bowl</a>. Season (vinegar, <a href='/misc/veggies#garlic'>minced garlic</a>, <a href='/misc/fats'>oil</a>, basil, garlic powder, and lemon pepper), and transfer to an <a href='https://amzn.to/3YY2H9q'>9" square baking pan</a> lined with aluminum foil

- Roast at 400F for about 25-30 minutes, or until done to your liking
- <img src='/assets/Food/Chicken/Caprese/caprese-2.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- As the <a href='/misc/veggies#artichoke'>artichokes</a> cook, start on the <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a>. Cut up the <a href='/misc/veggies#tomato'>tomatoes</a>, and set aside. Cut the <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> into cutlets, about 3-4 per <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken breast</a>. Pound the <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> to even thickness if necessary

- In a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>large bowl</a>, combine the <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> with the seasonings (<a href='/misc/fats'>oil</a>, vinegar, basil, and salt). Mix with your hands, and place onto a <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>cookie sheet</a> lined with parchment paper. Bake for 12-14 minutes at 400F, or until just <a href='https://amzn.to/4kmobG2'>165F</a>, not any over

- Meanwhile, in a <a href='https://amzn.to/4rdR0HI'>large pan</a> over medium heat, add balsamic vinegar to the skillet, and add <a href='/misc/veggies#garlic'>garlic</a> and cook until fragrant (about 1 minute). Add in the <a href='/misc/veggies#tomato'>tomatoes</a> and basil, and cook until the <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> is done, stirring occasionally

- Return <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> to skillet and nestle with <a href='/misc/veggies#tomato'>tomatoes</a>. Top each piece of <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> with <a href='/misc/dairy#mozzarella'>cheese</a>, and cover the skillet to melt the <a href='/misc/dairy#mozzarella'>cheese</a>. Cover and cook for an additional minute until the <a href='/misc/dairy#mozzarella'>cheese</a> is melted, and serve
- <img src='/assets/Food/Chicken/Caprese/caprese-6.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
