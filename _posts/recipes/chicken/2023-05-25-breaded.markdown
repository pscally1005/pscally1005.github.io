Description: An Italian style breaded chicken that's both gluten free and not deep fried sounds like a dream come true. This dish is elevated even more when made into Chicken Parmesan. It goes well with a side of Bruschetta mix on top of a <a href="/recipes/whole-wheat-baguettes">Whole Wheat Baguette</a>
Instructions:
- Cut the chicken into cutlets. Make each piece even thickness by adding it to a bag and smashing it down to make it wide and thin. Sprinkle each side with salt. Refrigerate for at least 1 hour<br><br>

- Preheat your oven (or air fryer) to 400F and line a cookie sheet with parchment paper<br><br>

- On a plate, measure out the flour. Add the garlic and onion powder, black pepper, and oregano to the plate and mix<br><br>
- <center><img src="/assets/Food/Chicken/Breaded/flour-plate.jpg" alt="" class="instruction-image"></center><br>

- To a medium bowl, add in the egg and water, and beat the egg with a fork<br><br>
- <center><img src="/assets/Food/Chicken/Breaded/egg-bowl.jpg" alt="" class="instruction-image"></center><br>

- To a separate plate, add the breadcrumb mix - oat flour, grated cheese, thyme, basil, salt, and pepper<br><br>
- <center><img src="/assets/Food/Chicken/Breaded/bc-plate.jpg" alt="" class="instruction-image"></center><br>

- In order, coat the chicken in the flour, egg, and breadcrumb mixes. Add it to the pan<br><br>
- <center><img src="/assets/Food/Chicken/Breaded/raw.jpg" alt="" class="instruction-image"></center><br>

- Cook the chicken until it registers 165F, about 15 minutes in a 400F oven, or 8 minutes in a 400F air fryer.  Transfer to a wire rack to keep it crispy<br><br>
- <center><img src="/assets/Food/Chicken/Breaded/cooked.jpg" alt="" class="instruction-image"></center><br>

- Optional (Chicken Parm) - add sauce (about 1/2 cup or 120 g for the whole batch) and cheese (about 3/4 cup or 84 g for the whole batch) to the top of the chicken, and broil until the cheese is melted (about 2 minutes).  Note - DO NOT USE PARCHMENT PAPER FOR CHICKEN PARM. IT WILL BURN UNDER THE BROILER<br><br>

- As the chicken cooks, you can make the Bruschetta topping. Finely dice the tomatoes and onion, and add to a medium bowl. Season with garlic, lemon, parsley, basil, and salt.  Serve on a slice of toasted <a href="/recipes/whole-wheat-baguettes">Whole Wheat Baguettes</a>, or spoon on top of the chicken
