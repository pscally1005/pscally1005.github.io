Description: |
  On the rare chance I need to buy some <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> on the go, <a href='https://amzn.to/4joguO0'>Quest Bars</a> are normally what I reach for. They're convenient and tasty, but so damn expensive.  At $8.88 for a measly  box of 4, these can't be more than a once in a while treat. So today I'm making them myself for a third of the price and a fraction of the ingredients.
  <br><br>

  The result is a <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a> <a href='/protein-bar'>protein bar</a> with over 20 g of <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> in less than 200 <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a>, as well as healthy <a href='/misc/fats'>fats</a> from the <a href='/misc/nuts'>nuts</a> and no <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>added sugar</a>. These bars store well in the freezer long term, but they're also solid enough to hold their shape in your backpack, making them great for long days on the go!<br><br>

  If you enjoy <a href='/copycat'>copycat</a> <a href='/protein-bar'>protein bar recipes</a>, also see my <a href='/recipes/copycat-barebell'>Copycat Barebell Protein Bars</a> and <a href='/recipes/rx-bars'>Copycat Chocolate RX Bars</a>
Instructions:
- In a <a href='https://amzn.to/4q0AUjI'>food processor</a>, blend together all ingredients except the water - <a href='/misc/dairy#whey'>whey protein</a>, <a href='https://amzn.to/4sgDH9S'>powdered peanut butter</a>, <a href='https://amzn.to/4ceFnvP'>granulated sweetener</a>, cinnamon, salt, vanilla, and <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a>. It should be very dry and crumbly, but stay together slightly when pressed

- Thin out with water until you have a dough that you can mold in your hands, but not too wet where it's sticky
- <img src='/assets/Food/Protein Powder/Quest/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Line a small container with parchment paper (I'm using a <a href='https://amzn.to/3Ffts2R'>glass meal prep container</a>, which is <a href='https://amzn.to/3Ffts2R'>6.2 x 4.5"</a>). Evenly press the dough into all sides of the pan
- <img src='/assets/Food/Protein Powder/Quest/pan.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Refrigerate for at least 1 hour before slicing into 4 <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> bars. Wrap each in parchment paper, and store in an airtight bag in the freezer or refrigerator
- <img src='/assets/Food/Protein Powder/Quest/cut.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- Any kind of <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>natural nut butter</a> will work here.  Today I'm going with <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut</a>, but good options are <a href='/misc/nuts#almonds'>almond</a>, <a href='/misc/nuts#pistachios'>pistachio</a>, <a href='/misc/seeds#sesame-seeds'>tahini</a>, <a href='/misc/seeds#sunflower-seeds'>sunflower seed</a>, <a href='/misc/seeds#pumpkin-seeds'>pumpkin seed</a>, <a href='/misc/nuts#cashews'>cashew</a>, <a href='/misc/nuts#walnuts'>walnut</a>, etc.

- These bars are solid enough to hold their shape at room temperature, and purposely only use ingredients that don't need to be refrigerated. That way, you keep one in an airtight bag during the day for <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> on the go

- You can replace half of the <a href='https://amzn.to/4ceFnvP'>granulated monk fruit</a> with <a href='https://amzn.to/47w8h7R'>inulin</a> to lower the <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#sugar-substitutes'>sugar alcohols</a> and increase the <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a>, more matching the macros of a standard Quest bar.  <a href='https://amzn.to/47w8h7R'>Inulin</a> is a <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a> powder with 0 <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a> and a mildly sweet taste.  Use 2 tbsp (24 g) each of <a href='https://amzn.to/4ceFnvP'>granulated monk fruit</a> and <a href='https://amzn.to/47w8h7R'>inulin</a>
