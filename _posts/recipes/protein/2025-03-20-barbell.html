But Vic prefers Barbell Protein Bars, so today I'm making my own version. This protein bar has a chewy, nougat-like protein filling, coated with melted chocolate. We end up with a sugar free protein bar with 200 calories, 20 g of protein, and no junk ingredients you've never heard of. Plus, it's less half the price, just $0.91 per bar instead of $8.98 for a measly box of 4. If you enjoy copycat protein bar recipes, also see my Copycat Quest Protein Bars and Copycat Chocolate RX Bars Instructions: - In a medium bowl, mix together all dry ingredients for the filling - whey, granulated sweetener, coconut flour, cinnamon, and salt. Add in the vanilla, and thin out little by little with water until you have a sticky dough -
- Line a plate with parchment paper, and divide the dough into 4 even balls. Lightly wet your hands, and shape into bars. Freeze for 1.5 hours to harden -
- In a small bowl, microwave your coconut oil for about 1 minute, until melted. Add in the cocoa powder, and stir until fully combined. Optionally, add some sweetener - Coat the protein bars in chocolate, and add to a parchment lined plate. Freeze for 30 minutes to harden, wrap each bar in parchment paper, and store in an airtight bag in the fridge or freezer -
--- The following are all good variations I've tried of these protein bars:

  1. Homemade chocolate (as written above)
    • Mix melted coconut oil with cocoa powder to make your melted chocolate
    • Add any sweetener to taste; I like it unsweetened, but you may like 1 tbsp (21 g) honey

  2. Sugar free chocolate chips
    • Replace the coconut oil and cocoa powder with 1/3 cup (60 g) sugar free chocolate chips
    • Add 1/2 tsp (2.5 g) coconut oil or olive oil to the chocolate chips to help with melting
    • Microwave the chocolate chips in 20 second intervals, stirring in between, until fully melted

  3. Oat Flour
    • Replace coconut flour with oat flour (1:1)
    • Reduce water to 1/4 cup (60 g)

  4. Almond Flour
    • Replace coconut flour with allmond flour (1:1)
    • Reduce water to 1/4 cup (60 g)

  5. Candy Bar
    • Replace granulated monk fruit with granulated sugar (1:1)
    • Replace coconut oil and cocoa powder with 1/3 cup (60 g) semi sweet chocolate chips
    • Add 1/2 tsp (2.5 g) coconut oil or olive oil to the chocolate chips to help with melting
    • Microwave the chocolate chips in 20 second intervals, stirring in between, until fully melted

  6. Flavored Whey
    • Use a flavored whey protein powder in place of the unflavored. Vanilla is better but chocolate is good too
    • Omit the granulated monk fruit

  7. Liquid Monk Fruit
    • Use 1 tsp (5 g) of liquid monk fruit (or stevia) as your sweetener in place of the granulated monk fruit

  8. Honey
    • Use 4 tbsp (84 g) of honey (or maple syrup) as your sweetener in place of the granulated monk fruit
    • Reduce water to 1/4 cup (60 g)
    • Replace cococnut oil and cocoa powder with 1/3 cup (60 g) semi sweet chocolate chips
    • Add 1/2 tsp (2.5 g) coconut oil or olive oil to the chocolate chips to help with melting
    • Microwave the chocolate chips in 20 second intervals, stirring in between, until fully melted

  9. Sugar Free Syrup
    • Use 4 tbsp (80 g) of sugar free syrup as your sweetener in place of the granulated monk fruit
    • Reduce water to 1/4 cup (60 g)
