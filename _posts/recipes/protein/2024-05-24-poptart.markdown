Description: Vic got me a pair of PopTart socks, so now I'm legally obligated to make a healthy version myself (those are the rules). It's a <a href='/misc/celiac'>gluten free</a> crust made of coconut flour, <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oat flour</a>, and whey, with a homemade <a href='/recipes/apple-spread'>No Sugar Added Apple Spread</a> filling, giving you a toaster pastry you can actually eat for breakfast. I also drizzled some melted <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut</a> butter on top for healthy <a href='/misc/fats'>fats</a> and some more <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a>.  You can do everything the night before, and just toast/airfry the PopTart in the morning, making for a quick and easy breakfast with over 40 g of <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a>! For a different <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> pastry breakfast, see my <a href='/recipes/protein-cinnamon-rolls'>Fluffy Protein Cinnamon Rolls</a>
Instructions:
- In a bowl, combine together the crust ingredients (whey, syrup, cinnamon, salt, almond extract, yogurt, coconut flour, and oat flour).  You should have a slightly sticky dough.  Refrigerate for about 30 minutes for the oats to absorb the liquid

- Place the dough onto a piece of parchment paper, and lightly wet your hands.  Flatten to an even thin rectangle, and scoop a little bit of batter onto half of it, spreading evenly
- <img src='/assets/Food/Breakfast/PopTart/poptart-2.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- I've used <a href='/recipes/apple-spread'>No Sugar Added Apple Spread</a> for these PopTarts, but unsweetened applesauce would also work.  Any low or no <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar</a> jelly or jam (such as <a href='/recipes/berry-jam'>Low Sugar Berry Jam</a>) would also work well as a filling, as well as date paste

- Fold the PopTart over on itself.  Crimp all the edges together with a fork.  Poke the top a few times with a fork
- <img src='/assets/Food/Breakfast/PopTart/poptart-3.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Air fry at 350F for about 6 minutes, or until the PopTart is a golden brown color
- <img src='/assets/Food/Breakfast/PopTart/poptart-5.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Meanwhile, in a small bowl, melt peanut butter in the microwave.  Drizzle on top of the PopTart, and serve
