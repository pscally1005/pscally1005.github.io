Description: <a href='/vic'>Vic</a> got me a pair of PopTart socks, so now I'm legally obligated to make a healthy version myself (those are the rules). It's a <a href='/misc/celiac'>gluten free</a> crust made of <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut flour</a>, <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oat flour</a>, and <a href='/misc/dairy#whey'>whey</a>, with a homemade <a href='/recipes/apple-spread'>No Sugar Added Apple Spread</a> filling, giving you a toaster pastry you can actually eat for <a href='/recipes/breakfast'>breakfast</a>. I also drizzled some melted <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a> on top for <a href='/misc/fats'>healthy fats</a> and some more <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a>.  You can do everything the night before, and just toast/airfry the PopTart in the morning, making for a quick and easy <a href='/recipes/breakfast'>breakfast</a> with over 40 g of <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a>! For a different <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> pastry <a href='/recipes/breakfast'>breakfast</a>, see my <a href='/recipes/protein-cinnamon-rolls'>Fluffy Protein Cinnamon Rolls</a>.
Instructions:
- In a bowl, combine together the crust ingredients (<a href='/misc/dairy#whey'>whey</a>, <a href='/recipes/sugar-free-syrup'>syrup</a>, cinnamon, salt, almond extract, <a href='/misc/dairy#yogurt'>yogurt</a>, <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut flour</a>, and <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oat flour</a>).  You should have a slightly sticky dough.  Refrigerate for about 30 minutes for the <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oats</a> to absorb the liquid

- Place the dough onto a piece of parchment paper, and lightly wet your hands.  Flatten to an even thin rectangle, and scoop a little bit of batter onto half of it, spreading evenly
- <img src='/assets/Food/Breakfast/PopTart/poptart-2.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Fold the PopTart over on itself.  Crimp all the edges together with a fork.  Poke the top a few times with a fork
- <img src='/assets/Food/Breakfast/PopTart/poptart-3.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- <a href='https://amzn.to/3FuWETp'>Air fry</a> at 350F for about 6 minutes, or until the PopTart is a golden brown color
- <img src='/assets/Food/Breakfast/PopTart/poptart-5.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Meanwhile, in a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>small bowl</a>, melt <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a> in the microwave.  Drizzle on top of the PopTart, and serve
Notes:
- I've used <a href='/recipes/apple-spread'>No Sugar Added Apple Spread</a> for these PopTarts, but <a href='/misc/fruit#apple'>unsweetened applesauce</a> would also work.  Any low or <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>no sugar</a> jelly or jam (such as <a href='/recipes/berry-jam'>Low Sugar Berry Jam</a>) would also work well as a filling, as well as <a href='/misc/fruit#dates'>date</a> paste
