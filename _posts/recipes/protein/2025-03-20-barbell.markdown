Description: My go to <a href='/recipes/protein-bar'>protein bar</a> is a <a href='https://amzn.to/4joguO0'>Quest Bar</a>, which I've already <a href='/copycat'>recreated</a> to great success. But <a href='/vic'>Vic</a> prefers <a href='https://amzn.to/3HwsPlW'>Barebell Protein Bars</a>, so today I'm making my own version. This <a href='/recipes/protein-bar'>protein bar</a> has a chewy, nougat-like <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> filling, coated with melted chocolate. We end up with a <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar free</a> <a href='/recipes/protein-bar'>protein bar</a> with 200 <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a>, 20 g of <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a>, and no junk ingredients you've never heard of.  Plus, it's less half the price, just $0.92 per bar instead of $8.98 for a measly box of 4. If you enjoy <a href='/copycat'>copycat</a> <a href='/recipes/protein-bar'>protein bar</a> recipes, also see my <a href='/recipes/copycat-quest-bars'>Copycat Quest Protein Bars</a> and <a href='/recipes/rx-bars'>Copycat Chocolate RX Bars</a>.
Instructions:
- In a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>medium bowl</a>, mix together all dry ingredients for the filling - <a href='/misc/dairy#whey'>whey</a>, <a href='https://amzn.to/4ceFnvP'>granulated sweetener</a>, <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut flour</a>, cinnamon, and salt.  Add in the vanilla, and thin out little by little with water until you have a sticky dough
- <img src='/assets/Food/Protein Powder/Barbell/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Line a plate with parchment paper, and divide the dough into 4 even balls (see the table below for the weight of each filling).  Lightly wet your hands, and shape into bars
- <img src='/assets/Food/Protein Powder/Barbell/no-choc.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- In a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>small bowl</a>, microwave your <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut oil</a> for about 1 minute, until melted.  Add in the <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>cocoa powder</a>, and stir until fully combined

- Pour the chocolate over the protein bars, painting it over the top and the sides with a <a href='https://amzn.to/40F2aK0'>silicone spatula</a>.  Freeze for 30 minutes to harden, wrap each bar in parchment paper or aluminum foil, and store in an airtight bag in the fridge or freezer
- <img src='/assets/Food/Protein Powder/Barbell/choc.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- Optionally, add some sweetener to the chocolate.  I prefer it unsweetened, but you can add 1-2 tbsp of any <a href='https://amzn.to/4ceFnvP'>granular</a> (12-24 g) or <a href='/recipes/sugar-free-syrup'>liquid</a> (20-40 g) sweetener, or 1/2 tsp (2.5 g) <a href='https://amzn.to/44ZDhwi'>liquid monk fruit</a>

- Alternatively, you can make "kettlebells" or "bareballs", aka barebell protein bars, but as cookie dough balls instead.  Instead of melting chocolate over the top, fold chocolate chips into the filling batter, and roll into cookie dough bites.  I ended up with 24, meaning each serving of 6 <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> balls is equivalent to 1 <a href='/recipes/protein-bar'>protein bar</a>

  The following are all good variations I've tried of these protein bars:<br><br>
  <ol>
      <li><i>Original recipe with <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>unsweetened chocolate</a> (<a href='#recipe'>as written above</a>)</i>
        <ul>
          <li>Mix melted <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut oil</a> with <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>cocoa powder</a> to make your melted <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>chocolate</a></li>
          <li>I prefer it unsweetened, but you can add 1-2 tbsp of any <a href='https://amzn.to/4ceFnvP'>granular</a> (12-24 g) or <a href='/recipes/sugar-free-syrup'>liquid</a> (20-40 g) sweetener, or 1/2 tsp (2.5 g) <a href='https://amzn.to/44ZDhwi'>liquid monk fruit</a></li>
        </ul><br>

      <li><i><a href='/recipes/monkfruit-chocolate-chunks'>Sugar free chocolate chips</a></i>
        <ul>
          <li>Replace the <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut oil</a> and <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>cocoa powder</a> with 1/3 cup (60 g) <a href='/recipes/monkfruit-chocolate-chunks'>sugar free chocolate chips</a></li>
          <li>Add 1/2 tsp (2.5 g) <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut oil</a> or <a href='/misc/olive-oil'>olive oil</a> to the chocolate chips to help with melting</li>
          <li>Microwave the chocolate chips in 20 second intervals, stirring in between, until fully melted</li>
        </ul><br>

      <li><i><a href='/misc/grains#oats'>Oat Flour</a></i>
        <ul>
          <li>Replace <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut flour</a> with <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oat flour</a> (1:1)</li>
          <li>Reduce the water to 1/4 cup (60 g)</li>
        </ul><br>

      <li><i><a href='/misc/nuts#almonds'>Almond Flour</a></i>
        <ul>
          <li>Replace <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut flour</a> with <a href='/misc/nuts#almonds'>almond flour</a> (1:1)</li>
          <li>Reduce the water to 1/4 cup (60 g)</li>
        </ul><br>

      <li><i>Candy Bar</i>
        <ul>
          <li>Replace <a href='https://amzn.to/4ceFnvP'>granulated monk fruit</a> with granulated <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar</a> (1:1)</li>
          <li>Replace <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut oil</a> and <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>cocoa powder</a> with 1/3 cup (60 g) semi sweet chocolate chips</li>
          <li>Add 1/2 tsp (2.5 g) <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut oil</a> or <a href='/misc/olive-oil'>olive oil</a> to the chocolate chips to help with melting</li>
          <li>Microwave the chocolate chips in 20 second intervals, stirring in between, until fully melted</li>
        </ul><br>

      <li><i>Flavored <a href='/misc/dairy#whey'>Whey</a></i>
        <ul>
          <li>Use a flavored <a href='/misc/dairy#whey'>whey protein powder</a> in place of the unflavored.  Vanilla is better but <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>chocolate</a> is good too</li>
          <li>Omit the <a href='https://amzn.to/4ceFnvP'>granulated monk fruit</a></li>
        </ul><br>

      <li><i>Double Flavored <a href='/misc/dairy#whey'>Whey</a></i>
        <ul>
          <li>Use a flavored <a href='/misc/dairy#whey'>whey protein powder</a> in place of the unflavored.  Vanilla is better but <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>chocolate</a> is good too</li>
          <li>Omit the <a href='https://amzn.to/4ceFnvP'>granulated monk fruit</a></li>
          <li>Replace the <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>cocoa powder</a> in the coating with flavored <a href='/misc/dairy#whey'>whey protein powder</a>.  Use vanilla for a white <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>chocolate</a> coating, or <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>chocolate</a> for a milk <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>chocolate</a> coating</li>
        </ul><br>

      <li><i><a href='https://amzn.to/44ZDhwi'>Liquid Monk Fruit</a></i>
        <ul>
          <li>Use 1 tsp (5 g) of <a href='https://amzn.to/44ZDhwi'>liquid monk fruit</a> (or <a href='https://amzn.to/45wOzIv'>stevia</a>) as your sweetener in place of the <a href='https://amzn.to/4ceFnvP'>granulated monk fruit</a></li>
        </ul><br>

      <li><i><a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#honey'>Honey</a></i>
        <ul>
          <li>Use 4 tbsp (84 g) of <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#honey'>honey</a> (or <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#maple-syrup'>maple syrup</a>) as your sweetener in place of the <a href='https://amzn.to/4ceFnvP'>granulated monk fruit</a></li>
          <li>Reduce the water to 1/4 cup (60 g)</li>
          <li>Replace <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut oil</a> and <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>cocoa powder</a> with 1/3 cup (60 g) semi sweet chocolate chips</li>
          <li>Add 1/2 tsp (2.5 g) <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut oil</a> or <a href='/misc/olive-oil'>olive oil</a> to the chocolate chips to help with melting</li>
          <li>Microwave the chocolate chips in 20 second intervals, stirring in between, until fully melted</li>
        </ul><br>

      <li><i>Double <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#honey'>Honey</a></i>
        <ul>
          <li>Use 4 tbsp (84 g) of <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#honey'>honey</a> (or <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#maple-syrup'>maple syrup</a>) as your sweetener in place of the <a href='https://amzn.to/4ceFnvP'>granulated monk fruit</a></li>
          <li>Reduce the water to 1/4 cup (60 g)</li>
          <li>Still use <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut oil</a> and <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>cocoa powder</a>, but add 2 tbsp (42 g) <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#honey'>honey</a> (or <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#maple-syrup'>maple syrup</a>) to the melted <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>chocolate</a></li>
        </ul><br>

      <li><i><a href='/recipes/sugar-free-syrup'>Sugar Free Syrup</a></i>
        <ul>
          <li>Use 4 tbsp (80 g) of <a href='/recipes/sugar-free-syrup'>sugar free syrup</a> as your sweetener in place of the <a href='https://amzn.to/4ceFnvP'>granulated monk fruit</a></li>
          <li>Reduce the water to 1/4 cup (60 g)</li>
          <li>Still use <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut oil</a> and <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>cocoa powder</a>, but optionally add 2 tbsp (40 g) <a href='/recipes/sugar-free-syrup'>sugar free syrup</a> to the melted <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>chocolate</a> </li>
        </ul><br>

      <li><i>Reese's</i></li>
        <ul>
          <li>Replace the <a href='/misc/dairy#whey'>whey</a> in the filling with 1 cup (96 g) <a href='https://amzn.to/4sgDH9S'>powdered peanut butter</a></li>
          <li>If your <a href='https://amzn.to/4sgDH9S'>powdered peanut butter</a> is unsalted, then double the salt in the filling to 1/2 tsp (3 g)</li>
          <li>Increase the water to 10 tbsp (150 g)</li>
          <li>Keep the <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>chocolate</a> coating the same</li>
        </ul><br>

      <li><i>Double <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>Peanut Butter</a></i></li>
      <ul>
        <li>Replace the <a href='/misc/dairy#whey'>whey</a> in the filling with 1 cup (96 g) <a href='https://amzn.to/4sgDH9S'>powdered peanut butter</a></li>
        <li>If your <a href='https://amzn.to/4sgDH9S'>powdered peanut butter</a> is unsalted, then double the salt in the filling to 1/2 tsp (3 g)</li>
        <li>Increase the water to 10 tbsp (150 g)</li>
        <li>Replace the <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>cocoa powder</a> in the coating with <a href='https://amzn.to/4sgDH9S'>powdered peanut butter</a></li>
        <li>If your <a href='https://amzn.to/4sgDH9S'>powdered peanut butter</a> is unsalted, then add a 1/4 tsp (1.25 g) of salt to the <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a> coating</li>
      </ul><br>

      <li><i>White Chocolate Peanut Butter</i></li>
      <ul>
        <li>Replace the <a href='/misc/dairy#whey'>whey</a> in the filling with 1 cup (96 g) <a href='https://amzn.to/4sgDH9S'>powdered peanut butter</a></li>
        <li>If your <a href='https://amzn.to/4sgDH9S'>powdered peanut butter</a> is unsalted, then double the salt in the filling to 1/2 tsp (3 g)</li>
        <li>Increase the water to 10 tbsp (150 g)</li>
        <li>Replace the <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>cocoa powder</a> in the coating with vanilla <a href='/misc/dairy#whey'>whey protein powder</a>.  You can also use chocolate for a milk chocolate peanut butter <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> bar</li>
      </ul><br>

      <li><i><a href='/misc/dairy#casein'>Casein Protein Powder</a></i></li>
        <ul>
          <li>Replace the <a href='/misc/dairy#whey'>whey</a> in the filling with 3 scoops (90 g) of <a href='/misc/dairy#casein'>casein protein powder</a></li>
          <li>I'm using unflavored, but flavored will work too.  Like with the flavored <a href='/misc/dairy#whey'>whey</a>, omit the sweetener if using flavored <a href='/misc/dairy#casein'>casein</a></li>
          <li>Increase the water to 1.25 cup (300 g).  No that's not a typo</li>
        </ul><br>

      <li><i>Lower <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>Sugar</a></i></li>
        <ul>
          <li>This variation is for those who want less <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>added sugar</a> than the candy bar, <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#honey'>honey</a>, or double <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#honey'>honey</a> versions, but not <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar free</a> like the rest.  It's a good compromise of <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar free</a> flavored <a href='/misc/dairy#whey'>protein powder</a> and <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#honey'>honey</a> sweetened <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>chocolate</a></li>
          <li>Use a flavored <a href='/misc/dairy#whey'>whey protein powder</a> in place of the unflavored.  Vanilla is better but <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>chocolate</a> is good too</li>
          <li>Omit the <a href='https://amzn.to/4ceFnvP'>granulated monk fruit</a></li>
          <li>Still use <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut oil</a> and <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>cocoa powder</a>, but add 2 tbsp (42 g) <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#honey'>honey</a> and 1/2 tsp (2.5 g) <a href='https://amzn.to/3SqwsMO'>liquid monk fruit</a> to the melted <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>chocolate</a></li>
        </ul><br>
  </ol>
