Description: My go to protein bar is a <a href="https://www.walmart.com/ip/Quest-Protein-Bar-Chocolate-Chip-Cookie-Dough-21g-Protein-Gluten-Free-4-Count/52642271?classType=VARIANT&athbdg=L1600&from=/search">Quest Bar</a>, which I've already recreated to great success. But Vic prefers <a href="https://www.walmart.com/ip/Barebells-Protein-Bars-Cookies-Cream-4-Count-1-9oz-Bars-Protein-Snacks-with-20g-of-High-Protein/2388248165?classType=VARIANT&athbdg=L1600&from=/search">Barbell Protein Bars</a>, so today I'm making my own version. This protein bar has a sticky, nougat-like protein filling, coated with melted chocolate. We end up with a sugar free protein bar with 200 calories, 20 g of protein, and no junk ingredients you've never heard of.  Plus, it's less half the price, just $1.04 per bar instead of $8.98 for a measly box of 4. If you enjoy copycat protein bar recipes, also see my <a href="/recipes/copycat-quest-bars">Copycat Quest Protein Bars</a> and <a href="/recipes/rx-bars">Copycat Chocolate RX Bars</a>
Instructions: 
- In a medium bowl, mix together all dry ingredients for the filling - whey, coconut flour, sweetener, cinnamon, and salt

- Add in the vanilla, and thin out little by little water until you have a sticky dough
- <center><img src="/assets/Food/Protein Powder/Barbell/bowl.jpg" alt="" class="instruction-image"></center>

- Line a small container with parchment paper (I'm using a <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0C5ZZ32ZX?ref_=ppx_hzsearch_conn_dt_b_fed_asin_title_27">glass meal prep container</a>, which is 6.2 x 4.5"). Evenly press the dough into all sides of the pan.  Freeze for 30 minutes to harden
- <center><img src="/assets/Food/Protein Powder/Barbell/no-chocolate.jpg" alt="" class="instruction-image"></center>

- In a small bowl, microwave half your chocolate chips and coconut oil for 20 seconds, and stir until smooth. Spread on top of the protein bars, and freeze for anither 30 minutes
- <center><img src="/assets/Food/Protein Powder/Barbell/chocolate.jpg" alt="" class="instruction-image"></center>

- Flip over, and repeat with the other side, melting the other half of the chocolate on the bottom half

- Freeze for 30 minutes before slicing into 4 bars. Wrap each in parchment paper, and store in an airtight bag in the fridge or freezer
- <center><img src="/assets/Food/Protein Powder/Barbell/cut.jpg" alt="" class="instruction-image"></center>
