Description: My go to protein bar is a <a href="https://www.walmart.com/ip/Quest-Protein-Bar-Chocolate-Chip-Cookie-Dough-21g-Protein-Gluten-Free-4-Count/52642271?classType=VARIANT&athbdg=L1600&from=/search">Quest Bar</a>, which I've already recreated to great success. But Vic prefers <a href="https://www.walmart.com/ip/Barebells-Protein-Bars-Cookies-Cream-4-Count-1-9oz-Bars-Protein-Snacks-with-20g-of-High-Protein/2388248165?classType=VARIANT&athbdg=L1600&from=/search">Barbell Protein Bars</a>, so today I'm making my own version. This protein bar has a chewy, nougat-like protein filling, coated with melted chocolate. We end up with a sugar free protein bar with 200 calories, 20 g of protein, and no junk ingredients you've never heard of.  Plus, it's less half the price, just $1.04 per bar instead of $8.98 for a measly box of 4. If you enjoy copycat protein bar recipes, also see my <a href="/recipes/copycat-quest-bars">Copycat Quest Protein Bars</a> and <a href="/recipes/rx-bars">Copycat Chocolate RX Bars</a>
Instructions: 
- In a medium bowl, mix together all dry ingredients for the filling - whey, coconut flour, sweetener, cinnamon, and salt.  Add in the vanilla, and thin out little by little with water until you have a sticky dough
- <center><img src="/assets/Food/Protein Powder/Barbell/bowl.jpg" alt="" class="instruction-image"></center>

- Line a small container with parchment paper (I'm using a <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0C5ZZ32ZX?ref_=ppx_hzsearch_conn_dt_b_fed_asin_title_27">glass meal prep container</a>, which is 6.2 x 4.5"). Evenly press the dough into all sides of the pan.  Freeze for 2 hours to harden
- <center><img src="/assets/Food/Protein Powder/Barbell/no-chocolate.jpg" alt="" class="instruction-image"></center>

- Cut into 4 protein bars.  In a small bowl, microwave your chocolate chips and coconut oil for about 30 seconds, and stir until smooth. Coat the protein bars in chocolate, and add to a parchment lined plate.  Freeze for 30 minutes to harden, wrap each bar in parchment paper, and store in an airtight bag in the fridge or freezer
- <center><img src="/assets/Food/Protein Powder/Barbell/chocolate.jpg" alt="" class="instruction-image"></center>

- Oat or almond flour will also work in place of coconut flour.  Just cut down the water to 1/4 cup (60 g), and keep the flour the same.  Coconut flour absorbs much more water, so you will need less water if you swap it out

- Instead of sugar free chocolate chips, you can choose to make your own homemade chocolate with 2 tbsp (28 g) melted coconut oil and 6 tbsp (30 g) cocoa powder.  Add sweetener to taste; I'm good with 100% (unsweetened) chocolate, but you can add 1-2 tbsp of any sweetener you like

- If you don't mind all the added sugar, you can also make these with sugar and standard chocolate chips instead of monk fruit and sugar free chocolate chips for a higher protein candy bar
