Description: <a href='/misc/protein'>Protein</a> ice cream sandwiches are one of my favorite <a href='/recipes/healthier-dessert'>desserts</a> to make. They are 2 no bake <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> oat cookies, with a layer of <a href='/recipes/nice-cream'>Banana Nice Cream</a> in the middle. The ice cream is very versatile, so you can mix up the flavor by using other frozen <a href='/misc/fruit'>fruits</a> or extracts.
Instructions:
- In a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>large bowl</a>, mash your <a href='/misc/fruit#banana'>bananas</a> with a fork. Mix in the rest of the cookie ingredients - <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a>, vanilla, almond extract, <a href='https://amzn.to/3SqwsMO'>liquid monk fruit</a>, cinnamon, salt, <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>cocoa</a>, <a href='/misc/dairy#whey'>whey</a>, <a href='/misc/dairy#casein'>casein</a>, and <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>quick oats</a>. Chill in the fridge for the <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oats</a> to soak up the liquid, about 15 minutes

- Shape dough into balls (about 24 cookies, 28 g each), and place on a parchment lined <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>cookie sheet</a>

- Slightly wet your fingers, and flatten each cookie as thin as possible. It's okay if they touch a little bit, since they won't be baking. Freeze to fully harden, about 2 hours
- <img src='/assets/Food/Protein Powder/Nice Cream/nice-cream-3.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- In a <a href='https://amzn.to/4q0AUjI'>food processor</a>, combine the ice cream ingredients - frozen <a href='/misc/fruit#banana'>bananas</a>, frozen <a href='/misc/fruit'>fruit</a> (<a href='/misc/fruit#mangos'>mangos</a> or <a href='/misc/fruit#blueberries'>berries</a> work well), and <a href='https://amzn.to/4sgDH9S'>powdered peanut butter</a>. Process until you have an ice cream like texture
- <img src='/assets/Food/Protein Powder/Nice Cream/nice-cream-5.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Take 2 cookies and a scoop of ice cream (about 50 g). Make into a sandwich, and wrap in aluminum foil. Store ice cream sandwiches in the freezer. Makes 12 sandwiches. Work quickly, the cookies thaw fast since they're so thin. Put the cookies back in freezer if needed
