Description: Chickpea brownies, my original <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> based dessert, that I think I have refined to near perfection. These bars are a great <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> and <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a> packed snack to take on the go. They're much less sweet than traditional brownies, as they're more of a baked <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> bar, but I personally prefer the <a href='/misc/nuts#almonds'>almond</a> and <a href='/misc/fruit#banana'>banana</a> taste that these provide. Don't count out the <a href='/misc/beans'>beans</a> until you try them!  For more <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> packed <a href='/misc/beans#chickpeas'>chickpea</a> desserts, make the high <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> cookie dough <a href='/recipes/chickpea-chows'>Chickpea Chows</a>, or check out any of my other <a href='/brownies'>brownie recipes</a>!
Instructions:
- Drain and rinse your beans, and blend all ingredients in a food processor until fully combined and smooth
- <center><img src='/assets/Food/Protein Powder/Chickpea Brownie/chickpea-brownie-batter.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'></center>

- The following are some ingredients swaps that I've done, all to great success:<br><br><ul><li>Any kind of beans will work.  Normally I use chickpeas, but I've also made them with black, kidney, cannellini, and pinto beans with no difference</li><br><li>My canned beans are salted, but unsalted will work.  Just add another 1/8 tsp (0.75 g) salt to the batter</li><br><li>I've gone with a 50/50 blend (1 scoop, 30 g, each) of whey and casein, but either all casein or all whey (2 scoops, 60 g, total) will work as well</li><br><li>If you have flavored protein powder, either reduce or omit the honey.  Adjust the sweetness to your tastes</li><br><li>Any liquid sweetener will work in place of honey, like maple syrup, <a href='/recipes/sugar-free-syrup'>sugar free syrup</a>, or <a href='/misc/homemade-fruit-syrup'>Homemade Fruit Syrup</a></li><br><li>You can also use liquid stevia or monk fruit.  Replace the honey with about 2 tsp (10 g) of liquid monk fruit or stevia.  Again, taste the batter and ajust the sweetness to your personal preferences</li><br><li>Unsweetened applesauce, <a href='/recipes/apple-spread'>No Sugar Added Apple Spread</a>, <a href='/recipes/sweet-potato-puree'>Roasted Sweet Potato Puree</a>, <a href='/recipes/roasted-butternut-squash-puree'>Roasted Butternut Squash Puree</a>, or <a href='/recipes/pumpkin-puree'>Homemade Pumpkin Puree</a> will all work in place of ripe bananas</li><br><li>Feel free to use any <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>natural nut or seed butter</a> in place of peanut butter.  Good options are almond butter, cashew butter, tahini, or sunflower butter</li><br><li>I've used both immitation vanilla and almond extract, but if you use real extracts, you may want to cut the amounts in half</li><br></ul>

- Line a 9x13” baking pan with parchment paper and lightly spray it. Pour batter in, and even out. Bake for 50 minutes at 350F, covered, until a toothpick comes out almost clean
- <center><img src='/assets/Food/Protein Powder/Chickpea Brownie/chickpea-brownie-toothpick.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'></center>

- Move to the fridge and let cool for around an hour. Cut into slices and store in a ziploc bag or airtight container in either the fridge or the freezer
- <center><img src='/assets/Food/Protein Powder/Chickpea Brownie/chickpea-brownie-sliced.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'></center>

- Optionally, you can also make a frosting with the following ingredients and spread on top of the brownies, or use it to make a layer cake:<br><br><ol><li>1 cup (226 g) Nonfat cottage cheese</li><br><li>1/4 cup (60 g) Unsweetened applesauce</li><br><li>10 tbsp (60 g) Powdered peanut butter</li><br><li>1 tsp (5 g) Almond extract</li><br></ol>You can also use my either <a href='/recipes/banana-bread-hummus'>Banana Bread Hummus Spread</a> or <a href='/recipes/date-frosting'>Date Sweetened Frosting</a> as frostings too!

- For a layer cake, bake the batter in two 9" circle cake pans for about 40 minutes, covered with foil. Spread the frosting on the middle layer, the top, and down the sides. Dust the top with cocoa powder
- <center><img src='/assets/Food/Protein Powder/Chickpea Brownie/chickpea-cake.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'></center>
