Description: Today I'm making a spin off my <a href='/recipes/chili'>Slow Cooker Chili</a> in 2 ways. Firstly, I'm going with <a href='/misc/beans#brown-lentils'>lentils</a> instead of <a href='/misc/beans'>beans</a> to save someone from being a <a href='/vic'>gassy girl</a> at work. Secondly, I'm using a leftover block of <a href='/misc/beans#tofu'>tofu</a> in my fridge as my main <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> source instead of <a href='/misc/meat#ground-turkey'>turkey</a> or <a href='/misc/meat#ground-beef'>beef</a>, which makes this <a href='/recipes/chili'>chili</a> both <a href='/recipes/meatless'>vegetarian and vegan</a>.
Instructions:
- Add <a href='/misc/beans#brown-lentils'>lentils</a>, water, and <a href='https://amzn.to/3TbDFnv'>better than bouillon</a> to a medium pot. Cover and bring to a boil over high heat. Reduce heat to medium low, and let simmer (covered) for about 30 minutes, or until all the water has been absorbed. Transfer the cooked <a href='/misc/beans#brown-lentils'>lentils</a> to a <a href='https://amzn.to/49TUS9E'>large slow cooker</a>.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meatless/Lentil Chili/lentils.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Heat a <a href='https://amzn.to/4rdR0HI'>large pan</a> over medium heat with <a href='/misc/olive-oil'>oil</a>. When hot, add in the drained block of <a href='/misc/beans#tofu'>tofu</a>. Let it sear without touching it for about 5 minutes to develop browning before mashing with a <a href='https://amzn.to/3Fw6MeC'>wooden spoon</a>. Cook for an additional 5 minutes, or until the water has cooked off. Transfer the cooked <a href='/misc/beans#tofu'>tofu</a> to a <a href='https://amzn.to/49TUS9E'>large slow cooker</a>.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meatless/Lentil Chili/tofu.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Meanwhile, cut your <a href='/misc/veggies#pepper'>peppers</a> and <a href='/misc/veggies#onion'>onions</a> into a medium dice. Once the <a href='https://amzn.to/4rdR0HI'>pan</a> from the <a href='/misc/beans#tofu'>tofu</a> is empty, add in your <a href='/misc/olive-oil'>oil</a>, <a href='/misc/veggies#pepper'>peppers</a> and <a href='/misc/veggies#onion'>onions</a> with chili powder, cumin, and salt. Add a splash of water, cover, and cook with occasional stirring over medium heat until soft, about 10 minutes. Transfer the <a href='/misc/veggies'>veggies</a> to a <a href='https://amzn.to/49TUS9E'>large slow cooker</a>.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meatless/Lentil Chili/veggies.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- In your <a href='https://amzn.to/49TUS9E'>slow cooker</a>, add <a href='/misc/veggies#tomato'>crushed tomatoes</a>, <a href='/misc/veggies#tomato'>diced tomatoes</a>, <a href='/misc/veggies#garlic'>minced garlic</a>, and <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>cocoa powder</a>. Mix with the <a href='/misc/beans#brown-lentils'>lentils</a>, <a href='/misc/beans#tofu'>tofu</a>, and <a href='/misc/veggies'>veggies</a>.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meatless/Lentil Chili/before.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Cover and cook on low for 8 hours.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meatless/Lentil Chili/after.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meatless/Lentil Chili/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- Any <a href='/misc/bone-broth'>broth</a> can be substituted for water + <a href='https://amzn.to/3TbDFnv'>better than bouillon</a>.
