Description: If you love sundried <a href='/misc/veggies#tomato'>tomatoes</a> and <a href='/pesto'>pesto</a>, then you're sure to love this pasta salad. The base is <a href='/misc/celiac'>gluten free</a> <a href='/misc/beans#red-lentils'>red lentil pasta</a>, which is less <a href='/misc/diabetes'>glycemic</a>, higher in <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> and <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a>, and free of <a href='/misc/carbs'>refined starches</a>. Incidentally, the <a href='/salad-dressings'>dressing</a> is vegan and <a href='/misc/fats'>oil</a> free, as there's neither <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#dips'>mayo</a> nor <a href='/misc/fats'>oil</a> in the <a href='/salad-dressings'>dressing</a>. Instead, I'm utilizing <a href='/misc/fruit#avocado'>avocados</a> and <a href='/misc/nuts#pistachios'>pistachios</a> in the <a href='/salad-dressings'>dressing</a> for some whole food <a href='/misc/fats'>fats</a> that also bundle <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a>, <a href='/misc/nutrient-alphabet'>vitamins, and minerals</a>. Also check out my <a href='/recipes/mediterranean-pasta-salad'>Mediterranean Pasta Salad</a> and <a href='/recipes/mediterranean-potato-salad'>Mediterranean Sweet Potato Salad</a> recipes with a light oil and vinegar <a href='/salad-dressings'>dressing</a>, <a href='/misc/dairy#feta'>feta cheese</a>, roasted red <a href='/misc/veggies#pepper'>peppers</a>, and <a href='/misc/beans#chickpeas'>chickpeas</a>.
Instructions:
- Cook your <a href='/misc/grains#pasta'>pasta</a> according to its instructions. Drain, and set aside in a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>large bowl</a>

- Meanwhile, add your <a href='/misc/veggies#spinach-fresh'>frozen spinach</a> to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4qFE9y3'>small pan</a> over medium heat. Cook until lightly browned and most of the water has cooked off. If you don't mind the higher cost, replace the <a href='/misc/veggies#spinach-fresh'>frozen spinach</a> with either fresh <a href='/misc/veggies#spinach-fresh'>spinach</a> or fresh basil. You do not need to cook the fresh <a href='/misc/veggies#spinach-fresh'>spinach</a>/basil; only cook it if you're using <a href='/misc/veggies#spinach-fresh'>frozen spinach</a>

- Cut your <a href='/misc/veggies#tomato'>tomatoes</a> into a medium dice, finely dice an <a href='/misc/veggies#onion'>onion</a>, and halve your <a href='/misc/fruit#olives'>olives</a>. Add all 3 to the bowl with the <a href='/misc/grains#pasta'>pasta</a>, and mix. 1 pint (10 oz, 283 g) of <a href='/misc/veggies#tomato'>cherry tomatoes</a> (halved) will also work

- Add your sun dried tomatoes to the <a href='https://amzn.to/4q0AUjI'>food processor</a>, and pulse until roughly chopped. Add in the <a href='/misc/veggies#spinach-fresh'>spinach</a> and the rest of the dressing ingredients, and blend until well combined. Thin out with water as needed
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meatless/Vegan Pasta Salad/separate.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Add the <a href='/salad-dressings'>dressing</a> into the <a href='/misc/grains#pasta'>pasta</a> bowl, and mix until fully combined. Serve cold or warm
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meatless/Vegan Pasta Salad/no-sardines.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- To make it <a href='/misc/nuts'>nut</a> free, replace the <a href='/misc/nuts#pistachios'>pistachios</a> with <a href='/misc/seeds#pumpkin-seeds'>pumpkin seeds</a>

- If you don't have <a href='/misc/fruit#avocado'>avocado</a>, here's a substitution.  Microwave 1/4 cup + 2 tsp (70 g) water in a medium glass for a minute.  Add 1/4 cup (30 g) of <a href='/misc/nuts#pistachios'>pistachios</a> (or <a href='/misc/seeds#pumpkin-seeds'>pumpkin seeds</a>), and let soak for at least 10 minutes.  This will yield a similar creaminess and nutritional profile as the 1 medium (100 g) <a href='/misc/fruit#avocado'>avocado</a>

- For some <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> and <a href='/misc/nutrient-alphabet#O'>omega 3s</a>, I'm adding a can of <a href='/misc/fish#sardine'>sardines</a> onto my pasta salad. You can also go with any leftover cooked <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a>, <a href='/misc/meat#ground-beef'>beef</a>, <a href='/misc/meat#ground-turkey'>turkey</a>, or <a href='/misc/fish'>fish</a>
