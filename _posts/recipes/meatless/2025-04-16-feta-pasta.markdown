Description: Yes I know I'm probably 4 years late, but this viral <a href='/misc/dairy#feta'>feta</a> <a href='/misc/grains#pasta'>pasta</a> was making rounds all throughout the Internet. It features baked <a href='/misc/dairy#feta'>feta cheese</a> and <a href='/misc/veggies#tomato'>cherry tomatoes</a>, mashed together with cooked <a href='/misc/grains#pasta'>pasta</a> to make a creamy Mediterranean <a href='/misc/grains#pasta'>pasta</a> dish. This is my take on the recipe, making it a bit healthier than the original. You could make this a more complete meal by mixing in some shredded <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> or roasted <a href='/misc/veggies#broccoli'>broccoli</a>, but today I'm going to keep those <a href='/recipes/sides'>on the side</a> and have this dish as is
Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 350F. Wash your <a href='/misc/veggies#tomato'>tomatoes</a>, and add to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4aiCsjh'>9x13" baking dish</a>. Toss with oil, basil, oregano, black pepper, salt, and red pepper flakes.  Place the block of <a href='/misc/dairy#feta'>feta</a> in the center, with the <a href='/misc/veggies#tomato'>tomatoes</a> on the outside
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meatless/Feta pasta/raw.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Roast at 350F for about 45 minutes. The <a href='/misc/veggies#tomato'>tomatoes</a> should burst and the <a href='/misc/dairy#mozzarella'>cheese</a> should melt
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meatless/Feta pasta/cooked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Meanwhile, cook your <a href='/misc/grains#pasta'>pasta</a> in salted water according to the instructions.  When you drain the <a href='/misc/grains#pasta'>pasta</a>, reserve a little bit of the <a href='/misc/carbs'>starchy</a> water for emulsifying the sauce

- When the <a href='/misc/veggies#tomato'>tomatoes</a> and <a href='/misc/dairy#feta'>feta</a> are done, add in the <a href='/misc/veggies#garlic'>garlic</a> and <a href='/misc/fruit#lemon-juice'>lemon juice</a>. Mash the <a href='/misc/dairy#mozzarella'>cheese</a> and <a href='/misc/veggies#tomato'>tomatoes</a> with a <a href='https://amzn.to/3Fw6MeC'>wooden spoon</a>. Add in the <a href='/misc/grains#pasta'>pasta</a> with a splash of the water, and combine everything together
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meatless/Feta pasta/mixed.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- I'm using <a href='/misc/grains#whole-wheat'>whole wheat</a> rotini today, but any <a href='/misc/grains#pasta'>pasta</a> shape or variant will work, like <a href='/misc/beans#red-lentils'>red lentil</a>, <a href='/misc/beans#chickpeas'>chickpea</a>, or even <a href='/misc/veggies#spaghetti-squash'>spaghetti squash</a> or <a href='/misc/veggies#zucchini'>zoodles</a>

- You can prepare this ahead of time and heat it up when it's time for dinner.  Just mix everything together, cover, and refrigerate.  To reheat, bake straight from the fridge at 350F (covered with foil) for about 30 mintes, or until steamy

- Feel free to customize this recipe.  Below is a variation I made with <a href='/recipes/spaghetti-squash'>spaghetti squash</a> instead of <a href='/misc/grains#pasta'>pasta</a>.  I diced some full sized <a href='/misc/veggies#tomato'>tomatoes</a> instead of using <a href='/misc/veggies#tomato'>cherry tomatoes</a>, as large <a href='/misc/veggies#tomato'>tomatoes</a> are 1/3 of the price.  Because of the <a href='/misc/veggies#spaghetti-squash'>spaghetti squash</a>, this looks more like slop, but I honestly preferred this to the original
