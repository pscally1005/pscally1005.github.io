Description: Falafel is a <a href='/misc/beans#chickpeas'>chickpea</a> based <a href='/recipes/meatless'>meatless dish</a> originating from Egypt.  They can be a healthy vegan option, but are unfortunately often deep fried.  These falafel are either <a href='https://amzn.to/3FuWETp'>air fryer</a> or baked, simple and cheap to make, and full of flavor.  Nutrition facts are for each small falafel ball
Instructions:
- With a strainer, drain and rinse your can of <a href='/misc/beans#chickpeas'>chickpeas</a>. Add to a parchment lined <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>cookie sheet</a>, and roast at 400F for about 20-25 minutes, or until the <a href='/misc/beans#chickpeas'>chickpeas</a> are dry and slightly cracked
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meatless/Falafel/falafel-1.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Meanwhile, dice your <a href='/misc/veggies#onion'>onion</a>, and add to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4q0AUjI'>food processor</a>. Add in the roasted <a href='/misc/beans#chickpeas'>chickpeas</a>, and lightly pulse a full time to chop and combine. Don't over-blend, you want it to be a little bit coarse. Here is the texture you're looking for
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meatless/Falafel/falafel-2.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Add the rest of the ingredients to the <a href='https://amzn.to/4q0AUjI'>food processor</a>, and pulse to combine and roughly chop the cilantro. Be careful not to over-blend
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meatless/Falafel/falafel-3.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Using a cookie scoop (mine is 1 tbsp), scoop the batter into a parchment lined <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>cookie sheet</a>. Roll into balls (refrigerating for at least 30 minutes makes this step easier)
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meatless/Falafel/falafel-4.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- <a href='https://amzn.to/3FuWETp'>Air fry</a> at 375F for 12 minutes, flipping halfway. Alternatively, bake in a preheated 375F oven for 30 minutes, flipping halfway
Notes:
- I used some <a href='/recipes/sweet-potato-puree'>Roasted Sweet Potato Puree</a>, but either <a href='/recipes/pumpkin-puree'>Homemade Pumpkin Puree</a> or <a href='/recipes/roasted-butternut-squash-puree'>Roasted Butternut Squash Puree</a> will work.  Canned <a href='/recipes/pumpkin-puree'>pumpkin puree</a> will work as well

- Instead of <a href='/misc/nuts#almonds'>almond butter</a>, you can use <a href='/misc/seeds#sesame-seeds'>tahini</a>, <a href='/misc/nuts#pistachios'>pistachio butter</a>, or any other <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>natural nut or seed butter</a>

- Or 1 tbsp (15 g) extra virgin olive oil + 1 tbsp (15 g) aquafaba can be used in place of <a href='/misc/nuts#almonds'>almond butter</a>

- Fresh parsley can be used instead of cilantro as well

- Eat the falafel as is, put on a wrap with <a href='/hummus'>hummus</a> (check out my <a href='/recipes/red-lentil-wraps'>Red Lentil Wraps</a> and <a href='/recipes/sweet-potato-hummus'>Golden Sweet Potato Hummus</a>), or crumble over a <a href='/salad'>salad</a>
