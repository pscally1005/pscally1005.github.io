Description: The Blue Zones are 5 towns studied for longevity, as they have the highest rates of centenarians. One in particular, Nicoya, Costa Rica has a food combo they refer to as the "Three Sisters". By pairing together <a href='/misc/veggies#butternut-squash'>butternut squash</a>, <a href='/misc/beans'>beans</a>, and <a href='/misc/grains#corn'>corn</a>, you get a <a href='/misc/protein'>complete protein</a> with lots of <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a>, <a href='/misc/nutrient-alphabet#A'>vitamin A</a>, and <a href='/misc/nutrient-alphabet#F'>folate</a>. This can work as a larger bowl for a <a href='/recipes/meatless'>meatless meal</a>, or as a <a href='/recipes/sides'>side</a> to some <a href='/misc/veggies'>vegetables</a> and <a href='/misc/meat'>meat</a>.
Instructions:
- The night before, soak your dry <a href='/misc/beans'>beans</a> with 5 cups (1,200 g) water and 1 tbsp (18 g) salt. In the morning, discard the water, and add your <a href='/misc/beans'>beans</a> to a <a href='https://amzn.to/49TUS9E'>large slow cooker</a>. Add 1 tsp (6 g) salt and 3 cups (720 g) water. Cover with a lid, and cook on low for about 8 hours, or until the <a href='/misc/beans'>beans</a> are tender and the water is absorbed. This is just my <a href='/recipes/slow-cooked-beans'>Slow Cooker Dried Beans</a> Recipe
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meatless/3 Sisters/beans.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- With about an hour left on the <a href='/misc/beans'>beans</a>, you can prepare the <a href='/misc/veggies#butternut-squash'>butternut squash</a>. Preheat your oven to 400F, and line a large <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>cookie sheet</a> with parchment paper. Cut into a small dice, and add to the tray. Season with oil, <a href='/misc/veggies#garlic'>minced garlic</a>, garlic powder, onion powder, black pepper, and salt. Roast at 400F until fork tender and lightly browned, about 30 minutes
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meatless/3 Sisters/squash.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Lastly, we have the <a href='/misc/grains#corn'>corn</a>. Add frozen <a href='/misc/grains#corn'>corn</a> to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>large glass bowl</a>, and microwave for 5 minutes to thaw. Season with <a href='/misc/fruit#lime-juice'>lime juice</a>, cumin, oregano, and salt

- Finally, combine all 3 sisters together in a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>large bowl</a>, and serve.  Here I'm pairing the sisters with some roasted <a href='/misc/veggies#brussel-sprout'>brussel sprouts</a> and <a href='/misc/fish#salmon'>salmon</a>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meatless/3 Sisters/plate.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
