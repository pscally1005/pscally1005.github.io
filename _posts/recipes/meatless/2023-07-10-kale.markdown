Description: This vegetarian Mexican inspired dish mixes together sauteed <a href='/misc/veggies#kale'>kale</a>, <a href='/misc/beans#pinto-beans'>pinto beans</a>, <a href='/recipes/bbq-sauce'>Unsweetened BBQ Sauce</a>, and is topped with <a href='/misc/dairy#cheddar'>shredded cheese</a>. This goes well with some meatloaf or taco meat as well
Instructions:
- Cut the <a href='/misc/veggies#onion'>onions</a> into a small dice, and add to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4rdR0HI'>large pan</a> over medium heat with olive oil and <a href='/misc/veggies#kale'>kale</a>. Cook until the <a href='/misc/veggies#onion'>onions</a> are translucent

- Add the seasonings to the <a href='/misc/veggies'>veggies</a> - chili powder, cumin, paprika, and salt

- Add in the <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#dips'>BBQ sauce</a> (I used my <a href='/recipes/bbq-sauce'>Unsweetened BBQ Sauce</a>) and <a href='/misc/beans'>beans</a> to the skillet. Cook over medium heat for a few minutes. Top with <a href='/misc/dairy#cheddar'>shredded cheese</a>
