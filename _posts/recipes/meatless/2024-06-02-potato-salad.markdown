Description: This recipe is essentially the exact same as my <a href='/recipes/mediterranean-pasta-salad'>Mediterranean Pasta Salad</a>, but I've just swapped the <a href='/misc/beans#chickpeas'>chickpea pasta</a> for roasted <a href='/misc/veggies#sweet-potato'>sweet potatoes</a>. They're sweeter (duh) but more importantly cheaper than <a href='/misc/beans#chickpeas'>chickpea pasta</a>, but with less <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a>. You can't go wrong with either one!  The <a href='/dressing'>homemade salad dressing</a> is delicious as well.  You can also check out any of my other <a href='/salad'>salad recipes</a> or <a href='/salad-dressings'>salad dressings</a>
Instructions:
- Start with the <a href='/misc/veggies#potato'>potatoes</a> first, since they'll take the longest. Preheat your oven to 425F, and line a <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>cookie sheet</a> with parchment paper. Wash and cut your <a href='/misc/veggies#sweet-potato'>sweet potatoes</a> into small cubes, and add to the sheet. Drizzle with oil and add a pinch of salt. Roast for about 30 minutes, or until soft and lightly charred

- Now onto the <a href='/misc/veggies#pepper'>peppers</a>. Line a <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>cookie sheet</a> with parchment paper. Cut the <a href='/misc/veggies#pepper'>peppers</a> into long thin strips, and add to the sheet. Drizzle with oil and add a pinch of salt. Roast for about 25 minutes, or until soft and lightly charred. The <a href='/misc/veggies#potato'>potatoes</a> and <a href='/misc/veggies#pepper'>peppers</a> should finish around the same time. Alternatively, you can use a (drained and rinsed) jar of roasted red <a href='/misc/veggies#pepper'>peppers</a>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meatless/Potato Salad/potato-salad-2.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- As the <a href='/misc/veggies#potato'>potatoes</a> and <a href='/misc/veggies#pepper'>peppers</a> roast, prepare the rest of the <a href='/misc/veggies'>vegetables</a>. Drain and rinse your can of <a href='/misc/beans#chickpeas'>chickpeas</a>, and add to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>large bowl</a>. Cut up your <a href='/misc/veggies#tomato'>tomatoes</a> (small dice), <a href='/misc/veggies#cucumber'>cucumber</a> (thin quarters), and <a href='/misc/veggies#spinach-fresh'>spinach</a>/<a href='/misc/veggies#arugula'>arugula</a> (rough chop). 1 pint of halved <a href='/misc/fruit#cherry'>cherry</a> <a href='/misc/veggies#tomato'>tomatoes</a> would work as well. Crumble in the <a href='/misc/dairy#feta'>feta cheese</a>

- In a small dry pan over medium heat, add the <a href='/misc/nuts#pine-nuts'>pine nuts</a> and lightly toast for about 3 minutes, until fragrant and lightly browned.  Add to the bowl

- Finally, in a large glass, prepare the <a href='/dressing'>dressing</a>.  Pour in the <a href='/misc/apple-cider-vinegar'>apple cider vinegar</a>, <a href='/misc/fruit#lemon-juice'>lemon juice</a>, extra virgin olive oil, balsamic vinegar, Dijon mustard, and <a href='/misc/veggies#garlic'>minced garlic</a>.  Add in your spices (basil, thyme, oregano, garlic powder, onion powder, lemon pepper, and red pepper flakes).  Stir with a spoon

- Pour the <a href='/dressing'>dressing</a> over your <a href='/salad'>salad</a>, and serve either cold or warm
