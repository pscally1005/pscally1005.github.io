Description: I've been in love with <a href='/soups-and-stews'>soup</a> lately, and this <a href='/misc/veggies#broccoli'>broccoli</a> <a href='/misc/dairy#cheddar'>cheddar</a> <a href='/soups-and-stews'>soup</a> is hitting the spot in these cold winter months. I've removed the flour roux to keep it <a href='/misc/celiac'>gluten free</a>, as well as removed the cream to keep the <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a> in check. Instead, I'm making this <a href='/soups-and-stews'>soup</a> creamy with <a href='/misc/dairy#yogurt'>plain nonfat Greek yogurt</a>, which is high in <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a>, creamy, and tangy, just what a <a href='/soups-and-stews'>soup</a> needs. That in combination with the <a href='/misc/veggies#broccoli'>broccoli</a> and <a href='/misc/dairy#cheddar'>cheddar cheese</a> makes this a good vegetarian <a href='/soups-and-stews'>soup</a> with minimal prep
Instructions:
- Preheat a large pot over medium heat with olive oil. Cut your <a href='/misc/veggies#onion'>onions</a> and <a href='/misc/veggies#carrots'>carrots</a> into a small dice, and add to the pot. Cover, and cook until the <a href='/misc/veggies#carrots'>carrots</a> are just fork tender and the <a href='/misc/veggies#onion'>onions</a> are translucent, about 15 minutes

- Add <a href='/misc/veggies#garlic'>garlic</a> to the pan, and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds

- Add in the broth, mustard, spices, and (defrosted) <a href='/misc/veggies#broccoli'>broccoli</a>. Cover and bring to a boil over high heat. Reduce heat to medium, and let simmer (covered) for 10 minutes
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meatless/Broccoli Cheddar/chunky.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Remove from the heat, and stir in the <a href='/misc/dairy#yogurt'>yogurt</a> and <a href='/misc/dairy#mozzarella'>cheese</a>. Optionally puree with an <a href='https://amzn.to/3VHhgMM'>immersion blender</a> to desired consistency. You can leave it fully chunky, blend some of the <a href='/misc/veggies'>vegetables</a>, or puree it totally smooth. Season with salt to taste, and serve
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meatless/Broccoli Cheddar/puree.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- Instead of using preshredded <a href='/misc/dairy#mozzarella'>cheese</a>, I would recommend finding the sharpest block of <a href='/misc/dairy#cheddar'>cheddar cheese</a> and shredding it yourself.  Not only does fresh <a href='/misc/dairy#cheddar'>shredded cheese</a> taste way better, but extra sharp means a stronger flavor <a href='/misc/dairy#mozzarella'>cheese</a>, meaning you can get away with using less

- Note that my broth is the full <a href='/misc/sodium'>sodium</a> variety.  If yours is low sodium, you will need to add some salt, probably around 1/2 tbsp (9 g)

- Optionally mix in a few tablespoons of miso paste for some extra flavor

- <a href='/misc/dairy#cottage-cheese'>Nonfat cottage cheese</a> will work in place of <a href='/misc/dairy#yogurt'>plain nonfat greek yogurt</a>
