Description: These easy and healthy lasagna boats use <a href='/misc/veggies#spaghetti-squash'>spaghetti squash</a> as both your bowl and a noodle replacement for less <a href='/misc/carbs'>carbs</a> and more <a href='/misc/nutrient-alphabet'>micronutrients</a>.  Just roast a <a href='/misc/veggies#spaghetti-squash'>spaghetti squash</a>, mix with <a href='/recipes/pasta-sauce'>sauce</a> and <a href='/recipes/cashew-ricotta-cheese'>cheese</a>, and serve.  I'm going with my <a href='/recipes/pasta-sauce'>Simple Pasta Sauce</a> here, but feel free to use any homemade or store bought sauce.  I'm also opting for my <a href='/recipes/cashew-ricotta-cheese'>Dairy Free Cashew Ricotta Cheese</a> to keep it lactose free, but standard <a href='/misc/homemade-cheese#ricotta'>ricotta</a> would work too
Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 400F, and line a large <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>cookie sheet</a> with parchment paper

- Cut the <a href='/misc/veggies#spaghetti-squash'>spaghetti squash</a> in half lengthwise, and scoop out the seeds with a spoon. Season with salt, and add to your <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>baking sheet</a> cut side down. Prick the top with a fork a few times

- Roast the <a href='/misc/veggies#spaghetti-squash'>spaghetti squash</a> at 400F for about 1 hour, or until fork tender. Flip over, and let steam out for 5 minutes

- Fluff the <a href='/misc/veggies#spaghetti-squash'>spaghetti squash</a> with a fork, keeping it in the skin and the skin intact. Add half your sauce to each half of the <a href='/misc/veggies#spaghetti-squash'>spaghetti squash</a>, and mix until fully combined. Top each half with half the <a href='/misc/dairy#mozzarella'>cheese</a>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meatless/Lasagna Boats/squash.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meatless/Lasagna Boats/sauce.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Heat up your broiler, and swap out the parchment paper for aluminum foil. We used parchment earlier for less sticking when roasting the <a href='/misc/veggies#spaghetti-squash'>spaghetti squash</a>, but it will burn under the broiler, so you'll need foil now

- Broil the <a href='/misc/veggies#spaghetti-squash'>spaghetti squash</a> cut side up for about 5 minutes, or until the <a href='/misc/dairy#mozzarella'>cheese</a> is nicely browned. Keep a very close eye on it; the broiler can burn very quickly
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meatless/Lasagna Boats/broil.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Serve as 2 halves in the skin shell, and garnish with parsley
Notes:
- As mentioned above, you can either use my homemade <a href='/recipes/cashew-ricotta-cheese'>Dairy Free Cashew Ricotta Cheese</a> as your <a href='/misc/dairy#mozzarella'>cheese</a>, or just a standard <a href='/misc/homemade-cheese#ricotta'>ricotta cheese</a> (either store bought or homemade).
