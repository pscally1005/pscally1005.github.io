Description: <a href='/misc/beans#tofu'>Tofu</a> is both a good <a href='/misc/meat'>meat</a> and <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>egg</a> replacement for vegans and vegetarians, being a good cheap source of <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a>. This <a href='/misc/beans#tofu'>tofu</a> scramble can be used in any way you would <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>eggs</a>, like on <a href='/recipes/ww-bread'>toast</a> or mixed with <a href='/misc/veggies'>vegetables</a>.  Here, I've having it with a plate of <a href='/recipes/vegetable-medley'>Spiced Vegetable Medley</a> and a <a href='/recipes/sides'>side</a> of <a href='/recipes/slow-cooked-beans'>Slow Cooker Beans</a>.  Or you can use ground <a href='/misc/beans#tofu'>tofu</a> as a <a href='/misc/meat#ground-beef'>ground meat</a> replacement; I've made <a href='/recipes/chili'>Slow Cooker Chili</a> using ground <a href='/misc/beans#tofu'>tofu</a> before to great success
Instructions:
- <br><center><img src='/assets/Food/Meatless/Tofu Scramble/tofu-packet.jpg' alt='' class='larger-image'></center>
- In a large pan over medium, heat the oil with garlic until fragrant. Add your drained block of tofu, and mash with a wooden spoon
- <center><img src='/assets/Food/Meatless/Tofu Scramble/tofu-plain.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'></center>

- Season with soy sauce, nutritional yeast, and dried spices (oregano, chili powder, paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, cumin, black pepper, and turmeric)

- Cook uncovered until most of the water has evaporated and the tofu has started to brown. Mix in lemon juice, and serve
- <center><img src='/assets/Food/Meatless/Tofu Scramble/tofu-seasoned.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'></center>
