Description: I've previously posted a full in-depth post as to why your salads are lame, with a guide for building the perfect salad; check out <a href="/misc/salad">You're Doing Salad Wrong</a>.  But today, I decided to actually measure out and document the salad that I make all the time.  I make this large salad base at least twice a week, and use it as the basis of many of my meals. Just add this as a base to a bowl, and top it with whatever <a href="/misc/protien">protein</a>, <a href="/misc/fats">fat</a>, and <a href="/recipes/carbs">carbs</a> you desire for a delicious and nutritious meal
Instructions: 
- Thinly slice your lettuce, and add to a <a href="/misc/kitchen-products#salad-spinner">salad spinner</a>. Rinse in the sink, spin the salad, and drain out the water

- Wash your peppers and tomatoes, and cut into a medium dice. Add to the salad

- Crumble the feta cheese on top of the vegetables

- Add in your seasonings - minced garlic, lemon pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, and black pepper. Mix with tongs
- <center><img src="/assets/Food/Meatless/Salad/mixed.jpg" alt="" class="instruction-image"></center>

- Seasoning the vegetables makes them taste way better, but it also releases their water. Store the salad in the strainer part of the <a href="/misc/kitchen-products#salad-spinner">salad spinner</a>, with the strainer in the large bowl. When you go to eat the salad, just lift the strainer out of the bowl and discard the released water
