Description: Typical hot chocolate is <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>full of added sugars</a>, which is not what I'm about. The <a href='/misc/dairy#whey'>whey protein</a> here makes this like a warm protein shake, and it's sweetened with <a href='https://amzn.to/4ceFnvP'>granulated monk fruit</a> too. This is great after being outside in the snow for hours, and is a much healthier way to enjoy the classic <a href='/recipes/drinks'>drink</a>.  Or you can make my <a href='/recipes/unsweetened-hot-cocoa'>Unsweetened Hot Cocoa</a>.
Instructions:
- Add the dry ingredients to a <a href='https://amzn.to/46ccg8m'>small saucepot</a>, and whisk in the <a href='/misc/dairy#skim-milk'>milk</a>.

- Heat over medium, whisking frequently, until just boiling and creamy, about 5 minutes

- Remove from the heat, and whisk in vanilla and <a href='https://amzn.to/4khDgIM'>almond extract</a> (optional, but this is my preference)

- Serve immediately warm, or store it in the fridge for later as <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#milk'>chocolate milk</a>
Notes:
- You can also use 1/2 tsp (2.5 g) <a href='https://amzn.to/3SqwsMO'>liquid monk fruit</a> as your sweetener

- There may still be some small <a href='/misc/dairy#whey'>protein powder</a> clumps when you first try to mix in the <a href='/misc/dairy#skim-milk'>milk</a>, and that's okay.  They'll break down as the <a href='/misc/dairy#skim-milk'>milk</a> comes to a boil, and won't be present at the end
