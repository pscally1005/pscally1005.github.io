Description: |
  Chia water is exactly what it sounds like. It's an easy way to work some extra <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a> into your diet. <a href='/misc/fiber'>Fiber</a> promotes satiety, keeping you fuller for longer, as well as aiding in weight loss and reducing <a href='/misc/cholesterol'>cholesterol</a>.<br><br>

  Just let <a href='/misc/seeds#chia-seeds'>chia seeds</a> soak in water for about 10 minutes in order for the <a href='/misc/seeds#chia-seeds'>chia seeds</a> to gelatinize and thicken the water before drinking.  I also like to add some <a href='/misc/apple-cider-vinegar'>apple cider vinegar</a> for some flavor and additional health benefits.
Instructions:
- Add <a href='/misc/seeds#chia-seeds'>chia seeds</a>, <a href='/misc/apple-cider-vinegar'>apple cider vinegar</a>, and water to a large glass or <a href='https://amzn.to/4vedGsO'>mason jar</a>.

- Stir, and let sit for at least 10-15 minutes before drinking. You can also refrigerate overnight.
Notes:
- Optionally sweeten with <b>1 tsp (5 g) <a href='https://amzn.to/3SqwsMO'>liquid monk fruit</a></b>.

- It's normal for it to separate a little bit.  Just give it another shake before drinking.

- You can alternatively use <b>2 tbsp (13 g) <a href='/misc/seeds#flax-seeds'>ground flaxseed</a></b> instead of <a href='/misc/seeds#chia-seeds'>chia seeds</a>.  Again, wait at least 10-15 minutes before drinking.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Drinks/Chia water/flax.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Or you can use <b>1 tbsp (5 g) <a href='/misc/psyllium-husk'>psyllium husk</a></b>.  This time, you should consume it right away instead of letting it gel, as <a href='/misc/psyllium-husk'>psyllium</a> will thicken it too much and it will have a gross texture.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Drinks/Chia water/psyllium.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- You can also use <b>1 tsp (4 g) <a href='https://amzn.to/47w8h7R'>inulin</a></b> for a <a href='/misc/fiber'>high fiber</a> <a href='/recipes/drinks'>drink</a> that fully dissolves.  This feels just like slightly sweet water, and can be drunk either right away or refrigerated for later.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Drinks/Chia water/inulin.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Finally, <b>1 tsp (3 g) <a href='https://link.amazon/B04N3ngTi'>oat fiber</a></b> can also be used. Like the <a href='https://amzn.to/47w8h7R'>inulin</a>, this will also not thicken, and can either be consumed immediately or made ahead of time.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Drinks/Chia water/oat.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Because of the <a href='/misc/fiber'>high fiber</a> content of these <a href='/recipes/drinks'>drinks</a>, it's recommended to slowly work your way up.  I'd suggest starting with maybe half the amounts of <a href='/misc/seeds#chia-seeds'>chia</a>/<a href='/misc/seeds#flax-seeds'>flax</a>/<a href='/misc/psyllium-husk'>psyllium</a>/<a href='https://amzn.to/47w8h7R'>inulin</a>/<a href='https://link.amazon/B04N3ngTi'>oat fiber</a> in order to avoid any digestion issues.
