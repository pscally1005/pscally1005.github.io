Description: |
  Nothing is better than making <a href='/recipes/apple-spread'>apple spread</a> from fresh picked <a href='/misc/fruit#apple'>apples</a> in the fall. But that got me thinking: can I make apple cider using my homemade <a href='/recipes/apple-spread'>No Sugar Added Apple Spread</a>?<br><br>

  <a href='/recipes/apple-spread'>Apple spread</a> is just concentrated <a href='/misc/fruit#apple'>apples</a>, like <a href='/misc/fruit#apple'>applesauce</a> on crack. By reconstituting the <a href='/recipes/apple-spread'>apple spread</a> with water, you can make a gram for gram homemade apple cider that sweet and rich in apple cinnamon flavor.
Instructions:
- Whisk together <a href='/recipes/apple-spread'>apple spread</a> and water in a <a href='https://amzn.to/4zyFnzN'>large bowl</a>. Pour into a medium glass or mug.
