Description: Here's my simple formula for the perfect <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> packed <a href='/misc/fruit'>fruit</a> smoothie. I always use a <a href='/misc/fruit#banana'>banana</a>, and whatever frozen <a href='/misc/fruit'>fruit</a> I have in my kitchen at the time. The <a href='/misc/dairy#yogurt'>yogurt</a> and <a href='/misc/dairy#whey'>protein powder</a> help to make it a bit more filling as a meal that could hold you over until lunch. This also works well as a smoothie bowl, just use a frozen <a href='/misc/fruit#banana'>banana</a>.
Instructions:
- Blend all ingredients together in a <a href='https://amzn.to/4bO4VQ3'>blender</a>, and pour into a glass
Notes:
- Turn into a smoothie bowl by using a frozen <a href='/misc/fruit#banana'>banana</a> and leaving out the <a href='/misc/dairy#skim-milk'>milk</a>, and blending in a <a href='https://amzn.to/4q0AUjI'>food processor</a> instead.  Transfer to a bowl. Add any toppings if desired, such as <a href='/misc/nuts'>nuts</a> or <a href='/misc/seeds'>seeds</a>
