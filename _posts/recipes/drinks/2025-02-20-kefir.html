Emerging research is showing a new essential fatty acid known as Pentadecanoic Acid (C15), which is only found in dairy fat. With 9 g of fat, 11 g of protein, and no added sugar, this drink is highly satiating and great for your gut microbiome! Instructions: - In a medium glass, mix together about 3 tbsp (45 g) of your kefir with cocoa powder using a spoon, until all the cocoa has fully mixed in. Add in the rest of the kefir, as well as vanilla extract and liquid monk fruit to taste Notes: - Plain kefir comes in 2 different variations, whole milk (3.25% fat) and low fat (1% fat).
