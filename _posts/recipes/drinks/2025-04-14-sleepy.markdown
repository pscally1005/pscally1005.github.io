Description: Golden milk is a traditional Indian turmeric <a href='/misc/dairy#skim-milk'>milk</a> used for <a href='/misc/chronic-inflammation'>reducing inflammation</a>. The black pepper aids in activating the <a href='/misc/chronic-inflammation'>anti inflammatory</a> effects of turmeric, and my addition of <a href='/misc/magnesium'>magnesium</a> will relax your muscles and calm your mind.  Pair this with a <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> and <a href='/misc/veggies'>vegetable</a> as your <a href='/misc/carbs'>carb</a> <a href='/recipes/sides'>side</a> at dinner time, and you'll be sleeping like a baby.
Instructions:
- Break your <a href='/misc/fruit#banana'>banana</a> into chunks, and freeze overnight

- Blend your frozen <a href='/misc/fruit#banana'>banana</a> with the rest of the ingredients
Notes:
- Optionally, add a scoop of <a href='/misc/dairy#whey'>protein powder</a>. You can also use whatever <a href='/misc/dairy#skim-milk'>milk</a> you have on hand; today was <a href='/misc/nuts#almonds'>almond</a> for me
