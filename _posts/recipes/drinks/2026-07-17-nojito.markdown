Description: The "no" in this Nojito has a double meaning. Not only is it a mocktail, being alcohol free, but it's also <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar free</a>, being sweetened with <a href='https://amzn.to/3SqwsMO'>liquid monk fruit</a>. The combination of fresh mint and <a href='/misc/fruit#lime'>lime</a> makes a perfectly refreshing beverage for these hot summer months.
Instructions:
- Add your mint leaves and <a href='/misc/fruit#lime-juice'>lime juice</a> to a tall glass. Using the back of a <a href='https://amzn.to/3Fw6MeC'>wooden spoon</a>, crush together the mint and <a href='/misc/fruit#lime'>lime</a>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Drinks/Nojito/crush.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Add <a href='https://amzn.to/3SqwsMO'>liquid monk fruit</a>, ice, and water to the glass. Stir together, and garnish with a lime wedge
- <img src='/assets/Food/Drinks/Nojito/cover2.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- You can either use standard (flat) water or sparking water / club soda / seltzer

- |
  <div id='mojito' class='table-of-contents-2'></div>
  A standard mojito would have 2 oz (60 g) of rum, as well as use 1/2 oz (14 g) of simple syrup in place of <a href='https://amzn.to/3SqwsMO'>liquid monk fruit</a>.<br><br>

  Remember that alcohol has 7 <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a> per gram.  This amount of rum contains approximately 20 g of alcohol, meaning 140 <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a> from the rum alone, plus an additional ~20 <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a> from the simple syrup.
