Description: An <a href='/oatmeal'>overnight oats recipe</a> I developed for my girlfriend, which I made a little sweeter than my standard <a href='/oatmeal'>overnight oats</a>, but still with a good dose of <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a>, <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a>, and <a href='/misc/fats'>healthy fats</a> to start your morning off right.  For a variation without <a href='/misc/dairy#whey'>protein powder</a>, check out my <a href='/recipes/no-protein-powder-oatmeal'>Overnight Oats with no Protein Powder</a>.  Or you can check out any of my other <a href='/oatmeal'>oatmeal recipes</a>!
Instructions:
- In a small airtight container, add your <a href='/misc/fruit#banana'>banana</a>, and mash with the back of a fork.  Add in the rest of the ingredients, and stir together with a spoon until fully combined.  Store in the fridge overnight (or at least 2 hours)
Notes:
- |
  Some ingredient swaps include
  <ul>
    <li>Unsweetened applesauce (6 tbsp, 90 g) in place of mashed banana</li>
    <li><a href='/recipes/berry-jam'>Low Sugar Berry Jam</a> or <a href='/recipes/strawberry-chia-jam'>Strawberry Chia Jam</a> (1 tbsp, 20 g) instead of <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#honey'>honey</a> (1/2 tbsp, 10 g)</li>
    <li>Whey protein instead of casein (same amount by weight and volume, but it'll be less thick)</li>
    <li>Optionally, you can also add 1/4 tsp (1.25 g) <a href='https://amzn.to/3SqwsMO'>liquid monk fruit</a> or 1 tbsp (15 g) mini chocolate chips if you want it slightly sweeter</li>
  </ul>

- |
  You can turn this into baked oats by doing the following
  <ul>
    <li>Double the milk to 6 tbsp (90 g)</li>
    <li>Add 1/2 tsp (2.5 g) baking powder</li>
    <li>Lightly grease a mini muffin pan</li>
    <li>Bake at 350F for 14 minutes, or until a toothpick comes out nearly clean</li>
    <li>Makes 16 mini muffins</li>
  </ul>
