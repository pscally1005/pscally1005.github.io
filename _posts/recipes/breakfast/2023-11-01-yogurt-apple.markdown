Description: Plain nonfat <a href='/misc/dairy#yogurt'>greek yogurt</a> combined with whey protein powder makes a <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> packed bowl to start your day off right.  I've made 4 different variations of yogurt bowls that you can easily prep the night before for an easy breakfast. We have <a href='/recipes/yogurt-pbj'>PB&J</a>, Apple Pie, <a href='/recipes/yogurt-choc'>Chocolate</a>, and <a href='/recipes/yogurt-banana'>PB Banana</a> for you to enjoy.  You can also check out any of my other <a href='/yogurt'>yogurt recipes</a>!
Instructions: 
- Heat a small pan over medium heat.  Finely dice your apple, and add to the pan with a splash of water.  Cover and cook until soft and lightly browned.  Add your spices, toast until fragrant (about 30 seconds), and remove from the heat.  

- Mix all ingredients together in a bowl, and top with the cooked apple.  Cover, and refrigerate overnight
