Description: <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>Eggs</a> are like nature's <a href='/misc/nutrient-alphabet'>multivitamin</a>. They're a rich source of <a href='/misc/nutrient-alphabet#S'>selenium</a>, <a href='/misc/nutrient-alphabet#P'>vitamins B5</a> and <a href='/misc/vitamin-b12'>B12</a>, <a href='/misc/phosphorus'>phosphorus</a>, <a href='/misc/phytochemicals'>antioxidants</a>, <a href='/misc/choline'>choline</a>, and more. They're cheap and easy to prepare, and of course a <a href='/misc/protein'>complete protein</a>. The problem is that 1 <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>egg</a> only has about 6 g of <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> to 5 g of <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a>. The <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a> in the <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>egg yolks</a> is essential (the <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>yolk</a> is where all the <a href='/misc/nutrient-alphabet'>nutrients</a> come from), whereas the <a href='/misc/meat#egg-whites'>egg white</a> is basically just <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a>. So combining the 2 (<a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>whole eggs</a> and <a href='/misc/meat#egg-whites'>liquid egg whites</a>) is a great way to up the <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> content, while still keeping the healthful <a href='/misc/fats'>fats</a> and various <a href='/misc/nutrient-alphabet'>nutrients</a> you need. Combine that with <a href='/misc/veggies'>vegetables</a>, and you have a delicious and filling meal with <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a>, more <a href='/misc/nutrient-alphabet'>vitamins and minerals</a>, and <a href='/misc/carbs'>complex carbs</a> as well!
Instructions:
- Preheat a medium nonstick pan over medium heat with a <a href='https://amzn.to/3Hdg0gk'>spray</a> of <a href='/misc/olive-oil'>olive oil</a>. Cut your <a href='/misc/veggies#pepper'>pepper</a> into a medium dice, and add to the pan with lemon pepper. Mix, cover, and cook until softened and lightly charred (about 5 minutes)

- Dump in your <a href='/misc/veggies#tomato'>tomatoes</a> and add the spices (chili powder, paprika, garlic powder, and onion powder). Mix, cover, and let simmer until thickened, about 5 minutes. Remove the pan from the heat

- In a medium glass, crack your <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>eggs</a> and beat with a fork. Pour in the <a href='/misc/meat#egg-whites'>egg whites</a>, and mix to combine

- Dump the <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>eggs</a> into the pan, fully incorporate with the <a href='/misc/veggies'>vegetables</a>, and return the pan to the heat. Cook for just 1-2 minutes with frequent stirring, or until the <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>eggs</a> have reached a soft scramble

- Top with <a href='/recipes/hot-sauce'>hot sauce</a> and everything bagel seasoning (optional) and serve
Notes:
- You can mix up your <a href='/misc/veggies'>veggies</a> too.  Here, I've used 1/2 lb frozen chopped <a href='/misc/veggies#spinach-fresh'>spinach</a> in each bowl instead of the diced <a href='/misc/veggies#tomato'>tomatoes</a> and <a href='/misc/veggies#pepper'>bell peppers</a>.  You can also meal prep this as well by scaling it up!
