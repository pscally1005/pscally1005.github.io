Description: These delicious banana muffins are free of any <a href='/misc/fats'>oils</a>, <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>added sugars</a>, and <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#sugar-substitutes'>artificial sweeteners</a>. Just 4 simple ingredients - <a href='/misc/fruit#banana'>bananas</a>, <a href='/misc/veggies#sweet-potato'>sweet potatoes</a>, <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>eggs</a>, and <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>nut butter</a>. These are <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#muffins'>muffins</a> that are actually a <a href='/recipes/breakfast'>breakfast</a> and not a dessert, and can have any mix-ins you like, such as chopped <a href='/misc/nuts'>nuts</a>, <a href='/misc/fruit'>fruit</a>, or even <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>dark chocolate</a>.  The nutrition facts below are assuming just the 4 base ingredients; no mix-ins.
Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 375F and liberally <a href='https://amzn.to/3Hdg0gk'>spray</a> a <a href='https://amzn.to/3T1ymDy'>mini muffin tin</a> with oil

- In a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>large bowl</a>, add your cooked <a href='/misc/veggies#sweet-potato'>sweet potato</a> (without skin) and your ripe <a href='/misc/fruit#banana'>banana</a>. Mash with the back of a fork, or mix with a <a href='https://amzn.to/45yqsbM'>hand mixer</a>. You can also use a <a href='https://amzn.to/4q0AUjI'>food processor</a>

- Add in the <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>eggs</a> and <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>nut butter</a>, and mix until fully combined

- Transfer your batter to the <a href='https://amzn.to/4mzzEDl'>muffin pan</a>, and bake for about 16 minutes at 375F, or until a toothpick comes out clean.  Let cool in the pan for about 30 minutes, before transferring to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4qQNmn2'>wire rack</a> to cool.  Store in the fridge

Notes:
- Canned <a href='/misc/veggies#sweet-potato'>sweet potato</a> or <a href='/recipes/pumpkin-puree'>pumpkin puree</a> will work, but I prefer to use my homemade <a href='/recipes/sweet-potato-puree'>Roasted Sweet Potato Puree</a>.  I've never tried with <a href='/recipes/roasted-butternut-squash-puree'>Roasted Butternut Squash Puree</a>, but I don't see why that wouldn't be great as well

- You can also use <a href='/misc/fruit#apple'>unsweetened applesauce</a> or <a href='/recipes/apple-spread'>No Sugar Added Apple Spread</a> in place of mashed <a href='/misc/fruit#banana'>bananas</a>

- Any <a href='/misc/nuts'>nut</a> or <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>seed butter</a> will work; here I've used some <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>Homemade Natural Nut Butter</a> using a combination of <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanuts</a> and <a href='/misc/nuts#pistachios'>pistachios</a>, but <a href='/misc/nuts#almonds'>almond butter</a> or <a href='/misc/seeds#sunflower-seeds'>sunflower seed butter</a> would also work great

- Finally, to make it vegan, you can replace the <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>eggs</a> with <a href='/misc/seeds#flax-seeds'>flax</a> or <a href='/misc/seeds#chia-seeds'>chia eggs</a>, where 1 tbsp of <a href='/misc/seeds#flax-seeds'>flax</a> or <a href='/misc/seeds#chia-seeds'>chia seeds</a> + 3 tbsp water is equivalent to 1 <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>egg</a>.  Mix together in a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>small bowl</a> and let sit for 5 minutes to thicken before adding to the muffin batter

- Add in any additional ingredients if desired. I'll go with cinnamon and almond extract. I'm also going to top them with a few mini chocolate chips
