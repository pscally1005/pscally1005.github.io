Description: <a href="/misc/protein">Protein</a> <a href="/misc/grains#oats">oats</a> can can come in all shapes and sizes. This bigger bowl offers over 50 g of <a href="/misc/protein">protein</a> for 600 <a href="/misc/calories">calories</a>. It gets its <a href="/misc/protein">protein</a> from the <a href="/misc/dairy#yogurt">yogurt</a> and <a href="/misc/dairy#casein">casein</a>, healthy <a href="/misc/fats">fats</a> from the <a href="/misc/seeds#chia-seeds">chia seeds</a>, <a href="/misc/fiber">fiber</a> from the <a href="/misc/grains#oats">oats</a> and <a href="/misc/seeds#chia-seeds">chia</a>, and natural <a href="/misc/carbs">sweetness</a> from the <a href="/misc/fruit#banana">banana</a> and <a href="/misc/fruit#strawberries">strawberries</a>. A high <a href="/misc/protein">protein</a>, high <a href="/misc/fiber">fiber</a> breakfast with healthy <a href="/misc/fats">fats</a> and no <a href="/misc/hidden-sugar">added sugar</a> is the best way to start off your day, without the midmorning <a href="/misc/insulin-resistance">crash</a>.  You can also check out any of my other <a href="/oatmeal">oatmeal recipes</a>!
Instructions: 
-  In a medium bowl, mash your banana with the back of a fork until smooth. Combine with the rest of the ingredients (except the strawberries), and mix until creamy and fully combined

- Top with finely diced strawberries. Cover with a plate, and refrigerate overnight. Either eat cold for overnight oats, or microwave on high for 2 minutes (stirring halfway) to eat warm
