Description: <a href='/misc/protein'>Protein</a> <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oats</a> can can come in all shapes and sizes. This bigger bowl offers over 50 g of <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> for 600 <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a>. It gets its <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> from the <a href='/misc/dairy#yogurt'>yogurt</a> and <a href='/misc/dairy#casein'>casein</a>, <a href='/misc/fats'>healthy fats</a> from the <a href='/misc/seeds#chia-seeds'>chia seeds</a>, <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a> from the <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oats</a> and <a href='/misc/seeds#chia-seeds'>chia</a>, and natural sweetness from the <a href='/misc/fruit#banana'>banana</a> and <a href='/misc/fruit#strawberries'>strawberries</a>. A <a href='/misc/high-protein'>high protein</a>, <a href='/misc/fiber'>high fiber</a> <a href='/recipes/breakfast'>breakfast</a> with <a href='/misc/fats'>healthy fats</a> and no <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>added sugar</a> is the best way to start off your day, without the midmorning crash.  You can also check out any of my other <a href='/oatmeal'>oatmeal recipes</a>!
Instructions:
-  In a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>medium bowl</a>, mash your <a href='/misc/fruit#banana'>banana</a> with the back of a fork until smooth. Combine with the rest of the ingredients (except the <a href='/misc/fruit#strawberries'>strawberries</a>), and mix until creamy and fully combined

- Top with finely diced <a href='/misc/fruit#strawberries'>strawberries</a>. Cover with a plate, and refrigerate overnight. Either eat cold for <a href='/oatmeal'>overnight oats</a>, or microwave on high for 2 minutes (stirring halfway) to eat warm
