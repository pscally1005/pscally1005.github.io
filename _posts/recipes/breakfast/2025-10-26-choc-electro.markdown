Description: |
  It's time to start meal prepping your <a href='/recipes/breakfast'>breakfasts</a>!  Prep this Sunday night, and you have <a href='/recipes/breakfast'>breakfast</a> every morning Monday through Thursday.  <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>Peanut butter</a>, <a href='/misc/fruit#banana'>banana</a>, and chocolate are a classic combination for a reason, and these provide healthy <a href='/misc/fats'>fats</a>, whole food <a href='/misc/carbs'>carbs</a>, and <a href='/misc/phytochemicals'>antioxidants</a>. Additionally, the <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> from the <a href='/misc/dairy#casein'>casein</a> aids in satiety and managing <a href='/misc/diabetes'>blood sugar spikes</a>.
  <br><br>
  I've also fueled up these bowls with electrolytes, namely <a href='/misc/sodium'>sodium</a>, <a href='/misc/potassium'>potassium</a>, and <a href='/misc/magnesium'>magnesium</a>.  <a href='/misc/sodium'>Sodium</a> aids in fluid balance, nerve function, and hydration.  <a href='/misc/potassium'>Potassium</a> supports digestion, electrolyte balance, and prevention of muscle cramps.  <a href='/misc/magnesium'>Magnesium</a> is correlated with better <a href='/misc/sleep'>sleep</a>, lower stress, and reduced <a href='/misc/chronic-inflammation'>inflammation</a>.  Due to the <a href='/misc/dairy#casein'>casein protein powder</a>, these bowls are also a significant source of <a href='/misc/calcium'>calcium</a>, benefitting bone health and regular heart beat.
  <br><br>
  Finally, I've added a scoop of <a href='/misc/creatine'>creatine</a> into each <a href='/recipes/breakfast'>breakfast</a> bowl.  Typically found in red <a href='/misc/meat'>meat</a> and <a href='/misc/fish'>fish</a>, <a href='/misc/creatine'>creatine</a> is a nutrient that most people are under consuming, not just vegans and vegetarians.  <a href='/misc/creatine'>Creatine</a> leads to increased production and storage of ATP, aiding in not just muscle growth and recovery, but also improving memory and benefitting healthy brain aging!
Instructions:
- Preheat your <a href='https://amzn.to/3FuWETp'>air fryer</a> to 400F, and roast your unsalted <a href='/misc/nuts'>nuts</a> until lightly browned. About 5 minutes if the <a href='/misc/nuts'>nuts</a> are already roasted, and 10 minutes if they're raw

- Transfer the <a href='/misc/nuts'>nuts</a> to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4q0AUjI'>food processor</a>, and blend for about 5 minutes, until you have a super smooth <a href='/misc/natural-peanut-butter'>nut butter</a>. Add in the salt and <a href='/misc/potassium'>potassium</a> chloride, and blend for another 30 seconds to incorporate

- Store leftovers in the fridge for up to 4 days, or in the freezer indefinitely
Notes:
- I've gone with <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanuts</a> here, but any <a href='/misc/nuts'>nuts</a> or <a href='/misc/seeds'>seeds</a> will work, such as <a href='/misc/nuts#almonds'>almonds</a>, <a href='/misc/nuts#brazil-nuts'>Brazil nuts</a>, <a href='/misc/nuts#cashews'>cashews</a>, <a href='/misc/nuts#hazelnuts'>hazelnuts</a>, <a href='/misc/nuts#macadamia-nuts'>macadamia nuts</a>, <a href='/misc/nuts#pecans'>pecans</a>, <a href='/misc/nuts#pistachios'>pistachios</a>, <a href='/misc/seeds#pumpkin-seeds'>pumpkin seeds</a>, <a href='/misc/seeds#sesame-seeds'>sesame seeds</a>, <a href='/misc/seeds#sunflower-seeds'>sunflower seeds</a>, or <a href='/misc/nuts#walnuts'>walnuts</a>

- |
  Prepare 4 airtight containers. Evenly divide your ingredients 4 ways across the containers. That means that each bowl gets the following
  <ul>
    <li>1/2 medium (55 g) <a href='/misc/fruit#banana'>Banana</a>, mashed</li>
    <li>1/4 cup (64 g) <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>Natural peanut butter</a></li>
    <li>2 tbsp (10 g) Cocoa powder</li>
    <li>1 scoop (30 g) <a href='/misc/dairy#casein'>Casein protein powder</a>, unflavored</li>
    <li>1 scoop (5 g) <a href='/misc/creatine'>Creatine monohydrate</a></li>
    <li>1 scoop (1.8 g) <a href='/misc/magnesium'>Magnesium glycinate</a></li>
    <li>1/2 tsp (1.5 g) Cinnamon, ground</li>
    <li>1/4 tsp (0.5 g) Ginger, ground</li>
    <li>1/2 tsp (2.5 g) Vanilla extract</li>
    <li>1/4 tsp (1.25 g) Liquid monk fruit</li>
    <li>1 tbsp (15 g) Balsamic vinegar</li>
    <li>1/2 cup (120 g) Water</li>
  </ul>

- Why vinegar? The slight fruity sourness provides a great flavor contrast to the chocolate and <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a> that <a href='/misc/dairy#yogurt'>yogurt</a> would normally provide.  However, I've used <a href='/misc/dairy#casein'>casein protein powder</a> as the sole <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> source here instead of <a href='/misc/dairy#yogurt'>Greek yogurt</a>, so the vinegar helps round out the flavor.  Good options include balsamic, red wine, or <a href='/misc/apple-cider-vinegar'>apple cider vinegar</a>
