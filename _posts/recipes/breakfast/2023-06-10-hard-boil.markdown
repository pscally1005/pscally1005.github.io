Description: Hard boiled <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>eggs</a> are a quick snack or <a href='/recipes/breakfast'>breakfast</a>, and they are also a quick <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> source. Great when chopped up on a <a href='/salad'>salad</a>, these boiled <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>eggs</a> can be made either on the stove, or in the <a href='/misc/kitchen-products#air-fryer'>air fryer</a> (my preferred way)
Instructions:
- Place eggs in a large pot and cover with water, about an inch over the top of the eggs. Bring to a boil, and let boil for 2 minutes. Reduce heat a simmer, and simmer for 12 minutes, uncovered

- You could also air fry the eggs at 250F for 15 minutes (this is what I prefer)

- When done, add to a strainer and rinse with cold water until the eggs are cold to the touch
