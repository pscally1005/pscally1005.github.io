Description: A frittata is the perfect meal for leftover <a href='/misc/veggies'>veggies</a>.  I'm making fresh <a href='/misc/veggies#spinach-fresh'>spinach</a> and <a href='/misc/veggies#onion'>onion</a> here, but you can really just throw in any leftovers you want.  Each slice of this delicious <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>egg</a> bake has 11 g of <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> for a little over 100 <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a>, and is quick to throw together.  Just combine cooked <a href='/misc/veggies'>vegetables</a>, <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>eggs</a>, and spices, and bake.  Let rest, then slice!
Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 375F, and dice 2 <a href='/misc/veggies#onion'>onions</a>.  Add <a href='/misc/veggies#onion'>onions</a> and <a href='/misc/veggies#spinach-fresh'>spinach</a> to a <a href='https://amzn.to/465aAxx'>10" cast iron pan</a> over medium high heat with <a href='/misc/olive-oil'>olive oil</a>

- Cover with foil, and cook until the water from the <a href='/misc/veggies#spinach-fresh'>spinach</a> has evaporated and the <a href='/misc/veggies#onion'>onions</a> are translucent.  Mix in the <a href='/recipes/salsa'>salsa</a>, <a href='/recipes/hot-sauce'>hot sauce</a>, <a href='/misc/veggies#garlic'>minced garlic</a>, and dried spices (garlic powder, onion powder, chili powder, paprika, and black pepper).  Remove from the heat
- <img src='/assets/Food/Breakfast/Frittata/frittata-veggies.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Meanwhile, crack your <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>eggs</a> into a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>medium bowl</a>, and add the <a href='/misc/dairy#cottage-cheese'>cottage cheese</a>.  Whisk together until combined
- <img src='/assets/Food/Breakfast/Frittata/frittata-eggs.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Pour in the <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>egg</a> mixture, and mix with a <a href='https://amzn.to/40F2aK0'>silicone spatula</a> until the <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>eggs</a> and <a href='/misc/veggies'>veggies</a> are fully combined. This will take a few minutes of gentle mixing
- <img src='/assets/Food/Breakfast/Frittata/frittata-raw.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Bake in the oven for about 30 minutes.  The frittata should be set in the center and golden around the edges (at least <a href='https://amzn.to/4kmobG2'>160F</a>).  Optionally, broil the frittata for about 2 minutes to brown the top
- <img src='/assets/Food/Breakfast/Frittata/frittata-cooked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Let the frittata rest for at least 15 minutes before slicing
- <img src='/assets/Food/Breakfast/Frittata/frittata-cut.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
