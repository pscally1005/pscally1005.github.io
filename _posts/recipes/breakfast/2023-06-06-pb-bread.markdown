Description: This Peanut Butter Bread is inspired by the 1932 Great Depression Peanut Butter Bread. It's full of <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a> flavor, without being too sweet. It's perfect for <a href='/recipes/breakfast'>breakfast</a>, and goes well with some <a href='/misc/dairy#yogurt'>yogurt</a>, <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a>, or <a href='/recipes/berry-jam'>jam</a> on top of it. I cut down the <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar</a> and replaced it with <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#honey'>honey</a>, as well as swapped to <a href='/misc/grains#whole-wheat'>whole wheat flour</a>. I'm sure you'll enjoy this healthier quick <a href='/recipes/bread'>bread</a>.
Instructions:
- In a <a href='https://amzn.to/4zyFnzN'>large bowl</a>, combine together the flour, salt, and baking powder with a whisk

- Add in the <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a> and <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#honey'>honey</a>, and combine with your fingers. It should have the consistency of wet sand

- Finally, using a <a href='https://amzn.to/40F2aK0'>spatula</a>, mix in the <a href='/misc/dairy#almond-milk'>almond milk</a>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Breakfast/PB Bread/pb-bread-3.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Add to a greased <a href='https://amzn.to/3YUjIkN'>loaf pan</a>, and score down the center with a butter knife or your <a href='https://amzn.to/40F2aK0'>spatula</a>

- Bake at 325F for about an hour. A toothpick should come out clean. Allow to cool before turning onto a <a href='https://amzn.to/4qQNmn2'>cooling rack</a>.  Only slice when fully cooled
