Description: Most of the time, I just throw together <a href='/oatmeal>oatmeal</a> ingredients in a container, refrigerate overnight, and eat it cold the next day. Occasionally I'll do this in a bowl instead, and microwave it to eat warm. But if you want to level your <a href='/oatmeal'>oatmeal</a> up to the next level, consider making them on the stove. It's more work, but it's worth it if you have the time in the morning. This variation has over 40 g of <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a>, <a href='/misc/fats'>healthy fats</a> from the <a href='/misc/nuts'>nuts</a>, and no <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>added sugar</a>.  You can also check out any of my other <a href='/oatmeal'>oatmeal recipes</a>!
Instructions:
- Dice an <a href='/misc/fruit#apple'>apple</a>, and add to a <a href='https://amzn.to/46ccg8m'>medium pot</a> with cinnamon, nutmeg, <a href='/misc/veggies#ginger'>ginger</a>, cloves, and a splash of water. Cover and cook over medium heat until soft and syrupy

- Add in the <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oats</a> and toast until they smell nutty. Toasting the <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oats</a> brings out even more flavor

- Pour in the <a href='/misc/dairy#skim-milk'>milk</a>, and let simmer for about 3 minutes with ocassional stirring

- Remove from the heat and mix in the rest of the ingredients, and transfer to a bowl.  Serve now warm, or refrigerate overnight and serve the next day cold.
