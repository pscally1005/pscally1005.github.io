Description: I got used to eating a bit less at meals, <a href='/recipes/breakfast'>breakfast</a> included, when I got injured, as I was exercising a lot less. But I've been able to get back into running recently, meaning I need to eat a bit more in the morning. It's got a good source of <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> (<a href='/misc/dairy#yogurt'>yogurt</a> and <a href='/misc/dairy#whey'>protein powder</a>), <a href='/misc/fats'>healthy fats</a> (<a href='/misc/overshadowed-healthy-foods#chocolate'>dark chocolate</a> and <a href='/misc/nuts'>nuts</a>), and <a href='/misc/carbs'>complex carbs</a> (<a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oats</a> and <a href='/misc/fruit#banana'>banana</a>) to power my run a few hours later.  You can also check out any of my other <a href='/oatmeal'>oatmeal recipes</a>!
Instructions:
- In a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>medium bowl</a>, mash half your <a href='/misc/fruit#banana'>banana</a> with the back of a fork. Mix the rest of the ingredients into the bowl, and top with the other half of the <a href='/misc/fruit#banana'>banana</a>, chopped <a href='/misc/nuts'>nuts</a>, and chopped chocolate
Notes:
- You can make this <a href='/oatmeal'>oatmeal</a> the night before and eat them as <a href='/oatmeal'>overnight oats</a>, but I'm going to eat this warm, in order to melt the chocolate and make it creamy. Cover with a plate, and microwave on high for 2-3 minutes
