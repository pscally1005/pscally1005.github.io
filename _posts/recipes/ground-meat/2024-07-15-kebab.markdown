Description: These simple kebabs are made with <a href='/misc/meat#ground-turkey'>ground turkey</a> (either <a href='/misc/meat#ground-beef'>beef</a> or <a href='/misc/meat#lamb'>lamb</a> will work as well), and they can be cooked inside on a pan or grilled outdoors. The simple combination of <a href='/misc/fats'>lean</a> <a href='/misc/meat'>meats</a> and spices makes this a super quick and delicious summer main that pairs well with some <a href='/recipes/salmon-and-tzatziki'>tzatziki</a>. Nutrition facts are for a serving size of 2 skewers/patties.
Instructions:
- In a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>small bowl</a>, whisk together the spices - garlic powder, onion powder, allspice, black pepper, turmeric, oregano, cinnamon, nutmeg, salt, and cloves

- In a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>large bowl</a>, combine together the spice mix, finely diced <a href='/misc/veggies#onion'>onion</a>, fresh dill, and <a href='/misc/meat#ground-beef'>ground meat</a>.  Optionally, you can sautee the <a href='/misc/veggies#onion'>onion</a> with a pinch of salt until translucent for extra flavor

- Divide the <a href='/misc/meat'>meat</a> into 8 pieces, about 3 oz (85 g) each. Either make 8 patties for cooking inside, or 8 skewers for grilling.  You can also use skewers for inside cooking too if you have a big enough pan. Brush or <a href='https://amzn.to/3Hdg0gk'>spray</a> the outside of the <a href='/misc/meat'>meat</a> with <a href='/misc/olive-oil'>olive oil</a>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Ground Meat/Kebab/kebab-raw.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Stovetop - heat a large skillet to medium high heat, and cook the patties on each side for about 5 minutes, or until cooked through (at least <a href='https://amzn.to/4kmobG2'>165F</a> for <a href='/misc/meat#ground-turkey'>turkey</a>)
- <img src='/assets/Food/Ground Meat/Kebab/kebab-pan.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Grill - heat a grill to medium heat, and cook the skewers for about 5 minutes per side, or until cooked through (at least <a href='https://amzn.to/4kmobG2'>165F</a> for <a href='/misc/meat#ground-turkey'>turkey</a>)
