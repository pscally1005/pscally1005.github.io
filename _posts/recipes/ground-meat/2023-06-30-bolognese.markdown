Description: My Bolognese recipe is a sauce that can honestly work as a meal on it own. It goes great over some <a href='/misc/grains#pasta'>pasta</a>, <a href='/misc/veggies#zucchini'>zucchini noodles</a>, or <a href='/misc/veggies#spaghetti-squash'>spaghetti squash</a>.  Try it out with <a href='/recipes/meatballs'>Zoodles and Meatballs</a> or <a href='/recipes/spaghetti-squash'>Spaghetti Squash Cooked 3 Ways</a>. It also serves as a great <a href='/soups-and-stews'>stew</a> between the <a href='/misc/meat'>meat</a> and all the <a href='/misc/veggies'>vegetables</a>.  You can also check out any of my other <a href='/soups-and-stews'>soups and stews</a>!
Instructions:
- Add <a href='/misc/veggies'>vegetables</a> (<a href='/misc/veggies#onion'>onion</a>, <a href='/misc/veggies#garlic'>garlic</a>, <a href='/misc/veggies#carrots'>carrots</a>, and <a href='/misc/veggies#pepper'>peppers</a>) to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4q0AUjI'>food processor</a> and pulse until fine

- <a href='https://amzn.to/3Hdg0gk'>Spray</a> a <a href='https://amzn.to/3LNGdVy'>dutch oven</a> with oil, and cook the <a href='/misc/veggies'>vegetables</a> until soft over medium high heat until water has boiled off. Stir constantly. Remove to a bowl

- Over medium high, add the <a href='/misc/meat'>meat</a> into the pan and mash with a <a href='https://amzn.to/3Fw6MeC'>wooden spoon</a>, mixing with the <a href='/misc/veggies'>vegetables</a> until <a href='/misc/meat'>meat</a> is cooked and no pink remains. Stir constantly, and cook until water is evaporated and pan goes from hissing to cracking (this is the sound of the <a href='/misc/meat'>meat</a> frying in it's own rendered <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a>)

- Add the <a href='/misc/veggies#tomato'>tomato</a> puree and mix until fully combined. Pour in water. Scrape up the fond with your <a href='https://amzn.to/3Fw6MeC'>wooden spoon</a>. Mix back in the <a href='/misc/veggies'>vegetables</a>. Add the spices (bouillon powder, oregano, basil, parsley, themy, garlic powder, and black pepper) and balsamic vinegar

- Bring to a boil on high, then cover and let simmer simmer (reduce heat to medium-low).  Let cook for 30 minutes - 1 hour, stirring every 15 minutes
