Description: Chili is such a classic cold weather hearty meal, and this version comes together pretty quickly and makes a lot of food. Mix in some elbow noodles or <a href='/misc/grains#brown-rice'>rice</a> if you want to bulk it up even more. Nothing beats the smell of coming home and smelling fresh chili just waiting for you for dinner.  Chili pairs so well with <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#cornbread'>cornbread</a>, so check out my <a href='/recipes/cornbread'>Cheesy Protein Cornbread</a> for a <a href='/misc/high-protein'>high protein</a> and delicious <a href='/recipes/sides'>side dish</a>!  You can also check out any of my other <a href='/soups-and-stews'>soups and stews</a>!
Instructions:
- Chop up the <a href='/misc/veggies'>vegetables</a>, and add them to a large skillet with olive oil, <a href='/misc/veggies#garlic'>garlic</a>, and salt. Cook over medium heat (covered) until soft. Set <a href='/misc/veggies'>vegetables</a> aside in <a href='https://amzn.to/49TUS9E'>crock pot</a>

- Meanwhile, drain and rinse the cans of <a href='/misc/beans'>beans</a>. Transfer all the <a href='/misc/beans'>beans</a>, <a href='/misc/overshadowed-healthy-foods#chocolate'>cocoa</a>, and <a href='/misc/veggies#tomato'>tomatoes</a> to the <a href='https://amzn.to/49TUS9E'>crock pot</a>

- Add in the <a href='/misc/meat'>meat</a> and cook until no longer pink over medium heat, and transfer to <a href='https://amzn.to/49TUS9E'>crockpot</a>

- Cover and cook for 8 hours on low or 6 hours on high
