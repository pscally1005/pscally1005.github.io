Description: <a href='/misc/veggies#cabbage'>Shredded cabbage</a> makes such a good base for a meal, just like <a href='/misc/grains#brown-rice'>rice</a> or noodles would. These bowls are like a deconstructed version of an egg roll, and they're packed with <a href='/misc/veggies'>vegetables</a> and feature a flavorful sauce.
Instructions:
- You can either buy a bags of <a href='/misc/veggies#cabbage'>shredded cabbage</a> and <a href='/misc/veggies#carrots'>carrots</a>, or shred them yourself.  I like to cut them into big chunks, pulse them in my <a href='https://amzn.to/4q0AUjI'>food processor</a>, and use that.  It's cheaper and simpler, but you don't get the strips that a mandolin would get you

- Heat a large wok over medium to medium high heat. Add the <a href='/misc/veggies#cabbage'>cabbage</a>, <a href='/misc/veggies#carrots'>carrots</a>, a pinch of salt, and a drizzle of olive oil

- Cover the wok with aluminum foil and cook the <a href='/misc/veggies'>vegetables</a> until softened to desired texture
- <img src='/assets/Food/Ground Meat/Egg Roll/egg-roll-2.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Meanwhile, in a <a href='https://amzn.to/4rdR0HI'>large pan</a> or wok, brown the <a href='/misc/meat'>meat</a> with the spices (onion and garlic powder, chili powder, cumin, <a href='/misc/veggies#ginger'>ginger</a>, black pepper, and salt) over medium heat. Mix in soy sauce and gochujang at the end
- <img src='/assets/Food/Ground Meat/Egg Roll/egg-roll-3.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Mix together the sauce in a bowl or glass. Optionally add some <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a>

- Pour the sauce onto the cooked <a href='/misc/veggies'>vegetables</a>. Mix in the <a href='/misc/meat'>meat</a>, season to taste with <a href='/misc/fruit#lemon'>lemon</a>, soy sauce, and salt, and serve

- This can also work as a great filling for dumplings. Lightly wet the edges of the wrapper with water, place a small spoonful on the inside, and seal it shut, crimping the edges. Steam in a pan over medium heat until soft, or <a href='https://amzn.to/3FuWETp'>air fry</a> at 400F until crispy
- <img src='/assets/Food/Ground Meat/Egg Roll/egg-roll-7.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
