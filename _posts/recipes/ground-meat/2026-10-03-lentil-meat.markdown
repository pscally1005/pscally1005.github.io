Description: |
  I've been seeing a lot of recipes pop up lately for adding <a href='/misc/beans#brown-lentils'>lentils</a> to your <a href='/misc/meat'>meat</a>, helping to stretch it out. feed more people, and save money. Truly a <a href='/recipes/recession-beans'>recession</a> indicator.<br><br>

  But it's a great idea, allowing you to add roughly 25% more servings, as well as some <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a>, <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a>, <a href='/misc/iron'>iron</a>, and <a href='/misc/nutrient-alphabet#F'>folate</a>. With <a href='/misc/meat#ground-beef'>beef</a> prices these days, it's damn near necessary to stretch that out as much as possible. So today, I'm making a <a href='/recipes/taco'>taco meat</a> by mixing <a href='/misc/meat#ground-beef'>ground beef</a> with cooked <a href='/misc/beans#brown-lentils'>lentils</a> and taco seasoning.
Instructions:
- Add <a href='/misc/beans#brown-lentils'>lentils</a>, water, and salt to a <a href='https://amzn.to/46ccg8m'>small pot</a>, and cover with a lid. Bring to boil over high heat, then reduce heat to low. Simmer with the cover on for about 25 minutes, or until the <a href='/misc/beans#brown-lentils'>lentils</a> are tender and the water has been absorbed.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Ground Meat/Lentil meat/Lentil/lentils.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Meanwhile, heat a <a href='https://amzn.to/4rdR0HI'>large pan</a> over medium heat with <a href='/misc/olive-oil'>olive oil</a>. Cut an <a href='/misc/veggies#onion'>onion</a> into a small dice. When the <a href='/misc/olive-oil'>oil</a> is hot, add in the <a href='/misc/veggies#onion'>onion</a>, and cook until it starts to brown and turn translucent, about 3-5 minutes.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Ground Meat/Lentil meat/onions.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- |
  Add in the <a href='/misc/meat'>meat</a>, and press it down to make sure as much of the <a href='/misc/meat'>meat</a> is touching the pan. Let it sear for about 5 minutes without touching it to develop good browning, until fragrant.<br><br>

  Mash with a <a href='https://amzn.to/3Fw6MeC'>wooden spoon</a>, and continue cooking until no longer pink.<br><br>

  Make a hole in the center, and add the <a href='/misc/veggies#garlic'>garlic</a> to the pan. Cook for about 30 seconds, until fragrant. Add in the <a href='/misc/fruit#lime-juice'>lime juice</a> and water, and mix the <a href='/misc/fruit#lime'>lime</a> and <a href='/misc/veggies#garlic'>garlic</a> into the <a href='/misc/meat'>meat</a>.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Ground Meat/Lentil meat/Lentil/meat.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Meanwhile, mix together all the spices in a <a href='https://amzn.to/4zyFnzN'>small bowl</a>.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Ground Meat/Lentil meat/spices.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- When the <a href='/misc/beans#brown-lentils'>lentils</a> are done cooking and the <a href='/misc/meat'>meat</a> is no longer pink, add all your <a href='/misc/beans#brown-lentils'>lentils</a> and spice mix into the <a href='/misc/meat'>meat</a>. Mix together, and cook for an additional 2 minutes, or until you can smell the spices. Season with salt and <a href='/misc/fruit#lime-juice'>lime juice</a> to taste, and serve.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Ground Meat/Lentil meat/Lentil/pan.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- Any <a href='/misc/beans#brown-lentils'>lentils</a> will work, like <a href='/misc/beans#brown-lentils'>brown</a>, <a href='/misc/beans#green-lentils'>green</a>, or <a href='/misc/beans#red-lentils'>red lentils</a>.

- Any <a href='/recipes/ground-meat'>ground meat</a> will work, like <a href='/misc/meat#ground-beef'>ground beef</a>, <a href='/misc/meat#ground-turkey'>turkey</a>, <a href='/misc/ground-chicken'>chicken</a>, or <a href='/misc/meat#pork-tenderloin'>pork</a>.

- 1/2 cup (96 g) of dry <a href='/misc/beans#brown-lentils'>lentils</a> yields about 1 1/3 cup (265 g) of cooked <a href='/misc/beans#brown-lentils'>lentils</a>.

- The total cooked weight of the whole recipe with <a href='/misc/beans#brown-lentils'>lentils</a> weighs about <b>775 g</b>, or 5 servings of <b>155 g</b> each.

- Since the <a href='/misc/beans#brown-lentils'>lentils</a> make up <b>265 g</b> of the total weight, then cooking the <a href='/misc/meat#ground-beef'>meat</a> on its own would yield about <b>510 g</b> (775-265) total, or <b>102 g</b> for each of the 5 servings.

- Adding in the <a href='/misc/beans#brown-lentils'>lentils</a> makes each serving about 50% larger compared to only <a href='/misc/meat#ground-beef'>meat</a>.

- You can even use <a href='/misc/beans#tofu'>tofu</a> instead of <a href='/misc/beans#brown-lentils'>lentils</a> to bulk up the <a href='/misc/meat#ground-beef'>meat</a>. Simply mash a 1 lb (454 g) block of drained <a href='/misc/beans#tofu'>tofu</a> directly into the raw <a href='/misc/meat#ground-beef'>ground meat</a>, then cook them together according to the recipe.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Ground Meat/Lentil meat/Tofu/tofu.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Using <a href='/misc/beans#tofu'>tofu</a> instead of <a href='/misc/beans#brown-lentils'>lentils</a> will change the final weight. While the cooked weight with the lentils was <b>775 g</b> total and <b>155 g</b> per serving, the cooked weight with <a href='/misc/beans#tofu'>tofu</a> instead will be about <b>950 g</b> total and <b>190 g</b> per serving, since <a href='/misc/beans#tofu'>tofu</a> contains more water than cooked <a href='/misc/beans#brown-lentils'>lentils</a>.

- Adding in the <a href='/misc/beans#tofu'>tofu</a> makes each serving about 85% larger compared to only <a href='/misc/meat#ground-beef'>meat</a>.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Ground Meat/Lentil meat/Tofu/cover.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'></center>

- A typical serving of <a href='/misc/meat#ground-beef'>ground meat</a> uses about 1 lb (454 g) of raw <a href='/misc/meat'>meat</a> for 4 people. By adding the <a href='/misc/beans#brown-lentils'>lentils</a> or <a href='/misc/beans#tofu'>tofu</a>, you can stretch that same 1 lb (454 g) of <a href='/misc/meat'>meat</a> into 5 servings, a 25% increase in servings.

- The nutrition facts below assume 1 lb (454 g) 93% <a href='/misc/meat#ground-beef'>ground beef</a> with all the spices, divided into 5 servings. The three columns compare the recipe made with <a href='/misc/beans#brown-lentils'>lentils</a>, <a href='/misc/beans#tofu'>tofu</a>, and with just the <a href='/misc/meat#ground-beef'>beef</a>.

- Five servings of just 1 lb (454 g) of <a href='/misc/meat'>meat</a> would make relatively small portion sizes (just <b>102 g</b>). Adding <a href='/misc/beans#brown-lentils'>lentils</a> or <a href='/misc/beans#tofu'>tofu</a> increases the amount of food in each serving (to <b>155 g</b> & <b>190 g</b>, respectively), while keeping the same amount of <a href='/misc/meat'>meat</a>.

- The <a href='/misc/beans#brown-lentils'>lentils</a> also add <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a>, <a href='/misc/nutrient-alphabet#F'>folate</a>, and plant-based <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> & <a href='/misc/iron'>iron</a>. <a href='/misc/beans#tofu'>Tofu</a> meanwhile adds <a href='/misc/calcium'>calcium</a>, <a href='/misc/iron'>iron</a>, <a href='/misc/nutrient-alphabet#M'>manganese</a>, and <a href='/misc/nutrient-alphabet#S'>selenium</a>.
