Description: |
  If you somehow don't know by now, I am obsessed with both <a href='/recipes/chili'>chili</a> and <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut</a> butter. This dish is a remix combining my <a href='/recipes/chili'>Slow Cooker Chili</a> with <a href='/recipes/maafe'>Maafe (African Peanut Stew)</a>.  It has a similar flavor to my <a href='/recipes/peanut-chicken'>Peanut Chili Chicken Skillet</a>, but instead served as a delicious slow cooked stew, ready for you when you get home from work
  <br><br>
  This simple meal prep yields 6 full meals, each with 50 g of <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> from the <a href='/misc/meat#ground-beef'>ground beef</a>, <a href='/misc/beans'>beans</a>, and powdered <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>peanut butter</a>.  It's also got <a href='/misc/fats'>healthy fats</a> from the extra virgin olive oil and <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanuts</a>, in addition to <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a> and <a href='/misc/carbs'>complex carbs</a> from the <a href='/misc/beans'>beans</a>, <a href='/misc/veggies#pepper'>peppers</a>, and <a href='/misc/veggies#onion'</a>onions</a>.  Or check out any of my other <a href='/soups-and-stews'>soup or stew</a> recipes!
Instructions:
- Heat a large pan over medium heat.  Add the ground meat to the pan, and brown over medium heat.  Cook until no longer pink, and transfer to slow cooker

- Meanwhile, cut your peppers and onions into a medium dice, and add to the now empty pan with chili powder, cumin, and salt. Cook over medium heat (covered) until soft.  Add the garlic, cook for about 30 seconds (until fragrant), and transfer the veggies to your slow cooker

- Drain and rinse the cans of beans. Transfer all the beans, canned tomatoes, cocoa powder, and powdered peanut butter to the slow cooker

- Mix, cover, and cook for 8 hours on low or 6 hours on high
- <img src='/assets/Food/Ground Meat/Peanut Chili/crockpot.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Divide into 6 servings, and garnish with chopped peanuts
