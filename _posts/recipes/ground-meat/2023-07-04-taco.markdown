Description: Taco Tuesday was a tradition among friends in college, and this recipe suits a crowd really well. Just like my <a href='/recipes/fajitas'>Chicken Fajitas with Peppers</a> recipe, these are an awesome way for each person to build their own meals and have fun at the table. For a no <a href='/misc/veggies#garlic'>garlic</a> and <a href='/misc/veggies#onion'>onion</a> version, see my <a href='/recipes/low-fodmap-tacos'>Low FODMAP Chicken Tacos</a> recipe.  Invite some people over to make tacos and whip up some <a href='/recipes/salsa'>Five Minute Salsa (No Garlic/Onion)</a> or <a href='/recipes/guacamole'>Holy Guacamole</a>; what are you waiting for!
Instructions:
- Mix together seasonings in a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>medium bowl</a> or <a href='https://amzn.to/4vamaBm'>mason jar</a> and set aside

- Start with the <a href='/misc/veggies'>veggies</a>, since they will take the longest.  Cut your <a href='/misc/veggies#onion'>onions</a> into long thin slices, and add to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4rdR0HI'>12" pan</a> with <a href='/misc/olive-oil'>olive oil</a> over medium heat

- Season the <a href='/misc/veggies#onion'>onions</a> with 2 tbsp of the spice mix. Cover and cook until soft, browned, and lightly charred, about 20 minutes. Finish with <a href='/misc/fruit#lime-juice'>lime juice</a>

- Meanwhile, heat a <a href='https://amzn.to/4bOh0on'>10" pan</a> over medium heat with oil.  When hot, add in the <a href='/misc/meat'>meat</a>, and press down to brown the <a href='/misc/meat'>meat</a>.  Cook until no longer pink, and season with the rest of your spice mix

- Add in the <a href='/misc/veggies#garlic'>garlic</a> and water, and cover with a lid or foil.  Reduce heat to medium low, and let simmer for 10 minutes with occasional stirring

- Stir in the <a href='/misc/apple-cider-vinegar'>apple cider vinegar</a> and <a href='/misc/fruit#lime-juice'>lime juice</a>. Simmer until flavors combine, about another 5 minutes
- <img src='/assets/Food/Ground Meat/Taco/taco-counter.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Serve your tacos with <a href='/recipes/tortilla'>tortillas</a>, <a href='/misc/dairy#cheddar'>shredded cheese</a>, and <a href='/recipes/rice-and-beans'>rice and beans</a>, and <a href='/salad'>salad</a>.  Other good toppings are <a href='/misc/fruit#olives'>olives</a>, <a href='/recipes/guacamole'>guac</a>, <a href='/recipes/salsa'>salsa</a>, and <a href='/misc/veggies#tomato'>tomatoes</a>.
