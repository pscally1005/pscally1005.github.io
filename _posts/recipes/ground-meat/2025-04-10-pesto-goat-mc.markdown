Description: I saw this on the menu at Pressed Cafe, and <a href='/vic'>Vic</a> and I both thought that sounded delicious. So for her birthday, I'm making it at home. My version has the addition of <a href='/misc/veggies#broccoli'>broccoli</a> and <a href='/misc/meat#ground-turkey'>ground turkey</a> for extra <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a> and <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a>, as well as healthier <a href='/misc/beans#red-lentils'>red lentil pasta</a> instead of refined <a href='/misc/grains#pasta-white'>white pasta</a>. The homemade <a href='/pesto'>pesto</a> is creamy from <a href='/misc/dairy#goat-cheese'>goat cheese</a> with a minimal amount of oil, keeping it lower in <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a> for the same delicious flavor!
Instructions:
- The <a href='/misc/veggies#broccoli'>broccoli</a> takes the longest, so you should start that first. Preheat your oven to 400F, and line a large <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>baking sheet</a> with parchment paper. Toss the <a href='/misc/veggies#broccoli'>broccoli</a> with the <a href='/misc/fruit#lemon'>lemon</a>, salt, and black pepper on the sheet, and roast at 400F for 30 minutes, or until tender and lightly charred. Set aside

- Next up is the <a href='/misc/grains#pasta'>pasta</a>. Add your <a href='/misc/grains#pasta'>pasta</a> to a <a href='https://amzn.to/46ccg8m'>medium pot</a>. Add in just enough water to barely cover the <a href='/misc/grains#pasta'>pasta</a>, as well as a large pinch of salt. Bring to a boil, reduce to medium, and let simmer until al dente. Reserve some of the pasta water, strain the <a href='/misc/grains#pasta'>pasta</a>, and set aside

- As the <a href='/misc/veggies#broccoli'>broccoli</a> and <a href='/misc/grains#pasta'>pasta</a> cook, you can cook the <a href='/misc/meat'>meat</a> in a <a href='https://amzn.to/4rdR0HI'>large pan</a> over medium heat until no longer pink. Season with basil, oregano, thyme, salt, and black pepper. Add <a href='/misc/fruit#lemon-juice'>lemon juice</a> at the end when the <a href='/misc/meat'>meat</a> is fully cooked, and set aside

- Last is the <a href='/pesto'>pesto</a>. In a <a href='https://amzn.to/4qFE9y3'>small pan</a>, saute the <a href='/misc/veggies#spinach-fresh'>spinach</a> for a few minutes over medium heat, until the water has evaporated. You can also microwave it and pat out the water with paper towels.  Add all pesto ingredients (<a href='/misc/veggies#spinach-fresh'>spinach</a>, <a href='/misc/dairy#goat-cheese'>goat cheese</a>, <a href='/misc/veggies#garlic'>minced garlic</a>, <a href='/misc/fruit#lemon'>lemon</a>, <a href='/misc/olive-oil'>oil</a>, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and black pepper) to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4q0AUjI'>food processor</a>, and blend until smooth
- <img src='/assets/Food/Ground Meat/Pesto Goat MC/seperate.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Finally, combine together the <a href='/pesto'>pesto</a> with the <a href='/misc/grains#pasta'>pasta</a> in your pot, using a small splash of reserved pasta water to thicken. Transfer to 5 bowls, and top with <a href='/misc/veggies'>vegetables</a> and <a href='/misc/meat'>meat</a>. Garnish with some crumbles of <a href='/misc/dairy#goat-cheese'>goat cheese</a>, basil leaves, <a href='/misc/nuts#pine-nuts'>pine nuts</a>, or sun dried tomatoes. Serve
