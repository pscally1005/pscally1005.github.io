Description: Who doesn't love spaghetti and meatballs?  Lately I've been obsessed with <a href='/misc/veggies#spaghetti-squash'>spaghetti squash</a>, also making <a href='/recipes/lasagna-boats'>Spaghetti Squash Lasagna Boats</a> and <a href='/recipes/pad-thai'>Chicken Pad Thai with Spaghetti Squash</a>.  But today I'm making spaghetti and meatballs with <a href='/misc/veggies#spaghetti-squash'>spaghetti squash</a>, <a href='/misc/celiac'>gluten free</a> <a href='/misc/meat#ground-beef'>beef</a> meatballs, and <a href='/recipes/pasta-sauce'>homemade pasta sauce</a>.  Feel free to also pair this with some standard <a href='/misc/grains#pasta'>pasta</a> as well!  This recipe here is very similar to my <a href='/recipes/meatballs'>Zoodles and Meatballs</a>, where that recipe instead uses <a href='/misc/veggies#zucchini'>zoodles</a> instead of <a href='/misc/veggies#spaghetti-squash'>spaghetti squash</a> and <a href='/misc/meat#ground-turkey'>turkey</a> instead of <a href='/misc/meat#ground-beef'>beef</a>.
Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 400F, and line 2 large <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>cookie sheets</a> with parchment paper (one for the <a href='/misc/veggies#spaghetti-squash'>spaghetti squash</a>, and the other for the meatballs)

- Cut your <a href='/misc/veggies#spaghetti-squash'>spaghetti squash</a> in half lengthwise. Scoop out the seeds with a spoon, season the <a href='/misc/veggies#spaghetti-squash'>spaghetti squash</a> (basil, oregano, salt, and black pepper), and add to the sheet cut side down. Prick the top with a fork

- Roast the <a href='/misc/veggies#spaghetti-squash'>spaghetti squash</a> at 400F for about 1 hour, or until fork tender
- <img src='/assets/Food/Ground Meat/Spaghetti and Meatballs/squash.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Meanwhile, in a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>large bowl</a>, mix together your meatball ingredients - <a href='/misc/meat#ground-beef'>ground meat</a>, <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>egg</a>, <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oat flour</a>, <a href='/misc/dairy#mozzarella'>cheese</a>, basil, oregano, salt, and black pepper

- Scoop your meat mix into 30 meatballs, about 24 g each. Roll into a ball, and place on the other <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>cookie sheet</a>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Ground Meat/Spaghetti and Meatballs/meatballs.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Roast the meatballs at 400F for about 16 minutes, or until the <a href='https://amzn.to/4kmobG2'>internal temperature</a> is at least <a href='https://amzn.to/4kmobG2'>160F</a>

- Serve your <a href='/misc/veggies#spaghetti-squash'>spaghetti squash</a> and meatballs with <a href='/recipes/pasta-sauce'>sauce</a>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Ground Meat/Spaghetti and Meatballs/all.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- You can also use this meatball recipe to make a meatloaf.  Below is a <a href='/misc/meat#ground-turkey'>ground turkey</a> meatloaf I made.  Just shape the meat mixture on a parchment lined pan, and baked at 400F for 1 hour.<br><br>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Ground Meat/Spaghetti and Meatballs/meatloaf2.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Ground Meat/Spaghetti and Meatballs/meatloaf.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
