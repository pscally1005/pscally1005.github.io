Description: I recently had Nam Sod for the first time at a Thai place, and I was in love. The combination of <a href='/misc/fruit#lime'>lime</a>, <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanuts</a>, and <a href='/misc/veggies#ginger'>ginger</a> was absolutely delicious, and it was a light but filling meal. It's traditionally made with <a href='/misc/meat#pork-tenderloin'>ground pork</a>, but it can be made with <a href='/misc/meat#ground-beef'>ground beef</a>, <a href='/misc/meat#ground-turkey'>turkey</a>, or <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> (what I had at the restaurant). This is my non-traditional take on the dish.  The <a href='/misc/veggies#carrots'>carrots</a> and <a href='/misc/veggies#onion'>onion</a> are normally raw, but I opted to cook them here and bring out their sweet flavors. It can be served over a bed of <a href='/misc/veggies#lettuce'>lettuce</a> or <a href='/misc/grains#brown-rice'>rice</a> (or both!)
Instructions:
- In a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>large bowl</a> (large enough to combine all the food later) mix the <a href='/misc/fruit#lime-juice'>lime juice</a> (<a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#juice'>juice</a> of 2 <a href='/misc/fruit#lime'>limes</a>), gochujang, <a href='/misc/veggies#ginger'>ginger</a>, and <a href='https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07SXBL1GF?ref=t_ac_view_request_product_image&campaignId=amzn1.campaign.2L4DOI4F1KV3G&linkCode=tr1&tag=poormanprotei-20&linkId=amzn1.campaign.2L4DOI4F1KV3G_1767022097417'>powdered peanut butter</a>. Thin out with some soy sauce (or water). Typically fish sauce is used, but I couldn't find it at my grocery store, so I've swapped it for gochujang. If you use fresh <a href='/misc/fruit#lime'>limes</a>, you can choose to zest them as well

- Cut the <a href='/misc/veggies#onion'>onions</a> and <a href='/misc/veggies#carrots'>carrots</a> into a small dice, and add to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4rdR0HI'>large pan</a> over medium heat with oil and a pinch of salt. Cook until the <a href='/misc/veggies#carrots'>carrots</a> have fully softened, and the <a href='/misc/veggies#onion'>onions</a> are translucent and begin to caramelize. Add the <a href='/misc/veggies#garlic'>garlic</a>, and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds. Set the <a href='/misc/veggies'>vegetables</a> aside in the bowl with the <a href='/dressing'>dressing</a>

- In the pan over medium heat, add in the <a href='/misc/meat'>meat</a> with a <a href='https://amzn.to/3Hdg0gk'>spray of oil</a>, and flatten with your hands to optimize browning. Cook until no longer pink, and transfer to the bowl with the <a href='/misc/veggies'>vegetables</a> and <a href='/dressing'>dressing</a>

- Meanwhile (as the <a href='/misc/meat'>meat</a> cooks) roughly chop the <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanuts</a>. Add to the bowl. Combine together the <a href='/misc/meat'>meat</a>, <a href='/misc/veggies'>vegetables</a>, <a href='/misc/nuts'>nuts</a>, and <a href='/dressing'>dressing</a>

- Thinly slice the <a href='/misc/veggies#lettuce'>lettuce</a>, and add to 4 serving bowls. Add the meat mix on top of it, and optionally garnish with your lime zest. Serve with a <a href='/recipes/sides'>side</a> of <a href='/misc/grains#brown-rice'>brown rice</a> if desired (mix some zest into the <a href='/misc/grains#brown-rice'>rice</a> too)
