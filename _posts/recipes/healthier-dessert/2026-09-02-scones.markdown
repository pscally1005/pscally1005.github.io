Description: |
  Lemon blueberry scones are a classic <a href='/recipes/healthier-dessert'>dessert</a>, <a href='/recipes/finger-food'>snack</a>, or even <a href='/recipes/breakfast'>breakfast</a> addition.  But instead of tons of <a href='/misc/dairy#butter'>butter</a> or <a href='/misc/olive-oil'>oil</a>, or nutritionally void <a href='/misc/grains#white-wheat'>white flour</a>, these scones get their <a href='/misc/carbs'>carbohydrates</a> primarily from nutrient-rich <a href='/misc/grains#whole-wheat'>whole wheat flour</a>, while having minimal added <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a> from <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>virgin coconut oil</a>.<br><br>

  Compared to traditional scones made with <a href='/misc/grains#white-wheat'>refined white flour</a>, these scones are higher in <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a>, <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a>, <a href='/misc/nutrient-alphabet#B'>B vitamins</a>, and <a href='/misc/iron'>iron</a>.  These <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar free</a> scones are sweetened with <a href='https://amzn.to/4ceFnvP'>granulated monk fruit</a>, get some additional <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> from the <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>egg</a> and <a href='/misc/dairy#yogurt'>Greek yogurt</a>, and are flavored with <a href='/misc/fruit#lemon'>lemon</a> and <a href='/misc/fruit#blueberries'>frozen blueberries</a>!
Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 350F, and line a large <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>cookie sheet</a> with parchment paper.

- Whisk together the wet ingredients in a <a href='https://amzn.to/4zyFnzN'>large bowl</a> - <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>egg</a>, <a href='/misc/dairy#yogurt'>yogurt</a>, <a href='/misc/fruit#lemon-juice'>lemon juice</a>, (melted & cooled) <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut oil</a>, <a href='https://amzn.to/4ceFnvP'>granulated monk fruit</a>, and <a href='https://amzn.to/43MkDqr'>vanilla</a>.

- Mix in the dry ingredients with a <a href='https://amzn.to/40F2aK0'>silicone spatula</a> - salt, baking powder, and <a href='/misc/grains#whole-wheat'>whole wheat flour</a>.

- Fold in the <a href='/misc/fruit#blueberries'>frozen blueberries</a> with a <a href='https://amzn.to/40F2aK0'>silicone spatula</a>.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Scones/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Divide the dough into 5 even pieces, about <b>100 g</b> each.  Shape into round, thick circles on your <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>cookie sheet</a>, about 3/4" tall.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Scones/rawNew.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Bake at 350F for about 20 minutes, or until lightly golden brown and a toothpick comes out clean.  Let them cool totally on the <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>cookie sheet</a>.  Store in the fridge.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Scones/bakedNew.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- |
  I would recommend using a fresh <a href='/misc/fruit#lemon'>lemon</a> instead of bottled <a href='/misc/fruit#lemon-juice'>lemon juice</a> for a stronger <a href='/misc/fruit#lemon'>lemon</a> flavor.  Additionally, with a fresh <a href='/misc/fruit#lemon'>lemon</a>, you can also add the zest.<br><br>

  Here, I used the zest of a <b>whole</b> medium-sized <a href='/misc/fruit#lemon'>lemon</a> (~1 tbsp, 3 g), plus the juice of <b>half</b> the <a href='/misc/fruit#lemon'>lemon</a> (~2 tbsp, 30 g).

- You can eat these fresh from the oven, chilled out of the fridge, or warmed back up.  For the latter, <a href='https://amzn.to/3FuWETp'>air fry</a> at 400F for about 3 minutes, or until warm and slightly crispy.

- Below is a nutritional comparison of <a href='#recipe'>these scones</a> made <a href='#recipe'>as-is</a> (with <a href='https://amzn.to/4ceFnvP'>granulated monk fruit</a> and <a href='/misc/grains#whole-wheat'>whole wheat flour</a>) versus with <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>granulated sugar</a> and <a href='/misc/grains#white-wheat'>white flour</a>, where the latter is higher in <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar</a>, <a href='/misc/carbs'>net carbs</a>, and <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a>.
