Description: It's Girl Scout cookie season, which means Tagalongs, Samoas, and of course, Thin Mints.  Today I'm making a healthier thin mint cookie with less <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar</a> and <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a>.  This crispy chocolate <a href='/cookies'>cookie</a> is coated in a bittersweet chocolate, and is a delicious and healthier alternative.  Plus, they're way cheaper; no more spending $6 on a tiny box of 32 <a href='/cookies'>cookies</a>; this batch of 34 <a href='/cookies'>cookies</a> costs just $2.70.  You can also check out any of my other <a href='/cookies'>cookie recipes</a>!
Instructions: 
- In a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>large bowl</a>, mix together the wet ingredients - <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>egg</a>, honey, <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a>, <a href='/misc/fruit#apple'>applesauce</a>, and mint extract

- Add the dry ingredients - <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oat flour</a>, <a href='/misc/overshadowed-healthy-foods#chocolate'>cocoa powder</a>, cornstarch, baking soda, and salt. Combine with a <a href='https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0C37QM1K3?ref=t_ac_view_request_product_image&campaignId=amzn1.campaign.1OO1S5W7ZMYBB&linkCode=tr1&tag=poormanprotei-20&linkId=amzn1.campaign.1OO1S5W7ZMYBB_1751469633605'>silicone spatula</a> until you have a dough ball. Chill in the fridge for 30 minutes
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Thin Mints/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Preheat your oven to 350F, and line a <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>cookie sheet</a> with parchment paper

- Place a large piece of parchment paper on your counter, and add the dough ball on the paper. Top with another piece of parchment, and roll out the dough to about 1/4" thick
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Thin Mints/rolled.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Cut your cookies into your desired shape, and transfer to the sheet. Roll out any dough scraps left over to use up all the cookies
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Thin Mints/raw.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Bake at 350F for about 12 minutes, or until firm. Transfer to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4qQNmn2'>wire rack</a> to totally cool.  When cooled, the cookies with be crispy, and snap when broken in half
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Thin Mints/baked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- In a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>medium bowl</a>, microwave <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut oil</a> for 1 minute to melt. Stir in <a href='/misc/overshadowed-healthy-foods#chocolate'>cocoa powder</a> and sweetener with a <a href='https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0C37QM1K3?ref=t_ac_view_request_product_image&campaignId=amzn1.campaign.1OO1S5W7ZMYBB&linkCode=tr1&tag=poormanprotei-20&linkId=amzn1.campaign.1OO1S5W7ZMYBB_1751469633605'>silicone spatula</a>. Honey, maple syrup, or <a href='/recipes/sugar-free-syrup'>sugar free syrup</a> will all work

- Lightly coat each cookie in the chocolate, using 2 forks to drip out excess chocolate. Transfer back to the <a href='https://amzn.to/4qQNmn2'>wire rack</a>. Chill for 30 minutes to harden the chocolate before removing from the <a href='https://amzn.to/4qQNmn2'>wire rack</a>
Notes:
- Any liquid sweetener will work, like honey, maple syrup, or <a href='/recipes/sugar-free-syrup'>sugar free syrup</a>

- For the <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>nut butter</a>, I'm using <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>natural peanut butter</a> today, but <a href='/misc/seeds#sesame-seeds'>tahini</a>, <a href='/misc/nuts#almonds'>almond butter</a>, or any other <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>nut/seed butter</a> will work as well

- Be careful with the mint extract; it's very strong. Start with less, and adjust it to your taste

  I have never tried to make these into Tagalongs, but here's what I would suggest if you're interested
  <ul>
    <li>Replace <a href='/misc/overshadowed-healthy-foods#chocolate'>cocoa powder</a> with either <a href='https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07SXBL1GF?ref=t_ac_view_request_product_image&campaignId=amzn1.campaign.2L4DOI4F1KV3G&linkCode=tr1&tag=poormanprotei-20&linkId=amzn1.campaign.2L4DOI4F1KV3G_1767022097417'>powdered peanut butter</a> or <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oat flour</a></li>
    <li>Replace mint extract with almond extract</li>
    <li>Add a small spoonful of <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>peanut butter</a> on top of each baked cookie</li>
    <li>Freeze for 30 minutes to keep the <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>peanut butter</a> in place before coating the cookie in chocolate</li>
  </ul>
