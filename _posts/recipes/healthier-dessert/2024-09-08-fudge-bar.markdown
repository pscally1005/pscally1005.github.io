Description: These oatmeal fudge bars combine together the delicious internet <a href='/recipes/healthier-dessert'>healthy dessert</a> ingredients - <a href='/misc/fruit#banana'>bananas</a>, chocolate, <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a>, and <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oats</a>. They can be totally <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>added sugar</a> free if you use <a href='/recipes/monkfruit-chocolate-chunks'>sugar free chocolate chips</a>, but here I've decided to make a homemade honey sweetened chocolate for the fudge filling instead. The bottom crust and crumble topping have a delicious <a href='/misc/fruit#banana'>banana</a> <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a> flavor, and the fudge is rich, chocolatey, and full of delicious <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut</a> buttery goodness
Instructions:
- In a bowl, combine together the crust/crumble ingredients - mashed <a href='/misc/fruit#banana'>banana</a>, honey, vanilla, <a href='https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07SXBL1GF?ref=t_ac_view_request_product_image&campaignId=amzn1.campaign.2L4DOI4F1KV3G&linkCode=tr1&tag=poormanprotei-20&linkId=amzn1.campaign.2L4DOI4F1KV3G_1767022097417'>powdered peanut butter</a>, and <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>quick oats</a>. Mix until well combined
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Oatmeal Fudge/of-oats.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- In another bowl, add the melted <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut oil</a>, <a href='/misc/overshadowed-healthy-foods#chocolate'>cocoa powder</a>, honey, and <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a>. Mix with a <a href='https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0C37QM1K3?ref=t_ac_view_request_product_image&campaignId=amzn1.campaign.1OO1S5W7ZMYBB&linkCode=tr1&tag=poormanprotei-20&linkId=amzn1.campaign.1OO1S5W7ZMYBB_1751469633605'>spatula</a>.  Melt chocolate in the microwave, and mix with the <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a> to make the fudge
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Oatmeal Fudge/of-fudge.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- In a lined <a href='https://amzn.to/3YY2H9q'>9" square baking pan</a>, spread 2/3 of the oat mixture. Lightly wet your hands to prevent sticking, and flatten to an even thickness
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Oatmeal Fudge/of-bottom.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Pour the chocolate peanut butter fudge over the <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oats</a> and spread evenly
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Oatmeal Fudge/of-middle.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Crumble the rest of the oat mixture on top, lightly pressing it into the fudge
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Oatmeal Fudge/of-top.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Refrigerate for at least 2 hours to set before slicing and serving
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Oatmeal Fudge/of-cut.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>Oat flour</a> will work in place of <a href='https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07SXBL1GF?ref=t_ac_view_request_product_image&campaignId=amzn1.campaign.2L4DOI4F1KV3G&linkCode=tr1&tag=poormanprotei-20&linkId=amzn1.campaign.2L4DOI4F1KV3G_1767022097417'>powdered peanut butter</a> if you desire

- You could also use 1 cup (170 g) <a href='/recipes/monkfruit-chocolate-chunks'>sugar free chocolate chips</a> in place of the <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut oil</a>, <a href='/misc/overshadowed-healthy-foods#chocolate'>cocoa powder</a>, and honey

- To make this recipe <a href='/misc/nuts'>nut</a> free, you can use <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut flour</a> or <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oat flour</a> in place of <a href='https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07SXBL1GF?ref=t_ac_view_request_product_image&campaignId=amzn1.campaign.2L4DOI4F1KV3G&linkCode=tr1&tag=poormanprotei-20&linkId=amzn1.campaign.2L4DOI4F1KV3G_1767022097417'>powdered peanut butter</a> for the crust, and <a href='/misc/seeds#sunflower-seeds'>sunflower seed butter</a> in place of <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a> for the filling

- |
  For a <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar free</a> and lower <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a> version, omit the honey in the crust/crumble, and replace the filling (<a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a>, <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut oil</a>, <a href='/misc/overshadowed-healthy-foods#chocolate'>cocoa powder</a>, and honey) with 6 servings of <a href='/recipes/quick-nutella'>Single Serving Quick Nutella</a>
