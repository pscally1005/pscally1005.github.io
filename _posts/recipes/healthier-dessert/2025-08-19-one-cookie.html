You want a nice and delicious chocolate chip cookie, but you don't want to be left with a whole batch? Well you're in the right spot. This recipe is essentially a scaled down version of my Keto Chocolate Chip Cookies, enough to make 1 large sized cookie. Or check out any of my other cookie recipes! Instructions: - Preheat your air fryer to 300F on the air fry setting - Mix together nut butter, sweetener, and vanilla extract until smooth and you have a cookie dough texture. Fold in 2/3 (10 g) of your chocolate chips - Cashew or almond butter are best for their neutral flavor and buttery texture. Peanut butter will work, but only if you want a distinctly peanut butter chocolate chip cookie. For nut free, try tahini or sunflower seed butter - Flatten the cookie onto a small piece of parchment paper, shape into a circle, and top with your remaining chocolate chips -
- Air fry at 300F for about 12 minutes, or until golden brown -

Alternatively, below is a recipe using coconut oil as the fat instead of a nut or seed butter. It's essentially my Classic Chocolate Chip Cookies recipe using coconut oil and granulated monk fruit in place of butter and sugar, and scaled down to 3 servings (1 large cookie)

  • 4 tsp (16 g) Granulated monk fruit
  • 2 tsp (9 g) Extra virgin coconut oil
  • 1 tsp (5 g) Unsweetened almond milk
  • 1/4 tsp (1.25 g) Vanilla extract
  • Small pinch (0.3 g) Salt
  • Small pinch (0.3 g) Baking soda
  • 3 tbsp (22.5 g) Whole wheat flour
  • 1 tbsp (15 g) Sugar free chocolate chips



The instructions are the same. Combine all ingredients together in a small bowl, flatten onto a sheet of parchment paper, and air fry at 300F for about 12 minutes, or until golden brown
