Description: Remember how I mentioned that most healthy recipes are a combo of <a href='/misc/fruit#banana'>banana</a>, <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a>, chocolate, and <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oats</a>? Well this is my baked version of that formula.  For a no-bake recipe with similar ingredients, see my <a href='/recipes/cookie-bar'>No Bake Cookie Bars</a>.
Instructions:
- Preheat the oven to 350F. Line a 9x5 <a href='https://amzn.to/3YUjIkN'>loaf pan</a> with parchment paper

- Mash the ripe <a href='/misc/fruit#banana'>bananas</a> with a fork (or blend them and add them to the bowl). Mix in the <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a>, vanilla, baking powder, salt until fully combined.  Stir in the <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oats</a>.  Fold in most of your chocolate chips, reserving some for the top
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/PB Banana Bake/pbb-raw-bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Transfer the mixture into the pan, and top with your remaining <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>chocolate</a>.  Bake for 25 minutes, or until a toothpick comes out almost clean
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/PB Banana Bake/pbb-raw-pan.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/PB Banana Bake/pbb-baked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Take out of the pan and place on a <a href='https://amzn.to/4qQNmn2'>cooling rack</a> in the fridge for at least 1 hour before slicing
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/PB Banana Bake/pbb-sliced.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- You can also bake these as mini muffins for about 15 minutes at 350F. Makes 24 (meaning that 1 serving is 3 <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#muffins'>mini muffins</a>)
