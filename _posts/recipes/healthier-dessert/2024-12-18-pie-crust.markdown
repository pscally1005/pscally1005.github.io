Description: Graham cracker crusts are staples of most pies, and typically made out of <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#crackers'>graham cracker</a> crumbs, melted <a href='/misc/dairy#butter'>butter</a>, and sometimes additional <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar</a>. Instead, I'm replacing store-bought <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#crackers'>crackers</a> with my own <a href='/recipes/gf-crackers'>Gluten Free Graham Crackers</a>, and subbing out the <a href='/misc/dairy#butter'>butter</a> for <a href='/misc/fruit#apple'>unsweetened applesauce</a>. The result is a very similar crust with a fraction of the <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a>, <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar</a>, and <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a>.
Instructions:
- Blend <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#crackers'>graham crackers</a> in a <a href='https://amzn.to/4q0AUjI'>food processor</a> until you have a fine crumb. Blend in the <a href='/misc/fruit#apple'>applesauce</a>. The dough should look too dry, but just come together with pressed with your hands

- Transfer to an 9" circular pan, and use a <a href='https://amzn.to/4dFNtMP'>measuring cup</a> to evenly press into the bottom and sides

- Blind bake the pie crust at 350F for about 10 minutes, or until lightly golden brown on the edges. Let cool completely before filling
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Pie Crust/baked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- I'm using my standard <a href='/recipes/gf-crackers'>Gluten Free Graham Crackers</a> here, but you can make this into a <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>chocolate</a> graham cracker pie crust by using <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>chocolate</a> graham crackers instead

- Alternatively, you can use the raw dough of the <a href='/recipes/gf-crackers'>Gluten Free Graham Crackers</a> and turn that straight into the pie crust; no <a href='/recipes/healthier-dessert'>baking</a> beforehand necessary.
