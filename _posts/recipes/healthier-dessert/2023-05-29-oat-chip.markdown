Description: These <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oatmeal</a> cookies are a healthier alternative to the classic chocolate chip cookie. They can be totally <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar</a> free if using <a href='/recipes/sugar-free-syrup'>sugar free syrup</a> and sugar free chocolate chips, but you can also use honey (or maple syrup) and regular chocolate chips if that's what you desire.  Or check out any of my other <a href='/cookies'>cookie recipes</a>!
Instructions:
- In a large bowl, mash your banana with the back of a fork, until smooth.  1/2 cup (120 g) unsweetened applesauce, <a href='/recipes/sweet-potato-puree'>Roasted Sweet Potato Puree</a>, or <a href='/recipes/apple-spread'>No Sugar Added Apple Spread</a> will all work in place of mashed banana

- To the bowl, add your honey, vanilla, baking powder, cinnamon, salt, and powdered peanut butter.  Any liquid sweetener will work, such as honey, maple syrup, or sugar free syrup.  Optionally, add 1/4 tsp butter extract.  Mix with a <a href='https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0C37QM1K3?ref=t_ac_view_request_product_image&campaignId=amzn1.campaign.1OO1S5W7ZMYBB&linkCode=tr1&tag=poormanprotei-20&linkId=amzn1.campaign.1OO1S5W7ZMYBB_1751469633605'>silicone spatula</a> until fully combined

- Fold in the rolled oats and chocolate chips (minis preferred).  Chill the dough in the fridge for 30 minutes, so the oats can absorb the moisture
- <center><img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Oatmeal Chip/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'></center>

- Preheat your oven to 350F and line a cookie sheet with parchment paper. Scoop dough onto the sheet, making 24 cookies of about 18 g each.  Lightly wet your hands, and flatten into a round and thin cookie (they won't spread or rise as they bake)
- <center><img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Oatmeal Chip/raw.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'></center>

- Bake at 350F for about 12 minutes, or until golden brown on the edges.  Let them cool totally on the pan in the fridge
- <center><img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Oatmeal Chip/baked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'></center>
