Description: Coconut macaroons are such a simple <a href='/recipes/healthier-dessert'>healthy dessert</a>, and are great for all you <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut</a> lovers out there. I personally don't like <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut</a>, but even I admit these are hay in the needle stack.  To use up those extra <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>yolks</a> though, check out my <a href='/recipes/hollandaise'>Greek Yogurt Hollandaise Sauce</a>.  Or check out any of my other <a href='/cookies'>cookie recipes</a>!
Instructions:
- With a <a href='https://amzn.to/45yqsbM'>hand mixer</a>, beat together <a href='/misc/meat#egg-whites'>egg whites</a> until soft peaks in a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>glass bowl</a>.  <a href='/misc/meat#egg-whites'>Carton egg white</a> will work as well.  66 g is about 4 tbsp + 1 tsp
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Coconut/egg-whites.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Fold in <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#honey'>honey</a>, vanilla, almond extract, and salt. Mix in <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut</a> and <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oats</a> until fully combined and sticky.  Chill the dough in the fridge for 30 minutes to harden

- Line a <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>baking sheet</a> with parchment paper.  Use a cookie scoop to scoop the dough, and press it down with your hands in the scoop.  Place onto the sheet, and you should have nice sphere-like <a href='/cookies'>cookies</a>.  Shape with your fingers if needed
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Coconut/raw.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Bake at 325F for 15 minutes. The cookies will be lightly browned in the top and golden/dark on the bottom. Transfer to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4qQNmn2'>wire rack</a> to cool
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Coconut/cooling.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- Optionally drizzle with melted <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>chocolate</a> (2 tbsp, 25 g), or dip the bottoms in <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>chocolate</a>
