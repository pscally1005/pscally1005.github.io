Description: These soft baked peanut butter cookies are naturally sweetened with honey (or maple syrup), and still feature that classic criss-cross design on the top. They're all about the <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>natural peanut butter</a>, have a subtle honey taste, <a href='/misc/celiac'>gluten free</a>, and not crumbly!  Make some <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>Homemade Natural Nut Butter</a> for an even better <a href='/cookies'>cookie</a>, and maybe try them with <a href='/misc/nuts#almonds'>almond</a> or <a href='/misc/seeds#sunflower-seeds'>sunflower butter</a> instead!  Or check out any of my other <a href='/cookies'>cookie recipes</a>!
Instructions:
- In a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>large bowl</a>, beat together <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a>, sweetener, <a href='/misc/fruit#apple'>applesauce</a> (or <a href='/misc/fruit#banana'>banana</a>), and vanilla with a <a href='https://amzn.to/45yqsbM'>hand mixer</a> (or fork) until smooth, about 1 minute

- Add the cornstarch, baking powder, and half of the <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oats</a>. Combine with a fork. Add in the other half of the <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oats</a> and mix until fully combined.  Chill the cookies to firm up a little bit if needed before shaping, about 30 minutes

- Scoop into cookies and place on a <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>baking sheet</a> lined with parchment paper. Lightly flatten, and cross with a fork. Wet the fork to prevent sticking if necessary
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/PB Cookie/pb-cookie-3.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Bake at 350F for 8 minutes. Let cool on the pan for 5 minutes, then transfer to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4qQNmn2'>wire rack</a> to cool. Optionally drizzle the cookies with melted chocolate
