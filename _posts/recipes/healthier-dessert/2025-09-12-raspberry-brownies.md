Description: Is there a better <a href='/recipes/healthier-dessert'>dessert</a> combination than chocolate and <a href='/misc/fruit#raspberry'>raspberries</a>?  These healthy <a href='/brownies'>brownies</a> are just that, being <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar free</a>, <a href='/misc/celiac'>gluten free</a>, and only having <a href='/misc/fats'>healthy fats</a>. With a neutral base of <a href='/misc/fruit#apple'>applesauce</a>, <a href='/misc/nuts#almonds'>almond butter</a>, and <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut oil</a>, sweetened with <a href='https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DD4YY92R?ref=t_ac_view_request_product_image&campaignId=amzn1.campaign.3UEEUG24MBC1R&linkCode=tr1&tag=poormanprotei-20&linkId=amzn1.campaign.3UEEUG24MBC1R_1767021852598'>granulated monk fruit</a>, brought together with <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oat flour</a> and <a href='/misc/overshadowed-healthy-foods#chocolate'>cocoa powder</a>, and topped with <a href='/misc/fruit#raspberry'>raspberries</a>, these <a href='/brownies'>brownies</a> are the perfect <a href='/recipes/healthier-dessert'>dessert</a> or snack without making you feel sluggish.  Or check out any of my other <a href='/brownies'>brownie recipes</a>!
Instructions: 
- Preheat your oven to 350F, and line a <a href='https://amzn.to/3YY2H9q'>9" square baking pan</a> with parchment paper. Lightly grease the paper

- In a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>large bowl</a>, mix together the wet ingredients with a <a href='https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0C37QM1K3?ref=t_ac_view_request_product_image&campaignId=amzn1.campaign.1OO1S5W7ZMYBB&linkCode=tr1&tag=poormanprotei-20&linkId=amzn1.campaign.1OO1S5W7ZMYBB_1751469633605'>silicone spatula</a> until fully combined

- Add the dry ingredients to the wet, and mix until just fully combined
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Raspberry Brownies/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Transfer the batter to your parchment lined pan, and top with your <a href='/misc/fruit#raspberry'>raspberries</a>, lightly pressing them into the batter
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Raspberry Brownies/raw.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Bake at 350F for about 25 minutes, or until the edges are set, and a toothpick to the center comes out almost clean with a few moist crumbs
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Raspberry Brownies/baked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Transfer the pan to the fridge, and let chill for at least 30 minutes before slicing.  Store leftover brownies in the fridge or freezer
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Raspberry Brownies/cut.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Raspberry Brownies/cover-1.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- Any <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>nut or seed butter</a> will work in place of <a href='/misc/nuts#almonds'>almond butter</a>, such as <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a>, <a href='/misc/nuts#cashews'>cashew butter</a>, <a href='/misc/seeds#sesame-seeds'>tahini</a>, or <a href='/misc/seeds#sunflower-seeds'>sunflower seed butter</a>

- Melted (unsalted) <a href='/misc/dairy#butter'>butter</a> or extra virgin olive oil will work in place of melted <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut oil</a>
