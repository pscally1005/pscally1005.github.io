--- layout: post title: Healthier Graham Cracker Pie Crust date: 2024-12-18 00:00:00 -0500 categories: - Recipes - Healthier Dessert showPercentage: no hummus: no oatmeal: no yogurt: no nut_butter: no pesto: no soup_stew: no salad: no dressing: no brownie: no cookie: no copycat: no permalink: /recipes/pie-crust image: /assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Pie Crust/pie.jpg ing: piecrust-ing facts: piecrust-facts servingSize: section1: start2: section2: start3: section3: start4: section4: start5: section5: Prep: 5 Rest: Cook: 10 Source1: Source2: Source3: Source4: Source5: whisk: https://s.samsungfood.com/JSWCl tags: - pie crust - cheese cake - cheesecake - graham crackers - apple pie - peanut butter pie - banana cream pie - pumpkin pie - unsweetened vanilla almond milk - unsweetened applesauce - gluten free Description: Graham cracker crusts are staples of most pies, and typically made out of graham cracker crumbs, melted butter, and sometimes additional sugar. Instead, I'm replacing store-bought crackers with my own Gluten Free Graham Crackers, and subbing out the butter for unsweetened applesauce. The result is a very similar crust with a fraction of the fat, sugar, and calories Instructions: - Blend graham crackers in a food processor until you have a fine crumb. Blend in the applesauce. The dough should look too dry, but just come together with pressed with your hands - I'm using my standard Gluten Free Graham Crackers here, but you can make this into a chocolate graham cracker pie crust by using chocolate graham crackers instead - Transfer to an 9" circular pan, and use a measuring cup to evenly press into the bottom and sides - Blind bake the pie crust at 350F for about 10 minutes, or until lightly golden brown on the edges. Let cool completely before filling -
Notes: - The pie pictured in the thumbnail is just this healthy pie crust filled with my Date Sweetened Frosting for a creamy, sweet, and delicious no bake pie! -
- I've also used this pie crust, along with Banana Bread Hummus Spread and Aquafaba Whipped Cream to make a Banana No-Cream Pie. -
- Alternatively, you can use the raw dough of the Gluten Free Graham Crackers and turn that straight into the pie crust; no baking beforehand necessary. Just take the raw ingredients of the graham cracker dough, shape in a 9" cake pan, and bake at 350F for about 15 minutes, until lightly browned. Note that this recipe calls for all the crackers plus some applesauce; the applesauce is not needed if you're baking the raw crackers for the crust -
