Instructions: - Preheat the oven to 350F and line an 9" square pan with parchment paper. Spray the parchment paper - In a food processor, blend the dates, bananas, and nut butter until a smooth paste forms. Blend in the rest of the ingredients - Transfer batter to the pan. Optionally top with 1-2 tbsp (15-30 g) chocolate chips, minis preferred - Bake for 30 minutes uncovered. Let them cool completely in the fridge before slicing -

Notes
  1. Any kind of unsweetened dried fruit wil work, not just dates. Try out dried figs, raisins, or prunes.

  2. Any kind of natural nut or seed butter will work for this recipe. I prefer almond butter, but other good options are peanut butter, walnut butter, sunflower seed butter, or tahini. Just use something that's only nuts/seeds and salt, without any added sugars or hydrogenated oils.
