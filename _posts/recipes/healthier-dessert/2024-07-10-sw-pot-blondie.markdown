Description: These delicious chocolate chip peanut butter <a href='/brownies'>blondies</a> use <a href='/misc/veggies#sweet-potato'>sweet potatoes</a> as a base, leaving them rich, delicious, and slightly cakey with much less <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a> and <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar</a>. You can swap out the chocolate chips for chopped <a href='/misc/nuts'>nuts</a> or <a href='/misc/seeds'>seeds</a> to make them a <a href='/recipes/breakfast'>breakfast</a> bar too!  You can use either <a href='/recipes/sweet-potato-puree'>Roasted Sweet Potato Puree</a>, or just use canned <a href='/recipes/pumpkin-puree'>pumpkin puree</a>.  For more <a href='/misc/veggies#sweet-potato'>sweet potato</a> based <a href='/recipes/healthier-dessert'>desserts</a>, see my fudgy <a href='/recipes/sweet-potato-brownies'>Sweet Potato Brownies (SF)</a>
Instructions:
- In a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>large bowl</a>, combine all the ingredients (except chocolate chips) with a <a href='https://amzn.to/45yqsbM'>hand mixer</a> until smooth and thoroughly mixed.  Fold in the chocolate chips with a <a href='https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0C37QM1K3?ref=t_ac_view_request_product_image&campaignId=amzn1.campaign.1OO1S5W7ZMYBB&linkCode=tr1&tag=poormanprotei-20&linkId=amzn1.campaign.1OO1S5W7ZMYBB_1751469633605'>spatula</a>

- Line an <a href='https://amzn.to/3YY2H9q'>9" square baking pan</a> with parchment paper, and lightly <a href='https://amzn.to/3Hdg0gk'>spray</a> the paper.  Transfer the batter to the pan with a <a href='https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0C37QM1K3?ref=t_ac_view_request_product_image&campaignId=amzn1.campaign.1OO1S5W7ZMYBB&linkCode=tr1&tag=poormanprotei-20&linkId=amzn1.campaign.1OO1S5W7ZMYBB_1751469633605'>silicone spatula</a>, smoothing out the top.  Optionally, top with a few extra chocolate chips
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Sw Pot Blondies/blondies-raw.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Bake in a preheated 350F oven for about 20 minutes, or until lightly golden brown and set. A toothpick should come out almost clean, and the <a href='https://amzn.to/4kmobG2'>internal temperature</a> will be around <a href='https://amzn.to/4kmobG2'>190F</a>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Sw Pot Blondies/blondies-baked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Let cool in the pan in the fridge for at least 30 minutes before slicing with a plastic knife
Notes:
- For more <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a> flavor and <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a>, replace the <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oat flour</a> with <a href='https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07SXBL1GF?ref=t_ac_view_request_product_image&campaignId=amzn1.campaign.2L4DOI4F1KV3G&linkCode=tr1&tag=poormanprotei-20&linkId=amzn1.campaign.2L4DOI4F1KV3G_1767022097417'>powdered peanut butter</a>.  To turn into a <a href='/brownies'>brownie</a> instead (pictured below), replace the <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oat flour</a> with <a href='/misc/overshadowed-healthy-foods#chocolate'>cocoa powder</a>.  Or check out any of my other <a href='/brownies'>brownie recipes</a>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Sw Pot Blondies/sw-pot-brownies.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- I've recently found these <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>no sugar</a> dark chocolate chips by <a href='https://www.walmart.com/ip/Bake-Believe-Keto-Friendly-Dark-Chocolate-Chips-9-oz-Pouch/314343390?athbdg=L1600&from=/search'>Bake Believe</a> at Walmart that are my favorite sugar free chocolate chips.  They use <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#sugar-substitutes'>erythritol</a> and stevia, and are cheaper than any other sugar free chocolate chip brand I've seen (sometimes they're even cheaper than the fancy brands of regular <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar filled</a> chocolate chips).  They also come in milk chocolate and semi sweet varieties.  Not an ad; just a fan
