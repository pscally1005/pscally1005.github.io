Description: The classic Internet/Pinterest/TikTok healthy dessert is some combination of bananas, chocolate chips, oats, and peanut butter (I'm sure you've noticed). This recipe is my take on that formula, and works really well out of the freezer as a dessert or even a breakfast. You could also use some protein powder in place of oats if you so desire!  For a baked recipe with similar ingredients, see my <a href="/recipes/peanut-butter-banana-bake">Peanut Butter Banana Bars</a>
Instructions: 
- Line an 8" square pan with parchment paper<br><br>

- Mash the ripe bananas with a fork (or blend them and add them to the bowl). Mix in the peanut butter, vanilla, and salt until fully combined.  Stir in the oat flour.  Fold in most of your chocolate chips, reserving some for the top<br><br>
- <center><img src="/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Cookie Bar/cookie-bar-4.jpg" alt="" class="instruction-image"></center><br>

- Evenly press your dough into all sides of the pan.  Freeze for about 2 hours, then slice. Store in the freezer
