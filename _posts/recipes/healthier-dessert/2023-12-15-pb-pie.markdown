Description: Peanut butter pie is a classic Christmas stable, but it's filled with tons of unhealthy <a href='/misc/fats'>fats</a> and <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugars</a>, making it super <a href='/misc/calories'>calorie</a> dense. This recipe boosts the <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> content while cutting back on the <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar</a> and <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a>, but still keeping that great flavor, while also being dairy free. It combines a <a href='/recipes/pie-crust'>Homemade Graham Cracker Pie Crust</a> with the <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>peanut butter</a> and <a href='/misc/fruit#banana'>banana</a> filling that you'll love.  If you have a peanut allergy, just swap out the <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>peanut butter</a> and <a href='https://amzn.to/4mZuQXM'>powdered peanut butter</a> for <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>almond butter</a> and <a href='https://amzn.to/48awAJ9'>powdered almond butter</a>
Instructions:
- Prepare your <a href='/recipes/pie-crust'>homemade pie crust</a> according to the recipe.  My standard <a href='/recipes/pie-crust'>Healthier Pie Crust</a> is a regular graham craham cracker pie crust, though you can make it chocolate by using <a href='/recipes/gf-crackers'>Chocolate Graham Crackers</a>.  Alternatively, you can also use any storebought pie crust if you prefer.
- <center><a href='/recipes/gf-crackers'><img src='/assets/Food/Finger Food/GF Crackers/cover.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'></a></center>
- <center><a href='/recipes/pie-crust'><img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Pie Crust/pie.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'></a></center>

- Mash your bananas with the back of a fork in a large bowl.  Combine together the rest of the filling ingredients with a <a href='https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0C37QM1K3?ref=t_ac_view_request_product_image&campaignId=amzn1.campaign.1OO1S5W7ZMYBB&linkCode=tr1&tag=poormanprotei-20&linkId=amzn1.campaign.1OO1S5W7ZMYBB_1751469633605'>silicone spatula</a> until fully combined

- Transfer the mixture into the crust, smoothing the top with a <a href='https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0C37QM1K3?ref=t_ac_view_request_product_image&campaignId=amzn1.campaign.1OO1S5W7ZMYBB&linkCode=tr1&tag=poormanprotei-20&linkId=amzn1.campaign.1OO1S5W7ZMYBB_1751469633605'>silicone spatula</a>. Optionally, top with chocolate chips (1/4 cup, 60g), or here I used a crumbled chocolate chip cookie

- Chill in the fridge for about 4 hours before slicing
