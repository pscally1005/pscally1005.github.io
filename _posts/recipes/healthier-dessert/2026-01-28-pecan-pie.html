A good dessert is a good dessert. These no bake pecan pie bars feature an oat flour based crust, a honey filling, and a crushed pecan topping. It's refined sugar free, gluten free, and can be made vegan if using maple syrup or sugar free syrup Instructions: - In a large glass bowl, combine together the crust ingredients - oat flour, melted coconut oil, vanilla extract, liquid monk fruit, cinnamon, and salt - - Line a 9x5" bread pan with parchment paper. Lightly wet your hands to prevent sticking, and evenly press the crust into the pan - - Rinse out your bowl, and combine together all filling ingredients with a silicone spatula - honey, almond butter, vanilla, cinnamon, and salt - - Microwave the filling for 30 seconds. Pour into the pan, smoothing it around - - Top with chopped pecans - - Chill in the fridge for at least 2 hours before slicing - Notes: - For the sweetener, you can either use honey (1/4 cup, 84 g), maple syrup (1/4 cup, 80 g), or sugar free syrup (1/4 cup, 80 g) --- {% assign files = "pecanpiebarshoney," | append: "pecanpiebarsmaple," | append: "pecanpiebarssfs," | split: "," %} {% assign titles = "Honey," | append: "Maple
