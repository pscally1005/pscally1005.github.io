Description: |
  These <a href='/brownies'>brownies</a> are lower in both <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar</a> and <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a> while compromising on flavor as little as possible. They're not <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar free</a> nor <a href='/misc/fats'>fat free</a>. They still contain <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>added sugar</a> from <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#honey'>honey</a> and chocolate chips, and well as the <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a> from the <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>unsweetened chocolate</a>.<br><br>

  These fudgy, flourless, <a href='/misc/celiac'>gluten free</a> <a href='/brownies'>brownies</a> serve as a great introductory <a href='/recipes/healthier-dessert'>healthy baking dessert</a>. By splitting the sweetening between <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#honey'>honey</a> and <a href='https://amzn.to/3SqwsMO'>liquid monk fruit</a>, this keeps the <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>added sugar</a> and <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a> lower.<br><br>

  The <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>unsweetened chocolate</a> provides a rich chocolate flavor for less <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a> than a stick of <a href='/misc/dairy#butter'>butter</a>. And the <a href='/misc/veggies#sweet-potato'>sweet potato</a> provides both sweetness and moistness that would typically come from the <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>white sugar</a> and added <a href='/misc/dairy#butter'>butter</a>.<br><br>

  Compared to <a href='/recipes/brownies'>my standard brownie recipe</a>, this batch contains about half the <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a> and a quarter of the <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>added sugar</a>, even when you account for adding chocolate chips
Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 350F. Line a <a href='https://amzn.to/3YY2H9q'>9" baking pan</a> with parchment paper, and <a href='https://amzn.to/3Hdg0gk'>lightly grease with oil</a>

- In a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>large bowl</a>, mash your <a href='/misc/veggies#sweet-potato'>sweet potato</a> with the back of a fork until smooth. Stir in the following with a <a href='https://amzn.to/40F2aK0'>silicone spatula</a> - <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>egg</a>, <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#honey'>honey</a>, <a href='https://amzn.to/3SqwsMO'>liquid monk fruit</a>, vanilla, baking powder, and salt

- Break your <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>chocolate</a> into chunks, and add to a small microwave safe bowl. Microwave for 1 minute, stirring halfway, until you have a smooth melted chocolate

- Pour the melted chocolate into the <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>large bowl</a>, and mix until fully combined. Fold in most of the chocolate chips, reserving some for the top
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Reduced Sugar Brownies/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Transfer the batter to the pan, and smooth out the top. Top with your remaining chocolate chips, and lightly them press down into the batter
# - <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Reduced Sugar Brownies/raw.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Bake at 350F for about 30 minutes, or until the top is just set and a toothpick to the center comes out with some wet crumbs. The <a href='https://amzn.to/4kmobG2'>internal temperature</a> should <a href='https://amzn.to/4kmobG2'>register</a> about <a href='https://amzn.to/4kmobG2'>200F</a>
# - <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Reduced Sugar Brownies/baked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Transfer to the fridge. Let chill for at least 30 minutes to cool down before cutting with a plastic knife
# - <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Reduced Sugar Brownies/cut.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- If you want to save on some <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar</a>, <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a>, and <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a>, you can omit the chocolate chips
