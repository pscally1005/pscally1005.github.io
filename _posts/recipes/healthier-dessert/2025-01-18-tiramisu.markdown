Description: Tiramisù is a classic Italian dessert that consists of layers of <a href='/misc/caffeine'>coffee</a> and rum dipped Ladyfingers and sweet whipped mascarpone cheese, all topped with dusted <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>cocoa powder</a>. Compared to the traditional, my version is healthier, as it contains <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>no sugar</a> or <a href='/misc/celiac'>gluten</a>, and is also <a href='/misc/caffeine'>coffee</a> and alcohol free. I never used to be a big Tiramisù fan until this Christmas where I was converted, and I instantly knew I had to try making a variation of it myself. This recipe is very similar to my <a href='/recipes/cheesecake-bars'>No Bake Cheesecake Bars</a>, using a very similar base and filling.
Instructions:
- Line an <a href='https://amzn.to/3YY2H9q'>9" square baking pan</a> with parchment paper, and set aside

- Mash your <a href='/misc/fruit#banana'>bananas</a> with the back of a fork in a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>large bowl</a>. Combine with the rest of the base ingredients using a <a href='https://amzn.to/40F2aK0'>silicone spatula</a> - cinnamon, <a href='https://amzn.to/3SqwsMO'>liquid monk fruit</a>, vanilla extract, <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut flour</a>, and <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>rolled oats</a>. Optionally, add 1/2 tsp (2.5 g) of either rum extract or instant <a href='/misc/caffeine'>coffee</a> (or both!)
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Tiramisu/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Firmly press half the dry mixture (196 g) into the base of your pan
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Tiramisu/bottom.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- In a <a href='https://amzn.to/4q0AUjI'>food processor</a>, blend together <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>nut butter</a> and <a href='/misc/fruit#dates'>dates</a> until fully chopped. Add in the <a href='/misc/dairy#yogurt'>Greek yogurt</a> and vanilla extract, and blend to fully combine
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Tiramisu/processor.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Transfer half your yogurt filling (350 g) on top of the crust. Crumble the remaining oatmeal mixture on top of the first layer of filling, and top with a second layer of filling
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Tiramisu/middle-filling.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Tiramisu/middle-crumble.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Using a small <a href='https://amzn.to/4q2FwWu'>fine mesh strainer</a>, dust the top of the filling with <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>cocoa powder</a>. Use a damp paper tower to clean up the edges of the pan
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Tiramisu/cocoa.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Cover the pan with foil, and refrigerate overnight before slicing
Notes:
- Alternatively, you can use 1/4 cup (80 g) of <a href='/recipes/sugar-free-syrup'>sugar free syrup</a> in the filling in place of 4 oz (113 g) <a href='/misc/fruit#dates'>dates</a>.  Note that half the filling weight will be 333 g, not 350 g
