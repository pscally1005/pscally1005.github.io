Instructions: 
- Preheat your oven to 350F, and line a cookie sheet with parchment paper.  In a food processor, blend together chickpeas, raisins, nut butter, cinnamon, and vanilla until smooth, and no chunks of beans or raisins remain
- <center><img src="/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Oatmeal Raisin/no-milk.jpg" alt="" class="instruction-image"></center>

- <center></center><i>Substitutions</i>:<br><ul><li>Any light colored beans will work, not just chickpeas. Try cannellini, navy, or pinto</li><li>Dates or dried figs can be used in place of raisins</li><li>Any <a href="/recipes/natural-peanut-butter">natural nut butter</a> will work; I'm using almond here, but cashew butter or tahini are also good options</li><li>Unsweetened almond milk is what I have today, but either dairy milk or water will work as well</li><li>Both my beans and nut butter are salted.  If yours are unsalted, add 1/4 tsp (1.5 g) salt</li></ul>

- When fully combined, blend in milk, baking soda, and baking powder briefly to combine.  Transfer to a large bowl
- <center><img src="/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Oatmeal Raisin/with-milk.jpg" alt="" class="instruction-image"></center>

- Fold in the rolled oats and raisins with a <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0C37QM1K3?ref=t_ac_view_request_product_image&campaignId=amzn1.campaign.1OO1S5W7ZMYBB&linkCode=tr1&tag=poormanprotei-20&linkId=amzn1.campaign.1OO1S5W7ZMYBB_1751469633605">silicone spatula</a>.  The cookie dough should be very loose, almost more like the consistency of a brownie batter
- <center><img src="/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Oatmeal Raisin/bowl.jpg" alt="" class="instruction-image"></center>

- Using spoon, scoop the cookie dough onto to the pan, about 23 g each. These cookies won't flatten or spread as they bake, and will only puff up slightly
- <center><img src="/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Oatmeal Raisin/raw.jpg" alt="" class="instruction-image"></center>

- Bake for about 15 minutes at 350F, or until the tops and edges are lightly golden brown and the cookies are set to the touch.  Let them cool totally on a pan before removing to an airtight container.  Store in the fridge
- <center><img src="/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Oatmeal Raisin/baked.jpg" alt="" class="instruction-image"></center>
