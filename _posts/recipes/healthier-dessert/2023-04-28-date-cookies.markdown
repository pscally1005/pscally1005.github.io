Description: |
  These healthy chocolate chip <a href='/cookies'>cookies</a> use <a href='/misc/fruit#dates'>date</a> paste and <a href='/misc/nuts#almonds'>almond flour</a> as a base, and avoids using <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar</a>, <a href='/misc/dairy#butter'>butter</a>, and <a href='/misc/grains#white-wheat'>white flour</a>. If you're anything like me and find most <a href='/recipes/healthier-dessert'>desserts</a> way too sweet, you'll find that you enjoy these <a href='/cookies'>cookies</a> much more than any traditional chocolate chip <a href='/cookies'>cookie</a>.<br><br>

  <a href='/misc/celiac'>Gluten free</a>, <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>refined sugar free</a>, only sweetened with natural <a href='/misc/fruit#dates'>dates</a> (<a href='/misc/fruit#raisins'>raisins</a> will work as well), and the only <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a> is delicious <a href='/misc/nuts#almonds'>almond flour</a>.  Or see my <a href='/recipes/date-brownie'>Almond Butter Date Brownies</a> or <a href='/recipes/chocolate-chip-date-cookies'>Chocolate Chip Date Cookies</a> for a similar <a href='/misc/celiac'>gluten free</a> <a href='/recipes/healthier-dessert'>desserts</a> using <a href='/misc/fruit#dates'>dates</a> and <a href='/misc/nuts'>nuts</a>.  Or check out any of my other <a href='/cookies'>cookie recipes</a>!
Instructions:
- Preheat oven to 350F and line a large <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>cookie sheet</a> with parchment paper

- Blend the <a href='/misc/fruit#dates'>dates</a> in a <a href='https://amzn.to/4q0AUjI'>food processor</a>. Blend in <a href='/misc/nuts#almonds'>nut flour</a> too.  Blend until you have a crumbly looking dough, but slightly sticky

- Add the rest of the ingredients (except for the chocolate chips) and blend to combine

- Fold in chocolate chips. Let the batter chill in the fridge for an hour to make it easier to work with
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Date Cookie/date-cookie-4.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Scoop and roll into balls. Place on the <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>cookie sheet</a>. Lightly press down, and shape into a circle. Wetting your hands a little helps the dough from sticking everywhere
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Date Cookie/date-cookie-5.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Bake at 350F for about 8 minutes. The cookies should just turn golden but still be soft in the middle. They will puff up a little but not really expand outward.  Let cool on the sheet for a few minutes, then transfer to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4qQNmn2'>wire rack</a>
Notes:
- You can also use whole <a href='/misc/nuts'>nuts</a> (any <a href='/misc/nuts'>nuts</a> will do) instead of <a href='/misc/nuts#almonds'>nut flour</a> too.  I used whole <a href='/misc/nuts#almonds'>almonds</a>, so I technically used <a href='/misc/nuts#almonds'>almond meal</a> instead of <a href='/misc/nuts#almonds'>almond flour</a>

- Try making an ice cream sandwich with some <a href='/recipes/nice-cream'>banana ice cream</a>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Healthier Dessert/Date Cookie/date-cookie-7.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Or leave out the baking soda and enjoy as edible cookie dough bites
