Description: |
  Do you know those <a href='https://link.amazon/B030CixA0'>Carb Balance wraps</a>? The <a href='/misc/carbs'>low carb</a> ones with a whopping 18 g of <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a> per wrap, and only 60 <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a>? Well I made a <a href='/copycat'>copycat</a> version of those today.<br><br>

  <a href='https://link.amazon/B04N3ngTi'>Oat fiber</a> is our star ingredient, providing a substantial amount of <a href='/misc/fiber'>dietary fiber</a> with no <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a>. It's essentially the isolated <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a> from <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oats</a>. <a href='/misc/grains#vital-wheat-gluten'>Vital wheat gluten</a> adds <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> and structure, keeping these wraps from falling apart.<br><br>

  These wraps contain nearly identical nutrition to the <a href='https://link.amazon/B030CixA0'>storebought ones</a>, containing very similar <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a>, <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a>, <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a>, <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a>, and <a href='/misc/carbs'>net carbs</a>!<br><br>

  For another version of a low carb wrap, check out my <a href='/recipes/psyllium-wrap'>High Fiber Psyllium Wraps</a>, which contains only 11 <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a> and 7 g of <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a>.
Instructions:
- Whisk together the dry ingredients in a <a href='https://amzn.to/4zyFnzN'>large bowl</a> - <a href='https://link.amazon/B04N3ngTi'>oat fiber</a>, <a href='/misc/grains#vital-wheat-gluten'>vital wheat gluten</a>, <a href='/misc/grains#whole-wheat'>whole wheat flour</a>, salt, and baking powder.

- Add in the <a href='/misc/olive-oil'>oil</a> and water, and mix until you form a dough. Knead for about 3 minutes. Cover and let the dough rest on the counter for 30 minutes.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/Carb Balance/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Divide the dough into 8 equal pieces, about <b>61 g</b> each.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/Carb Balance/divided.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Roll each portion as thin and evenly as possible between 2 sheets of parchment paper, about 1/16" thick and 8" diameter.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/Carb Balance/raw.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Heat a dry, <a href='https://amzn.to/465aAxx'>cast iron</a> (or <a href='https://amzn.to/4rdR0HI'>nonstick</a>) pan over medium heat. Cook each tortilla for about 1 minute on each side, or until the bottom develops a few light brown spots.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/Carb Balance/cooked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Transfer to a plate, and cover with a clean towel to prevent it from drying out. Repeat with the remaining tortillas.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/Carb Balance/plate.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Store in the fridge in an airtight bag for up to 1 week, or in the freezer indefinitely.
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/Carb Balance/pizza.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
