Description: After the success of my <a href='/recipes/almond-flour-rolls'>Almond Flour Rolls</a>, I knew I had to scale the recipe up into a loaf of bread. The result is easily the best <a href='/misc/celiac'>gluten free</a> <a href='/recipes/bread'>bread</a> I've ever made or had (sorry <a href='/recipes/gluten-free-millet-bread'>Gluten Free Millet Bread</a>, you've been dethroned). This <a href='/misc/nuts#almonds'>almond flour</a> <a href='/recipes/bread'>bread</a> is able to perfectly mimic the texture of standard <a href='/misc/grains#whole-wheat'>wheat based</a> <a href='/recipes/bread'>bread</a>, without the <a href='/misc/celiac'>gluten</a> and <a href='/misc/carbs'>refined grains</a>.  Plus, this bread is higher in <a href='/misc/fats'>healthy fats</a>, <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a>, and <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a> than traditional bread. All without any yeast, meaning there's no waiting time
Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 375F, and lightly grease a <a href='https://amzn.to/3YUjIkN'>9x5" bread pan</a>

- Whisk together your dry ingredients in a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>large bowl</a> - <a href='/misc/nuts#almonds'>almond flour</a>, psyllium husk, <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut flour</a>, baking powder, <a href='/misc/seeds#flax-seeds'>ground flax</a>, and salt

- Pour the water into a glass, and microwave for 1 minute. Add the water and <a href='/misc/apple-cider-vinegar'>apple cider vinegar</a> to the dry ingredients, and mix until you have a fully combined dough

- Shape the dough into a large ball, and let the dough rest for about 10 minutes
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/Almond Flour Loaf/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Transfer the dough to the loaf pan, and firmly press down
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/Almond Flour Loaf/raw.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Bake for about 75 minutes at 375F, or until lightly browned on the top, and a toockpick comes out totally clean. Transfer to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4qQNmn2'>wire rack</a> to cool before slicing
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/Almond Flour Loaf/baked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- |
  The sandwich pictured below and above combines 2 slices of this almond flour bread with 1 sliced banana, a dash of cinnamon, and a peanut butter spread consisting of the following
  <ul>
    <li>4 tbsp (24 g) powdered peanut butter</li>
    <li>1 tsp (4 g) granulated monk fruit</li>
    <li>1/2 tsp (2.5 g) almond extract</li>
    <li>Tiny pinch of salt</li>
    <li>3 tbsp (42 g) unsweetened almond milk</li>
  </ul>

  This is my healthier take on a Peanut Butter Banana Sandwich, which has <a href='/misc/fats'>healthy fats</a> and <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a> from the almond flour bread, <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> from the powdered peanut butter, and <a href='/misc/carbs'>complex carbs</a> from the banana, while avoiding the <a href='/misc/carbs'>simple carbs</a> from <a href='/misc/grains#white-wheat'>white bread</a> and <a href='/misc/fats'>hydrogenated oils</a> and <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>added sugar</a> of conventional peanut butter.  I could have used <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>natural peanut butter</a> here, but with the bread already being high in <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a>, I wanted a lower <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a> peanut spread.  The sandwich has the following macros
