Description: After the success of my <a href='/recipes/almond-flour-rolls'>Almond Flour Rolls</a>, I knew I had to scale the recipe up into a loaf of bread. The result is easily the best <a href='/misc/celiac'>gluten free</a> bread I've ever made or had (sorry <a href='/recipes/gluten-free-millet-bread'>Gluten Free Millet Bread</a>, you've been dethroned). This almond flour bread is able to perfectly mimic the texture of standard wheat based bread, without the gluten and refined grains.  Plus, this bread is higher in <a href='/misc/fats'>healthy fats</a>, <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a>, and <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a> than traditional bread. All without any yeast, meaning there's no waiting time
Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 375F, and lightly grease a 9x5" bread pan

- Whisk together your dry ingredients in a large bowl - almond flour, psyllium husk, coconut flour, baking powder, ground flax, and salt

- Pour the water into a glass, and microwave for 1 minute. Add the water and apple cider vinegar to the dry ingredients, and mix until you have a fully combined dough

- Shape the dough into a large ball, and let the dough rest for about 10 minutes
- <center><img src='/assets/Food/Bread/Almond Flour Loaf/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'></center>

- Transfer the dough to the loaf pan, and firmly press down
- <center><img src='/assets/Food/Bread/Almond Flour Loaf/raw.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'></center>

- Bake for about 75 minutes at 375F, or until lightly browned on the top, and a toockpick comes out totally clean. Transfer to a wire rack to cool before slicing
- <center><img src='/assets/Food/Bread/Almond Flour Loaf/baked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'></center>

- The sandwich pictured below and above combines 2 slices of this almond flour bread with 1 sliced banana, a dash of cinnamon, and a peanut butter spread consisting of the following:<ul><li>4 tbsp (24 g) powdered peanut butter</li><li>1 tsp (4 g) granulated monk fruit</li><li>1/2 tsp (2.5 g) almond extract</li><li>Tiny pinch of salt</li><li>3 tbsp (42 g) unsweetened almond milk</li></ul><br>This is my healthier take on a Peanut Butter Banana Sandwich, which has <a href='/misc/fats'>healthy fats</a> and <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a> from the almond flour bread, <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> from the powdered peanut butter, and <a href='/misc/carbs'>complex carbs</a> from the banana, while avoiding the <a href='/misc/carbs'>simple carbs</a> from <a href='/misc/grains#white-wheat'>white bread</a> and <a href='/misc/fats'>hydroginated oils</a> and <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>added sugar</a> of conventional peanut butter.  I could have used <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>natural peanut butter</a> here, but with the bread already being high in <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a>, I wanted a lower <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a> peanut spread.
