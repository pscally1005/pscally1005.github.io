Description: These tortillas are made of a healthy blend of <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oat flour</a> and <a href='/misc/grains#whole-wheat'>whole wheat flour</a>, providing a great source of <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a>. The <a href='/misc/fruit#apple'>applesauce</a> serves to replace <a href='/misc/dairy#butter'>butter</a> or <a href='/misc/fats'>oil</a>, and you can't taste it at all, so feel free to top it with some <a href='/misc/fats'>healthy fats</a> like <a href='/misc/fruit#avocado'>avocado</a> or <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a>.  Or use them as a base for <a href='/recipes/fajitas'>Chicken Fajitas with Peppers</a>, <a href='/recipes/taco'>Sauteed Taco Meat and Fajita Veggies</a>, or <a href='/recipes/low-fodmap-tacos'>Low FODMAP Chicken Tacos</a>
Instructions:
- In a bowl, whisk together <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oat flour</a>, <a href='/misc/grains#whole-wheat'>whole wheat flour</a>, cornstarch, baking powder and salt

- Microwave water in a small glass for 1 minute. Add the hot water and <a href='/misc/fruit#apple'>applesauce</a>, and mix with a spoon

- Transfer the dough to a floured surface and lightly knead for 1-2 minutes. Wrap it up in plastic wrap, and let rest for 15 minutes

- Cut the dough into 6 equal portions (~72 g each), roll them into balls, cover, and let rest for 10 more minutes

- Roll each ball with a rolling pin into a round shape tortilla. Roll as thin as possible
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/Tortillas/tortilla-5.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Cook on dry stainless steel <a href='https://amzn.to/4bOh0on'>10"</a> skillet over high heat (~7.5) for about 30-45 seconds per side, or until bubbles start to form and lightly browned on the bottom

- Cover your tortillas with clean kitchen towel to keep warm and soft

- When cooled, transfer to a bag and store in fridge or freezer
