Description: These tortillas are made of a healthy blend of <a href="/misc/grains#oats">oat flour</a> and <a href="/misc/grains#whole-wheat">whole wheat flour</a>, providing a great source of <a href="/misc/fiber">fiber</a>. The applesauce serves to replace <a href="/misc/dairy#butter">butter</a> or oil, and you can't taste it at all, so feel free to top it with some healthy <a href="/misc/fats">fats</a> like <a href="/misc/fruit#avocado">avocado</a> or <a href="/misc/nuts#peanuts">peanut</a> butter.  Or use them as a base for <a href="/recipes/fajitas">Chicken Fajitas with Peppers</a>, <a href="/recipes/taco">Sauteed Taco Meat and Fajita Veggies</a>, or <a href="/recipes/low-fodmap-tacos">Low FODMAP Chicken Tacos</a>
Instructions: 
- In a bowl, mix together ground oats, whole wheat flour, cornstarch, baking powder and salt. Mix well

- Microwave water in a small mason jar for a minute. Add the hot water and applesauce, and using a spoon, mix until dough starts to form

- Transfer the dough to a floured surface and lightly knead for a minute or 2. Wrap it up in plastic wrap and let rest for 15 minutes

- Cut the dough into 6 equal portions (~72 g each), roll them into balls, cover and let rest for 10 more minutes, covered

- Roll each ball with a rolling pin into a round shape tortilla. Roll as thin as possible
- <center><img src="/assets/Food/Bread/Tortillas/tortilla-5.jpg" alt="" class="instruction-image"></center>

- Cook on dry stainless steel 10" skillet over high heat (~7.5) for about 30-45 seconds per side, or until bubbles start to form and lightly browned on the bottom

- Cover your tortillas with clean kitchen towel to keep warm and soft

- When cooled, transfer to a bag and store in fridge or freezer
