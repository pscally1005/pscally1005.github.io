Description: These easy and healthy <a href='/recipes/bread'>bread</a> rolls are made from just <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>rolled oats</a>, <a href='/misc/dairy#cottage-cheese'>cottage cheese</a>, and <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>eggs</a>. They take only a few minutes to prep and rest, and they bake up nice and thin and crispy, perfect for a sandwich or toast.  Here, I made a pulled <a href='/misc/meat#chicken-breast'>chicken</a> sandwich using my <a href='/recipes/salsa-chicken'>Two Ingredient Salsa Chicken</a>.
Instructions:
- In a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>medium bowl</a>, beat your <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>eggs</a> with a fork until smooth. Add in your (blended) <a href='/misc/dairy#cottage-cheese'>cottage cheese</a>, baking powder, and salt. Mix with a <a href='https://amzn.to/40F2aK0'>silicone spatula</a> until fully combined

- Add in the <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>rolled oats</a>, and fold together until well combined. The batter should look too wet; let chill in the fridge for at least 30 minutes, so the <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oats</a> can soak up the excess moisture
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/CC rolls/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Preheat your oven to 400F, and line a <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>cookie sheet</a> with parchment paper. Scoop the batter onto the pan (about 100 g each). Lightly wet your hands, and shape into an even circle. Optionally, top with everything bagel seasoning
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/CC rolls/raw.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Bake at 400F for about 20 minutes, or until browned and firm to the touch
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/CC rolls/baked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Transfer to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4qQNmn2'>wire rack</a> to cool before slicing in half. Store in an airtight bag in the fridge for up to 1 week, or indefinitely in the freezer
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/CC rolls/closed.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
