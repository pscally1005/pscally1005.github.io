Description: Cloud <a href='/recipes/bread'>bread</a> is a <a href='/misc/carbs'>low carb</a> <a href='/recipes/bread'>bread</a> substitute made with whipped <a href='/misc/meat#egg-whites'>egg whites</a>. It's lower in <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a> and higher in <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> than regular <a href='/recipes/bread'>bread</a>, and it can be made in just a few minutes. It can be even lower in <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a> and <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a> if you omit the <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>yolks</a>, but I choose not to as <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>egg yolks</a> are still highly nutritious; plus what would I do with the extra <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>yolk</a> then? Just 1 <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>egg</a> is enough 2 small slices for a sandwich, or one large flatbread for a pizza.
Instructions:
- Separate your <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>egg yolks</a> from the <a href='/misc/meat#egg-whites'>whites</a>. Add the <a href='/misc/meat#egg-whites'>whites</a> to a large metal or <a href='https://amzn.to/4zyFnzN'>glass bowl</a>. Not plastic, or the <a href='/misc/meat#egg-whites'>egg whites</a> won't beat properly. Carton <a href='/misc/meat#egg-whites'>egg whites</a> are not recommended as those are harder to beat as well
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/Cloud Bread/cloud-1.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Add baking powder and baking soda to the <a href='/misc/meat#egg-whites'>egg whites</a>. Using a <a href='https://amzn.to/45yqsbM'>hand mixer</a>, whip to form stuff peaks
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/Cloud Bread/cloud-2.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- In a separate bowl, to the <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>yolks</a>, add garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and <a href='/misc/dairy#yogurt'>yogurt</a>. Mix together with a <a href='https://amzn.to/40F2aK0'>spatula</a>

- Carefully fold the <a href='/misc/meat#egg-whites'>egg whites</a> into the <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>yolks</a>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/Cloud Bread/cloud-4.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Scoop the batter into a parchment lined <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>baking pan</a>, forming 6 equal sized disks (or 3 larger ones for a pizza)
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/Cloud Bread/cloud-5.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Bake for 26 minutes in a preheated 300F oven. While the pizza is bigger, it's also a bit thinner, so both the pizza and the smaller bread should take the same time. Let cool totally on the pan before removing
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/Cloud Bread/cloud-6.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- To reheat for a pizza, top with <a href='/recipes/savory-sauces'>sauce</a>, <a href='/misc/dairy#mozzarella'>cheese</a>, and toppings, and bake at 400F for 5 minutes until warm and melty.  The <a href='/recipes/savory-sauces'>sauce</a> here is my <a href='/recipes/avocado-pesto'>Avocado Pesto - Vegan and Oil Free</a>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/Cloud Bread/cloud-7.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
