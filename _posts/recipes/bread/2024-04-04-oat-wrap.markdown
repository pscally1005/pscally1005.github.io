Description: These super simple oat flour wraps contain just 3 ingredients - oats, water, and salt. They're gluten free and oil free, great for both savory and sweet fillings. Great uses for these wraps are <a href="/recipes/taco">Sauteed Taco Meat</a> or <a href="/recipes/spaghetti-taco">Spaghetti Tacos with Zoodles</a> for dinner, or fill it with fruit, greek yogurt, and peanut butter for breakfast
Instructions: 
- Add all ingredients to a blender. Allow to soak for 30 minutes before blending until smooth

- Preheat your pan over medium heat with a light spray of oil. Pour batter in center the center of the pan, about 1/3 cup (80 g). Spread out the batter with a spoon

- Cook for about 2-3 minutes on the first side, until the edges lift off the pan, and you can flip the tortilla without breaking it.  Cook for an additional 1-2 minutes on the other side, and transfer to a plate to cool, covered with a towel to keep soft.  Repeat with the remaining batter

- With any broken tortillas, you can air fry at 300F for about 15 minutes to make tortilla chips
