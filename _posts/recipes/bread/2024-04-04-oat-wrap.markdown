Description: These super simple <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oat flour</a> wraps contain just 3 ingredients - <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oats</a>, water, and salt. They're <a href='/misc/celiac'>gluten free</a> and <a href='/misc/fats'>oil</a> free, great for both savory and sweet fillings. Great uses for these wraps are <a href='/recipes/taco'>Sauteed Taco Meat</a> or <a href='/recipes/spaghetti-taco'>Spaghetti Tacos with Zoodles</a> for dinner, or fill it with <a href='/misc/fruit'>fruit</a>, <a href='/misc/dairy#yogurt'>greek yogurt</a>, and <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a> for <a href='/recipes/breakfast'>breakfast</a>.
Instructions:
- Add all ingredients to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4bO4VQ3'>blender</a>. Allow to soak for 30 minutes before blending until smooth

- Preheat your pan over medium heat with a light <a href='https://amzn.to/3Hdg0gk'>spray of oil</a>. Pour batter in center the center of the pan, about 1/3 cup (80 g). Spread out the batter with a spoon

- Cook for about 2-3 minutes on the first side, until the edges lift off the pan, and you can flip the tortilla without breaking it.  Cook for an additional 1-2 minutes on the other side, and transfer to a plate to cool, covered with a towel to keep soft.  Repeat with the remaining batter
Notes:
- With any broken tortillas, you can <a href='https://amzn.to/3FuWETp'>air fry</a> at 300F for about 15 minutes to make tortilla chips
