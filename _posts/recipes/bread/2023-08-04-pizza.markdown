Description: Everybody loves pizza right? This is a 2 day process, but totally worth it for a good homemade pizza, plus it's a fun activity to do with a few people. Prep the dough the night before, and then have a few people over the next day for dinner. Whole wheat flour serves to add some extra nutrition and taste to your pizza as well.
Instructions: 
- In a large mixing bowl, combine the water, yeast, salt, and honey. Stir, and let sit for 5 minutes to make sure the yeast is alive

- To the bowl, add the oil, and flours. Mix with a dough scraper and lightly knead in the bowl.  Cover with a cloth and let rest for 1 hour
- <center><img src="/assets/Food/Bread/Pizza/pizza-2.jpg" alt="" class="instruction-image"></center>

- In the bowl, fold the dough into a smooth ball, and place back in the bowl. Cover and let rest for another 1 hour. Repeat one more time. (Total of three 1 hour rests with stretching and folding)

- Turn dough onto the table. Divide into 4 equal pieces (about 145 g), and shape into balls. Place dough balls in lightly oiled bowls, and cover with plastic wrap. Refrigerate until dinner tomorrow night
- <center><img src="/assets/Food/Bread/Pizza/pizza-5.jpg" alt="" class="instruction-image"></center>

- Place baking stone in the oven and preheat the oven to 550F, or as high as your oven goes. Preheat stone for an hour

- Shape dough into pizzas. Top with sauce, cheese, and toppings. I like to use either my <a href="/recipes/pasta-sauce">Simple Pasta Sauce</a> or <a href="/recipes/creamy-pesto">Creamy Pesto Dip</a>, as well as some <a href="/recipes/baked-chicken-thighs">Spiced Baked Chicken Thighs</a> and <a href="/recipes/roasted-vegetables">Simply Roasted Vegetables</a>

- Bake for about 7 minutes, or until the crust and bottom is lightly browned, and the cheese is just bubbly.  Transfer to a wire rack to cool for 5 minutes before cutting into 4 slices
