Description: This recipe makes 1 loaf of standard <a href='/misc/grains#whole-wheat'>whole wheat</a> <a href='/recipes/bread'>bread</a>, and is a staple in my freezer for sandwiches. The white vinegar gives it a slight sour flavor, without being too sour like sourdough for my taste. The <a href='/recipes/bread'>bread</a> is best served warm, and works great on a savory sandwich or a <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a> and jelly. I always advocate for gram measurements instead of volume, and it is especially important when <a href='/recipes/bread'>baking bread</a>. Please use a <a href='https://amzn.to/45yjx2X'>scale</a> for this, the volume measurements are just estimations. For the ultimate PB&J, you should make <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>Homemade Natural Nut Butter</a> and pair it with <a href='/recipes/apple-spread'>No Added Sugar Apple Spread</a>
Instructions:
- Add your water to a glass, and microwave for 1 minute to warm it.  Add yeast, salt, and warm water to a large mixing bowl, and mix.  Optionally add in any desired spices, vinegar, <a href='/misc/fats'>oil</a>, or honey.  Let sit for 5 minutes to make sure the yeast is still alive
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/Whole Wheat Bread/ww-1.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- In a separate bowl, whisk together <a href='/misc/grains#whole-wheat'>whole wheat flour</a> and <a href='/misc/grains#vital-wheat-gluten'>vital wheat gluten</a>.  Add the dry into the wet, mix until fully combined, and let rest for 30 minutes. Start with a <a href='https://amzn.to/44XmqKz'>dough scraper</a>, then switch to your hands. It will feel too dry and crumbly, but it will come together
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/Whole Wheat Bread/ww-2.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- 30 minutes later, turn the dough onto the table and knead for 10 minutes. When done, shape the dough into a tight ball, return dough to bowl (seam down), cover with a cloth, and let rise for 1-1.5 hours
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/Whole Wheat Bread/ww-3.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- It will have about doubled after the initial rest
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/Whole Wheat Bread/ww-4.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- If doubling the recipe to make 2 loaves - Lightly dust the counter with flour. Divide the dough in half, form each dough into a tight ball, cover, and let rest for 15 minutes. If you're just making 1 loaf, you can skip this intermediary rest

- Grease a <a href='https://amzn.to/3YUjIkN'>bread pan</a> with <a href='https://amzn.to/3Hdg0gk'>cooking spray</a> or olive oil. Shape the dough into a loaf and place in the pan. Optionally, dust the top with flour or everything bagel seasoning, and score with a knife or razor blade
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/Whole Wheat Bread/ww-6.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Let rest on the counter for 1-1.5 hours, or in the fridge overnight. It will have about doubled again
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/Whole Wheat Bread/ww-7.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Bake for about 45 minutes at 400F. The <a href='/recipes/bread'>bread</a> should release from the pan after a few minutes. If not, continue to bake until it does. If you get it out and the bottom isn't done (it should sound hollow when tapped) bake the <a href='/recipes/bread'>bread</a> directly on the bars until the bottom is crispy
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/Whole Wheat Bread/ww-8.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- When done, remove loaf from the pan and let cool on a <a href='https://amzn.to/4qQNmn2'>wire rack</a> until completely cooled, at least one hour. Cut into slices, and store in freezer or on the counter. Slicing the loaf upside down makes it easier
