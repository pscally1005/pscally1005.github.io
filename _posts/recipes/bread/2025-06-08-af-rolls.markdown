Description: If you need rolls or bagels and you need them now, then this is the perfect recipe for you. Not only do these require no proofing, but they're also <a href='/misc/celiac'>gluten free</a>, as they're made with <a href='/misc/nuts#almonds'>almond flour</a>, psyllium husk, and <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut flour</a> instead of <a href='/misc/grains#white-wheat'>wheat flour</a>. These rolls are free of empty <a href='/misc/carbs'>refined starches</a>, and instead contain satiating <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a>, <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a>, and <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> from the <a href='/misc/nuts#almonds'>almonds</a>, <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut</a>, psyllium, and <a href='/misc/seeds#flax-seeds'>flax</a>. This means that unlike typical bread, these rolls are a <a href='/misc/diabetes'>low glycemic</a> food, while also being good sources of <a href='/misc/nutrient-alphabet#E'>vitamin E</a>, <a href='/misc/calcium'>calcium</a>, and <a href='/misc/iron'>iron</a>.  If instead you want typical slices of bread, check out my <a href='/recipes/almond-flour-loaf'>Almond Flour Sandwich Loaf</a>.
Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 375F, and line a large <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>cookie sheet</a> with parchment paper

- Whisk together your dry ingredients in a <a href='https://amzn.to/4zyFnzN'>large bowl</a> - <a href='/misc/nuts#almonds'>almond flour</a>, psyllium husk, <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut flour</a>, baking powder, <a href='/misc/seeds#flax-seeds'>ground flax</a>, and salt

- Pour the water into a glass, and microwave for 1 minute. Add the water and <a href='/misc/apple-cider-vinegar'>apple cider vinegar</a> to the dry ingredients, and mix until you have a fully combined dough

- Shape the dough into 1 large ball, and let the dough rest for about 5 minutes before dividing into 6 servings, about 80 g each
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/Almond Flour Rolls/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Roll each piece into balls, and flatten down. You can either make these into rolls, or carve out a hole in the center and make it a bagel. I'll be doing both here
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/Almond Flour Rolls/raw.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Optionally, brush the tops with water, and top with everything bagel seasoning

- Bake for about 40 minutes at 375F, or until lightly browned on the bottom. Transfer to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4qQNmn2'>wire rack</a> to cool before slicing
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/Almond Flour Rolls/stack.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
