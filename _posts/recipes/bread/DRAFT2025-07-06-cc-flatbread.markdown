Description: Is cottage cheese still having its moment in the sun?  I hope so, because this gluten and grain free bread is high in <a href="/misc/protein">protein</a>, low in <a href="/misc/carbs">carbs</a>, and dead simple to make. Just blend together cottage cheese and eggs, spread on a cookie sheet, and bake.  This flatbread makes a great base for a healthy wrap or pizza
Instructions: 
- Preheat your oven to 350F, and line a 9x13" pan with parchment paper

- Combine together all ingredients in a food processor, and blend until smooth. Evenly spread the batter into your pan
- <center><img src="/assets/Food/Bread/CC flatbread/raw.jpg" alt="" class="instruction-image"></center>

- Bake at 350F for about 40 minutes, or until lightly browned. Let it totally cool before cutting in half and using it as a wrap
