Description: I have strong memories of French baguettes and Italian <a href='/recipes/bread'>bread</a> from holidays growing up.  This recipe is simply my <a href='/recipes/ww-bread'>100% Whole Wheat Bread</a>, but made into 4 small baguettes instead of a loaf of sandwich <a href='/recipes/bread'>bread</a>.  It's great toasted on it's own, or used for <a href='/recipes/breaded-chicken'>bruschetta</a>.
Instructions:
- Add your water to a glass, and microwave for 1 minute to warm it.  Add yeast, salt, and warm water to a large mixing bowl, and mix.  Let sit for 5 minutes to make sure the yeast is still alive

- In a separate bowl, whisk together <a href='/misc/grains#whole-wheat'>whole wheat flour</a> and <a href='/misc/grains#vital-wheat-gluten'>vital wheat gluten</a>.  Add the flours into the wet bowl, and mix until well combined with a <a href='https://amzn.to/44XmqKz'>dough scraper</a>.  Cover with a cloth and let rest for 30 minutes

- 30 minutes later, turn the dough onto the table and knead for 10 minutes. When done, shape the dough into a tight ball, return dough to bowl (seam down), cover with a cloth, and let rise for 1-1.5 hours.  It will have about doubled after the initial rest
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/Whole Wheat Bread/ww-3.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/Baguette/baguette-doubled.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Divide the dough in 4 equal pieces (about 210 g), form each dough into a tight ball, cover, and let rest for 15 minutes
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/Baguette/baguette-balls.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Roll each piece into a tight baguette, pinching the seam shut.  Transfer the loaves to a <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>baking sheet</a>, dust the tops with flour, and score with a <a href='https://amzn.to/43Cj65h'>bread lame</a>.  Cover and let rest for 1 hour
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/Baguette/baguette-raw.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Meanwhile, preheat your oven to 450F with the <a href='https://amzn.to/4ccXgLm'>baking stone</a> on the middle shelf, and a <a href='https://amzn.to/4aiCsjh'>9x13" pan</a> on the bottom shelf for the water.  You can bake on a <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>cookie sheet</a> instead, but the bottom won't be as crispy.  The water pan is to steam the bread, making the crust crispier.  This is optional as well

- Put the baguettes into the oven onto the <a href='https://amzn.to/4ccXgLm'>stone</a> (or on the <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>cookie sheet</a>), and fill the bottom tray with hot water.  Bake for 15 minutes, or until the <a href='https://amzn.to/4kmobG2'>internal temperature</a> is at least 205F, and the bottom sounds hollow when tapped.  Transfer to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4qQNmn2'>wire rack</a>, and let cool for 10 minutes before slicing
- <img src='/assets/Food/Bread/Baguette/baguette-cooled.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
