Description: Did you know that Sweetgreen has <a href='https://amzn.to/3FuWETp'>air fried</a> <a href='/misc/veggies#potato'>potato</a> <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#potatoes'>fries</a> with pickle <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#dips'>ketchup</a>? This here is my recreation of the healthy dip. It's a <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>refined sugar free</a> <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#dips'>ketchup</a>, that's both sweet and spicy from <a href='/recipes/hot-honey'>Homemade Hot Honey</a>. It get's its pickle flavor from the dried dill, and is the perfect condiment to <a href='/recipes/waffle-fries'>Sweet Potato Waffle Fries</a>, <a href='/recipes/black-bean-burger'>Protein Black Bean Burgers</a>, <a href='/recipes/chicken-fingers'>Gluten Free Chicken Fingers</a>, and <a href='/recipes/burger-patties'>Simple Burger Patties</a>.  But it's great on even more; try it on your <a href='/recipes/salad-base'>salads</a> for a great tangy and sweet <a href='/salad-dressings'>dressing</a>.  Check out my <a href='/recipes/ketchup'>Date Sweetened Ketchup</a> for a similar recipe.  Or if you're more of a <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#dips'>barbeque sauce</a> fan, be sure to make my <a href='/recipes/bbq-sauce'>Unsweetened BBQ Sauce</a>!
Instructions:
- Whisk together all the ingredients in a <a href='https://amzn.to/46ccg8m'>medium saucepot</a>. Cover, and cook over low heat for about 20 minutes, or until bubbling and thickened
- <img src='/assets/Food/Savory Sauces/Pickle Honey Ketchup/pot.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Transfer to a large <a href='https://amzn.to/4vamaBm'>mason jar</a>, and let cool at room temperature (uncovered) for about 30 minutes before covering with a lid and refrigerating. Store in the fridge for up to 1 week, or freeze indefinitely
Notes:
- Instead of <a href='/recipes/hot-honey'>hot honey</a>, you can also use 4 tbsp (84 g) <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#honey'>honey</a> and 1 tsp (2 g) red pepper flakes. You can also omit the red pepper flakes and dill for a more classic <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#dips'>ketchup</a>
