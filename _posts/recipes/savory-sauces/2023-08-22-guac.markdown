Description: Guacamole is one of my favorite dips, and it's already pretty healthy.  <a href='/misc/fruit#avocado'>Avocados</a> are a rich source of both <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a> and <a href='/misc/fats'>heart healthy mono-unsaturated fats</a>.  Instead of nullifying the health benefits of guac by pairing it with <a href='/misc/processed-foods'>ultra-processed</a> <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#chips'>tortilla chips</a>, you can use guacamole as a dip for raw <a href='/misc/veggies'>veggies</a> like <a href='/misc/veggies#pepper'>peppers</a> or <a href='/misc/veggies#carrots'>carrots</a>, as a <a href='/salad-dressings'>salad dressing</a>, or as a spread for your next <a href='/misc/fruit#avocado'>avocado</a> toast.  Guacamole is also perfect with <a href='/recipes/taco'>Sauteed Taco Meat and Fajita Veggies</a>!
Instructions:
- Cut your <a href='/misc/veggies#onion'>onion</a> and <a href='/misc/veggies#tomato'>tomato</a> into a fine dice, and add to a bowl (or mortar and pestle).  Scoop in your <a href='/misc/fruit#avocado'>avocado</a>, and add in the garlic powder, <a href='/misc/fruit#lime'>lime</a>, salt, and black pepper.  Mash until nearly smooth
Notes:
- Optionally add some spices (like paprika and chili powder). It will taste better, but the guac will lose its green color.  Optionally garnish with everything bagel seasoning and dried parsley or cilantro
