This <a href='/recipes/savory-sauces'>dip</a> takes inspiration from <a href='/recipes/balsamic-vinaigrette'>balsamic vinaigrette</a>, <a href='/misc/fruit#olives'>olive</a> tapenade, and <a href='/hummus'>hummus</a>, blending together <a href='/misc/fruit#olives'>olives</a> with balsamic vinegar and spices for the perfect <a href='/salad-dressings'>salad dressing</a> or raw <a href='/misc/veggies'>veggie</a> <a href='/recipes/savory-sauces'>dip</a>

- Drain and rinse your olives. Add all ingredients to a food processor, and blend until smooth. Season with salt to taste (will depend on your olives), and store in the fridge
