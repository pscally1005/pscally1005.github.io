Description: As someone who eats a lot of <a href='/salad'>salad</a>, you may be surprised to hear that I don't use <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#salad-dressing'>bottled salad dressing</a>.  That's because these are often filled with <a href='/misc/chronic-inflammation'>inflammatory</a> <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#seed-oil'>seed oils</a>, tons of <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>added sugar</a>, preservatives you can't pronounce, and artificial food dyes. Instead, you can make your own <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#salad-dressing'>Italian dressing</a> that contains <a href='/misc/fats'>healthy fats</a> from <a href='/misc/nuts#cashews'>cashews</a> and <a href='/misc/carbs'>natural sweetness</a> from sundried <a href='/misc/veggies#tomato'>tomatoes</a>.  Not only is <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#salad-dressing'>Italian dressing</a> great on <a href='/salad'>salads</a>, but I also use it in both my <a href='/recipes/fajitas'>Chicken Fajitas with Peppers</a> and <a href='/recipes/tropical-salsa'>Tropical Fruit Salsa</a>.  For a similar <a href='/salad-dressings'>salad dressing</a> recipe, make my <a href='/recipes/balsamic-vinaigrette'>Oil Free Balsamic Vinaigrette</a>, or you can check out any of my other <a href='/salad-dressings'>salad dressings</a>!
Instructions:
- Soak the sun dried tomatoes and <a href='/misc/nuts#cashews'>cashews</a> in warm water for 10 minutes.  Add all ingredients to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4bO4VQ3'>blender</a> or <a href='https://amzn.to/4q0AUjI'>food processor</a>, and blend until smooth.  It should be pretty thick
