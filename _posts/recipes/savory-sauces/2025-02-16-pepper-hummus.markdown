Description: I've always loved both roasted red <a href='/misc/veggies#pepper'>peppers</a> and roasted <a href='/misc/veggies#pepper'>pepper</a> <a href='/hummus'>hummus</a>, so I finally gave it a shot here. The <a href='/misc/veggies#pepper'>peppers</a> add a great smokey and sweet flavor to the <a href='/hummus'>hummus</a>, as well as a delicious orange color. It's mildly spicy from the cayenne, and works great on top of <a href='/salad'>salads</a>.  You can also check out any of my other <a href='/salad-dressings'>salad dressings</a> or <a href='/hummus'>hummus recipes</a>!
Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 400F, and line a <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>cookie sheet</a> with parchment paper. Slice the <a href='/misc/veggies#pepper'>peppers</a> into large pieces, season with oil and salt, and bake for 30 minutes, or until the <a href='/misc/veggies#pepper'>peppers</a> are soft and charred
- <img src='/assets/Food/Savory Sauces/Pepper Hummus/pepper-hummus-1.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Drain and rinse the <a href='/misc/beans#chickpeas'>chickpeas</a>, and add to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4q0AUjI'>food processor</a>. Blend with the <a href='/misc/veggies#pepper'>peppers</a>, <a href='/misc/fruit#lemon'>lemon</a>, <a href='/misc/nuts#almonds'>almond butter</a> (or <a href='/misc/seeds#sesame-seeds'>tahini</a>), and <a href='/misc/veggies#garlic'>minced garlic</a> until smooth

- Add in your spices, and blend to combine. Adjust anything to taste, and store in the fridge
