Description: Who doesn't love a good tzatziki?  This Greek sauce uses a base of <a href='/misc/dairy#yogurt'>plain Greek yogurt</a> and <a href='/misc/veggies#cucumber'>cucumber</a> mixed with <a href='/misc/fruit#lemon'>lemon</a> and dill for a light and refreshing <a href='/recipes/savory-sauces'>sauce</a> or <a href='/salad-dressings'>dressing</a> to nearly everything, in particular my <a href='/recipes/salmon-and-tzatziki'>Roasted Salmon and Sprouts</a>.  Pair this with a size of <a href='/recipes/stuffed-grape-leaves'>Stuffed Grape Leaves</a> and a <a href='/recipes/greek-salad'>Greek Salad</a>, and you have a delicious combo!
Instructions:
- Finely dice your <a href='/misc/veggies#cucumber'>cucumber</a>, and add to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>medium bowl</a> with salt. Cover and refrigerate for about an hour, so the salt can draw out water from the <a href='/misc/veggies#cucumber'>cucumber</a>

- Drain out the excess water.  Add in the <a href='/misc/dairy#yogurt'>yogurt</a>, <a href='/misc/fruit#lemon-juice'>lemon juice</a>, <a href='/misc/veggies#garlic'>minced garlic</a>, garlic powder, onion powder, and fresh dill. Cover and refrigerate until ready to use
- <img src='/assets/Food/Savory Sauces/Tzatziki/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- You can also use 1 tbsp (12 g) lemon pepper in place of 1 tsp (6 g) salt

- You can also use 2 tbsp (6 g) dried dill in place of 2 tbsp (2 g) fresh dill
