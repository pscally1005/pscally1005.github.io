Description: I'm back with more dip recipes. After the success of warm cheesy dips like <a href='/recipes/buffalo-chicken-dip'>High Protein Buffalo Chicken Dip</a> and <a href='/recipes/spinach-artichoke-dip'>Lightened Up Spinach Artichoke Dip</a>, I knew I had to try this dip when I came across it. Bean dip is like a warm, cheesy hummus, but this one is made without any <a href='/misc/dairy#cream-cheese'>cream cheese</a>, mayonnaise, or sour cream. Instead, with <a href='/misc/dairy#yogurt'>Greek yogurt</a> as it's base, this dip is lower in <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a> and higher in <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a>, leaving you feeling satisfied instead of gross
Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 400F. Drain and rinse a can of beans.  Any canned beans will work.  Today, I'm using red kidney beans, but black, pinto, navy, chickpeas, or anything else will work in place

- Combine all ingredients (except the cheese) in a food processor, and blend until smooth and no bean chunks remain
- mixture to a 9" square pan, and top with your shredded cheese
- for about 15 minutes (uncovered), or until lightly bubbly. Let rest for a few minutes before serving
