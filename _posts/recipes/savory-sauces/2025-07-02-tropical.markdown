Description: This sweet, sour, and spicy tropical <a href="/misc/fruit">fruit</a> salsa is perfect topping for <a href="/recipes/fajitas">Chicken Fajitas with Peppers</a>. It features finely diced <a href="/misc/fruit#pineapple">pineapple</a>, <a href="/misc/fruit#mangos">mango</a>, and red <a href="/misc/veggies#onion">onion</a>, flavored with hot sauce, lime juice, and a <a href="/recipes/italian-dressing">Homemade Sugar Free Italian Dressing</a>. 
Instructions: 
- Finely dice your pineapple, mango, and onion, and add to a large bowl

- Mix with the remaining ingredients - <a href="/recipes/italian-dressing">Italian dressing</a>, hot sauce, lime juice, cilantro (or parsley), thyme, allspice, nutmeg, and cinnamon
