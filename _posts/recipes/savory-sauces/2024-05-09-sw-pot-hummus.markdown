Description: After making my <a href='/recipes/air-fried-falafel'>🧆 Air Fryer Falafel (GF) 🥙</a>, I realized that <a href='/misc/veggies#sweet-potato'>sweet potato</a> and <a href='/misc/beans#chickpeas'>chickpeas</a> are a fantastic combination.  This <a href='/misc/veggies#sweet-potato'>sweet potato</a> <a href='/hummus'>hummus</a> combines the two together perfectly, and has the addition of <a href='/misc/chronic-inflammation'>anti-inflammatory</a> spices, such as <a href='/misc/veggies#ginger'>ginger</a>, turmeric, and cinnamon.  It's creamy and delicious on a <a href='/salad'>salad</a> or wrap, has a great orange hue, and is high in <a href='/misc/fiber'>fiber</a> while being lower in <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a> than store-bought hummus.  You can also check out any of my other <a href='/salad-dressings'>salad dressings</a> or <a href='/hummus'>hummus recipes</a>!
Instructions:
- Wash your <a href='/misc/veggies#sweet-potato'>sweet potato</a> with a brush, and prick with a fork. Wrap in aluminum foil, and <a href='https://amzn.to/3FuWETp'>air fry</a> at 400F for 1 hour. Slice open to let steam out as you prepare the rest of the ingredients

- Drain and rinse your <a href='/misc/beans#chickpeas'>chickpeas</a> using a strainer. Add to a large <a href='https://amzn.to/4q0AUjI'>food processor</a> with <a href='/misc/veggies#sweet-potato'>sweet potato</a> (including the skin!), <a href='/misc/fruit#lemon'>lemon</a>, <a href='/misc/veggies#garlic'>garlic</a>, and <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>nut butter</a>.  I've used <a href='/misc/nuts#pistachios'>pistachio butter</a> here, but you can use <a href='/misc/nuts#almonds'>almond butter</a>, <a href='/misc/seeds#sesame-seeds'>tahini</a>, or any other natural <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>nut/seed butter</a> you have. Blend until smooth

- Add in your spices, and blend to fully combine. Adjust anything to taste, and store in the fridge
