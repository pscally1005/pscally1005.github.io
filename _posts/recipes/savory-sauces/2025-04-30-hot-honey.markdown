Description: Hot Honey is a popular condiment that's basically just <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#honey'>honey</a> infused with <a href='/recipes/hot-sauce'>hot sauce</a>. Not only has Vic been wanting this for a while, but I also wanted to try the viral <a href='/recipes/hot-honey-beef-bowls'>Hot Honey Beef Bowls</a>. So instead of buying it, today I'm making my own hot <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#honey'>honey</a>. It's done in less than 10 minutes, and adds a fantastic sweet and spicy flavor into anything you drizzle it on.  Other uses of hot <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#honey'>honey</a> include my <a href='/recipes/hot-honey-cornbread'>Hot Honey Cornbread</a>
Instructions:
- Add <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#honey'>honey</a> to a medium sauce pot over medium low heat. Cook for about 2 minutes, or until the <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#honey'>honey</a> loosens up

- Remove from the heat, and stir in <a href='/recipes/hot-sauce'>hot sauce</a> and red pepper flakes

- Transfer to a mason jar, and let cool at room temperature before putting on the lid. Store in a cabinet
