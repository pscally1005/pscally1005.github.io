Description: This one is for all you <a href='/misc/veggies#garlic'>garlic</a> lovers out there. The <a href='/misc/veggies#garlic'>garlic</a> is roasty, pungent, and sweet. It's "cheesy" from the nutritional yeast, but you can use grated <a href='/misc/dairy#grated-cheese'>parmesan</a> instead if you like.  You can also check out any of my other <a href='/salad-dressings'>salad dressings</a> or <a href='/hummus'>hummus recipes</a>!
Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 400F. Cut the top off the <a href='/misc/veggies#garlic'>garlic</a>, <a href='https://amzn.to/3Hdg0gk'>lightly spray with oil</a>, and wrap in aluminum foil. Roast for 45 minutes, or until golden and soft.  You can also defrost 2 cubes of <a href='/misc/garlic-paste'>frozen garlic paste</a> if you desire instead
- <img src='/assets/Food/Savory Sauces/Garlic Hummus/garlic-hummus-1.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Drain and rinse the <a href='/misc/beans#chickpeas'>chickpeas</a>, and add to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4q0AUjI'>food processor</a>. Squeeze the cloves of <a href='/misc/veggies#garlic'>garlic</a> out of the head into the <a href='https://amzn.to/4q0AUjI'>food processor</a>. Blend the <a href='/misc/veggies#garlic'>garlic</a> and <a href='/misc/beans#chickpeas'>chickpeas</a> with the <a href='/misc/fruit#lemon-juice'>lemon juice</a> and <a href='/misc/nuts#almonds'>almond butter</a> (or <a href='/misc/seeds#sesame-seeds'>tahini</a>) until smooth

- Season with nutritional yeast and your spices, and blend to combine. Adjust anything to taste, and store in the fridge
