Description: Standard bottles of <a href="https://amzn.to/4kFH0n6">mayonnaise</a> are filled with cheap and inflammatory vegetable oils, <a href="/misc/hidden-sugar">sugar</a>, preservatives, and artificial ingredients. Instead, make this healthy swap in all your mayo-based dishes, like potato salad, deviled eggs, macaroni salad, French Onion dip, and tuna salad. The secret is Greek yogurt, which is low and <a href="/misc/fats">fat</a> and high in <a href="/misc/protein">protein</a>, making this mayo significantly lower in <a href="/misc/calories">calories</a>.  If you prefer hollandaise to mayo, be sure to check out my <a href="/recipes/hollandaise">Greek Yogurt Hollandaise Sauce</a>.  Or check out any of my other <a href="/salad-dressings">salad dressings</a>!
Instructions: 
- In a medium bowl, mix together all ingredients until smooth. Store in an airtight container in the fridge for up to a week
