Description: As someone who eats a lot of <a href='/salad'>salad</a>, you may be surprised to hear that I don't use <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#salad-dressing'>bottled salad dressing</a>.  That's because these are often filled with <a href='/misc/chronic-inflammation'>inflammatory</a> <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#seed-oil'>seed oils</a>, tons of <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>added sugar</a>, preservatives you can't pronounce, and artificial food dyes. Instead, you can make your own balsamic that contains <a href='/misc/fats'>healthy fats</a> from <a href='/misc/fruit#olives'>olives</a> and <a href='/misc/nuts#walnuts'>walnuts</a>, and natural sweetness from <a href='/misc/fruit#dates'>dates</a>.  For a similar recipe, make my <a href='/recipes/italian-dressing'>Homemade Sugar Free Italian Dressing</a>, or you can check out any of my other <a href='/salad-dressings'>salad dressings</a>!
Instructions:
- Soak the <a href='/misc/nuts#walnuts'>walnuts</a> and <a href='/misc/fruit#dates'>dates</a> in warm water for 10 minutes.  Add all ingredients to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4bO4VQ3'>blender</a> or <a href='https://amzn.to/4q0AUjI'>food processor</a>, and blend until smooth.  It should be pretty thick
