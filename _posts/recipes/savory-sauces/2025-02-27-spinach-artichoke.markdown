Description: Classic Spinach Artichoke Dip is a game day staple, but also a <a href='/misc/calories'>calorie</a> bomb. I lightened it up here by avoiding all the <a href='/misc/dairy#cream-cheese'>cream cheese</a>, <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#dips'>mayo</a>, sour cream, and <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#dips'>ranch</a>, but still while preserving that great taste. This goes great with some <a href='/misc/veggies#carrots'>carrots</a>, <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#chips'>chips</a>, or on a <a href='/salad'>salad</a>.  This dip is significantly lower in <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a> and <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a>, while also being higher in <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a>, making it more filling and full of <a href='/misc/nutrient-alphabet'>nutrients</a> compared to the original.  Other great gameday dips are <a href='/recipes/buffalo-chicken-dip'>High Protein Buffalo Chicken Dip</a> and <a href='/recipes/french-onion-dip'>Low Fat French Onion Dip</a>, which I both highly recommend checking out
Instructions:
- Preheat oven to 400F.  Drain and rinse your <a href='/misc/veggies#artichoke'>artichokes</a>, and chop into small pieces.  Squeeze your defrosted <a href='/misc/veggies#spinach-fresh'>spinach</a> in a paper towel over the sink until water no longer drips out

- Directly in a <a href='https://amzn.to/3YY2H9q'>9" square baking dish</a>, mix together the <a href='/misc/veggies#artichoke'>artichokes</a>, <a href='/misc/veggies#spinach-fresh'>spinach</a>, <a href='/misc/dairy#yogurt'>yogurt</a>, <a href='/misc/dairy#cottage-cheese'>cottage cheese</a>, 1/2 cup (56 g) <a href='/misc/dairy#mozzarella'>shredded mozzarella</a>, 1/2 cup (56 g) <a href='/misc/dairy#cheddar'>shredded cheddar</a>, <a href='/misc/dairy#grated-cheese'>grated cheese</a>, olive oil, and black pepper.  Top with your remaining 1/4 cup (28 g) of <a href='/misc/dairy#mozzarella'>shredded mozzarella</a> and 1/4 cup (28 g) <a href='/misc/dairy#cheddar'>shredded cheddar</a>
- <img src='/assets/Food/Savory Sauces/Artichoke/raw.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Bake at 400F for 25 minutes (uncovered), or until lightly bubbly.  Let cool for a few minutes before serving
