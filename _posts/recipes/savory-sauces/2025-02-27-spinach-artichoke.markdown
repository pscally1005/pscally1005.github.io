Description: Classic <a href='/misc/veggies#spinach-fresh'>Spinach</a> Artichoke Dip is a game day staple, but also a <a href='/misc/calories'>calorie</a> bomb. I lightened it up here by avoiding all the <a href='/misc/dairy#cream-cheese'>cream cheese</a>, mayo, sour cream, and ranch, but still while preserving that great taste. This goes great with some <a href='/misc/veggies#carrots'>carrots</a>, chips, or on a salad.  This dip is significantly lower in <a href='/misc/calories'>calories</a> and <a href='/misc/fats'>fat</a>, while also being higher in <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a>, making it more filling and full of nutrients compared to the original.  Other great gameday dips are <a href='/recipes/buffalo-chicken-dip'>High Protein Buffalo Chicken Dip</a> and <a href='/recipes/french-onion-dip'>Low Fat French Onion Dip</a>, which I both highly recommend checking out
Instructions:
- Preheat oven to 400F.  Drain and rinse your artichokes, and chop into small pieces.  Squeeze your defrosted spinach in a paper towel over the sink until water no longer drips out

- Directly in a 9" square baking dish, mix together the artichokes, spinach, yogurt, cottage cheese, 1/2 cup (56 g) shredded mozzarella, 1/2 cup (56 g) shredded cheddar, grated cheese, olive oil, and black pepper.  Top with your remaining 1/4 cup (28 g) of shredded mozzarella and 1/4 cup (28 g) shredded cheddar
- <img src='/assets/Food/Savory Sauces/Artichoke/raw.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Bake at 400F for 25 minutes (uncovered), or until lightly bubbly.  Let cool for a few minutes before serving
