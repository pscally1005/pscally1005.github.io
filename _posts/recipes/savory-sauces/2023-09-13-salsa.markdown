Description: This 5 minute salsa uses pantry staple canned ingredients and some spices. No chopping necessary! Just pulse everything in a food processor, and adjust the taste and texture to your liking.  This salsa also contains no garlic or onion for those that are allergic.  I love to make this with either my <a href="/recipes/taco">Sauteed Taco Meat and Fajita Veggies</a> or <a href="/recipeslow-fodmap-tacos">Low FODMAP Chicken Tacos</a>
Instructions: 
- Lightly drain the can of tomatoes through a gap in the can top.  Don't use a strainer or you'll lose too much

- Add all ingredients to a food processor, and pulse a few times until desired consistency is reached.  Season with salt to taste, and store in the fridge

- <center></center><i>Note 1</i> - If you have finely diced tomatoes you don't even need a food processor, just a bowl<br>

- <center></center><i>Note 2</i> - Save the tomato liquid for cooking. I like to throw a splash of it in with the <a href="/recipes/taco">taco meat</a>, since I often make these two at the same time<br>

- <center></center><i>Note 3</i> - You can substitute soy sauce with another 1 tbsp (15 g) of hot sauce
