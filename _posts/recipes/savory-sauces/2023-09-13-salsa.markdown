Description: This 5 minute salsa uses pantry staple canned ingredients and some spices. No chopping necessary! Just pulse everything in a <a href='https://amzn.to/4q0AUjI'>food processor</a>, and adjust the taste and texture to your liking.  This salsa also contains no <a href='/misc/veggies#garlic'>garlic</a> or <a href='/misc/veggies#onion'>onion</a> for those that are allergic.  I love to make this with either my <a href='/recipes/taco'>Sauteed Taco Meat and Fajita Veggies</a> or <a href='/recipeslow-fodmap-tacos'>Low FODMAP Chicken Tacos</a>
Instructions:
- Lightly drain the can of <a href='/misc/veggies#tomato'>tomatoes</a> through a gap in the can top.  Don't use a strainer or you'll lose too much

- Add all ingredients to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4q0AUjI'>food processor</a>, and pulse a few times until desired consistency is reached.  Season with salt to taste, and store in the fridge
Notes:
- If you have petite diced tomatoes you don't even need a <a href='https://amzn.to/4q0AUjI'>food processor</a>, just a bowl

- Save the <a href='/misc/veggies#tomato'>tomato</a> liquid for cooking. I like to throw a splash of it in with the <a href='/recipes/taco'>taco meat</a>, since I often make these two at the same time

- You can substitute soy sauce with another 1 tbsp (15 g) of <a href='/recipes/hot-sauce'>hot sauce</a>
