Description: This 5 minute salsa uses pantry staple canned ingredients and some spices. No chopping necessary! Just mix everything in a bowl, and adjust the taste and texture to your liking.  This salsa also contains no <a href='/misc/veggies#garlic'>garlic</a> or <a href='/misc/veggies#onion'>onion</a> for those that are allergic.  I love to make this with either my <a href='/recipes/taco'>Sauteed Taco Meat and Fajita Veggies</a> or <a href='/recipeslow-fodmap-tacos'>Low FODMAP Chicken Tacos</a>.
Instructions:
- Mix together all ingredients in a medium bowl.  Adjust any salt to taste, and store in the fridge for up to 1 week
Notes:
- You can substitute soy sauce with another 2 tbsp (30 g) of <a href='/recipes/hot-sauce'>hot sauce</a>
