Description: |
  Mint has a reputation for growing like a weed, so let's put some of <a href='/vic'>Vic's</a> to good use. Here's a recipe for dairy free creamy mint sauce. It has a base of soaked <a href='/misc/nuts#cashews'>cashews</a> and <a href='/misc/beans#chickpeas'>chickpeas</a> to make it creamy while still keeping it lactose free.<br><br>

  This Mediterranean-ish, <a href='/hummus'>hummus</a>-like <a href='/recipes/savory-sauces'>dip</a> is a perfect alternative to <a href='/recipes/tzatziki'>tzatziki</a>, and pairs well with <a href='/recipes/marinated-chicken'>grilled chicken</a>, <a href='/recipes/roasted-vegetables'>roasted veggies</a>, or <a href='/recipes/air-fried-falafel'>falafel</a>. Or try it as a <a href='/salad-dressings'>salad dressing</a> or <a href='/recipes/savory-sauces'>dip</a> for your raw <a href='/misc/veggies#carrots'>carrots</a>.
Instructions:
- Add your <a href='/misc/nuts#cashews'>cashews</a> to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4zyFnzN'>small bowl</a>. Pour in just the liquid from the can of <a href='/misc/beans#chickpeas'>chickpeas</a>. Let the <a href='/misc/nuts'>nuts</a> soak in the aquafaba for at least 2 hours at room temperature to soften

- Add all ingredients (<a href='/misc/nuts#cashews'>cashews</a>, aquafaba, <a href='/misc/beans#chickpeas'>chickpeas</a>, <a href='/misc/fruit#lemon-juice'>lemon juice</a>, <a href='/misc/apple-cider-vinegar'>apple cider vinegar</a>, <a href='https://amzn.to/3SqwsMO'>liquid monk fruit</a>, and fresh mint) to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4q0AUjI'>food processor</a>. Blend until smooth and creamy
