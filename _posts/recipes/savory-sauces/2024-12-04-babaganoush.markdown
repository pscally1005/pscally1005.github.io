Description: |
  Babaganoush is a delicious Mediterranean dip that's made from roasted <a href='/misc/veggies#eggplant'>eggplant</a> and <a href='/misc/seeds#sesame-seeds'>tahini</a>. It's similar to <a href='/hummus'>hummus</a>, another Mediterranean dip, but essentially just using <a href='/misc/veggies#eggplant'>eggplant</a> instead of <a href='/misc/beans#chickpeas'>chickpeas</a>. The two pair great together on a Greek salad, as a spread for <a href='/recipes/pita'>Whole Wheat Pita Bread</a>, or a dip for <a href='/recipes/stuffed-grape-leaves'>Dolmas (Stuffed Grape Leaves)</a><br><br>

  For a combination of babaganoush and <a href='/hummus'>hummus</a>, check out my <a href='/recipes/roasted-eggplant-hummus'>Mediterranean Roasted Eggplant Hummus</a>. Or view some of my other <a href='/hummus'>hummus recipes</a> as well for similar ideas, such as <a href='/recipes/classic-tahini-hummus'>Classic Tahini Hummus</a>.  You can also check out any of my other <a href='/salad-dressings'>salad dressings</a> or <a href='/hummus'>hummus recipes</a>!
Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 400F, and line a <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>cookie sheet</a> with parchment paper. Wash your <a href='/misc/veggies#eggplant'>eggplant</a>, slice off the ends, and cut in half

- Bake cut side down until the <a href='/misc/veggies#eggplant'>eggplant</a> collapses and is easily pierced with a fork (about 45 minutes). Remove from the oven, flip it over over, and allow it to cool for a few minutes
- <img src='/assets/Food/Savory Sauces/Babaganoush/roasted.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Add the <a href='/misc/veggies#eggplant'>eggplant</a> (with the skin!) and all other ingredients to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4q0AUjI'>food processor</a>. Blend until smooth.  Serve with <a href='/recipes/pita'>pita bread</a>, <a href='/recipes/stuffed-grape-leaves'>dolmas</a>, or over a <a href='/salad'>salad</a>. Store leftovers in the fridge or freezer
- <img src='/assets/Food/Savory Sauces/Babaganoush/blended.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
