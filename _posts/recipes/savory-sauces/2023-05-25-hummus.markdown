Description: <a href='/hummus'>Hummus</a> is a staple in my fridge.  It's so easy to make, very nutritious, and tastes great on just about anything.  I've omitted the oil, and chosen to use homemade <a href='/misc/nuts#cashews'>cashew butter</a> instead of <a href='/misc/seeds#sesame-seeds'>tahini</a>, but any <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>nut or seed butter</a> will work here.  If you like the classic <a href='/misc/seeds#sesame-seeds'>tahini</a> though, go for it!  You can also check out any of my other <a href='/salad-dressings'>salad dressings</a> or <a href='/hummus'>hummus recipes</a>!
Instructions:
- Drain and rinse the <a href='/misc/beans#chickpeas'>chickpeas</a>.  Blend up <a href='/misc/beans#chickpeas'>chickpeas</a> in a <a href='https://amzn.to/4q0AUjI'>food processor</a> with the <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>nut butter</a>, <a href='/misc/fruit#lemon-juice'>lemon juice</a>, and <a href='/misc/veggies#garlic'>garlic</a>

- Once fully blended, add the spices (cumin, chili powder, paprika, onion powder, black pepper, and salt) and blend until well mixed

- Optionally top with everything bagel seasoning. Transfer to a Tupperware and store in the fridge
