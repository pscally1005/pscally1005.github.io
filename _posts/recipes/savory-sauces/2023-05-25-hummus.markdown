Description: Hummus is a staple in my fridge.  It's so easy to make, very nutritious, and tastes great on just about anything.  I've omitted the oil, and chosen to use homemade <a href='/misc/nuts#cashews'>cashew</a> butter instead of <a href='/misc/seeds#sesame-seeds'>tahini</a>, but any <a href='/misc/nuts'>nut</a> or <a href='/misc/seeds'>seed</a> butter will work here.  If you like the classic tahini though, go for it!  You can also check out any of my other <a href='/salad-dressings'>salad dressings</a> or <a href='/hummus'>hummus recipes</a>!
Instructions:
- Drain and rinse the chickpeas.  Blend up chickpeas in a food processor with the nut butter, lemon juice, and garlic

- Once fully blended, add the spices (cumin, chili, paprika, onion, black pepper, and salt) and blend until well mixed

- Optionally top with everything bagel seasoning. Transfer to a Tupperware and store in the fridge
