That's because these are often filled with inflammatory seed oils, tons of added sugar, preservatives you can't pronounce, and artificial food dyes. Instead, you can make your own Italian dressing that contains healthy fats from cashews and natural sweetness from sundried tomatoes. Not only is Italian dressing great on salads, but I also use it in both my Chicken Fajitas with Peppers and Tropical Fruit Salsa. For a similar salad dressing recipe, make my Oil Free Balsamic Vinaigrette, or you can check out any of my other salad dressings! Instructions: - Soak the sun dried tomatoes and cashews in warm water for 10 minutes. Add all ingredients to a blender or food processor, and blend until smooth. It should be pretty thick -
