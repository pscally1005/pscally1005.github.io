Description: Hot Honey is a popular condiment that's basically just honey infused with hot sauce. Not only has Vic been wanting this for a while, but I also wanted to try the viral <a href="/recipes/hot-honey-beef-bowls">Hot Honey Beef Bowls</a>. So instead of buying it, today I'm making my own hot honey. It's done in less than 10 minutes, and adds a fantastic sweet and spicy flavor into anything you drizzle it on
Instructions: 
- Add honey to a medium sauce pot over medium low heat. Cook for about 2 minutes, or until the honey loosens up

- Remove from the heat, and stir in hot sauce and red pepper flakes

- Transfer to a mason jar, and let cool at room temperature before putting on the lid. Store in a cabinet
