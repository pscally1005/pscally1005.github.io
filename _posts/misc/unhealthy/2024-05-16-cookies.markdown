Description: Here's a standard chocolate chip <a href='/cookies'>cookie</a> recipe. I usually only make and post healthy recipes, but it's okay to enjoy the classic treat from time to time. Chocolate chip <a href='/cookies'>cookies</a> are usually what I make to bring for an occasion; that way I can have 1 or 2 every so often, and I can leave someone else with the rest.  I made these to celebrate Vic getting into UF for her Master's Degree.  <a href='/brownies'>Brownies</a> are the other classic dessert, so check out my <a href='/recipes/brownies'>Classic Fudgy Brownies</a>, or check out any of my other healthy <a href='/cookies'>cookie recipes</a>!
Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 350F, and line 2 large <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>cookie sheets</a> with parchment paper

- Mix together <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar</a>, <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#honey'>honey</a>, and (melted) <a href='/misc/dairy#butter'>butter</a> with a <a href='https://amzn.to/45yqsbM'>hand mixer</a> until fluffy. Beat in <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>egg</a> and mix in vanilla, salt, and baking soda. Fully combine, then mix in the flour. Fold in the chocolate chips

- Scoop the cookies, roll into balls, and place on the pan.  These will flatten and spread as they bake, so leave plenty of room in between.  Don't do more than 12 on a standard size <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>cookie sheet</a>

- Bake for about 8-9 min at 350F, or until lightly golden brown on the bottom and the sides. Allow them to cool on the pan for a few minutes, until they can be safely scraped off with a <a href='https://amzn.to/40F2aK0'>spatula</a> without breaking.  Transfer to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4qQNmn2'>wire rack</a> to cool
Notes:
- Chill the dough in the fridge (at least 30 minutes) if using melted <a href='/misc/dairy#butter'>butter</a>. This step is not necessary if you're <a href='/misc/dairy#butter'>butter</a> is softened

- I typically use vanilla extract, but almond extract is also great in these <a href='/cookies'>cookies</a>.  Or, consider using mint extract for mint chocolate chip <a href='/cookies'>cookies</a>.  Mint is much stronger though; don't use any more than 1/4 tsp

- If you're like me and typically only have <a href='/misc/grains#whole-wheat'>whole wheat flour</a> handy (instead of <a href='/misc/grains#white-wheat'>white flour</a>), use 215 g instead of 240 g (just under 2 cups), since <a href='/misc/grains#whole-wheat'>whole wheat flour</a> absorbs more water than <a href='/misc/grains#white-wheat'>all purpose</a>

- If you want to make the <a href='/cookies'>cookies</a> <a href='/misc/celiac'>gluten free</a>, just replace the <a href='/misc/grains#white-wheat'>all purpose flour</a> (2 cups, 240 g) with 1.5 cups (180 g) <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oat</a>, <a href='/misc/nuts#almonds'>almond</a>, or <a href='/misc/grains#millet'>millet flour</a>, and 1/2 cup (60 g) cornstarch

- You can make these into <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a> chocolate chip <a href='/cookies'>cookies</a> as well.  Just add 1/2 cup (128 g) of melted <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a> to the cookie dough along with the <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar</a> and <a href='/misc/dairy#butter'>butter</a>.  Additionally, cut the flour in half, from 2 cups (240 g) to 1 cup (120 g).  Bake as normal for about 9 minutes
- <img src='/assets/Misc/Unhealthy/Cookies/pb-cookie.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Cutting the flour in half also applies to <a href='/misc/celiac'>gluten free</a> and <a href='/misc/grains#whole-wheat'>whole wheat</a> <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a> <a href='/cookies'>cookies</a> as well.  For <a href='/misc/celiac'>GF</a>, use 3/4 cup (90 g) <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oat</a>, <a href='/misc/nuts#almonds'>almond</a>, or <a href='/misc/grains#millet'>millet flour</a>, and 1/4 cup (30 g) of cornstarch in place of the <a href='/misc/grains#white-wheat'>all purpose flour</a> (1 cup, 120 g).  For <a href='/misc/grains#whole-wheat'>whole wheat</a>, use just under 1 cup (108 g) <a href='/misc/grains#whole-wheat'>whole wheat flour</a> instead of the <a href='/misc/grains#white-wheat'>all purpose</a>

- If you want to make these healthier, replace the <a href='/misc/dairy#butter'>butter</a>, <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar</a>, and <a href='/misc/grains#white-wheat'>white flour</a> with <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut oil</a>, <a href='https://amzn.to/4ceFnvP'>granulated monk fruit</a>, and <a href='/misc/nuts#almonds'>almond flour</a>.  Check out my <a href='/misc/healthier-brownies-and-cookies-experiment#cookies-experiment'>Healthier Brownies & Cookies Experiment</a> and <a href='/recipes/best-healthy-cookie-dough'>Best Healthy Cookie Dough</a> for more information
