Description: |
  Marzipan, or almond paste, is a sweet dough made of <a href='/misc/nuts#almonds'>almond flour</a> and <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar</a> that serves as the basis for <a href='/recipes/amaretti'>Amaretti Cookies</a> and <a href='/recipes/rainbow-cookies-v3'>Rainbow Cookies</a>.<br><br>

  Or, you can cut it up into pieces, coat it in chocolate, and have a very simple candy recipe. <a href='/vic'>Vic</a> is a big fan of marzipan chocolate candies, so I'm making a homemade version for her today for Easter!
Instructions:
- Line a <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>cookie sheet</a> with parchment paper, and place a <a href='https://amzn.to/4qQNmn2'>wire rack</a> on top

- Cut your marzipan into 40 pieces, about 1/2 tbsp (10 g) each. Add to the <a href='https://amzn.to/4qQNmn2'>wire rack</a>
- <img src='/assets/Misc/Unhealthy/Marzipan/marzipan.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Add chocolate chips and <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut oil</a> to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>medium bowl</a>. Microwave in 20 second intervals, stirring in between with a <a href='https://amzn.to/40F2aK0'>silicone spatula</a>, until you have a smooth melted chocolate
- <img src='/assets/Misc/Unhealthy/Marzipan/chocolate.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Using 2 forks, dip the marzipan in the <a href='/misc/chocolate-benefits'>chocolate</a>. Transfer to the <a href='https://amzn.to/4qQNmn2'>wire rack</a> so the excess chocolate drips off
- <img src='/assets/Misc/Unhealthy/Marzipan/coated.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Chill for 30 minutes to harden. Store in the fridge
- <img src='/assets/Misc/Unhealthy/Marzipan/cover2.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- |
  Marzipan and almond paste are very similar, though slightly different products.  Below you can see marzipan on the left, and almond paste on the right.  Almond paste has a higher proportion of <a href='/misc/nuts#almonds'>almond flour</a>, while marzipan is a bit higher in <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar</a>.<br><br>

  I used a tube of each here, but either one will work.  Almond paste tastes slightly less sweet with a bit more of an almond flavor compared to marzipan in isolation.<br><br>

  But when they're covered in chocolate they taste identical.  Use whatever you have / can find in your store

  Marzipan can be quite expensive.  On <a href='https://amzn.to/3PCwCCq'>Amazon</a> three 7 oz containers will cost about $16.20. That's quite expensive to make some homemade candy!  That means the entire batch will cost $13.03!<br><br>

  <a href='https://amzn.to/3PCwCCq'>Storebought marzipan</a> <i>may</i> also contain some "wheat starch", meaning that it's not <a href='/misc/celiac'>gluten free</a>.<br><br>

  For less than half the price (and <a href='/misc/celiac'>gluten free</a>), you can make your own marzipan.  Blend together <a href='/misc/nuts#almonds'>almond flour</a> and granulated <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar</a> until combined. Add in almond extract and water to form your marzipan dough.  The whole batch of chocolate covered candies will cost $5.01, with each serving being $0.13
