Description: Vic has been sampling and craving this cake every time we visit the Troy farmer's market, so I've attempted to recreate it here. She loved both the <a href='/misc/fruit#banana'>banana</a> rum cake and the <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut</a> rum cake, so I've combined the two together here. This is a standard dessert recipe, though it does seem to be mildly healthier than a standard cake. The <a href='/misc/fruit#banana'>banana</a> and <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut</a> combination with the rum and maple leads to a delicious tasting cake, like a fancier <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#banana-bread'>banana bread</a>. Vic and I aren't big frosting people, so I just left it out here. But feel free to <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>glaze</a> with any frosting you desire!
Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 350F, and oil a <a href='https://amzn.to/3YUjIkN'>9x5" loaf pan</a>

- In a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>medium bowl</a>, whisk together the dry ingredients - <a href='/misc/grains#white-wheat'>flour</a>, pudding mix, baking powder, baking soda and salt. Set aside

- In a separate <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>medium bowl</a>, mash your <a href='/misc/fruit#banana'>bananas</a> with the back of a fork until smooth. Mix together the wet ingredients until fully combined - <a href='/misc/dairy#yogurt'>yogurt</a>, <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#maple-syrup'>maple syrup</a>, <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>eggs</a>, and rum extract

- In a separate <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>large bowl</a>, cream together <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>coconut oil</a> and <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar</a> with a <a href='https://amzn.to/45yqsbM'>hand mixer</a> until light and fluffy, about 2-3 minutes
- <img src='/assets/Misc/Unhealthy/Rum Cake/4-parts.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Alternate adding in the dry and wet ingredients (about 1/2 cup each at a time) to the bowl with the oil and <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar</a>. Mix with a <a href='https://amzn.to/40F2aK0'>silicone spatula</a>
- <img src='/assets/Misc/Unhealthy/Rum Cake/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Transfer the batter to your loaf pan, and top with <a href='/misc/nuts#coconut'>shredded coconut</a>
- <img src='/assets/Misc/Unhealthy/Rum Cake/raw.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Bake at 350F for about 60 minutes, or until a toothpick comes out clean and the cake has an <a href='https://amzn.to/4kmobG2'>internal temperature</a> of <a href='https://amzn.to/4kmobG2'>205F</a>.  Let cool in the pan for 15 minutes before transferring to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4qQNmn2'>wire rack</a> to cool completely before slicing
- <img src='/assets/Misc/Unhealthy/Rum Cake/baked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
