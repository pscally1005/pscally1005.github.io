Description: |
  What a great way to use up any leftover <a href='/misc/slow-cooker-chicken'>chicken fat</a> rendered out from <a href='/misc/chicken-leg-quarters'>roasting chicken leg quarters</a>.  Although not a healthy recipe, these biscuits are a minimal waste recipe, using any leftover <a href='/misc/slow-cooker-chicken'>chicken fat</a> or <a href='/misc/bone-broth'>broth</a> you acquired when prepping some <a href='/recipes/shredded-chicken'>shredded chicken</a> using <a href='/misc/chicken-leg-quarters'>leg quarters</a>.  They're delicious, roasty, crusty, and crumbly, making them a fun <a href='/recipes/sides'>side</a> at a gathering.<br><br>

  For a completely different biscuit recipe, check out my <a href='/recipes/vegan-biscuits'>Cauliflower Biscuits</a>, which are vegan, <a href='/misc/celiac'>gluten-free</a>, <a href='/misc/calories'>low calorie</a>, and healthy!
Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 425F, and line a <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>cookie sheet</a> with parchment paper.

- In a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>large bowl</a>, whisk together the dry ingredients - <a href='/misc/grains#white-wheat'>flour</a>, baking powder, rosemary, garlic powder, onion powder, and salt.

- Add in cold <a href='/misc/slow-cooker-chicken'>chicken fat</a> and cold <a href='/misc/bone-broth'>broth</a>. Mix with your hands (wearing <a href='https://amzn.to/414BfHP'>kitchen gloves</a>) until you have a fully combined dough.
- <img src='/assets/Misc/Unhealthy/Biscuits/White/bowl.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Divide the dough into 8 equal pieces, about <b>71 g</b> each. Roll into balls, place on the <a href='https://amzn.to/45sRAsB'>cookie sheet</a>, and lightly flatten.
- <img src='/assets/Misc/Unhealthy/Biscuits/White/raw1.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Optionally, brush the tops with <a href='/misc/olive-oil'>oil</a>, <a href='/misc/dairy#butter'>butter</a>, or <a href='/misc/slow-cooker-chicken'>chicken fat</a>. Optionally top with flaky salt, <a href='/misc/seeds#sesame-seeds'>sesame seeds</a>, or everything bagel seasoning
- <img src='/assets/Misc/Unhealthy/Biscuits/White/raw2.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Bake at 425F for about 15 minutes. They should be lightly golden brown, lightly cracked, a toothpick to the center will come out clean, and the <a href='https://amzn.to/4kmobG2'>internal temperature</a> will be about <a href='https://amzn.to/4kmobG2'>205F</a>.
- <img src='/assets/Misc/Unhealthy/Biscuits/White/baked.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- <a href='/misc/dairy#butter'>Butter</a> will work in place of <a href='/misc/slow-cooker-chicken'>chicken fat</a>.

- Water or <a href='/misc/dairy#buttermilk'>buttermilk</a> will work in place of <a href='/misc/slow-cooker-chicken'>chicken broth</a>.

- For a slightly healthier biscuit, replace the 2 2/3 cup of <a href='/misc/grains#white-wheat'>white flour</a> with either <a href='/misc/grains#whole-wheat'>whole wheat flour</a> (320 g) or <a href='/misc/grains#oats'>oat flour</a> (240 g).
