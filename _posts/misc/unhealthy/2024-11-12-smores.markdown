Description: It's starting to get cold outside, but that doesn't mean you can't still enjoy a s'more every now and then. You can whip one up in less than 5 minutes using your <a href='https://amzn.to/3FuWETp'>air fryer</a> or oven, and dare I say that these taste better than the ones over the campfire
Instructions:
- Preheat your <a href='https://amzn.to/3FuWETp'>air fryer</a> to 400F, and prepare a small piece of parchment paper. Break your <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#crackers'>graham cracker</a> in half
- <img src='/assets/Misc/Unhealthy/Smores/smores-ing.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- <a href='https://amzn.to/3FuWETp'>Air fry</a> the <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#crackers'>graham cracker</a>, chocolate, and marshmallow for about 2 minutes, or until golden. Top with the other half of the <a href='/misc/fake-healthy-foods#crackers'>cracker</a>, and enjoy
- <img src='/assets/Misc/Unhealthy/Smores/smores-airfry.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>
Notes:
- You can also use the oven as well. Use foil instead of parchment paper, and cook under the broiler for about 2 minutes, until golden
