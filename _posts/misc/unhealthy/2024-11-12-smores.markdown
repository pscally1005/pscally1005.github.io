Description: It's starting to get cold outside, but that doesn't mean you can't still enjoy a s'more every now and then. You can whip one up in less than 5 minutes using your air fryer or oven, and dare I say that these taste better than the ones over the campfire
Instructions: 
- Preheat your air fryer to 400F, and prepare a small piece of parchment paper. Break your graham cracker in half
- <center><img src='/assets/Misc/Unhealthy/Smores/smores-ing.jpg' alt="" class="instruction-image"></center>

- Air fry the graham cracker, chocolate, and marshmallow for about 2 minutes, or until golden. Top with the other half of the cracker, and enjoy
- <center><img src='/assets/Misc/Unhealthy/Smores/smores-airfry.jpg' alt="" class="instruction-image"></center>

- You can also use the oven as well. Use foil instead of parchment paper, and cook under the broiler for about 2 minutes, until golden
