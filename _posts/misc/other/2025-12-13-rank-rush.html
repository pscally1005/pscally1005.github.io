The game was developed in Unity, and is available to play from the link below. The game is playable both from your computer or your phone.

It features 2 levels, a final boss fight, character and difficulty selection, and a final score at the end. The goal of the game is to the highest rank possible. It is meant to be replayed in order to optimize your score and get a final S rank.
