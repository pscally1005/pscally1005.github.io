Description: These stuffed peppers are filled with ground turkey, red lentils, and my homemade <a href="/recipes/ketchup">Date Sweetened Ketchup</a>.  But they're super customizable.  This post is more of a guide on how to make stuffed peppers; below are different filling options you can try:<br><ul>
    <li>Replace the ground turkey with either ground beef or <a href="/misc/ground-chicken">ground chicken</a></li>
    <li>For a more classic (and vegetarian) stuffed pepper, use <a href="/recipes/rice-and-beans">Classic Rice and Beans</a></li>
    <li>Mediterranean stuffed peppers using my <a href="/recipes/mediterranean-potato-salad">Mediterranean Sweet Potato Salad</a> or <a href="/recipes/bean-salad">Mediterranean 3 Bean Salad</a></li>
    <li>Fill it with just beans, such as <a href="/recipes/slow-cooked-beans">Slow Cooker Black Beans</a>, <a href="/recipes/refried-beans">Refried Pinto Beans</a>, or <a href="/recipes/baked-beans">No Added Sugar Baked Beans</a><br></li>
    <li>Replace the <a href="/recipes/red-lentils">lentils</a> with quinoa, <a href="/recipes/cauliflower-rice">cauliflower rice</a>, brown rice, beans, or couscous<br></li></ul>
Instructions: 
- In a small pot, add your lentils, water, and chicken bouillon (or salt). Bring to a boil over high heat. Cover, reduce to medium, and let simmer for about 5 minutes, until fully cooked. Set aside. This is just my <a href="/recipes/red-lentils">Easy Red Lentils</a><br><br>
- <center><img src="/assets/Food/Ground Meat/Stuffed/stuffed-lentils.jpg" alt="" class="instruction-image"></center><br>

- Meanwhile, preheat your oven to 400F, and line an 8" square pan and a large cookie sheet with aluminum foil<br><br>

- Cut the tops of the peppers, and carefully scoop out the ribs and seeds without breaking the peppers. You can also use a small knife to carve a design like I did here. Reserve the pepper tops<br><br>
- <center><img src="/assets/Food/Ground Meat/Stuffed/stuffed-empty.jpg" alt="" class="half-page">&emsp;&emsp;<img src="/assets/Food/Ground Meat/Stuffed/stuffed-carved.jpg" alt="" class="half-page"></center><br>

- In a large bowl, mix together the filling - cooked lentils, ground meat, cheese, BBQ sauce, minced garlic, chili powder, garlic powder, onion powder, cumin, and salt<br><br>
- <center><img src="/assets/Food/Ground Meat/Stuffed/stuffed-filling.jpg" alt="" class="instruction-image"></center><br>

- Stuff the peppers, packing it down. Optionally add some more cheese on top. Place the top of the pepper back on<br><br>
- <center><img src="/assets/Food/Ground Meat/Stuffed/stuffed-cheese.jpg" alt="" class="half-page">&emsp;&emsp;<img src="/assets/Food/Ground Meat/Stuffed/stuffed-top.jpg" alt="" class="half-page"></center><br>

- Loosely cover the pan with foil, place on top of the cookie sheet (in case of dripping or spilling), and roast at 400F for about 1 hour. Remove the foil, and roast an additional 20-30 minutes. The peppers should be tender and the meat fully cooked (165F). Slice in half to eat<br><br>
- <center><img src="/assets/Food/Ground Meat/Stuffed/stuffed-cooked-1.jpg" alt="" class="half-page">&emsp;&emsp;<img src="/assets/Food/Ground Meat/Stuffed/stuffed-cooked-2.jpg" alt="" class="half-page"></center><br><br>

- To celebrate Halloween, feel free to carve a face into your peppers like Vic did below.  I also paired this with my <a href="/recipes/bbq-meatloaf">standard meatloaf recipe</a> that I instead shaped into a scary face.  I used fresh mozzarella and olives for the eyes, garlic cloves for the teeth, and <a href="/recipes/ketchup">Date Sweetened Ketchup</a> for the blood<br><br>
