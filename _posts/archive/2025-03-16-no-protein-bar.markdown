Description: This is a stupid idea I had the other day:<b></b> a protein bar, with no protein powder. Instead, I'll just be mixing together nonfat cottage cheese with powdered peanut butter until I get a thick dough, chill it, and slice into bars. With no protein powder, these lean protein bars still have 19 g of protein in under 150 calories, with no added sugar or artificial sweeteners
Instructions: 
- In a medium bowl, mix together all ingredients until you have a thick, sticky cookie dough - blended cottage cheese, powdered peanut butter, and (optional) vanilla extract
- <center><img src="/assets/Food/Meme/No protein bar/bowl.jpg" alt="" class="instruction-image"></center>

- Line a small container with parchment paper (I'm using a <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0C5ZZ32ZX?ref_=ppx_hzsearch_conn_dt_b_fed_asin_title_27">glass meal prep container</a>, which is 6.2 x 4.5"). Evenly press the dough into all sides of the pan
- <center><img src="/assets/Food/Meme/No protein bar/pan.jpg" alt="" class="instruction-image"></center>

- Freeze for about 1 hour before slicing into 4 bars. Wrap bar each in parchment paper, and store in an airtight bag in the freezer or refrigerator
