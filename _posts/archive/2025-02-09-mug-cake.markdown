Description: A protein mug cake a very quick and tasty way to make sure you're getting all your protein in. Instructions: 
- Mix together all ingredients in a mug. Optionally top with mini chocolate chips (1 tsp, 5 g) or honey (1 tsp, 7 g)

- Microwave on high for 30 seconds, mix with a spoon, and microwave for another 30 seconds. Let rest for 1 minute before eating
