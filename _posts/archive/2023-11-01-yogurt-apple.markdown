Description: Plain nonfat greek yogurt combined with whey protein powder makes a protein packed bowl to start your day off right.  I've made 4 different variations of yogurt bowls that you can easily prep the night before for an easy breakfast. We have <a href="/recipes/yogurt-pbj">PB&J</a>, Apple Pie, <a href="/recipes/yogurt-choc">Chocolate</a>, and <a href="/recipes/yogurt-banana">PB Banana</a> for you to enjoy
Instructions: 
- Dice up your apple, and air fry for 10 minutes at 300F to soften.  

- Mix all ingredients together in a bowl, and top with the cooked apple.  Cover, and refrigerate overnight

- If you want to soften the apple, you can air fry it in slices for 10 minutes at 300F before finely dicing it
