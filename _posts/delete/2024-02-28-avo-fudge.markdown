Description: This <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> fudge is free of both <a href='/misc/nuts'>nuts</a> and lactose.  Normal fudge tends to use evaporated or condensed milk, and most healthy fudges use <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut</a> or <a href='/misc/nuts#almonds'>almond butter</a>.  I've used <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>Homemade Natural Nut Butter</a> with <a href='/misc/seeds#sunflower-seeds'>sunflower seeds</a> instead, as well as a ripe <a href='/misc/fruit#avocado'>avocado</a>.  It's totally free of any <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar</a> or sweeteners, but still has a rich taste and texture, while being packed with <a href='/misc/protein'>protein</a> and healthy <a href='/misc/fats'>fats</a>!  For another fudge recipe, check out the <a href='/recipes/fudge'>Coconut Chocolate Fudge Squares</a>
Instructions:
- I've chosen to use homemade <a href='/misc/seeds#sunflower-seeds'>sun butter</a> here, but you can also make <a href='/misc/nuts#almonds'>almond</a> or <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a> if you desire. For my 8 cup <a href='https://amzn.to/4q0AUjI'>food processor</a>, I use about 12 oz (336 g) of <a href='/misc/seeds'>seeds</a> or <a href='/misc/nuts'>nuts</a>. Blend on high for a few minutes, until a super smooth <a href='/nut-butter'>nut butter</a> forms. Reserve 1/2 cup (128 g) in the <a href='https://amzn.to/4q0AUjI'>food processor</a>, and transfer the rest to a mason jar. Store the leftovers at room temperature for about a week
- <img src='/assets/Food/Protein Powder/Avo Fudge/avo-fudge-1.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Blend in the rest of the ingredients in the order above. Add the solids (cocoa powder, <a href='/misc/dairy#whey'>whey</a>, and <a href='/misc/dairy#casein'>casein</a>) last, in order to make sure everything else is fully combined. You will have a very thick paste. Optionally, add 1 tbsp (21 g) of honey if you want it a little sweeter
- <img src='/assets/Food/Protein Powder/Avo Fudge/avo-fudge-2.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Line an <a href='https://amzn.to/3YY2H9q'>9" square baking pan</a> with parchment paper, and press the mixture into all corners, making sure it is of even thickness. Remember, it's very thick, so this may take a bit of work
- <img src='/assets/Food/Protein Powder/Avo Fudge/avo-fudge-3.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Transfer to the freezer for a few hours to harden before slicing. Store leftovers in the freezer
