Description: This <a href='/misc/veggies#carrots'>carrot</a> jam is a twist on my classic <a href='/recipes/apple-spread'>No Sugar Added Apple Spread</a>. It simmers <a href='/misc/veggies#carrots'>carrots</a> with all the carrot cake spices, leaving your home smelling amazing. It tastes great on a PB&J or in your morning <a href='/yogurt'>yogurt</a> or <a href='/oatmeal'>oatmeal</a>. It's very simple, so give it a go.
Instructions:
- Wash and cut your <a href='/misc/veggies#carrots'>carrots</a>. If using <a href='/misc/veggies#carrots'>baby carrots</a>, you can add those right to the pot as is, no cutting needed. For large <a href='/misc/veggies#carrots'>carrots</a>, cut into strips, about the size of a <a href='/misc/veggies#carrots'>baby carrot</a>. Transfer to your pot

- To the pot, add your spices (cinnamon, <a href='/misc/veggies#ginger'>ginger</a>, nutmeg, cloves, and allspice), vanilla, and <a href='/misc/fruit#apple'>applesauce</a>. Mix together

- Pour in just enough water to cover the <a href='/misc/veggies#carrots'>carrots</a>. Cover and cook on medium heat with occasional stirring and mashing with a <a href='https://amzn.to/3Fw6MeC'>wooden spoon</a> (make sure nothing sticks and burns), until the <a href='/misc/veggies#carrots'>carrots</a> are tender. If all the water has cooked off but the <a href='/misc/veggies#carrots'>carrots</a> aren't soft yet, just cover again with water, and cook until the <a href='/misc/veggies#carrots'>carrots</a> are fork tender. I had to fill mine up with water 3 times

- Use an <a href='https://amzn.to/3VHhgMM'>immersion blender</a> to blend the jam as smoothly as possible. It should be about the consistency of <a href='/recipes/pumpkin-puree'>pumpkin puree</a>. Squeeze in the some <a href='/misc/fruit#lemon'>lemon</a> (the acid helps preserve it in the fridge for longer). Transfer to a jar, and store in the fridge
- <img src='/assets/Food/Sweet Spreads/Carrot/carrot-4.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- My batch weighed about 570g at the end. Assuming a 2 tbsp (32 g) serving, there are about 18 servings
