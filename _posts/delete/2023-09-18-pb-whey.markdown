Description: I came up with this idea, as it rhymes with PB&J, and serves as a tastier and healthier option for the classic sandwich. The added proteins in the <a href='/recipes/natural-peanut-butter'>peanut butter</a> and jelly serve to make the sandwich more filling, and without the added sugars of typical jelly or jam. To make it even better, serve this on <a href='/recipes/ww-bread'>100% Whole Wheat Bread</a>
Instructions:
- For the <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a> - in a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>small bowl</a> or glass, combine <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a>, <a href='https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07SXBL1GF?ref=t_ac_view_request_product_image&campaignId=amzn1.campaign.2L4DOI4F1KV3G&linkCode=tr1&tag=poormanprotei-20&linkId=amzn1.campaign.2L4DOI4F1KV3G_1767022097417'>powdered peanut butter</a>, and <a href='/misc/dairy#skim-milk'>milk</a>

- For the jelly - In a <a href='https://amzn.to/4adxMft'>small bowl</a>, mash the <a href='/misc/fruit#banana'>banana</a>. Mix with the <a href='/misc/dairy#whey'>protein powder</a> and cinnamon.  Add some sweetner if you like (1/4 tsp, 1.25 g <a href='https://amzn.to/3SqwsMO'>liquid monk fruit</a> or stevia)

- Bake in a preheated 350F oven for about 30 minutes or until a toothpick comes out almost clean, and <a href='https://amzn.to/4kmobG2'>internal temperature</a> is around 205F.  Transfer to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4qQNmn2'>wire rack</a> to cool completely

- Spread <a href='/misc/fruit#banana'>banana</a> spread on one slice of <a href='/recipes/bread'>bread</a>. Spread the <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanut butter</a> on the other.
