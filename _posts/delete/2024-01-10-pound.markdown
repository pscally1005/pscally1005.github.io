Description: A traditional pound cake is made with a pound each of <a href='/misc/meat#eggs'>eggs</a>, <a href='/misc/hidden-sugar'>sugar</a>, <a href='/misc/dairy#butter'>butter</a>, and <a href='/misc/grains#white-wheat'>flour</a>; that's it. I wanted to create a healthier version with equal parts of 4 ingredients, so I came up with this. I made this as a joke, but it works wells as a 4 ingredient healthy cake.
Instructions:
- Preheat your oven to 350F, and line 2 <a href='https://amzn.to/3YUjIkN'>9x5" bread pans</a> with parchment paper (only 1 if doing a 1/4 batch). Lightly <a href='https://amzn.to/3Hdg0gk'>spray the paper with oil</a>

- In a <a href='https://amzn.to/4q0AUjI'>food processor</a>, blend together all 4 ingredients (drained and rinsed <a href='/misc/beans#chickpeas'>chickpeas</a>, <a href='/misc/nuts#peanuts'>peanuts</a>, <a href='/misc/dairy#yogurt'>yogurt</a>, and <a href='/misc/fruit#banana'>bananas</a>) until everything is smooth
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meme/Pound/pound-2.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Evenly split the batter across the 2 pans. For a 1/4 batch, you'll only need 1 pan
- <img src='/assets/Food/Meme/Pound/pound-4.jpg' alt='' class='instruction-image'>

- Bake in a preheated 350F oven for about 45 minutes (uncovered), or until a toothpick comes out almost clean, and the <a href='https://amzn.to/4kmobG2'>internal temperature</a> is around <a href='https://amzn.to/4kmobG2'>200F</a>. For a 1/4 batch, it should take about 30 minutes, uncovered

- Transfer to a <a href='https://amzn.to/4qQNmn2'>wire rack</a> to cool completely. Slice the loaves, and store in the fridge or freezer
Notes:
- Optionally, add in some additional flavors. I went with a little salt and vanilla extract, but you can also add cinnamon or any other extract you have. Add a little <a href='/misc/natural-sweeteners#maple-syrup'>maple syrup</a> if you like it sweeter.  For some lift, add some baking powder (I've never tried, but I'd guess 2 tsp (9.6 g) for a full batch)
