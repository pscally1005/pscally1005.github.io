Description: This was something stupid I did for April Fool's Day, where I passed them off as chocolate covered <a href='/misc/fruit#raisins'>raisins</a>. This is a fun and harmless prank to anyone not expecting it.
Instructions:
- Preheat oven to 400F and line small baking sheet with aluminum foil

- Cut <a href='/misc/veggies#garlic'>garlic</a> in half and take out the cloves. Leave the skin on

- Line small baking sheet or plate with parchment paper. Add <a href='/misc/veggies#garlic'>garlic</a> into chocolate and mix, being carful to not break up cloves. Transfer cloves to parchment paper

- Refrigerate to cool and solidify the chocolate. Store in a Ziploc in the fridge

- Serve on April Fool's Day or to your worst enemy
Notes:
- If you're really evil, coat the <a href='/misc/veggies#garlic'>garlic cloves</a> raw, not roasted
