Description: Leave your peanut allergies at the door, because they're not allowed here. I was bored, and wanted to see if I could create something with only peanuts and peanut-like ingredients. Bonus points if you also have peanut oil on hand I guess then. These can serve as little <a href="/misc/fats">fat</a> and <a href="/misc/protein">protein</a> balls for a snack, or crumbled into yogurt.
Instructions: 
- Combine peanut butter and pb2 together in a medium bowl. Using a chopper, chop your nuts, and fold into the dough

- Roll the dough into balls, and store in the fridge
